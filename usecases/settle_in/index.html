Moment 6 · Protected

Protected from her very first tap

A new card in a new country looks like a target. Just before sunset, someone abroad tries her card details on an electronics site. The payment is declined before it completes, and her app tells her straight away. Behind that one alert:

  • Decision Intelligence Pro scores every authorisation in real time. It uses generative AI to weigh the relationships around a transaction against about a trillion data points, in under 50 milliseconds.
  • Identity Check (EMV 3-D Secure) verifies her when she pays online, usually invisibly and with a fingerprint or face when the risk calls for it.
  • Tokenisation (MDES) means the phone pays with a token, so her real card number is never handed to a shop.
  • Transaction Notifications alert her the moment the card is used, and zero liability means she isn't held responsible for fraud.

# Equity Bank: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Equity_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Equity × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (HY 2026 investor booklet, 19 Aug 2026; FY2025 results, 18 Mar 2026) unless marked; product and network detail is from Equity Bank Kenya's products and services handbook (Jul 2025). equitygroupholdings.com blocks automated readers, so Equity pages were read from Wayback captures, and Mastercard's newsroom was read from its content-exchange mirror. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources, are read from charts, or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Equity Group Holdings Plc (NSE: EQTY; cross-listed in Uganda, 2009). Founded 1984 as Equity Building Society. HQ Equity Centre, Upper Hill, Nairobi. 14,265 staff. Largest holder Arise BV, 12.76% (Mar 2023 ⚠). Market cap at Oct 2026 not found ⚠.
- H1 2026: PAT KES 45.5bn (+32%), total income KES 124.9bn (NFI 44.5%), assets KES 2.16tn, deposits KES 1.59tn, loans KES 981bn, ROE 26.5%, CIR 48.6%, NPL 9.5%. FY2025: PAT KES 75.5bn (+55%), dividend KES 5.75.
- Six banks: Kenya (14.3m accounts, No. 2 by assets at 12.4%), DRC (Equity BCDC, 4.2m, No. 2), Uganda (2.2m), Rwanda (1.9m, No. 2), Tanzania (455k), South Sudan (250k); Ethiopia representative office. Outside Kenya: 42% of banking profit, 54% of loans.
- Non-bank: Equity Insurance Group (GWP KES 6.4bn), Finserve/Equitel (revenue KES 1.77bn, +84%), Equity Investment Bank, Equity Afya (156 centres), Equity Group Foundation.
- Deals: ProCredit Congo (2015), BCDC 66.5% for US$95m (2020), Cogebanque 91.93% (2023–24).
- Leadership: Prof Isaac Macharia (Chair), Dr James Mwangi (Group MD and CEO), Samuel Kirubi (COO), Anthony Murage (CFO and transformation), Brent Malahay (strategy), Rene Kalonji (product); Moses Nyabanda (MD, Equity Bank Kenya).
- Strategy to 2030: 15 countries, 100 million customers, AI-enabled systems; "One Equity" shared KYC, wallet rails, AI models and APIs; a technology group with a digital-asset settlement line ⚠ no detail.
- Channels: 89.7% of transactions digital, 98.3% outside branches; 410 branches, 886 ATMs, 92,572 agents, 1.4m merchants. PWE 213.6m transactions and Equitel 195.7m in H1 2026 ⚠ read from charts.

## 2. Equity × Mastercard
- Debit: Visa and Mastercard classic and premium side by side; Mastercard Platinum, World and World Elite named in Fanikisha tiers; proprietary Equity card; USD Gold debit on Visa.
- Credit: Visa Classic and Gold; Amex Green and Gold (exclusive Kenyan issuer); Mastercard corporate and SME credit.
- Prepaid on Mastercard at launch (Feb 2015) ⚠ current network not stated.
- Acquiring: all networks; exclusive Amex acquirer in Kenya (2018); Visa acquiring awards (2021).
- Jan 2013 Mastercard partnership (five million debit cards targeted). 18 Sep 2023 ten-year Customer Business Agreement for six countries (World/World Elite, corporate, virtual, cross-border, e-commerce, QR, tap on phone, Community Pass, anti-cybercrime); signed by James Mwangi and Samuel Makome with Mastercard's Mark Elliott and Shehryar Ali. Apr 2024 Mastercard Cross-Border Services at Kenyan branches (30 countries).
- Not found: live Community Pass, QR or tap on phone; tokenisation; Mastercard fraud tools; any 2025–26 Visa agreement ⚠.
- Market: M-Pesa 89.1% of mobile-money subscriptions, KES 182.7bn revenue FY2026; PesaLink about KES 1.1tn (2024), M-Pesa can send to banks via PesaLink (Sep 2025); CBK fast payment system planned ⚠; KCB issues Mastercard World Elite credit (2023).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Community Pass for value chains and agents; QR and tap on phone for 1.4m PWE merchants; cross-border send in the app and on Equitel; virtual commercial cards for Equity Online for Business; fraud and cyber tools on the One Equity KYC build; Agent Pay for the AI plan; digital-asset settlement ⚠.

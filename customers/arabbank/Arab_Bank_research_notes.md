Companion to `Arab_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Arab Bank × Mastercard), condensed. ## 1. The company
- Arab Bank plc, Amman (ASE-listed; 640.8m shares, JOD 6.85 at end-2025, about JOD 4.4bn ⚠ derived). Founded Jerusalem 1930; Amman since 1948. 600+ branches in 28 countries; Arab Bank plc 187 branches, 7,216 staff; group 13,013 staff.
- H1 2026: net profit USD 570.9m (+7%), assets USD 80.3bn, loans USD 42.1bn, deposits USD 58.8bn, CAR 17.3%, LDR 72%. FY2025: record USD 1.13bn (+12%), revenue about USD 3.6bn, 40% cash dividend.
- First in Jordan: 25.7% of assets, 25.9% of deposits, 20.0% of credit. Palestine about 20%; Oman Arab Bank (49%) about 10%; Arab National Bank (40%) about 6–7% in Saudi Arabia.
- Owners: Social Security Corporation 17.2%, Shoman Foundation 5.0%, Saudi Ministry of Finance 4.5%, Masri family interests. Ratings: CI BB- (Jul 2025) ⚠; big three not confirmed ⚠.
- Leadership: Sabih Masri (Chair), Khaled Masri (Deputy Chair), Randa Sadik (CEO), Mohammed Masri, Naim Al-Hussaini, Eric Modave (Deputy CEOs), Firas Zayyad (CFO).
- Business lines: Consumer Banking & Wealth Management; Corporate & Institutional Banking; Treasury. Brands: Reflect (2021), Arabi Mobile, Acabes (2021) and Omnify (2024), AB Xelerate, AB Ventures, Arab Bank Switzerland (CHF 18bn AUM after Gonet–Swiss One merger, 2025).
- Strategy: Iraq (2025), Syria reactivated and Algeria Islamic window (2026); 30 GenAI uses, Arabi GPT, ReVA (Mar 2026); Kinexys (Apr 2026), Citi 24/7 USD (Oct 2026), Reflect crypto with Fuze (Oct 2026).

## 2. Arab Bank × Mastercard
- Jordan cards: 12 Visa, 2 Mastercard (World Elite; UEFA Champions League Platinum), 1 Amex (issuing from 2025). All debit, Reflect cards, co-brands (Nashama, LALIGA, Royal Jordanian), Visa Infinite Privilege and the corporate card are Visa.
- UAE, Egypt, Palestine: Mastercard World Elite, Platinum (and Titanium in UAE, Egypt) beside Visa. Jaywan and Himyan integrated 2025.
- RemitEx on Mastercard Cross-Border Services, 28 countries (22 Jan 2024; Cynthia El Khoury, Mastercard Levant & Iraq).
- Campaigns: UEFA final (2019, 2025; Ahmed Abdel-Karim Hussein, Mastercard EEMEA), World Elite Cannes (2023).
- Visa firsts: corporate digital card (2021), Visa Direct (2023), Visa Arabi Switch (2023), Click to Pay (2025), CyberSource in Egypt (2025). No public network agreement ⚠.
- Acceptance: Visa, Mastercard, Amex (2022), JCB (2025), CliQ, MobiCash; government acquirer and consular gateway (2025).

## 3. Market
- JoPACC 2025: 363.8m payments, JOD 93.3bn; CliQ 167.9m (JOD 20bn, volume doubled), 2.13m users; JoMoPay 102m (JOD 6.3bn), 2.81m wallet users. Open-finance instructions end-2022 ⚠ secondary. JoRegBox sandbox.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- More RemitEx corridors and push payments; issuing for Omnify's Cards as a Service; fraud and identity for CliQ; commercial and virtual cards; crypto-linked cards and stablecoin settlement.

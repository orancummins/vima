# MCB Group (The Mauritius Commercial Bank) — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Mauritius_Commercial_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and MCB × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (FY 2025/26 results, year to 30 June 2026, announced 28 Sep 2026; Annual Report 2025, year to 30 June 2025) unless marked. MCB publishes no card counts by network. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q1 FY27 figures were not out at 17 October 2026.

## 1. The company
- MCB Group Ltd (SEM-listed, about Rs 112bn market cap and ~36% of SEMDEX at June 2025 ⚠ not updated); main bank The Mauritius Commercial Bank Ltd, open since 1 Sep 1838 in Port Louis. Moody's Baa3/P-3 deposits; CARE AAA domestic.
- FY26: profit to shareholders Rs 20.1bn (+11.3%), operating income Rs 47.1bn, ROE 16.0%, C/I 38.1%, assets Rs 1,283bn, loans Rs 500bn, customer deposits Rs 881bn, CAR 20.3%, NPL 2.0%, tax rate 26.4% after new Mauritian measures. About 56% of MCB Ltd profit foreign-sourced.
- Pillars: Home Markets (Rs 22.1bn income; retail and business banking, Mauritian and regional corporates, overseas banks) and African CIBPB (Rs 24.9bn; commodity trade finance about Rs 101bn).
- Market share (MCB Ltd): ~39% of domestic credit, ~50% of rupee deposits. ~1.29m group customers, ~4,900 staff (June 2025).
- Footprint: MCB Madagascar (80%), Seychelles, Maldives; BFCOI 49.99%; hubs in Johannesburg, Nairobi, Lagos, Paris, Dubai.
- Leadership: Jayananda Nirsimloo (Chair), Jean Michel Ng Tseung (Group CE), Dipak Chummun (Group CFO), Thierry Hebraud (CEO MCB Ltd; MBA chair Jun 2026), Stephanie Ng Tseung (Head of Payments).
- Vision 2030 (Jun 2025). Cloud (Calypso on Nasdaq CapCloud), MCB Wave, AI strategy; IT costs +19.2% in FY26. USD 400m bond, USD 450m syndicated loan, USD 1bn African trade envelope (May 2026), first e-Bill of Exchange with ETG (Sep 2026).

## 2. MCB × Mastercard
- Personal debit in Mauritius is an MCB Mastercard Debit Card; online debit payments from Feb 2016 (Nov 2015 deal) and 675,000 cards made contactless from Oct 2016 (Binesh Mangar, MCB; Daniel Monehin, Mastercard).
- Credit: Classic as Mastercard or Visa; Mastercard Primo; Gold network not stated ⚠; premium Visa Platinum and Signature; Amex Green and Gold.
- Corporate FCY Mastercard credit card (USD/EUR/GBP/ZAR, FY 2024/25). Visa Business Debit for SMEs (21,000+). Fleetman network not stated ⚠.
- Prepaid: Visa; UnionPay (2013); Cash Passport with Mastercard Prepaid Management Services (policy Jun 2022) ⚠ current scope.
- Juice Tap launched with Visa (Dec 2024), Mastercard added later ⚠ date; ~13% of Visa cards tokenised by Jun 2025; Tap to Pay 1.4% of transactions in 2025. Apple Pay on Visa and Mastercard from 19 May 2026, announced by Visa.
- Acquiring: about 18,000 merchants (card or MauCAS QR), first UnionPay acquirer (2010), Amex acquirer, ~3,400 Android POS.
- Overseas: Seychelles and Madagascar on Visa; MCB Maldives ended all card services on 30 Sep 2026.
- No public network agreement, renewal or switch found 2015–2026.

## 3. Competitors and market
- Visa (premium, SME, prepaid, islands, wallet launches), Amex, UnionPay. MauCAS (BoM switch, instant payments since Aug 2019) and MauCAS QR: QR payments +67% in 2025 vs contactless +4%. Telco wallets my.t money and Blink ⚠. Banks SBM (World Elite Mastercard, 2019), Absa, MauBank ⚠ no shares. National Fintech Strategy 2026–2030 (25 Jun 2026) ⚠.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Token growth (MDES, Click to Pay) past 1.4% of transactions; fraud and identity for the 93% of online spend on foreign sites; a World or World Elite tier; Mastercard Move for remittances and payouts; commercial and virtual cards for the African CIB push; cards for the Visa-only Seychelles and Madagascar banks; card-linked offers.

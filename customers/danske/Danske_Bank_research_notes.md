# Danske Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Danske_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Danske × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (H1 2026 interim report, 17 Jul 2026; Forward '28 update, 30 Apr 2026; Annual Report 2025, 5 Feb 2026) unless marked. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are due 29 October 2026.

## 1. The company
- Danske Bank A/S (Nasdaq Copenhagen: DANSKE), Copenhagen; founded 1871 as Den Danske Landmandsbank. Denmark's largest bank. About 5m retail customers ⚠ (secondary; no group count published).
- H1 2026: net profit DKK 11.9bn, ROE 13.9%, C/I 44.4%, CET1 17.0%, loans DKK 1,794bn, deposits DKK 1,135bn, 19,472 FTE. FY2025: net profit DKK 23.0bn, ROE 13.3%. 2026 guidance raised to DKK 23–25bn. Market value about DKK 290bn at 30 Jun 2026 ⚠ derived.
- Segments: Personal Customers; Business Customers; Large Corporates & Institutions; Danica; Northern Ireland; Group Functions.
- Brands: Danske Bank, Realkredit Danmark, Danica, Danske Invest, Nordania, Danske Bank UK (Northern Bank). Associate: Vipps MobilePay (about 28% ⚠).
- Leadership: Martin Blessing (Chair), Carsten Egeriis (CEO), Cecile Hillary (CFO), Frans Woelders (COO), Magnus Agustsson (CRO, Risk and Compliance), Christian Bornfeld, Johanna Norberg, Joachim Alpen; Julie-Ann Haines (NI CEO from 1 Jul 2026).
- Forward '28 (2024–28), updated 30 Apr 2026: ROE >14.5%, C/I ≤43%, CET1 ~16%, income ~DKK 63bn by 2028; tech/AI spend ~DKK 4.5bn a year; AWS cloud migration.
- Deals: Aiia stake to Mastercard (2021); MobilePay into Vipps MobilePay (2022); Norway personal banking to Nordea (Nov 2024); Qivalis stablecoin founder (Sep 2025). DOJ probation closed Dec 2025.

## 2. Danske × Mastercard
- Denmark: Mastercard Hverdag+ debit (can replace Visa/Dankort), Mastercard Basis/Guld/Platin credit, World Elite (Private Banking). Visa/Dankort is the only Visa card. Split of debit by network not public ⚠.
- Finland, Sweden, Northern Ireland: all cards Mastercard; Click to Pay in Finland and Sweden; Priceless Cities on NI debit.
- Business: Mastercard Corporate Platinum/Gold/Classic, Business Debit, Corporate Travel Account, Global Data Repository feeds.
- Cashback til Mastercard (2,000+ merchants, Loyalty Key); Mastercard-only.
- Aiia sold to Mastercard (7 Sep 2021) ⚠ current use of its aggregation.
- Agent Pay: Denmark's first AI-agent payment, 21 Sep 2026 (Mark Wraa-Hansen, Danske; Erik Gutwasser, Mastercard Northern Europe), with passkeys, tokens, PayOS.
- Nets processes all Nordic card transactions (five-year deal, Feb 2019) ⚠ renewal; Dankort deal Feb 2019.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Move remaining Visa/Dankort holders to Mastercard debit; take Agent Pay to customers and to corporate AI assistants via Danske's MCP server; open finance and pay-by-bank; passkeys and fraud tools at scale; virtual and purchasing cards for businesses; fleet cards for Nordania.
- Limits: Visa/Dankort and Dankort's price advantage; MobilePay owns P2P; Nets processing; Qivalis.

# Isracard — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Isracard_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Isracard × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Isracard's own where its website gives them (About page: 4.8m active cards, NIS 13.1bn credit at end-Q2 2026) and otherwise from Globes (2019–2026). Isracard's investor-relations pages and the Bank of Israel site were blocked, so 2025 and Q2 2026 results come from third-party summaries (Bakshi Finance, Simply Wall St). Card networks are read from card art on isracard.co.il. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Isracard Ltd (TASE: ISCD), Bnei Brak. Israel's largest card group: >4.8m active cards (Isracard, Mastercard, American Express exclusive, Visa), >100,000 SMEs, NIS 13.1bn credit (Q2 2026). About 47% card share in 2020 ⚠.
- Ownership: separated from Bank Hapoalim (IPO Apr 2019; last 33% distributed Mar 2020). Harel deal blocked (Jan 2024). Delek Group (Yitzhak Tshuva) took control: shareholder vote Feb 2025 at NIS 3.56bn; BoI permit 9 Jul 2025; about 40% ⚠. One third-party profile wrongly names Harel as controller.
- Leadership: CEO Itamar Furman (2026); chair Tamar Yassur ⚠; earlier CEOs Ran Oz, Ron Wexler.
- Results ⚠: 2025 revenue NIS 3.52bn, reported loss NIS 40m after a ~NIS 345m one-off provision (unexplained), adjusted profit NIS 305m; Q2 2026 net income NIS 80m on revenue NIS 871m. Ratings Baa1/A/A ⚠.
- Group: Premium Express (Amex issuing), Europay (Eurocard) Israel, Isracard Finance (loans), Isracard Insurance Agency (2020).
- Deals: FLY CARD ten-year deal with El Al (Mar 2026; NIS 120–160m pre-tax a year expected; NIS 75m settlement with CAL, Jun 2026); Esh Bank MOU (Mar 2026) lapsed Jul 2026.

## 2. Isracard × Mastercard
- Mastercard on TOP, BASIC, CashBack+, World Elite, LifeStyle (Super-Pharm), Superfly (Rami Levy), FLY CARD Premium and Premium Select, Nateanchik prepaid. Base FLY CARD network not shown ⚠.
- Visa on W and Mami; Visa for some Hapoalim bank cards ⚠. American Express: Platinum, Gold, CashBack Premium, FLY CARD Amex Premium.
- Heritage: Eurocard Israel 1975; Mastercard agreement renewed 2013 ⚠. First Digital Bank cards on Isracard Mastercard (May 2020) ⚠ status.
- First agentic purchase in Israel on Agent Pay with Hapoalim and Isracard (LinkedIn, undated) ⚠.
- Acquiring of all brands for 100,000+ SMEs; business payment account at 0.95%.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- FLY CARD migration (updater, BIN tools); card-linked offers for dozens of clubs; Agent Pay at scale; merchant risk tools for acquiring; commercial-card controls for purchasing and B2B cards; FX and travel tools.

## 4. Market
- Three card groups: Isracard (Delek), Max (Clal), CAL (Discount 72%/FIBI 28%; sale to Union/Harel collapsed Sep 2026). Bit and PayBox lead P2P ⚠; Bank of Israel instant payments and request-to-pay ⚠. Lean-bank reform expected within a year.

## 5. How the page reads Isracard
- Five discs: Foundation (scale, ownership, leadership, licences) · Rails (Mastercard cards, Visa, Amex, FLY CARD, bank issuing, acquiring, wallets and instant, agentic) · Services (fraud and disputes, data, clubs, service) · Business lines (consumer credit, business finance and cards, employer cards, insurance and savings, retail co-brands) · Access (app, branches, business site).

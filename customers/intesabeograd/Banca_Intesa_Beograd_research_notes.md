## 1. The company
- Banca Intesa a.d. Beograd, Novi Beograd; founded 1991; 100% Intesa Sanpaolo (since 2005); unlisted. 1.34m clients, 134 branches, 3,044 staff (end-2024).
- 2024: total assets RSD 1,029bn (about €8.8bn), profit after tax RSD 26.8bn, CAR 19.9%. 2025: net profit about €265m (RSD 31bn), first in Serbia; loans +14.4%; cash loans >€980m (+63%). End-2025 shares: 15.3% of net assets, 15.6% of deposits.
- Parent: International Banks Division (12 countries) earned €611m in H1 2026; group €5.6bn.
- Divisions (Jan 2026 chart): Retail & Wealth Management (incl. small business), Corporate & SME, Governance (ESG, Data & AI, antifraud), Risk, Operations & Transformation, Finance. The page's revenue keys follow this ⚠ (segment note not read).
- Leadership: Draginja Đurić (chair), Darko Popović (CEO since 2022; chair of the Association of Serbian Banks from 1 Oct 2026), Dragica Mihajlović (CFO).
- 2022–2025 strategy: revenue and fees, digitalisation, lending value, credit quality, reskilling, ESG. No published 2026+ local plan ⚠.
- Awards: The Banker Bank of the Year 2025 (12th); Euromoney Best Bank 2026 (9th).

## 2. Banca Intesa × Mastercard
- 2002: first Mastercard issuer in Serbia ⚠ year inferred; July 2005: >90% of Serbian Mastercard turnover; June 2006: 670,000 of 800,000 cards on Mastercard, first chip Mastercard.
- Joined Visa October 2003; Visa Inspire packaged debit from 2012; Visa 78.7% of cards by Nov 2017; Visa Infinite debit 2017.
- May 2026 tariff: debit is Visa (Inspire, Classic Debit, Platinum, Infinite, Internet) and DinaCard only. Credit: Mastercard Standard ×2, Gold, Platinum; Visa Classic, Gold, Paralympic; Amex (exclusive since 2006). Business: Visa and Dina debit, Mastercard Business credit.
- Apple Pay for Visa Dec 2020, Mastercard Apr 2021; Mastercard Platinum credit 2021 with Mastercard concierge and Flight Delay Pass.
- Acquiring: 20,000 POS; 21% of POS terminals, 26% of POS turnover, 71% of internet acquiring (2024). Member of the "Bolji način" cashless initiative launched by GIZ, Mastercard, Visa, the finance ministry and NALED.
- No network agreement or switch announcement found ⚠; no Click to Pay, Installments, fraud or Agent Pay work found ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Air Serbia co-brand card (letter of intent 21 May 2026; network not announced ⚠); offers in Galerija popusta; Click to Pay and tokens on the dominant e-commerce gateway; online instalments; commercial and virtual cards; scam and identity signals.

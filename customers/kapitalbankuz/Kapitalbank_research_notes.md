## 1. The company
- JSCB Kapitalbank, Tashkent; licensed April 2001; merged with Aviabank 2004; Central Bank licence No. 69. Uzum group (Finance TCI / Djuzum SPV) has held 61.54% since 28 Dec 2022; Telecominvest exited. Uzum raised ~$70m at ~$1.5bn (Aug 2025).
- Apelsin, built by Kapitalbank, became Uzum Bank in Nov 2022 and is now a separately capitalised sister bank ⚠ separation date. Kapitalbank's own retail app is KAPITALBANK.Online (v3.0, Feb 2025); SME app is Uzum Business Kapitalbank (Oct 2023).
- FY2025: assets UZS 57.1trn ($4.75bn, +21.3%), loans UZS 36.8trn, retail deposits UZS 25.7trn, corporate UZS 19.5trn, net profit UZS 1.94trn ($156m), ROE 28.9%, ROA 3.7%, C:I 35.8%, NPL 4.9%.
- 1 Jul 2026: assets UZS 62.7trn — largest bank without a state share (~6% of system); 3rd by deposits; 1st in retail deposits (UZS 28.1trn). Systemically important for a second year.
- Ratings: Moody's Ba3 stable (2 Jul 2026), S&P BB-/B positive (Jul 2026), Fitch B positive (Jul 2025).
- 2m+ retail clients, ~100k entrepreneurs, 90+ offices, 1,500+ ATMs, 1.7m+ cards, ~4,000 staff (2025–26).
- Leadership: Supervisory Board chair Maksud Abdusamadov; Management Board chair Kirill Maevskiy (since Jan 2023); First Deputy Sardor Ismailov; Konstantin Tyan; Ulugbek Tavakkalov (retail) ⚠ boards from 2025 snapshots.
- Tech: CS "B2" core (Feb 2025); four processing links (Uzcard domestic and international, HUMO, own Tieto-based centre for international cards); ISO 27001/27701 (2025). BNY dollar account (Sep 2026); BACEE (Aug 2026).

## 2. Kapitalbank × Mastercard
- Network split: domestic on Uzcard and HUMO; international debit Visa first (best Visa issuer 2022–24, Visa Direct leader, first on CyberSource, Visa Business Platinum first) and Mastercard second (Standard, Gold, World, Sum/Euro, Braille Gold).
- Premium: Kapital Premium pairs Visa Infinite with Mastercard World Elite Metal Edge; Kapital Privilege pairs Visa Infinite with Mastercard Black Edition. Multicurrency Black Edition and World Elite from Dec 2025 ⚠.
- Mastercard Business corporate card (Jul 2024); MoneySend in the app; Cashback Abroad (Nov 2024); travel draw incl. Champions League final (Dec 2025); Yandex Eats/Lavka, My Taxi, iTicket offers to 31 Dec 2026.
- Mastercard issuing followed a Sep 2022 tender for 300,000 cards ⚠ first issue date. No network agreement, renewal or named executives found.
- Sister Uzum Bank is Visa-only: 5m+ Uzum Visa cards with embedded credit (Mar 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Tokenisation as Apple Pay and Google Pay arrive (tokenisation the open issue, Jul 2026) on the bank's own processing; scam and fraud tools; AI credit scoring (Mastercard–Xalq Bank template, May 2026); acceptance and merchant risk as UzQR (mandatory Jul 2026) and the 2027 National Payment Switch shift flows; commercial cards against Visa Business; remittances against Visa Direct; Uzum's mass market.

## 4. Market
- 68.3m cards (1 Jan 2026), 430,762 terminals, UZS 460trn terminal payments in 2025. HUMO bought by Paynet ($65m, Dec 2024), UnionPay–HUMO co-badge (Jul 2026), UPI via UzQR (Aug 2026). Mastercard: Central Bank partnership (2022), Tashkent office (May 2023), HUMO co-badge (2021). State banks hold 62% of assets.

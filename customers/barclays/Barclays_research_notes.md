## 1. Barclays — the company
- Barclays PLC, 1 Churchill Place, London (999-year leasehold bought June 2026); LSE (BARC) and NYSE (BCS). About 93,000 employees. Market value about £57.7bn (16 Oct 2026).
- H1 2026: income £16.5bn (+11%), PBT £6.1bn, RoTE 14.8%, CET1 14.3%, assets £1,730bn. FY2025: income £29.1bn, RoTE 11.3%. 2026 income guidance ~£31.5bn; 2028 RoTE >14%.
- Divisions: Barclays UK (ring-fenced; >20m customers; £176.7bn mortgages; 206 branches), UK Corporate Bank, Private Bank & Wealth Management (£142.5bn invested), Investment Bank (#6 globally), US Consumer Bank (20 partners, ~$29.7bn receivables, Best Egg).
- Deals: Kensington (Apr 2023), Tesco Bank (1 Nov 2024), GM cards (Aug 2025), Best Egg (1 May 2026), GoHenry (agreed 12 Jun 2026); German consumer finance, Entercard and AAdvantage (to Citi, 24 Apr 2026) out; Barclaycard Payments partnership with Brookfield (Apr 2025; not a sale yet).
- Leadership: Nigel Higgins (Chair), C.S. Venkatakrishnan (CEO), Anna Cross (FD), Vim Maru (UK), Matt Hammerstein (UK Corporate Bank), Denny Nealon (US Consumer Bank), Sasha Wiggins (PBWM).
- Technology: ~90% cloud; own AI platform; ~100,000 Copilot licences; BARXBot; 450m+ UK payments a month screened by ML; external fraud losses £180m (2025).

## 2. Barclays × Mastercard
- No group-level Barclays–Mastercard agreement or joint initiative found (2015–2026). Visa is the UK scheme partner (since 1966; multi-year European deal renewed 10 Feb 2020, term undisclosed).
- UK: debit Visa; Barclaycard Rewards Visa; Platinum, Forward, Amazon ⚠ likely Visa; Avios and Avios Plus Mastercard (2022); Tesco Bank credit cards historically Mastercard ⚠.
- US: JetBlue (Premier World Elite Mastercard, Jan 2025), Emirates, Miles & More, Frontier (renewed 30 Jun 2026), GM (Mastercard, from Goldman), AARP, Carnival (Sep 2026), Gap, Barnes & Noble (renewed 9 Jun 2026) on Mastercard; Wyndham, Holland America and the Samsung Galaxy Card (Jul 2026) on Visa; AAdvantage (Mastercard) to Citi; Hawaiian closed.
- Not named among Consumer Fraud Risk banks; no Agent Pay announcement; Ubyx stablecoin stake (Jan 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Consumer Fraud Risk under 50:50 APP reimbursement; Agent Pay on the Mastercard portfolios; keeping Tesco on Mastercard through any re-platforming; Ethoca, Click to Pay and merchant risk tools at Barclaycard Payments; new US co-brands; UK debit or Barclaycard if the Visa deal comes up (timing unknown).

## 1. The company
- Bank of China Limited (SSE: 601988; HKEX: 3988). Founded February 1912; once China's central bank, then its foreign-exchange bank; listed 2006. Central Huijin 58.59%, Ministry of Finance 8.64% (after a RMB165bn placement in 2025). S&P A, Moody's A1, Fitch A. G-SIB since 2011. Market value about US$209bn ⚠ (third-party, July 2026).
- H1 2026: operating income RMB357.1bn (+8.41%), profit RMB132.0bn (+4.67%), attributable RMB123.6bn (+5.10%), assets RMB40.19trn, loans RMB24.74trn, deposits RMB26.83trn, NIM 1.27%, ROE 8.66%, NPL 1.22%, CET1 12.04%. FY2025: profit RMB257.9bn, assets RMB38.36trn.
- Segments (H1 income): Corporate 38.0%, Personal 39.1%, Treasury 14.0%, Investment banking and insurance 5.5%. Geography: mainland 78.6%, HK/Macao/Taiwan 17.0%, other 4.4%.
- About 554m domestic personal customers, 8.4m+ corporate customers; 310,898 employees; 10,249 mainland institutions; 532 overseas institutions in 64 countries.
- Leadership: Ge Haijiao (Chairman, also BOCHK Chairman), Zhang Hui (Vice Chairman, President, CCO), EVPs Cai Zhao, Wu Jian, Yang Jun, Liu Chenggang, Huang Xueling. CRO post vacated Jan 2026 ⚠. BOCHK CE Sun Yu.
- AI+: BOCAI platform with agents, 2,200+ assistants, 32,000+ users; automation in 3,800+ scenarios; 55,000 cloud servers.
- Cards: 750.5m+ debit and 151.9m credit cards issued (cumulative, June 2026). Credit spending RMB535bn in H1 2026; card loans fell RMB486bn → RMB449bn. Mobile banking: 313m registered, 105m MAU (end-2025).
- Inbound: "leading" foreign-card acquirer; volume +75.73% (2025), +64.35% (H1 2026). e-CNY RMB10.8bn of spending in H1 2026; mBridge RMB600bn+ cumulative.
- BOCHK H1 2026: assets HK$4.77trn, profit HK$24.36bn, ROE 13.18%; card fees HK$1,474m (+12.2%); BoC Pay+ users +8.1%; BoC Bill +17.6%.

## 2. BOC × Mastercard
- 1987: first Chinese bank to join Mastercard and Visa. 1988: Great Wall International Card (V1), a Mastercard, China's first international credit card. 1992: card centre acquires Mastercard and Visa in China.
- 28 Jun 2022: BOCHK and Mastercard launch BOC Chill Card (Huang Jinyue, Joanne Lam of BOC Credit Card (International); Helena Chen, Mastercard).
- Nov 2023: Mastercard NetsUnion licensed; 9 May 2024 opened; BOC among first issuers with the Great Wall Mastercard series, usable at home and abroad, bindable to WeChat Pay and Alipay.
- 2025: Mastercard dual-application credit card system; domestic use enabled for single-branded Mastercard cards. H1 2026: Mastercard RMB debit at smart counters; Apple Pay for Visa and Mastercard cards.
- BOCHK: Chill (World/Platinum Mastercard), Taobao and Esso World Mastercards; Visa on Cheers (Infinite/Signature), Bliss, SOGO, Henderson, HK Airlines and business cards; UnionPay on Go, dual-currency and commercial cards.
- Others: Visa–UnionPay dual-brand cards to chip from May 2025; Visa partner at Beijing 2022; BOC Private Bank Amex Card (2011); BOC JCB and Maestro cards ⚠ (legacy pages).
- Visa won PBOC approval to prepare domestic clearing on 13 Oct 2026; UnionPay dominates bank cards.
- Not found: a BOC–Mastercard network agreement, tokenisation beyond Apple Pay, Click to Pay, Send/Move, open finance, fraud tools or Agent Pay.

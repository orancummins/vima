## 1. The company
- Bank of Cyprus Holdings plc (Irish holding company; Euronext Athens BOCHGR and Cyprus Stock Exchange; London delisted 19 Sep 2024). 436m shares; Senvest 9.7%, Lamesa 9.5%. Market cap not confirmed ⚠.
- Founded 1899; absorbed Laiki in the 2013 bail-in; Greek, Russian, Romanian and UK businesses sold or closed by 2024.
- H1 2026: profit after tax €252m (+7%), ROTE 18.8%, NII €369m, NIM 2.83%, cost:income 36%, performing loans €11.4bn, deposits €22.8bn, CET1 20.9%, NPE 1.0%. FY2025: profit €481m, ROTE 18.6%, record new lending €3.0bn.
- Ratings: Moody's A3 (deposits), S&P BBB-, Fitch BBB. 2,850 staff and 56 branches (end-2025). Serves about three quarters of Cypriots.
- Business lines: Consumer, SME, Corporate, IBU & International, Insurance (EuroLife #1 life ~33%; Genikes #2 non-life ~16%), JCC (75%), Jinius, REMU.
- Leadership: Takis Arapoglou (Chair), Georgios Syrichas (Vice-Chair), Panicos Nicolaou (CEO), Eliza Livadiotou (Finance), George Kousis (Technology & Operations), Demetris Nicolaou (CDO). Full ExCo list not read ⚠.
- 2026–28: mid-teens ROTE, payout up to 90% (2026) and 100% (2027–28), loans +4% a year, international book to ~€2bn.
- Deals: Ethniki Insurance (Jul 2025), CDB loans (~€150m) and deposits (~€500m) (Mar 2026, completion H2 2026), Wealthyhood 26% (Q1 2026).
- Digital: 517k digital users, 488k app users, 259k QuickPay users; Fleksy BNPL; digital housing loans; Joey; Avenga/Azure conversational AI (Dec 2025); Euromoney best digital bank 2026.

## 2. Bank of Cyprus × Mastercard
- Dual-network issuer: debit (Visa Ocean/Classic/Gold/Platinum; Mastercard Classic/Gold/Platinum), credit (same), business debit and credit on both. No split published ⚠.
- Mastercard only: Aegean Mastercard co-brand (c.2021 ⚠; benefits cut May 2026), Elite Mastercard, Mastercard Business Top Up prepaid.
- Visa only: Click to Pay, Visa+ behind QuickPay, Prepaid Visa.
- Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Garmin Pay for all Visa and Mastercard cards.
- JCC (75%): acquiring and processing, €9.2bn H1 2026, all networks; JCCsmart × Mastercard bill campaign (Sep–Dec 2025); JCCsmart × Visa (Sep 2026); ECB digital euro pilot (Jul 2026).
- No Mastercard fraud, open-banking, Send/Move or Agent Pay deployment found ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Click to Pay for Mastercard cards; Move against Visa+; Agent Pay and enrichment for conversational banking; card-linked offers in antamivi and Jinius; Installments behind Fleksy; acquirer-risk tools at JCC; commercial cards for international clients.

# Shinhan Card — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Shinhan_Card_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Shinhan Card × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Shinhan Financial Group's (2Q 2026 results, 23 Jul 2026; FY2025 results) unless marked. Most primary sources are Shinhan Card's own Korean press releases and history pages, translated. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary or undated sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Shinhan Card Co., Ltd., Seoul; 100% owned by Shinhan Financial Group (KRX: 055550; NYSE: SHG). Integrated with LG Card on 1 Oct 2007. About 28m cardholders ⚠ (secondary, May 2026).
- H1 2026: net income ₩253.4bn (+2.8%), Q2 ₩138.0bn; assets ₩44.1tn; ROE 6.07%; delinquency 1.21%. FY2025: net income ₩476.7bn (−16.7%); volume ₩235.7tn, check cards ₩31.7tn.
- H1 2026 peers: Samsung Card ₩310.5bn first, Shinhan second, KB ₩218.9bn, Hyundai ₩185.2bn. Hyundai led personal credit-card spending in 2025.
- Leadership: CEO Park Chang-hoon since Jan 2025 (2026 term not confirmed ⚠); EVP Kim Young-il. Dec 2025: staff leaked 192,088 merchant owners' contact details.
- Funding: ₩29.4tn, 71% debentures; US$700m raised abroad in 2025 (social ABS rated Aaa by Moody's), US$250m ABS (Feb 2026), US$400m Formosa bond (Jun 2026).
- Strategy: the Plan series (1m cards by Sep 2025), bank-built products (SOL Travel, SOL Plan+), AI (AI-SOLa, AINa, Agentic AI Alliance), stablecoins (six PoCs; Solana MOU Apr 2026).
- Overseas H1 2026: ₩18.9bn profit; Vietnam ₩10.7bn, Kazakhstan ₩6.0bn, Indonesia ₩2.6bn, Myanmar a loss.

## 2. Shinhan Card × Mastercard
- Cards come domestic-only or with an international brand. Mastercard is the international brand on new flagship and premium cards: Plan series (Simple Plan Feb 2026), The BEST-X/XO (2025), The CLASSIC NEO (Nov 2025), Mercedes-Benz PLCC (Apr 2026), E9pay card (May 2025).
- SOL Travel check card on Mastercard with Travel Rewards: 3m cards, ₩5.4tn spend (Mar 2026); SOL Travel J (May 2025).
- Agent Pay: Korea's first live agentic card payment (Mastercard release 17 Mar 2026, hoppa ride via CardInfoLink; Shinhan says it was the issuer, 30 Mar 2026 ⚠ Mastercard did not name it). Mastercard Korea's Jae-Youl Yang quoted.
- Stablecoin hybrid card PoC with Fireblocks and Mastercard (Apr 2026). Vietnam's THE FIRST card with Mastercard (2022).
- Others: Visa on older mass cards (B.Big, YOLO, Mr.Life, RPM+, YaY) and The BEST-F ⚠ undated page; Visa data MOU (2020) and stablecoin settlement PoC (2026). Amex: The CLASSIC Air (Sep 2026). JCB: Haru (Jun 2025). UnionPay: QR in SOL Pay (2023), WeChat Pay (2025). S& own brand ⚠. BC Card: legacy only ⚠.
- No public network agreement, renewal or switch found ⚠. Domestic spend runs on Korean rails; volume by network not disclosed.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay in travel and shopping; tokens for Apple Pay (terms approved Jun 2025; live status unconfirmed ⚠); scam and identity tools beside SK Telecom's FAME; a stablecoin-linked card; commercial cards; cards and transfers for foreigners (TripPASS, E9pay); Vietnam.

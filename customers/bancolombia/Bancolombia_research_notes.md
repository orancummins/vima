# Bancolombia — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Bancolombia_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Bancolombia × Mastercard), condensed. Bancolombia S.A. is the subject; Grupo Cibest S.A. (formerly Grupo Bancolombia, renamed May 2025) is the listed parent and is used as context. Figures are the group's own as reported by Colombian financial press (Q2 2026 results, 10 Aug 2026; 2025 results, 23 Feb 2026) unless marked. Cibest's results PDFs, the Bancolombia results newsroom and Mastercard's Latin America newsroom did not load. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Grupo Cibest S.A. (BVC; NYSE ADR: CIB). Holding company since May 2025 for Bancolombia, Banco Agrícola (El Salvador), BAM (Guatemala), Nequi, Wompi, Wenia, Sufi and Renting Colombia. About 34,000 staff ⚠; market cap about US$17bn ⚠ (third-party snapshot).
- Q2 2026: net income COP 2.7trn (+52% y/y), annualised ROE 28.73%; H1 COP 4.2trn. Assets COP 363trn, gross loans COP 262trn, equity COP 38trn. Bancolombia loans COP 217trn (+9.4%). 9.5m active digital clients.
- FY2025: net income COP 3.8trn (-38%), ROE 9.09%, after a COP 3.4trn Banistmo impairment (17.21% excluding it). Q4 NIM 6.16%. Dividend COP 4,512/share in four instalments.
- Banistmo sold to Inversiones Cuscatlán for US$1,418m (agreed 18 Dec 2025, closed 30 Jun 2026); COP 1.2trn extraordinary dividend.
- Nequi: separate finance company from 1 Sep 2026 (CEO Andrés Vásquez); about 29m clients, 22m monthly, loans about COP 2.3trn, break-even Sep 2025.
- Leadership: Juan Carlos Mora Uribe (President, Grupo Cibest), Mauricio Rosillo (business), Mauricio Botero (strategy and finance), Rodrigo Prieto (risk); Luis Fernando Restrepo (Chair). No separate Bancolombia S.A. CEO found ⚠.
- 2026 priorities: Banistmo, Nequi, Central America, COP 1.6trn for technology and continuity. From 2027: Latin American investment ⚠. Tech: 75% of apps in the cloud; LLMs in credit; planned purchasing agents.
- Gaps: ratings, deposits, capital ratios, loan market share ⚠.

## 2. Bancolombia × Mastercard
- Debit: all six Bancolombia debit cards are Mastercard (incl. Virtual and Transporte for five cities' transit); Maestro swapped to Mastercard debit.
- Credit (Superfinanciera data, 30 Apr 2026, via La República): 2,760,578 active, 17.5% of the market and number one; Mastercard about 1.2m, Visa 887,952, Amex 572,931. 24 cards on sale: 11 Mastercard, 6 Visa, 7 Amex.
- World Legend Mastercard: launched in Colombia 25 May 2026; Bancolombia first issuer (Mauricio Rosillo; Gabriel Hencker, Mastercard).
- Nequi: Visa debit card in Colombia (2024; an earlier digital card was Mastercard ⚠); Mastercard digital debit in El Salvador (Jul 2024).
- Visa: Tap to Add Card for Apple Pay (Jan 2025, first in region); Avianca LifeMiles (Sep 2025); Selección co-brand; Visa Infinite.
- No network contract or renewal found ⚠. 2017 La Guajira campaign with Mastercard and WFP.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay for Bancolombia's planned purchasing agents; scam scoring and identity for Bre-B (116m keys, COP 262trn in year one); tokens and card-on-file for Nequi's point-of-sale credit; Wompi merchant risk; Move for El Salvador remittances; fleet cards for Renting Colombia; Priceless and offers for premium clients.

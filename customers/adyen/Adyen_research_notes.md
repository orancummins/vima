## 1. The company
- Adyen N.V., Amsterdam; DNB-licensed credit institution; Euronext Amsterdam (ADYEN), S&P A-. Founded 2006; IPO 13 Jun 2018 at €240. Market cap ≈€29.9bn (16 Oct 2026) ⚠ own calculation. 5,020 FTEs (30 Jun 2026), 29 offices.
- H1 2026: volume €803.8bn (+24%), net revenue €1,302.9m (+19%), EBITDA margin 49%, take rate 16.2 bps, 838K terminals. FY2025: volume €1,394.3bn, net revenue €2,364.2m (+18%), EBITDA margin 53%.
- Pillars (H1 2026): Digital €719.7m (+13%), Unified Commerce €417.7m (+25%), Platforms €165.5m (+37%). Regions: EMEA 55%, North America 27%, APAC 11%, LATAM 7% (+43%).
- Leadership: co-CEOs Pieter van der Does and Ingo Uytdehaage; Hwa Tsao interim CFO from 1 Sep 2026 (Tandowsky left); Gayathri Rajan CPO; Supervisory Board chair Piero Overmars (Herna Verhagen nominated ⚠).
- Strategy: "complete financial operating system"; one platform, own licences, Dynamic Identification; EBITDA margin >55% by 2028; 2026 guidance 21–23% cc. Talon.One (€750m) and Orb ($335m) closed 1 Jul 2026. UAE licence; Cartes Bancaires and STET direct (2026); India acquiring with RuPay (2025).
- Products: acquiring; US debit and eftpos routing; Issuing on Mastercard and Visa (8x in 2025), Navan charge cards, virtual supplier cards; card and bank payouts, CashOut, IMM; Pix, UPI, Wero, Pay by Bank; Uplift, Optimize, Personalize; Talon.One, Orb; Adyen Agentic; Accounts, Capital; terminals, Tap to Pay on iPhone.

## 2. Adyen × Mastercard
- Direct acquiring incl. India; network tokens (MDES not named ⚠); Click to Pay embedded (2 Mar 2023), recurring only on Mastercard; Mastercard named Adyen a Click to Pay enabler (13 Nov 2024); Issuing on Mastercard; payouts to Mastercard cards (rail not named ⚠).
- Meses sin intereses, Mexico (Aug 2024 ⚠ title only); Payment Optimization Platform roll-out partner (Oct 2025 ⚠ snippet); Verifiable Intent partner ⚠ snippet.
- Agent Pay for Machines supporter (10 Jun 2026; Karan Katyal); Adyen Agentic with Mastercard as early partner (16 Jun 2026; Sherri Haymond). Mastercard's Sep 2025 and Sep 2026 agentic releases did not name Adyen.
- No bilateral agreement found. Visa at parity: issuing, Click to Pay, Trusted Agent Protocol (14 Oct 2025), Adyen Agentic; Adyen holds Visa preferred shares, last tranche sold 2027.

# Stripe — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Stripe_Platform.html`. Three research threads (company profile; products and channels; Stripe × Mastercard), condensed. Stripe is private: volumes and customer counts are its own claims (annual letters, Sessions, blog); revenue, cash flow and headcount are press estimates. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. Stripe — the company
- Stripe, Inc.; HQs in South San Francisco and Dublin (permanent Dublin HQ opened Oct 2025). Founded 2010 by Patrick (CEO) and John Collison (President); launched 2011. Mission: "increase the GDP of the internet".
- Licences: Stripe Technology Europe Ltd, e-money institution authorised by the Central Bank of Ireland (2019), passported across the EEA; Stripe Payments UK (FCA). Stripe MALPB, a Georgia merchant-acquirer limited-purpose bank, approved mid-2025 and open for business 28 May 2026 (direct Visa/Mastercard acquiring; no deposits or card issuing) ⚠ network-side approval not separately reported. Bridge National Trust: OCC preliminary conditional approval 12 Feb 2026, not yet operating.
- Valuation: $95bn (2021) → $50bn (2023) → $65bn (Feb 2024) → $91.5bn (Feb 2025) → $159bn (Feb 2026 tender led by Thrive, Coatue, a16z). No IPO "rush" (Jan 2026).
- Scale: total volume ~$1tn (2023), $1.4tn (2024), $1.9tn (2025, +34%, ~1.6% of world GDP); 5m+ businesses; 90% of the Dow and 80% of the Nasdaq 100; Atlas forms ~25% of new Delaware corporations; 18,000+ platforms; 1,500+ fintechs. Revenue ⚠ ~$5.1bn (2024), ~$6.8bn (2025); FCF ⚠ ~$3.2bn. "Robustly profitable".
- Leadership: Will Gaybrick (President, technology and business), CFO Steffan Tomlinson, CRO Tyler Bryson, Vice Chair Eileen O'Mara; CTO vacant since Rahul Patil left for Anthropic (Oct 2025) ⚠. Board: the Collisons, Christa Davies (lead independent), Bill Winters, Kevin Kelly, Luciana Lixandru, Matt Huang, Parker Conrad. Headcount ⚠ ~9,000–10,000.
- Acquisitions: Paystack (2020), TaxJar (2021), BBPOS (2022), Lemon Squeezy (2024), Bridge (~$1.1bn, closed 4 Feb 2025), Privy (Jun 2025), Metronome (closed Jan 2026), OpenRouter (agreed 19 Aug 2026, ⚠ $7–8bn reported), Clerky (~Sep 2026), Parafin (agreed 30 Sep 2026). Stripe and Advent bid ~$53bn for PayPal (15 Jul 2026); ⚠ reported abandoned late Aug.
- Strategy: "economic infrastructure for AI" (usage and token billing, OpenRouter, agentic commerce), stablecoins (Bridge → Privy → Tempo → OUSD), licences moving down the stack, enterprise and full-stack finance. FTC "debanking" warning letter (Mar 2026, also to PayPal, Visa and Mastercard).

## 2. Products and channels
- Acceptance: 125+ payment methods; Terminal and Tap to Pay; Reader T600 and 15 more markets (2026); principal acquirer in Europe and, via MALPB, the US.
- Payouts: Instant Payouts on Mastercard Send and Visa Direct (since 2016; ~30 minutes, $9,999 cap); Global Payouts (Jun 2025; 100+ countries fiat, 160 stablecoin).
- Issuing: 400m+ cards on Visa and Mastercard (Ramp flagship); managed consumer credit (2025), consumer debit and agent cards in preview (2026); Bridge stablecoin-linked cards on Visa (Apr 2025 → 65+ countries Oct 2026; ⚠ ~$1.2bn a month spend).
- Stablecoins: Bridge (volume 4x in 2025; Open Issuance Oct 2025); Privy (110m+ wallets); Tempo (Paradigm-incubated, $500m at $5bn; testnet Dec 2025; mainnet 18 Mar 2026 with the Machine Payments Protocol); OUSD (Open Standard: Coinbase, Mastercard, Shopify, Stripe, Visa; live 30 Sep 2026; Stripe's default stablecoin); stablecoin acceptance (US merchants) and Stablecoin Financial Accounts (101 countries).
- Treasury: relaunched Apr 2026 as a global business account (15 currencies, free instant US Stripe-to-Stripe transfers, "Stripe card powered by Mastercard" with 2% cash back on Mastercard only); multicurrency settlement in 18 currencies across 37 markets by end-2026.
- Engine room: Radar on the payments foundation model (May 2025; now multi-processor); Smart Disputes; Ethoca Alerts and Verifi in dispute prevention; Identity; Financial Connections (MX and Finicity); Authorization Boost (network tokens incl. Mastercard, account updater, +3.8% acceptance); Orchestration ⚠ GA unconfirmed; Link (300m+ users, 1m+ merchants; agent wallet Apr 2026); Sigma, Data Pipeline, Stripe Database.
- Agentic: ACP with OpenAI (29 Sep 2025); Shared Payment Tokens carrying Mastercard Agent Pay and Visa Intelligent Commerce tokens (3 Mar 2026); Agentic Commerce Suite (Dec 2025; Copilot, Gemini, Meta); MPP and streaming payments.
- Business lines: Checkout and Adaptive Pricing; Connect (18,000+ platforms, ~11m businesses); Billing and Metronome (revenue suite heading for a $1bn run rate); Tax (100+ countries), Invoicing, Managed Payments; Capital (Celtic Bank, YouLend; FedEx data from 2027) and Parafin; Atlas and Clerky; OpenRouter and Stripe Projects.
- Access: Dashboard and Stripe Console; API, SDKs, MCP server; Stripe Apps; partners (Shopify, Salesforce, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Meta, FedEx); Sessions (288 launches, Apr 2026); Stripe Climate and Frontier (⚠ ~$1.8bn commitments).

## 3. Stripe × Mastercard
- No bilateral strategic agreement announced; touchpoints are many but mostly multi-party.
- Timeline: Instant Payouts on Mastercard Send (Sep 2016); Financial Connections with Finicity among aggregators (May 2022); network tokens and Mastercard real-time account updater global (Feb 2023); MALPB approval (2025) and opening (May 2026); Stripe named among Agent Pay collaborators (Sep 2025); Mastercard a Tempo design partner (Dec 2025); Agent Pay tokens in SPT (3 Mar 2026; Pablo Fourez quoted); Stripe card powered by Mastercard (Apr 2026); Wizard on Agent Pay through Stripe (30 Apr 2026; Sherri Haymond); Mastercard on the Issuing settlement API (27 May 2026); Agent Pay for Machines with Stripe and Tempo among 32 partners (10 Jun 2026; Jorn Lambert); OUSD unveiled (30 Jun) and live (30 Sep 2026); Mastercard completes BVNK (3 Aug 2026) and launches the Global Money Platform (15 Oct 2026), a competitor to Bridge.
- Visa ahead: Bridge stablecoin cards (Visa-only); Visa Intelligent Commerce named Stripe at launch (Apr 2025); Visa shipped an MPP card spec on day one (Mar 2026); Visa is a Tempo anchor validator; Visa–Bridge on-chain settlement pilot with Lead Bank.
- Where it could land: a second network for stablecoin cards; a Mastercard MPP spec and Verifiable Intent in SPT; commercial and agent cards on Mastercard; Consumer Clarity for the long tail; Move where bank payouts fail; stablecoin settlement on Tempo; Click to Pay (low appetite, given Link); installments (no evidence).
- Market: Stripe $1.9tn vs PayPal TPV ~$1.79tn, Adyen ~€1.4tn, Checkout.com >$300bn; agentic standards race (ACP/SPT/MPP vs Google AP2/UCP vs Visa TAP vs Mastercard Agent Pay/Verifiable Intent/AP4M).

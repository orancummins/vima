# Nabil Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Nabil_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Nabil × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (Q4 FY2025/26 interim report and audited FY2025/26 extract, year to 16 July 2026; 2024/25 annual report) unless marked. Amounts in NPR. Nabil publishes no card counts by network. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q1 FY2026/27 results are not yet out.

## 1. The company
- Nabil Bank Limited (NEPSE: NABIL), Kathmandu; founded 12 July 1984 as Nepal Arab Bank, renamed Nabil around 2001 ⚠. About 2.5m customers, 268 branches, 321–323 ATMs, 2,402 permanent staff (3,755 including outsourced).
- FY2025/26: assets NPR 734.7bn, customer loans NPR 460.3bn, customer deposits NPR 592.1bn (total deposits NPR 602.9bn), profit NPR 7.91bn (up 33%; highest of Nepal's commercial banks), CAR 12.37%, CET1 8.87%, gross NPL 4.20%, ROE 11.8%. FY2024/25: deposit share 8.15%, loan share 8.72%.
- Revenue by product line (FY2025/26): financial intermediation NPR 35.2bn, treasury NPR 6.1bn, trade finance NPR 1.3bn, cards/e-banking/channels NPR 1.2bn.
- Owners: promoters 58.44% — NB (International) Ltd, Ireland 39.44% (Chaudhary-linked ⚠), IFIC Bank 7.77%, Rastriya Beema 7.63%. Market value about NPR 145bn ⚠. ICRA Nepal A@ ⚠ (down from AA- in 2022/23).
- Deals: United Finance (2021), Nepal Bangladesh Bank (July 2022); NPR 5bn 8% perpetual preference shares (FY2025/26).
- Leadership: Chairman Nirvana Kumar Chaudhary (Jan 2026), CEO Manoj Kumar Gyawali, deputy CEOs Bhupendra Pandey, Adarsha Bazgain, Ganesh Prasad Awasthi; Chief Digital Banking Officer Parmeswor Shrestha.
- Strategy: trillion-rupee ambition; AI strategy within two years; Tap N' Pay, account-based remittance and wealth services on the nBank roadmap. Finacle core; gen-AI assistants; Nabina chatbot.

## 2. Nabil × Mastercard
- Visa and Mastercard introduced 1999–2004; holds Mastercard Class B (11,140) and Visa Class C (6,166) shares — legacy principal membership of both ⚠ inferred.
- Visa: prepaid travel cards, USD iCard, FD Card and international debit all Visa; Visa Direct (Sep 2022); three Visa awards (Nov 2025) for consumer debit, contactless issuing and e-commerce acquiring.
- Mastercard: Smart Debit choice of Visa or Mastercard; Danphe Mastercard debit launched 16 Nov 2025 (Mastercard's Ravi Datla ⚠ title unknown); Mastercard Domestic credit card; EPG acquiring of Mastercard; SecureCode; nRemit funded by Mastercard or Visa cards (Hamro Patro, Oct 2024).
- Scheme fees to Visa and Mastercard NPR 929m in FY2025/26 (not split). Credit-card book only NPR 1.53bn.

## 3. Market
- Nepal (mid-July 2025): 13.7m debit, 318k credit, 251k prepaid cards; 2.9m QR merchants; QR value NPR 958bn (+92%); 26.8m wallet users (NRB).
- NCHL FY2025/26: 238m transactions, about NPR 21tn; NEPALPAY QR 36m transactions (NPR 266bn, +56%), 1.21m merchants; connectIPS 1.58m users; NEPALPAY card live with about 2,000 cards; UPI and LankaQR links. Nabil's NEPALPAY card membership not found ⚠.
- Fonepay: 2m+ merchants, Nabil QR rides it; Fonepay Credit Card 50,000+ users via nine banks (Aug 2026); UnionPay QR added May 2026.
- Peers adding Mastercard: NIC Asia (Jul 2024), Kumari Bank (Mar 2026).

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Mastercard Move for account-based remittance; tokenised Tap N' Pay in nBank; tourist acceptance (Nepal Now, EPG); fraud and dispute tools; instalments and offers to defend card credit against QR credit; commercial cards alongside corporatePAY; growing the new debit share.

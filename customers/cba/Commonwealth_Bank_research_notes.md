## 1. The company
- Commonwealth Bank of Australia (ASX: CBA), Sydney. Founded 1911. 51,714 FTE. Rated AA/Aa2/AA-. Market value not confirmed ⚠ (about 1.67bn shares).
- FY26: cash NPAT $10,982m (+7%), statutory $10,911m, ROE 14.0%, NIM 2.05%, CET1 12.0%, cost-to-income 45.5%, DPS $5.05. Assets $1,452bn; home loans $749bn; customer deposits about $1trn.
- Main bank for 1 in 3 Australians (MFI 34.2%) and 1 in 4 businesses (26.0%). 9.6m+ app users, 14.3m daily logins. ~640 branches, 1,770 ATMs.
- Shares: home loans 25.4%, credit cards 29.3%, household deposits 26.4%, business lending 18.3%.
- Segments (cash NPAT): Retail Banking Services $5,587m; Business Banking $4,544m (incl. CommSec); IB&M $1,258m; New Zealand (ASB) A$1,112m.
- Brands: CommBank, Bankwest, Unloan, CommSec, ASB, CommBank Yello; stakes in CFS (45%), Lendi (42%), PEXA (24%), Gemini.
- Leadership: Paul O'Malley (Chair), Matt Comyn (CEO), Alan Docherty (CFO), Andrew Hinchliff (CRO), Angus Sullivan (Retail), Michael Vacy-Lyle (Business), Sinead Taylor (IB&M; to ASB CEO 12 Dec 2026), Victoria Ledda (CIO) and Rodrigo Castillo (CTO) from 1 Jul 2026.
- Tech/AI: cloud core, CommBank Companion, 1,000+ ML models, OpenAI partnership (Aug 2025), Anthropic and AWS; $2.4bn a year of investment; $1bn+ a year on fraud and scams.

## 2. CBA × Mastercard
- Consumer debit: Debit Mastercard and World Debit Mastercard; eftpos on the card for domestic routing ⚠; Keycard is eftpos only.
- Consumer credit: Low Rate, Low Fee, Interest-free Low Fee, CommBank Neo, Smart, Ultimate — all Mastercard. Smart/Ultimate fee waiver ends 1 Jan 2027.
- Bankwest credit cards Mastercard; business and corporate credit Mastercard ⚠; StepPay card Mastercard ⚠.
- Business debit: Business Visa Debit (Visa). ASB cards Visa ⚠.
- Click to Pay live on all CBA Mastercard cards (2025 ⚠). Mastercard Presale tickets in Yello.
- Agent Pay: Australia's first authenticated agentic purchase, a CBA debit card at Event Cinemas, 28 Jan 2026.
- No public CBA–Mastercard agreement or renewal found; Mastercard credit issuer since 1984.
- Acquiring: terminals take all networks at 0.99% flat from 1 Oct 2026; FY26 merchant income down on higher scheme fees.

## 3. Market context
- RBA Conclusions Paper (31 Mar 2026): surcharging removed on eftpos, Mastercard and Visa from 1 Oct 2026; debit cap 8c/0.16%; consumer credit cap 0.3% (from 0.8%, average was 0.47%); commercial 0.8%; foreign cards 1.0% from 1 Apr 2027; wallets, BNPL and three-party schemes next.
- AP+ (eftpos, NPP, BPAY): zero-surcharge for eftpos from 1 Oct 2026; least-cost routing cuts debit costs nearly 20%. PayTo and PayID sold by CBA to businesses.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay for Companion; scam scoring on NPP/PayTo; merchant-funded offers in Yello under the 0.3% cap; acquirer risk and merchant data; business debit and commercial virtual cards.

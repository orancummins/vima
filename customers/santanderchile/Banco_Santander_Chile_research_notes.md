## 1. The company
- Banco Santander-Chile, Santiago; listed in Santiago (BSANTANDER) and NYSE (BSAC). Santander Group 67%, free float 33%. Market value US$15.4bn (30 Jun 2026). Moody's A2, S&P A-. 8,315 employees; 224 branches.
- H1 2026: net income Ch$656bn (+19.2%), ROAE 27.2% (31.5% in Q2 on UF inflation), efficiency 31.6%, NIM 4.3%, CET1 11.1%; assets Ch$70.3tn, loans Ch$41.4tn, deposits Ch$32.4tn. FY2025: net income Ch$1,053bn, ROAE 23.5%.
- 4.83m customers (2.69m active, 2.25m digital); 477,788 SMEs. Number one in loans (16.5%), consumer loans (19.0%), current accounts (20.8%) and credit-card purchases (24.4%); number two in mortgages (19.5%).
- Segments: Retail & Commercial (73% of profit), WM&I, CIB (18%), Payments (Getnet), Digital Consumer Bank (Santander Consumer Finance).
- Leadership: Andrés Trautmann, CEO since 1 Jul 2025; Rodrigo Vergara, chair since Mar 2026. Getnet CEO Fernando Benito.
- Strategy: 3.5m active customers; 2026 ROAE above 24%; Gravity cloud core (Q1 2025); group Plard processing. No Chile AI disclosure ⚠.

## 2. Santander Chile × Mastercard
- Cards carry Visa, Mastercard, American Express (and Magna) brands; no public split or Chile network deal ⚠.
- LATAM Pass co-brand (688,000+ clients) renewed for five years in Aug 2025; Mastercard, Visa and Amex versions ⚠.
- Cuenta Life (Aug 2020) launched with Mastercard debit; current page also shows Visa ⚠. Pulsera Chip, Mastercard only (Jul 2020) ⚠ status.
- The Platinum Card American Express (2025). Card range simplified Nov 2025; Mérito Life ended Dec 2025.
- Getnet: 287,000+ POS, 229,000+ merchants; second to Transbank; fees −15.1% in H1 2026.
- Visa Intelligent Commerce: agent purchase in Chile (16 Mar 2026). Mastercard agent payments with six unnamed banks in Chile (Sep 2026) ⚠ Santander not named.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay (group live in Spain and Brazil); tokens and Click to Pay on no-PAN cards; merchant, fraud and Tap on Phone tools for Getnet; card-linked offers and Priceless after Mérito Life; open finance before July 2027; transit and SME cards.

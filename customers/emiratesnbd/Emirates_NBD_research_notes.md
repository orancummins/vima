# Emirates NBD — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Emirates_NBD_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Emirates NBD × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (H1 2026 results, 23 Jul 2026; FY 2025 results, 26 Jan 2026; RBL completion filing, 18 Jun 2026) unless marked. Mastercard's newsroom was blocked and read through syndicated copies. ⚠ marks items that rest on inference or secondary sources. Q3 2026 results were not out at the time of writing.

## 1. The company
- Emirates NBD Bank (P.J.S.C.), Dubai; listed on the DFM at about USD 52bn (Jun 2026); 56% owned by the Government of Dubai (ICD, Dubai Holding). National Bank of Dubai (1963) and Emirates Bank International merged in 2007. 30,000+ staff; 13 countries; 1,425 branches and 4,948 ATMs/SDMs. Customers: 10m+ before RBL, "over 25 million" after ⚠.
- H1 2026: profit before tax AED 16.2bn (+5%), net profit about AED 12.9bn, income AED 27.9bn, assets above AED 1.3tn, loans AED 771bn, deposits AED 892bn, cost of risk 42bp, NPL 2.1%. FY 2025: PBT AED 29.8bn, net profit about USD 6.5bn.
- Segments: Retail Banking & Wealth Management, Corporate & Institutional Banking, Global Markets & Treasury, Islamic Banking, and international (DenizBank, Egypt, KSA, India) ⚠ grouping simplified.
- Brands: Emirates NBD, Emirates Islamic (PBT AED 3.9bn in 2025), Liv (500k+ customers), DenizBank, Emirates NBD Egypt, Emirates NBD KSA, RBL Bank (60% from 18 Jun 2026, about USD 2.75bn), Emirates NBD Capital.
- Leadership: Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum (Chairman), Hesham Abdulla Al Qassim (VC & MD), Shayne Nelson (Group CEO), Patrick Sullivan (CFO), Marwan Hadi (RBWM), Ahmed Al Qassim (Wholesale), Miguel Rio-Tinto (Digital & IT), Anith Daniel (Transaction Banking), Rohit Garg (Retail Products, RBWM CDO).
- Strategy: "data-first, digital-focused"; international about 35% of income; Saudi lending +48% in 2025; Evident AI #1 in MEA; first AED digital bond on Euroclear D-FMI (Jan 2026).
- Network International, built by the bank, was listed in 2019 and taken private by Brookfield and FAB in 2024; the bank's final exit date is not confirmed ⚠.

## 2. Emirates NBD × Mastercard
- Debit: all 16 debit cards on Visa; Visa five-year renewal (Jun 2020); a renewal after 2025 not seen ⚠. Liv debit and Multicurrency card on Visa.
- Credit: 35 listed cards, about 21 Visa (Skywards, SHARE, Emaar, Darna, Etihad Guest, noon) and 12 Mastercard (Voyager, Marriott Bonvoy, dnata, LuLu 247, Webshopper, Manchester United, MC Platinum/Titanium), Diners Club and Duo. Split by spend not published. 36% of UAE credit-card spend.
- Mastercard deals: Marriott Bonvoy World Elite/World (Mar 2024), DP World corporate card (Sep 2024), Gateway + Brighterion on Emirates NBD Pay (Feb 2025), Voyager (Dec 2025), Egypt partnership (Jan 2024) and TMG co-brands (2025), GlobalCash prepaid. Liv credit cards look to be Mastercard ⚠.
- Visa deals: Visa+ (Apr 2025), NNSS settlement agent (Apr 2026), AI Travel Assistant (Oct 2026), Visa Infinite Business Debit for SME VIP (Oct 2026).
- Domestic: first Jaywan debit (Jan 2025, Network International acceptance) and prepaid (Jun 2025, IDEMIA); Aani launch bank (Oct 2023).
- No Click to Pay, Send/Move, Agent Pay or scam-scoring deal with the bank found.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land

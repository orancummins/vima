# BMO Financial Group — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `BMO_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BMO × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are BMO's own (Q3 2026 results and MD&A, 25 Aug 2026; fiscal 2025 results, 4 Dec 2025; 2025 Annual Information Form) unless marked. bmo.com product pages timed out and mastercard.com was blocked, so card networks come from BMO press releases. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Bank of Montreal ("BMO Financial Group"), Canada's first bank (1817); Schedule I bank under the Bank Act. Head office Montreal, executive offices Toronto. TSX and NYSE: BMO. Market value about US$116bn ⚠ (third-party, 16 Oct 2026).
- Q3 2026: revenue C$9.9bn; net income C$1.75bn reported, C$2.86bn adjusted (after a C$962m after-tax charge on the Stonepeak sale); adjusted ROE 14.0%; CET1 13.0%; assets C$1,538.7bn. FY2025: revenue C$36.3bn, net income C$8.7bn.
- About 13m clients; more than 53,000 staff; more than 1,800 branches (Oct 2025) before 138 US branches went to First Citizens (closed 4 Sep 2026).
- Segments: Canadian P&C; U.S. Banking (US wealth folded in from Q4 2025); Wealth Management; BMO Capital Markets; Corporate Services.
- Leadership: Darryl White (CEO), Rahul Nalgirkar (CFO from Jan 2026), George Cope (Chair), Mathew Mehrotra and Sharon Haward-Laird (Canadian P&C), Aron Levine (U.S. Banking), Kristin Milchanowski (Chief AI & Quantum Officer).
- Investor Day (26 Mar 2026): line of sight to 15% ROE; timing "exiting fiscal 2027" or "by 2028" ⚠; other targets secondary-sourced ⚠.
- Deals: Bank of the West (Feb 2023, US$13.8bn); AIR MILES (Jun 2023, US$160m), now Blue Rewards; Burgundy (Nov 2025); Euroz Hartleys (agreed Jun 2026). Disposals: US branches; Transportation and Vendor Finance to Stonepeak (BMO keeps 19.9%); Moneris (50/50 with RBC) to Francisco Partners for about C$2.0bn, closing by end Q1 FY27, with referral arrangements kept.
- Digital money: tokenised cash with CME Group on Google Cloud Universal Ledger (2H 2026 target); six-bank CAD deposit token (Sep 2026); Project Agorá (Sep 2026).

## 2. BMO × Mastercard
- Canadian credit: Blue Rewards World Elite Mastercard and Blue Rewards Mastercard (2 Jun 2026), VIPorter Mastercard with Porter (Mar 2025), Ascend World Elite and AIR MILES Mastercard (line's future not stated ⚠). Mass-market cards (CashBack etc.) not verified ⚠.
- Visa: eclipse Visa Infinite and Privilege (Nov 2020) and eclipse rise Visa (Dec 2023) — the only visible move away from an all-Mastercard credit line-up.
- Business and commercial: first World Elite business cards in Canada (Nov 2021); Commercial Mastercard suite (2017); biometric corporate card on Identity Check (2016); Extend virtual cards on Mastercard tokens (2023); Mastercard Commercial Express, Canada and US (1 Oct 2026), first Canadian issuer.
- Debit: Interac in Canada; Debit Mastercard visible only in navigation ⚠. US debit on Mastercard since 2019 (True Name); legacy Bank of the West networks unverified ⚠.
- Global Pay on Mastercard Cross-Border Services (US, Oct 2020). True Name on Canadian credit (Mar 2022). BMO Perks with Mastercard Canada (2015) ⚠ current status.
- No public network agreement or renewal date found. Not found: Click to Pay, Installments, Send/Move, Finicity, Ethoca, Agent Pay, Multi-Token Network. MDES never named ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Card-linked offers and loyalty services in Blue Rewards; Debit Mastercard beside Interac; Agent Pay and Click to Pay on BMO's Google push provisioning; Consumer Clarity and Decision Intelligence; overlays on Canada's RTR (Q4 2026; Vocalink role ⚠) and open banking (legislated, not live); Multi-Token Network for the CAD deposit token; acceptance through Moneris's new owner.
- Watch-outs: Visa holds the premium lifestyle line; bank-led tokens and RTR could move flows off card rails.

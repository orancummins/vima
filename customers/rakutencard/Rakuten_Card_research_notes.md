# Rakuten Card — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Rakuten_Card_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Rakuten Card × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Rakuten's own unless marked: the Q2 2026 presentation (August 2026), the FY2025 presentation, the reorganisation agreement (20 May 2026) and the Business Card Platinum release (30 September 2026). Rakuten Card's own website blocked automated reading, so card brands per product rest partly on Japanese Wikipedia and consumer sites. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Rakuten Card Co., Ltd., Minato-ku, Tokyo. Founded December 2001 as Aozora Card; bought by Rakuten in 2004; renamed Rakuten Card in August 2011 ⚠. Unlisted.
- Ownership: Rakuten Group 85.01% and Mizuho 14.99% (bought for about ¥165bn, December 2024). On 1 October 2026 it became wholly owned by Rakuten Bank (TSE Prime). Mizuho now holds about 10.5% of Rakuten Bank's votes; Rakuten Group holds about 49.9%. Rakuten Payment moved to Rakuten Group before the effective date.
- Scale: 34.31m cards (June 2026). Shopping GTV ¥26.5tn in 2025 (+10.3%) and ¥7.1tn in Q2 2026 (+9.4%). Segment revenue ¥385.2bn and Non-GAAP OI ¥63.5bn in 2025; ¥107.9bn and ¥17.4bn in Q2 2026. IFRS 2025: revenue ¥462.3bn, net profit ¥45.8bn, total assets ¥4.63tn. 3,432 staff. JCR A- stable.
- No. 1 issuer by spend (Rakuten Bank, ⚠). About 20% of 2025 credit card value by this map's arithmetic ⚠.
- Leadership: Koichi Nakamura (President since March 2025 ⚠), Hiroshi Mikitani (Chairman), Tomotaka Tobayashi (Rakuten Bank).
- Strategy: synergies from bank funding and joint marketing, about ¥33bn by March 2028 and ¥85bn+ a year in the medium term. Business Card Platinum (Visa) and Corporate Card from 1 October 2026 with Mizuho's UPSIDER. AI agent in Rakuten Card Lite (Feb 2026). My Number verification in the app ⚠. Cash advance into the Rakuten Pay balance (Jul 2026).

## 2. Rakuten Card × Mastercard
- Principal member of Mastercard and Visa; also issues JCB and Amex (since Sep 2018) ⚠. No network split is published.
- Mastercard: standard, Gold, Premium, Black, ANA cards; Mastercard only on Alpen and My Color Selection; Black Card has no Visa option ⚠.
- Mastercard acquiring since about December 2011 ⚠. Apple Pay with Mastercard and JCB in October 2016; Visa followed in 2021 ⚠.
- Rakuten Rewards MasterCard in the US with FNBO (2014), status unknown ⚠. Rakuten Bank debit on Mastercard since May 2020.
- Visa: about 55.5% of users ⚠. The Mizuho Rakuten Card (Dec 2024), Business Card and Business Card Platinum are all Visa. Visa-only Apple Pay campaigns ran in June–July and September 2026 ⚠.
- No Mastercard–Rakuten Card announcement found for 2024–26.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Commercial controls for the new Rakuten Corporate Card, whose network is not stated ⚠.
- Agent Pay for Rakuten's AI agents.
- Tokens and passkeys against card-number theft (¥47.5bn of Japan's ¥51bn of fraud in 2025).
- A debit-plus-credit offer under Rakuten Bank.
- Acquirer tools as acquiring is shared with Mizuho.
- Premium travel on the Black Card.

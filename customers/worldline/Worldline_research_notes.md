## 1. The company
- Worldline SA (Euronext Paris: WLN), Paris-La Défense. From Sligos (1972) and Atos; IPO 2014; Equens (2016), SIX Payment Services (2018), Ingenico (2020). Payment institutions in BE, LU, SE, NL, GR, IT; PAYONE an EMI (BaFin); equensWorldline a processor (DNB); FCA licence (UK).
- FY2025: revenue €4,030m (−2.7% organic), adj. EBITDA €737m, net loss €5,157m after c.€4.7bn goodwill impairment, net debt €2,219m. H1 2026: revenue €1,897m (−1.3%), adj. EBITDA €328m; fully pruned €1,736m, MSV €245bn (+4.3%), 8.3bn acceptance transactions. 2026 outlook cut to flat to slightly positive.
- Segments: Merchant Services (€3,238m, 80%) and Financial Services (€792m, 20%; −7.1% in H1 2026). Go-to-market: SMB (50% of MS), Enterprise, Global Commerce (Global Collect), Financial Institutions.
- Footprint: 1.2m merchants, 320+ banks, 156m cards, 23,000 partner branches, 400 ISVs/ISOs, 14% of European MSV (pre-pruning).
- Capital: c.€500m raised Mar 2026 (Bpifrance, Crédit Agricole, Crédit Mutuel, BNP Paribas; SIX out); 1-for-40 reverse split; market cap ≈€0.67bn ⚠ own estimate; S&P BB negative.
- Leadership: Pierre-Antoine Vacheron (CEO, Mar 2025), Srikanth Seshadri (CFO), Madalena Cascais Tomé (FI & Processing), Candice Dillon (CTO), Stefaan D'Hoore (CRO, ex-Visa, Apr 2026); Chair Wilfried Verstraete.
- North Star 2030: ~4% organic growth 2027–30, adj. EBITDA €900m+ and FCF €300–350m by 2030. Divested MeTS, North America, PaymentIQ, NZ, ANZ JV, India and CAWL (all closed by 3 Aug 2026).
- 'Dirty Payments' press investigation (24 Jun 2025): shares −40%+, Belgian money-laundering probe, €10m guarantee (Mar 2026), BaFin monitor at PAYONE.

## 2. Worldline × Mastercard
- Acquirer and processor of Mastercard across its licensed entities; scheme fees booked as costs.
- MasterPass distribution (Oct 2013); SoftPOS with Mastercard and Axis Bank in India (Jun 2020; India since sold ⚠); first Mastercard Enhanced Contactless certification (Mar 2021).
- Named a Mastercard tokenisation (Global Collect) and Click to Pay (Ogone, Saferpay) partner (Jun 2025) ⚠ search summary; Click to Pay for recurring on Global Collect (GA 30 Jul 2026).
- Europe's first live end-to-end agentic payment on Agent Pay with ING NL (2 Jun 2026; Brice van de Walle, Mastercard); France's first with Crédit Agricole and Weezevent (25 Jun 2026); Agent Suite for merchants, named as a planned user (Sep 2026).
- Not found: framework agreement, Installments, Send/Move, Mastercard Open Banking, Ethoca.
- Rivals: Visa Decision Manager (Oct 2024), Visa agentic payment with ING DE (2 Jul 2026), an ex-Visa CRO; Bancontact, Cartes Bancaires, Girocard, TWINT; EPI shareholder (Nov 2020), Wero live in DE and BE; Klarna (May 2026); Fipto, Circle; digital-euro pilot (H2 2027).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay scaled across Worldline's issuing book and UCP/MCP channels; merchant-risk and AML tools for the remediation; tokens and Click to Pay through the GoPay migration; issuer value-added services and instalments for Financial Services; bank-led instalments against Klarna; cross-border and stablecoin settlement.

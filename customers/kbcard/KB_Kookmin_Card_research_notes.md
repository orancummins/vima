# KB Kookmin Card — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `KB_Kookmin_Card_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and KB Card × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are KB Financial Group's H1 2026 disclosure (July 2026) as reported by the Korean business press, unless marked. KB Card's own site loads its press, history and IR pages by script, so most product facts come from press reports of its releases, translated. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary or undated sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- KB Kookmin Card Co., Ltd., Seoul; 100% owned by KB Financial Group (KRX: 105560; NYSE: KB). Kookmin Bank's card business from 1980, spun off on 2 March 2011 ⚠ (Wikipedia). About 12.95m members at end-April 2026 (Shinhan 14.55m).
- H1 2026: net income ₩218.9bn (+20.7%), Q2 ₩111.4bn; operating revenue ₩2.76tn (+0.6%); card usage about ₩93.5tn (+5.3%); assets ₩31.3tn; ROE 7.86%; delinquency 1.07% (from 1.40%).
- H1 2026 peers: Samsung ₩310.5bn, Shinhan ₩253.4bn, KB ₩218.9bn third, Hyundai ₩185.2bn.
- Leadership: CEO Kim Jae-kwan since Jan 2025 (ex-KB Financial CFO); term ends Dec 2026, renewal undecided at 17 Oct ⚠. KB Financial moving to chairman-elect Lee Jae-geun ⚠.
- Strategy 2026: "execution"; a new Corporate Sales Division; ALL·YOU·NEED brand system (Jan 2026); AI Center and copilot; hybrid stablecoin-card patent (Jan 2026).
- Corporate cards: Korea's largest base, 456,000 members (Nov 2024). Networks not stated ⚠.
- KB Pay: 14m sign-ups (Feb 2025), 9.10m monthly users (Q1 2026).
- Overseas: KB J Capital (Thailand, ₩11.3bn Q1 2026), KB Daehan Specialized Bank (Cambodia), KB Finansia (Indonesia, loss); ₩18.8bn combined in H1 2026; Myanmar office closing.
- Missing: employee count, issuer ratings, card-loan balances, network volumes.

## 2. KB Card × Mastercard
- Mastercard on premium and lifestyle cards: HERITAGE Smart (Mastercard World services, Jan 2023), HERITAGE Classic (Mastercard Platinum/World beside JCB and Visa, Dec 2024), KB YOU Prime (Mastercard or K-World JCB, Feb 2026).
- Travelers check card on Mastercard ⚠ undated product page: Travel Rewards cashback up to 10% (Nov 2025); Macao Edition with Mastercard Korea and MGTO (Oct 2025). KB has 11.4% of personal overseas check-card spend vs Hana 42.3% and Shinhan 31.0% (Jul 2026).
- Mastercard Crypto Partner Program (Sep 2026): stablecoin test in KB Pay with a Coinbase wallet. No executives named.
- Others: K-World own brand on JCB (Jul 2014) and UnionPay (Sep 2014) at the domestic fee; Visa on HERITAGE Classic SkyPass, the Apple Pay test (Jun 2026) and the Agentic Commerce Ready Program (Sep 2026); UnionPay cards; Amex listed ⚠; BC Card for KT co-brands ⚠. Networks of KB ALL, NEED Pay, NEED Edu, youth and corporate cards not stated ⚠.
- No public network agreement, renewal or switch found ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay (KB is testing with Visa's programme and SY Polaris's K-AP); Apple Pay tokens; commercial cards; scam and fraud tools; travel debit share; a stablecoin-linked card; SBI's Japan corridor.

# Citi — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Citi_Platform.html`. Three research threads (group profile; products and channels; Citi × Mastercard), condensed. Every product card in the page carries its own source links; this is the working summary. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. Citigroup — the group
- Citigroup Inc. (Delaware), 388 Greenwich Street, New York; NYSE: C. Market value ≈ $206bn at the 16 Oct 2026 close (our calculation: $124.26 × 1,655.2m shares) ⚠.
- Heritage: City Bank of New York 1812; Citicorp–Travelers merger 8 Oct 1998; Banamex bought 2001 (~$12.5bn); $45bn TARP 2008 plus a $301bn asset guarantee; Treasury's last common shares sold 6 Dec 2010.
- Ratings (30 Jun 2026): Citigroup Inc. Fitch A (positive) / Moody's A3 / S&P BBB+; Citibank, N.A. AA- / Aa3 / A+.
- Scale: FY2024 revenue $80.7bn, net income $12.7bn; FY2025 $85.2bn, $14.3bn (adjusted $16.1bn, excluding the $1.2bn Russia sale loss and a $726m Banamex goodwill impairment). 3Q26: revenue $23.8bn (+8%), net income $5.2bn, EPS $2.85, ROTCE 11.5%, CET1 12.8%; assets $2.94tn, deposits $1.5tn, loans $820bn. 9M26: revenue $73.2bn, net income $16.9bn.
- Footprint: business in nearly 160 countries and jurisdictions (10-K); payments to 180+ countries; staff ~226k at end-2025 (31% in the US).
- Segments since 1Q26: Services, Markets, Banking, Wealth, U.S. Consumer Cards (USCC) plus All Other (Legacy Franchises incl. Banamex). Retail banking moved into Wealth; USPB no longer exists. 3Q26 revenue: Services $6.6bn (+15%), Markets $6.1bn (+6%), Banking $1.7bn, Wealth $3.3bn (+15%), USCC $4.7bn (flat), All Other $1.4bn.
- Leadership: Jane Fraser, CEO since Mar 2021, Chair since 22 Oct 2025; CFO Gonzalo Luchetti (from Mar 2026; Mark Mason Executive Vice Chair, leaving by end-2026); Shahmir Khaliq (Services), Andy Morton (Markets), Vis Raghavan (Banking, Executive Vice Chair), Andy Sieg (Wealth), Pam Habner (USCC); Anand Selvakesari COO; Tim Ryan, Technology and Business Enablement; Banamex chair Fernando Chico Pardo, CEO Edgardo del Rincón (from 1 Jun 2026).
- Transformation: "Project Bora Bora" reorganisation (2023–24) into five businesses, layers 13 → 8, 20,000 roles cut (Jan 2024). Fed/OCC consent orders of 7 Oct 2020 still in force; ~$136m penalties Jul 2024; OCC lifted the 2024 amendment 18 Dec 2025; ~90% of programmes at or near target; audit to regulators targeted by early 2027 ⚠ (Reuters via PYMNTS). Transformation spend ~$3.3bn in 2025; 548 applications retired.
- Targets (Investor Day, 7 May 2026): ROTCE >11% in 2026 (raised 13 Oct), 11–13% 2027–28, 14–15% 2029–31; efficiency ratio <60% now, <55% later; $5bn business investment through 2028; $30bn multi-year buyback; $16.5bn returned in 9M26.
- Exits: consumer banking sold in Australia, Bahrain, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Taiwan, Thailand, Vietnam and Poland (VeloBank, 12 Jun 2026); China wound down; Korea winding down; Russia sold to Renaissance Capital (18 Feb 2026).
- Banamex: 25% to Fernando Chico Pardo (~$2.3bn, closed 2025); 24% to General Atlantic, Afore SURA, BTG Pactual, Chubb, Blackstone funds, Liberty Strategic Capital and QIA (~$2.5bn; last tranche 11 Aug 2026). Citi holds 51%; deconsolidation expected early 2027; IPO proposed, no date.
- Technology and AI: AI tools to 180,000+ staff in 85 countries (2025); Citi Assist, Citi Stylus (agentic Workspaces, Sep 2025), Arc agent platform (Apr 2026), AI code review (~100,000 hours a week); Citi Sky with Google (Apr 2026); hybrid cloud; one institutional and one consumer data store.
- Digital money: Citi Token Services (2024; euro Nov 2025; 24/7 USD Clearing link Sep 2025; Japan and UAE 28 Sep 2026; seven markets); Coinbase collaboration (Oct 2025); bitcoin custody planned for late 2026 ⚠; one of 21 banks in a joint USD stablecoin venture (1 Sep 2026) ⚠.
- Citi Ventures (Arvind Purushotham): 26 investments in 2025, 30+ exits incl. DocuSign and Plaid; BVNK (Oct 2025), Wealth.com, Endowus, Clara ⚠.

## 2. Products and channels
- Services: ~$6tn a day of payments to 180+ countries; ~17k clients incl. ~80% of the Fortune 500; TTS 3Q26 revenue $4.9bn (+16%), average deposits $867bn; #1 in payments, liquidity and trade (11.6% share, Coalition Greenwich). WorldLink 135+ currencies; cross-border value $116bn in 3Q26. Citi Payments Express in 22 markets, up to ~10m payments a day. 24/7 USD Clearing: 300+ banks. Liquidity: pooling in 80+ countries, $24tn a year swept. Trade: ~$1tn throughput 2025; Supplier Finance ~440k relationships. Commercial cards: $20bn spend in 3Q26 (+10%); Virtual Card Account on Mastercard In Control in ~50 countries. Spring by Citi (2019, Thredd and PPRO). Citi Payment Exchange (payee-choice payouts; Dandelion wallets). First bank live on Swift's multi-market instant payments (Sep 2026).
- Securities Services: AUC/A $35tn (3Q26, +16%); 3Q26 revenue $1.7bn; direct custody in 100+ markets.
- Markets: 2025 revenue $22.4bn; 3Q26 FICC $4.4bn, equities $1.7bn (+14%), record prime balances. #1 FX.
- Banking: 2025 IB fees $4.6bn (#5); 3Q26 IB $1.35bn (+9%, ECM +77%); corporate lending $415bn committed; Commercial Bank for $10m–$3bn revenue companies.
- Wealth: client investment assets $759bn, client balances $1.39tn; Private Bank ~14k clients, ~25% of the world's billionaires, ~2,000 family offices; Citigold ~400k US and ~200k international clients; Wealth at Work ~50k clients; BlackRock manages ~$80bn and Aladdin Wealth deployed (Sep 2025); hubs in Singapore, Hong Kong, UAE and London; ~650 US branches in six metros.
- U.S. Consumer Cards: ~70m customers; #3 US card lender, 14% of loans; 2025 revenue $18.3bn; 3Q26 loans $182bn, spend $175bn (+11%). Double Cash; Strata (replaced Rewards+ Jul 2025); Strata Premier; Strata Elite (28 Jul 2025, $595, first World Legend); Custom Cash closed to new applicants 28 May 2026; Simplicity; Diamond Preferred. Citi / AAdvantage (Platinum Select, Executive — World Legend from Aug 2026 — Globe, MileUp, Business). Costco Anywhere Visa. Retail Services: 12 key partners (Home Depot, Best Buy, Macy's, Exxon Mobil, Tractor Supply, Wayfair, Dillard's, L.L.Bean…); 12 programmes exited since 2022; private-label loans $27.6bn. Flex Pay; Flex Loan; Citi Pay at checkout via FreedomPay (2023) ⚠ scale.
- Loyalty: ThankYou (14m+ eligible; 19 transfer partners incl. AAdvantage since Jul 2025); Citi Travel with Booking.com (Mar 2023); Citi Entertainment; Merchant Offers; Kard acquisition agreed 13 Aug 2026.
- Access: Citi Mobile (digital actives +18% since 2022; no user count published); CitiDirect (with an AI assistant); CitiConnect and developer.citi.com (10bn+ API calls 2022–25); Citi Velocity; Zelle participant; Paze (first non-owner bank, Mar 2026).
- Open finance: the CFPB 1033 rule is enjoined and under reconsideration (proposal to OIRA 6 Aug 2026); no public Citi stance; no Citi–Plaid data-access deal found; Finicity in Citi's 2020 open-banking marketplace.

## 3. Citi × Mastercard
- Network: Mastercard carries Citi's proprietary US credit and debit (10-year agreement, 4 Mar 2015) and all AAdvantage cards; Visa carries Costco (term to 19 Jun 2029 after Costco's extension ⚠ via a secondary reading of an SEC exhibit) and part of commercial/SmartPay; American Express carries Macy's and Bloomingdale's co-brands. Renewal of the 2015 deal: not reported. Middle East 10-year agreement (Aug 2018). Singapore Visa cards being retired in favour of Mastercard (Citi Rewards Visa ended 15 Oct 2026).
- Timeline: In Control virtual cards (2009); Masterpass/Citi Pay (2017); Click to Pay via Token Connect (~2020 ⚠); Citi Plex debit (Nov 2020); City Possible (Oct 2021); Strata Premier on World Elite (May 2024); WorldLink + Move (10 Oct 2024); AA exclusive (5 Dec 2024); Strata Elite on World Legend (28 Jul 2025); Agent Pay first access with U.S. Bank (15 Sep 2025); Commerce Media partner (1 Oct 2025); Mastercard Installments with Flex Pay (24 Oct 2025); Barclays AA conversion (24 Apr 2026); In Control upgrade (Jul 2026); The Curated Table (Jul 2026); AAdvantage Executive World Legend (3 Aug 2026); Smart Subscriptions in the UAE (Sep 2026).
- Where it could land: Costco after 2029; renewal of the 2015 deal; Consumer Clarity and Smart Subscriptions in the US; Decision Intelligence / Brighterion (no public use); Open Finance depending on 1033; Multi-Token Network with Citi Token Services; more Move corridors; Click to Pay against Paze; merchant-funded value (Citi says it wants to reduce dependence on points; USCC non-interest revenue −62% in 3Q26 on rewards and partner costs).
- Market: Capital One–Discover (closed 18 May 2025) is the largest US card lender (~$270bn loans) and is moving volume onto its own network; the revised Visa/Mastercard merchant settlement (~$38bn) had preliminary approval on 9 Jun 2026.
- People: John Levitsky (Mastercard US co-president, issuers); Jason Lane and Victor Nordenson (Global Account Management); Kiki Del Valle; Seema Chibber; Marc Pettican. Citi: Pam Habner, Scott Southall (Commercial Cards), Abhinav Anand (Flex Pay).

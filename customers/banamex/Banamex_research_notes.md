## 1. The company
- Grupo Financiero Banamex, S.A. de C.V. and Banco Nacional de México, S.A. (founded 1884). Unlisted. Owned by Citigroup 2001–; separated from Citi México on 1 Dec 2024; brand relaunched Mar 2025.
- Ownership: Fernando Chico Pardo 25% (closed 15 Dec 2025; Chairman); investors 24% for about MXN 43bn / US$2.5bn (General Atlantic, Afore SURA, BTG Pactual, Chubb, Blackstone funds, Liberty Strategic Capital, QIA), 22.6% closed 29 Apr 2026, last 1.4% due Q3 2026 ⚠; Citi about 51% ⚠. IPO targeted January 2027, >US$3bn (Bloomberg via Yahoo, 27 Sep 2026) ⚠.
- 2Q26: net income Ps 6.7bn; assets Ps 1.40tn; loans Ps 541.5bn (cards Ps 173.5bn, payroll 73.7bn, mortgages 81.9bn, commercial 186.6bn); deposits Ps 805bn; AUM Ps 2.45tn; efficiency 49.0%; NPL 3.2%; CET1 18.8%, total capital 24.5% after US$1.3bn Tier 2 (May 2026).
- 2025: net income Ps 22.18bn; second-largest card portfolio (24% share); fourth by assets; 20m+ customers; 9.4m digital; 37,794 employees; 1,261 branches, 9,112 ATMs, 13,152 correspondents.
- Leadership: Fernando Chico Pardo (Chairman, group and bank); Edgardo del Rincón (CEO from 1 Jun 2026, after Manuel Romo); Patricio Diez de Bonilla (CFO); René Pimentel (CIB); Marcelo Santos (Afore); Enrique Fernández (HR, from Sep 2026). Citi's Ernesto Torres Cantú on the board.
- Strategy: Modernization Office (2025); digital, young customers (Switch, LineUp, Joy), regions, SMEs, CIB.

## 2. Banamex × Mastercard
- Credit: 10 of 14 cards Mastercard (Clásica, Oro, Platinum, Descubre, Explora, Conquista, Joy, Home Depot, Teletón, La Comer); 4 Visa (Beyond, Costco, LineUp, Affinity). No volume split published ⚠.
- Debit: Priority carries Mastercard benefits; everyday debit and Switch advertise Mastercard offers ⚠ inferred.
- Commercial cards (Purchasing, Distribution, Executive, TU Banamex prepaid): network not stated ⚠.
- Joint work: Banamex Wallet HCE (Feb 2016); Simplicity dynamic CVC (Jun 2021, retired ⚠); Pago Shops with Global Payments (Jul–Aug 2025); La Comer card (Sep 2025 ⚠ date); LoungeKey, Elite Lounge, Concierge, MasterSeguro.
- Acceptance: Global Payments' EVO (900m transactions, 250,000 POS), renewed Jul 2025.
- Wallets: Google Pay ~600,000 tokens (2025); Apple Pay and PayPal enrolment.
- No published network agreement, switch or renewal found ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Token services and Click to Pay; scam scoring on SPEI transfers; merchant data for Instant Clarity; commercial cards; card-linked offers for co-brands; Agent Pay for presales ⚠; contestable Visa programmes (Beyond, Costco) as new owners review contracts before the IPO.

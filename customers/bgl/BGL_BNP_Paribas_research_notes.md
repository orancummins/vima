## 1. The company
- BGL BNP Paribas S.A., Luxembourg; founded 1919 as Banque Générale du Luxembourg; Fortis from 1999; State rescue 2008; BNP Paribas 65.96% and Luxembourg State 34% since May 2009; listed 1984 (effectively no free float ⚠). HQ seKoia, Kirchberg (Sep 2025).
- 2025: revenue €2,008.5m (+4%), costs €921.8m, cost of risk €175.4m, net income €514.5m (+8%); balance sheet €64.6bn; CET1 20.6%; 2,083 staff; 22 branches; about 185,000 clients ⚠. Q2 2026 (CPBL): revenue €187m (+12%), pre-tax €100m.
- Lines: Retail & Corporate Banking (revenue +7%), Wealth Management (AuM +8%), Leasing International (BNP Paribas Leasing Solutions, 50% + 1 share since 2012 ⚠ current stake; revenue +8%). Life insurance from Cardif Lux Vie.
- Ratings A2 / A+ / AA- (page dated Oct 2022 ⚠).
- Leadership: Bob Kieffer (Chair), Nicolas Otton (CEO from 1 Jan 2026, after Béatrice Belorgey), Fernand Lepage (COO) and Séverine Campagne (CCO) from 1 Apr 2026, Fabrice Cucchi (innovation and transformation), Markus Stegmann (retail).
- Strategy: GTS plan (2022) in final phase; core banking moved to group cloud (2025); Genius chatbot; My Business Banking; i-Hub KYC; 2027–30 plan in preparation. AI metamodel R&D with SnT (€7.4m, Jan 2024); LuxemBERT (2022).
- Moves: Bancomat shared ATMs (Nov 2023); ING referral of about 18,000 clients (Dec 2024); EPI/Wero (Jun 2025); Payconiq off 30 Sep 2026.

## 2. BGL × Mastercard
- Debit: Visa Debit in every pack, launched with Visa 24 Nov 2020 (successor to V PAY ⚠); €2 a month.
- Credit: Visa Classic (€2) entry; Mastercard Gold (€5.50), Platinum (€17) and Elite (€28, terms Jul 2022) — pack choice between Visa Classic and Mastercard Gold. In 2013: V PAY, Visa Classic, Mastercard Blue, Mastercard Gold ⚠.
- Business: Visa Business only (€2,500 default limit).
- Mastercard return-postage cover on Gold, Platinum, Elite (BE and LU). Apple Pay since 21 May 2019 for V PAY, Visa and Mastercard; Garmin and Fitbit; Google Pay not listed ⚠.
- Acceptance: Axepta BNP Paribas (Bancontact, Visa, Mastercard, Amex). No network agreement, no Click to Pay, Installments, Move, fraud-tool or Agent Pay announcement found (no evidence, not proof of absence).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Mastercard business and virtual cards with spend controls in My Business Banking; premium benefits for private clients (Best Bank for UHNW 2026); FX tools for cross-border workers; Agent Pay alongside Genius; open-finance data for the aggregator; merchant tools via Axepta; debit only in the long run (no renewal date public).

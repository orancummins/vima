# BDO Unibank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `BDO_Unibank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BDO × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own as reported in the Philippine press (H1 2026 results, 25 Jul 2026; 2025 results, 27 Feb 2026) unless marked. BDO's website (bdo.com.ph) and Mastercard's newsroom refused automated requests, so several BDO pages are cited from search-engine text. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- BDO Unibank, Inc. (Banco de Oro; PSE: BDO), Makati. Founded 1968 as Acme Savings Bank; SM Group (Henry Sy) since 1976; Equitable PCI merger 2007; One Network Bank 2014 (now BDO Network Bank). SM Investments the controlling shareholder ⚠ current stake.
- Number one in the Philippines by assets, loans, deposits and trust funds (end-2025 and Q1 2026), ahead of BPI and Metrobank.
- H1 2026: net income ₱40.7bn (ROE 12.7%), loans +15% to ₱3.9tn, deposits +13%, CASA +4%, NPL 1.64%, CET1 13.1%.
- FY2025: record net income ₱87.2bn (ROE 14.4%), NII ₱203.1bn, total assets ₱5.43tn ⚠ (Quartr only), deposits ₱4.19tn (CASA 68%), cost-to-income 57.4%.
- Network: 2,000+ branches, 7,900+ teller machines, 13 international offices (Aug 2026). Customer count not public ⚠.
- Board elected 24 Apr 2026: Teresita T. Sy (chair ⚠), Nestor V. Tan (President and CEO). CFO and cards head not confirmed ⚠.
- Strategy: wealth, insurance and MSMEs; digital transformation; coal loans ≤2% by 2033; ₱115bn ASEAN Sustainability Bond (Jul 2025).
- Deals: Nomura stake (2020), The Podium (2023–24).

## 2. BDO × Mastercard
- ATM debit on Mastercard: "millions" of cards; first EMV Mastercard ATM debit in the country (Mar 2016). EMV Visa debit from 2017 ⚠.
- Remittances: 20 years with Mastercard (2006–2026), 4m+ Filipinos abroad; Jason Crasto (Mastercard PH) and Genie Gloria (BDO), 19 May 2026; "innovation in 2026" promised.
- Credit: six brands — Mastercard, Visa, Amex, JCB, UnionPay, Diners (exclusive issuer since Oct 2016). Split not public ⚠.
- Acquiring: accepts all brands plus BancNet, MegaLink, Expressnet.
- No public tokenisation, Click to Pay, fraud-tool or open-finance work ⚠.

## 3. Domestic competition
- InstaPay and PESONet: ₱24.7tn in 2025; ₱16.09tn and 4.2bn transfers in H1 2026; free in BDO Pay from Jul 2026.
- QR Ph: 2.47bn transactions, ₱1.16tn in 2025 — more than cards for the first time; 55% of PayMongo volume in H1 2026.
- BancNet: 2.37bn switched transactions (₱11.38tn) in 2024; 110 members, 121.2m cardholders.
- Visa: RCBC debit (Jan 2026), Visa Direct with USSC (Apr 2025) and BPI, GCash cross-border funding.

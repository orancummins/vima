# HBL — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `HBL_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and HBL × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (H1 2026 results, early August 2026; 2025 results, 18 Feb 2026; 2025 annual report; December 2025 investor presentation) unless marked. Q3 2026 results were not yet out. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Habib Bank Limited (PSX: HBL), Karachi. Founded 1941, moved to Pakistan 1947, nationalised 1974, privatised 2004. AKFED owns 56.58%. VIS AAA / A-1+. About 21,800 staff.
- Scale: 40m customers; about 2,000 branches incl. HBL Microfinance; 140,000+ touchpoints. H1 2026: PBT Rs 73.1bn, PAT Rs 34.5bn, assets Rs 8.0tn, deposits Rs 5.9tn, advances Rs 2.1tn, total CAR 17.2%. FY2025: PBT Rs 148.1bn (+23%), PAT Rs 66.8bn, domestic deposit share 12.65%.
- Segments: Retail; Consumer, SME & Agriculture; Corporate, Commercial & Investment Banking; Treasury; Financial Institutions; Branchless banking (2025 loss of Rs 2.8bn); International; Asset management; Microfinance.
- Leadership: Sultan Ali Allana (Chairman), Muhammad Nassir Salim (President & CEO since Mar 2024), Irfan Ahmed Meer (CFO), Aalishaan Zaidi (Retail), Aamir Kureshi (card launches).
- Strategy: "Scale with Profitability"; current accounts, Islamic (608 branches), SME, agriculture; Temenos core (480+ branches by Jun 2026); brand refresh 2026; exits from Belgium, Oman, Mauritius.

## 2. HBL × Mastercard
- Partners since 2013. Debit on Mastercard, Visa, UnionPay and PayPak; 6.4m debit cards (1 in 5 in Pakistan); split by network not published ⚠.
- Mastercard: World debit (premium; launch date ⚠), Classic and World Business debit (27 Aug 2025; J.K. Khalil), Easy Savings, Priceless, Identity Check, tokens in HBL Pay and Google Pay.
- Credit: 39% of Pakistan's credit-card spend; fact sheet lists Visa and Mastercard; Platinum is Visa; card art is Visa ⚠ mostly Visa.
- Visa: mPOS (2021–22), PITB PayZen gateway (2024), Konnect Visa QR, She's Next (2024, 2025).
- UnionPay: 1.8m cards since 2013; PayPak–UnionPay co-badge (signed Nov 2025, launched 15 Jul 2026); strategic MoU 17 Sep 2026.
- Domestic: PayPak debit; HBL Microfinance to issue the first PayPak credit card (29 Sep 2026); Raast via HBL Mobile and Konnect. Apple Pay not live ⚠.

## 3. Market
- SBP FY26: 69.9m cards (3.3m credit); Raast 2,755m payments, PKR 80tn; QR merchants 1.1m → 3.8m; POS 337,791 (+49%, Soft-POS); 1LINK a designated payment system (Aug 2025). Easypaisa first digital bank (Jan 2025). Mastercard expanding with the Bank of Punjab (Jul 2026).

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Credit and commercial cards; acquirer risk and merchant data for the 40,000-device POS estate (26% of national volume); G2P and subsidy cards through Konnect; inbound remittances; tokens as Apple Pay arrives; scam and fraud scoring.

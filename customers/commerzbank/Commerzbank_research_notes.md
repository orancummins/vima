## 1. The company

**Identity**
- Commerzbank AG, Frankfurt (CBK, DAX). Founded in Hamburg in 1870; took over Dresdner Bank in 2009 with a state rescue.
- The German state holds 12.11% (May 2026).
- Employees about 40,000 ⚠.

**Results**
- Q2 2026: revenues €3.3bn, operating result €1.37bn, net result €898m, net RoTE 12.5%, CET1 14.4%.
- H1 2026: record net result €1.81bn.
- FY2025: revenues €12.2bn, operating result €4.5bn, net result €2.6bn after €562m of restructuring, RoTE 8.7%.
- 2026 guidance: net result at least €3.4bn; about €3.2bn capital return, including a buyback of up to €1.2bn.

**Segments (Q2 2026)**
- Private and Small-Business Customers: revenues €1.26bn; €285bn of securities; €96bn of mortgages.
- Corporate Clients: revenues €1.23bn; about 24,000 client groups; about 30% of German foreign trade; more than 40 countries.
- mBank: revenues €672m; about 6m customers in Poland, the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

**Brands**
- Commerzbank, comdirect, mBank and Commerz Real.
- Commerz Globalpay: 49%, with Global Payments holding 51% (announced Jan 2024).

**Leadership**
- Bettina Orlopp (CEO), Carsten Schmitt (CFO), Michael Kotzbauer, Thomas Schaufler, Bernhard Spalt, Christiane Vorspel and Sabine Mlnarsky.
- Jens Weidmann chairs the supervisory board.

**Strategy**
- Momentum 2030. 2028 targets: RoTE 15%, cost-income 50%, net result €4.2bn.
- About 3,900 job cuts ⚠.
- AI: Copilot, Gemini, cobaGPT, Fraud AI and the Ava assistant.
- Crypto custody licence (Nov 2023).

**UniCredit**
- Held 26.77% before its exchange offer of 16 Mar 2026. 17.60% was tendered, for an overall position of about 49.65% with swaps ⚠.
- ECB and BaFin clearances, including EURO Kartensysteme and Commerz Globalpay, came on 15–16 Oct 2026 ⚠.
- Commerzbank opposes the offer.

## 2. Commerzbank × Mastercard

**Visa is now the preferred partner**
- 13 Feb 2025: Visa became Commerzbank's preferred card partner under a long-term deal.
- Girocards now carry Visa Debit, on all accounts (GiroKonto €4.90, KlassikKonto €9.90, PremiumKonto €12.90).
- Classic, Gold and Premium credit cards are "von Visa".
- comdirect has been on Visa since 2020.
- Pliant corporate cards are Visa.

**Mastercard credit being switched**
- From July 2026, Mastercard Classic holders are being asked in tranches to switch to Visa Classic.
- Keeping a Mastercard reportedly needs the PremiumKonto ⚠.

**What Mastercard keeps**
- A standalone Mastercard Debit (€3.50 a month, free with KlassikKonto).
- Identity Check on its cards.
- Prepaid cards on Mastercard or Visa ⚠.
- Acceptance through Commerz Globalpay.
- mBank credit cards (Mastercard Me, World Elite).
- BLIK's 2018 contactless tie-up.

**Not found**
- No Commerzbank–Mastercard agreement, tokenisation, Click to Pay, Agent Pay or commercial-card deal.
- A 2021 report of a Mastercard Debit girocard co-badge was overtaken by the Visa deal ⚠.

## 3. Competition and where Mastercard could land

**Competition**
- Visa on cards.
- girocard for domestic debit; Maestro leaves girocards by 2027.
- Wero: Commerzbank will join, most likely as a user rather than a shareholder; no date set.
- Amex Platinum, offered by referral.
- BLIK in Poland.
- Other German banks: HVB (UniCredit) is moving its debit cards to Mastercard. Deutsche Bank co-badges Debit Mastercard.

**Where Mastercard could land**
- UniCredit control, which could reopen the network choice. This is the biggest variable.
- Business and Mittelstand commercial and virtual cards, whose network is not stated ⚠.
- mBank issuing and Priceless.
- Scam and identity tools as instant payments and Wero grow.
- Agent Pay for Ava.
- Merchant-risk services through Commerz Globalpay.

# Eurobank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Eurobank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Eurobank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own as reported in the press (H1 2026, 30–31 Jul 2026; Q1 2026, 8 May 2026; FY2025, Mar 2026) unless marked. Eurobank's own websites (eurobank.gr, eurobankholdings.gr, eurobank.com.cy) refused automated reads, so its releases were read through Cyprus Mail, euro2day, Parapolitika and Euronext Athens. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results were not out at 17 October 2026.

## 1. The company
- Eurobank S.A., listed in Athens; it absorbed its holding company (Eurobank Ergasias Services and Holdings) on 12 Dec 2025, and 3.63bn new shares trade from 15 Dec 2025. Fairfax holds 32.67%. Market cap, ratings and customer count not confirmed ⚠. 12,408 staff and 562 branches (2025, Wikipedia).
- H1 2026: net profit €738m (adjusted €776m), RoTBV 16.6%, assets €112.9bn, loans €58.1bn, deposits €86.4bn, CET1 15.4%, NPE 2.5%, cost-to-income 36.6%. FY2025: net profit €1.36bn, adjusted €1.41bn.
- Countries: Greece (assets €66bn), Cyprus (€29.3bn; adjusted profit €231m), Bulgaria/Postbank (€14.6bn; €119m), Luxembourg private bank. Cyprus and Bulgaria are 46.5% of adjusted profit.
- Deals: acquiring 80% to Worldline (Dec 2021) and the last 20% for €72m (May 2026); Serbia sold (2023); Hellenic Bank to 100% (Jun 2025) and merged into Eurobank Cyprus (2025 ⚠ date); CNP Cyprus Insurance (€182m); Eurolife Life 80% for €813m (signed 7 May 2026, completion due Q3 2026 ⚠); Inbank 50/50 pay-later JV (Jul 2026, launch Q1 2027).
- Leadership: Georgios Zanias (Chair), Fokion Karavias (CEO), Deputy CEOs Konstantinos Vasileiou, Iakovos Giannaklis (retail and digital), Stavros Ioannou; Kostis Paikos (Group CDO). CFO not confirmed ⚠.
- Strategy: plan 2026–28 RoTBV ≥16% rising to 17%, payout >50%. Banking Forward (29 Jun 2026): about €1bn of tech spend 2025–28; 96% of transactions digital, 61% in the app; EVA, myEVA, GenAI Factory.

## 2. Eurobank × Mastercard
- 1 Jul 2026: expanded multi-year partnership; Eurobank to issue all its payment cards, for individuals and businesses, under Mastercard; Priceless for its Mastercard holders, a first in Greece. Named: Iakovos Giannaklis (Eurobank), Panagiotis Polydoros (Mastercard Greece, Cyprus and Malta). Reissue timetable and reach beyond Greece not stated ⚠.
- Before that, Greek cards were on both Visa and Mastercard (Google Pay launch, Oct 2021 ⚠); a Personal Banking Debit Visa is still listed ⚠.
- Eurobank Cyprus range (May 2026): Visa (debit, credit, Gold, Teens, Youth, Prepaid) and Mastercard (debit, credit, Platinum, Business, World Elite).
- Postbank Bulgaria credit: Mastercard Standard, Premium, Cash, World; Visa Classic, Gold, Premium.
- Acceptance: Worldline Greece (fully Worldline's since May 2026); Smart POS SoftPOS launched with Visa and IBA Group (Nov 2021).
- Not found: Click to Pay, Installments, Move, fraud tools, Agent Pay ⚠.

## 3. Market and where Mastercard could land
- Greece: Mastercard leads debit (Piraeus largest issuer); Visa leads credit through exclusive deals (Alpha Bank renewed May 2025 ⚠); Eurobank was the credit issuer gaining most (Euromonitor). IRIS A2A with QR online payments since 2023.
- Could land: token and billing continuity through the reissue; Cyprus and Bulgaria still split; Installments beside the Inbank JV; Agent Pay for EVA; fraud and identity tools; Move for the UPI corridor to India; card-linked offers for €pistrofi; commercial-card controls.

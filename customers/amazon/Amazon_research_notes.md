Companion to `Amazon_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Amazon × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Amazon's own (Q2 2026 results, 30 Jul 2026; FY2025 10-K, 6 Feb 2026; Q2 2026 10-Q) unless marked, with Synchrony's and Affirm's filings, issuer pages and press for the card programmes. ## 1. The company
- Amazon.com, Inc. (Nasdaq: AMZN), Seattle; founded 1994. Market value ≈$2.7tn (16 Oct 2026); ≈1.58m staff.
- FY2025: net sales $716.9bn (+12%); North America $426.3bn, International $161.9bn, AWS $128.7bn; operating income $80.0bn. Lines: online stores $269.3bn, third-party seller services $172.2bn, ads $68.6bn, subscriptions $49.6bn, physical stores $22.6bn. US $489.7bn, Germany $45.9bn, UK $43.2bn, Japan $30.7bn.
- Q2 2026: $200.6bn (+20%), AWS +37%; Amazon Business $60bn annualised; standby LCs $13.4bn "primarily" for payment services. Trailing FCF −$7.6bn. Prime member count not disclosed ⚠.
- Leadership: Andy Jassy (CEO), Doug Herrington (Stores), Matt Garman (AWS), Brian Olsavsky (CFO), Jeff Bezos (Executive Chair). Global Amazon Pay head unverified ⚠.
- Deals: Axio (India NBFC, Sep 2025, ~$200m); Anthropic notes $8bn; OpenAI $35bn commitment (Feb 2026); Affirm warrant; PAYFORT (2017) ⚠.
- AI: Rufus (300m+ users, ~$12bn extra annualised sales) merged with Alexa+ into Alexa for Shopping (Auto-Buy); Buy for Me beta (Apr 2025); AgentCore Payments preview with Coinbase (x402, USDC) and Stripe (7 May 2026). Amazon One palm payments ended 3 Jun 2026; Go and Fresh closed.

## 2. Amazon × Mastercard
- Co-brands: US Prime Visa / Amazon Visa (Chase, Visa); UK Barclaycard (Visa, since 12 Aug 2024; previously a Mastercard card, issuer ⚠); Germany Zinia (Visa, Aug 2024); Japan Amazon Mastercard (SMBC Card, since Feb 2014); Mexico Amazon Recargable prepaid (Banorte, Mastercard, Mar 2018); India Amazon Pay ICICI (network ⚠); Canada and Brazil ⚠ unverified; Amazon Business cards with Amex (US ⚠).
- Off-network: Synchrony Store Card (closed loop, extended 2025) and Pay Later; Affirm (Feb 2026–Jan 2031); UPI (110m users); USDC for agents.
- Visa: UK Visa credit ban announced Nov 2021, global agreement 17 Feb 2022; Visa Intelligent Commerce on AWS (1 Dec 2025), "multi-network" flows "coming soon".
- No Amazon × Mastercard announcement found on tokens, Click to Pay, instalments, Agent Pay, B2B cards or AWS. No acceptance dispute found 2015–2026.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay parity on AWS; Agent Pay for Machines beside AgentCore's USDC; agentic tokens and consent standards for Buy for Me and Auto-Buy; co-brands in Canada, Brazil, Mexico credit and India ⚠, and UK/Germany re-tenders; virtual cards for Amazon Business and seller payouts; cross-border and FX; fraud and identity.

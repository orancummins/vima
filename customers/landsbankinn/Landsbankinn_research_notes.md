# Landsbankinn — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Landsbankinn_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Landsbankinn × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (H1 2026 results and fact book, 23 Jul 2026; Q1 2026, 30 Apr 2026; 2025 results presentation and annual report, Feb 2026) unless marked. Little card detail is published in English and no page names a card network. ⚠ marks items that rest on inference or secondary sources. Q3 2026 results are due 29 October 2026.

## 1. The company
- Landsbankinn hf., Reykjastræti 6, Reykjavík. Current bank set up 7 Oct 2008; history back to 1886 (140 years in Aug 2026). Icelandic State owns 98.2%; shares unlisted; about 770 shareholders. S&P A-/A-2 stable (affirmed Mar 2026).
- H1 2026: profit ISK 22.1bn, ROE 13.4%, cost-income 31.5%, total assets ISK 2,417bn, loans ISK 1,934bn, deposits ISK 1,331bn, total capital ratio 24.7%, 935 FTE. FY2025: profit ISK 38.0bn, ROE 11.6%.
- Market shares: retail 40.2%; deposits 36.8%; lending 29.1% (CBI, Nov 2025); about 40% of corporate lending; acquiring about 25% (end-2025) and 27% (Q1 2026).
- Segments: Personal Banking; Corporate Banking; Asset Management & Capital Markets; Treasury and Market Making; TM tryggingar; Other.
- Leadership: Jón Þorvarður Sigurgeirsson (Chair), Lilja Björk Einarsdóttir (CEO), Hreiðar Bjarnason (CFO).
- Deals: TM bought from Kvika (completed 28 Feb 2025, about ISK 32.3bn); Yayland with YAY for prepaid and gift cards (Jun 2025); 47.9% of Greiðslumiðlun Íslands sold to Síminn (Oct 2025) ⚠ completion.
- Strategy "an ever-smarter bank": 2026 guidance ROE 11–13%, cost-income 32–35%, CET1 >18%. Vild loyalty (summer 2026); Ellí chatbot (95.6% answer rate); TM insurance in the app.

## 2. Landsbankinn × Mastercard
- Issuing on Visa: price list charges a margin on Visa's rate for all foreign card transactions; Visa emergency line on debit and credit pages; Business and Business Premium artwork shows Visa; Visa bills from Rapyd Europe (ex-Valitor) since Apr 2023. Credit network inferred ⚠.
- Separate Visa and MasterCard credit-card terms merged and voided in Mar 2019 ⚠ date Mastercard cards ended unknown.
- Prepaid and gift cards (Yayland): network not stated ⚠.
- Acquiring (launched 2023): accepts Visa and Mastercard only; Verifone terminals; 3,300 POS; volumes tripled in 2025.
- Click to Pay from end-2025 on its (Visa) cards.
- No joint Mastercard initiative found. Íslandsbanki's debit range is Mastercard; Arion's is Visa.
- Market: >90% of retail payments on Visa and Mastercard; Central Bank planning a domestic A2A option (Jan 2025) ⚠ launch unconfirmed; offline card payments up to ISK 50,000 from 1 Jul 2026 ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Merchant risk, disputes (5,600 chargebacks after Play's collapse) and data for the acquirer; scam scoring (1,465 fraud cases in 2025); card-linked offers for Aukakrónur and Vild; Yayland prepaid; commercial cards; a future network tender.

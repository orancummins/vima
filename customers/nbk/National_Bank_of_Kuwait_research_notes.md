## 1. The company
- National Bank of Kuwait S.A.K.P., Kuwait City; opened 15 Nov 1952 as Kuwait's first indigenous bank; listed since 1984; PIFSS the only holder above 5% (6.19%). Market value KD 7.40bn (30 Jun 2026). Rated A1/A+/A+. 8,418 employees.
- H1 2026: net profit KD 324.8m (+3%), net operating income KD 662.0m, assets KD 46.2bn, loans KD 27.8bn, deposits KD 27.0bn, CET1 13.0%, NPL 1.22%, ROAE 14.4%. FY2025: net profit KD 576m (−5% after the new 15% tax), NOI KD 1,297m.
- More than 30% of Kuwaiti banking assets; over 30% of trade finance; international about 25% of profit. No customer count published.
- Businesses: Consumer (incl. Weyay Bank, Kuwait's first digital bank), Corporate and Transactional, Islamic (Boubyan Bank, 60.4%), NBK Wealth, International (13 countries; 70 branches in Kuwait, 53 in Egypt, three in Saudi Arabia).
- Leadership: Hamad Al-Bahar (Chairman), Isam Al-Sager (Vice-Chairman, Group CEO), Shaikha Al-Bahar (Deputy Group CEO), Salah Al-Fulaij (CEO Kuwait), Mohammed Al-Othman (Consumer and Digital), Sujit Ronghe (CFO).
- Deals: UPayments 51% (2025 ⚠ date); DIFC branch (Nov 2025).
- Tech 2025: TCS BaNCS, BASE24 card-switch upgrade, tokenisation and 3DS, AFAQ GCC instant payments, real-time fraud platform, Mobile ID onboarding, Copilot; aim to be Kuwait's leading AI-driven bank.
- 2026 backdrop: regional conflict and Hormuz closure slowed projects and travel card income.

## 2. NBK × Mastercard
- Visa: seven-year renewal Dec 2022 (Salah Al-Fulaij); KWT Visa Infinite (2022), Harrods Visa Infinite (2025), Kuwait Airways and KPC Visa cards, Privilege and Thahabi Visa Platinum debit, Visa commercial cards (Spend Clarity), 2026 summer campaign.
- Mastercard: NBK-Aura World Mastercard and Platinum prepaid with Alshaya (2025 ⚠); Miles World, World, Platinum, UEFA (Mar 2021 ⚠), Titanium cards; June 2025 foreign-spend campaign (Anwar Al Ballam); May 2018 biometric card trial and Identity Check ⚠ current status.
- KNET: standard debit domestically, Cirrus abroad ⚠ (scheme brand inferred); WAMD in the NBK app since 30 Jun 2024; Apple Pay since 6 Dec 2022, routed via KNET.
- Diners Club credit; acquiring accepts KNET, Visa, Mastercard, Diners and wallets. Weyay card networks not stated ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Offers and loyalty on Aura; commercial and virtual cards; network fraud and identity tools; Weyay, Jeel and fintech issuing; merchant tools for UPayments; cross-border flows (Move) across 13 countries; agentic payments.

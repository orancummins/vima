# Banque de Développement Local (BDL) — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Banque_de_Developpement_Local_BDL_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BDL × Mastercard), condensed. Sources are mostly French: BDL's H1 2026 COSOB report (October 2026), 2025 annual report (June 2026), AGM papers and product pages, GIE Monétique's statistics, and Algerian press. No English investor material, no credit rating, and no Mastercard or Visa newsroom item on BDL were found. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Public bank created 30 April 1985, HQ Staouéli (Algiers). Treasury 70%, market 30% since the 13 March 2025 listing (DA 61.88bn raised at DA 1,400; 11,583 shareholders). Market value DA 207.8bn at DA 1,410 (30 Jun 2026).
- H1 2026: assets DA 2,380.7bn (capital page says 2,397bn ⚠), deposits DA 1,900.2bn, net loans DA 1,127.6bn, commitments DA 2,481bn (includes off-balance-sheet ⚠), PNB DA 45.2bn (+49.7%), net profit DA 22.3bn (+66.3%), CAR 15.2%. FY2025: net profit DA 27.3bn, dividend DA 15.77bn.
- Revenue split H1 2026: private sector 48%, public sector 46%, households 6%. Monetics about 0.7% of revenue.
- 4,685 staff; 185 branches (press, Aug 2026). Customer count not published ⚠.
- Leadership: Mustapha Ferfera, chairman and CEO (named 23 Jul 2026; single-head model adopted 7 Sep 2026); Mohamed Mebarek, deputy DG. Youcef Lalmas was DG to 2025.
- Stakes: SATIM 14.7%, SNL 50%, FINALEP 66%, CPI 5.75%, stock exchange operator 16.3%, SAPS 15%.
- Technology: new core system with EADN ⚠, ISO 20022 on ARTS (H1 2026). No public AI work ⚠.

## 2. BDL × Mastercard
- Mastercard Titanium and Platinum on foreign-currency accounts since July 2018.
- Visa Classique and Gold alongside; launch about 2024 ⚠.
- From 19 Jul 2026 the €750 travel allowance is paid only onto an international card (Visa or Mastercard); BDL halved card fees in August 2026; card waits of months across banks.
- E-commerce international: cross-border acquiring on Visa (May 2025, a first in Algeria; over €1m by Dec 2025) and Mastercard (Dec 2025).
- CNAS diaspora contributions on Visa only; Mastercard "coming soon".
- SATIM convention (13 Dec 2023, Lalmas and SATIM's Madjid Messaouden): BDL as Mastercard principal member and sponsor of local Mastercard processing ⚠ go-live unknown.
- Everyday rails are domestic: CIB cards and corporate cards, 17,246 TPE and web merchants on CIB and EDAHABIA, WIMPAY on DZ Mob Pay.

## 3. Market
- GIE Monétique (2014; 18 banks and Algérie Poste) runs CIB; 23.3m CIB and EDAHABIA cards, 116,315 TPE, 4,960 ATMs (Jul 2026). E-payments DA 939bn in 2025 (+46%). DZ Mob Pay interbank QR since Jan 2025. Cashless target 2028. Governor Mohamed Lamine Lebbou since Feb 2026. Instant payments announced, not live ⚠.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Foreign-card acceptance at BDL's 17,000 terminals; fraud and authentication tools for exporters; Mastercard on the CNAS platform and diaspora flows; faster, tokenised allowance cards; an international corporate card; finishing SATIM processing.

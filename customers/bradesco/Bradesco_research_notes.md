# Banco Bradesco — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Bradesco_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Bradesco × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (2Q26 results, 6 Aug 2026; 4Q25 results, 6 Feb 2026; Form 20-F, 25 Mar 2026) unless marked. The search budget ran out after 11 searches; the rest came from EDGAR filings, the IR site, Bradesco's pages and trade press. ⚠ marks items that are uncertain, conflicting or inferred. 3Q26 results are due 22 October 2026 ⚠ (an earlier calendar said 4 November).

## 1. The company
- Banco Bradesco S.A., Cidade de Deus, Osasco (B3: BBDC3, BBDC4; NYSE: BBD). Founded 1943; renamed 1988. Market value not reliably known for October ⚠; PN R$20.39 on 16 Oct 2026. Bank ratings not retrieved ⚠ (Brazil sovereign Ba1/BB/BB).
- 2Q26: recurring net income R$7.05bn (tenth straight rise), ROAE 16.2%, revenue R$37.6bn, card income R$4.49bn (43% of fees), expanded loans R$1,136.6bn, efficiency 46.5%, NPL90 4.3%, CET1 11.3%. FY2025: R$24.7bn (+26.1%).
- 74.3m clients, 37.7m checking accounts, 28.5m active app users, 99% of transactions digital; 82,095 staff. Digio 10.0m clients; Principal >320k.
- Segments: banking (individuals R$479.7bn, MSMEs R$267.5bn, large corporates R$389.4bn of loans) and insurance (R$10.1bn 2025 net income; 22.8% share; "largest insurance group in Latin America" by its own account).
- Stakes: Elopar 50.01% (Elo, Livelo, Alelo, with BB); Cielo jointly controlled, delisted Sep 2024; Digio 100%; Banco John Deere 50%; Bradsaúde 91.35%; Atlântica D'Or. Elo ownership being equalised to thirds ⚠ (completion not confirmed).
- Leadership: Luiz Carlos Trabuco Cappi (Chair), Marcelo Noronha (CEO since Nov 2023, ex-Bradesco Cartões), Cassiano Scarpelli (CFO), José Ramos Rocha Neto (payments boards), Fernando Freiberger (Bradesco Payments, BaaS). Head of cards not identified ⚠.
- Strategy: ten priorities; Principal (Nov 2024, 70+ cities by end-2026); 150 SME branches; MEI app. R$10bn capital increase (29 Jul 2026; approved 8 Oct 2026). BIA generative-AI assistant for all clients.

## 2. Bradesco × Mastercard
- No network split published; the 20-F lists Elo, American Express, Visa, Mastercard. Credit-card spend R$360.4bn on 2.714bn transactions (2025).
- Elo: co-owned; incentive programme (2 Apr 2025) pays Bradesco for Elo issuance until 31 Dec 2034 — the only network agreement disclosed.
- Visa: Aeternum, Infinite, Signature, Platinum, the Disney co-brand; the debit page promotes Visa and Elo. Amex: Bradesco is the issuer (Centurion, Platinum, Gold, Green).
- Mastercard: Mastercard Black (relaunched Feb 2024), the Amazon co-brand (Aug 2023 ⚠ current status), Surpreenda tile. No master agreement, Click to Pay or tokenisation announcement found. A 2017 forum post said Mastercard Black had been dropped ⚠ (not authoritative).
- Agentic: Mastercard Agent Pay for LatAm (10 Dec 2025) did not name Bradesco; Visa Agentic Ready did (Aug 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay through BIA; a World Elite proposition for Principal and high-income growth (52% of card revenue, +29%); fraud and scam intelligence across cards and Pix; processing and BaaS for Digio's white label; cards for Principal's US International Account; small-firm commercial cards; card instalments while Pix Parcelado is suspended.

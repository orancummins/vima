# NatWest Group — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `NatWest_Group_Platform.html`. Two research threads (company profile; NatWest × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (H1 2026 results, 31 July 2026; FY2025 results, 13 February 2026) unless marked. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are due 30 October 2026.

## 1. NatWest Group — the company
- NatWest Group plc, Edinburgh-registered, London-run; LSE (NWG) and NYSE ADRs. More than 20 million customers and about 1.5 million businesses; 60,000 colleagues.
- H1 2026: income £8,862m, operating profit £4.3bn, RoTE 19.7%, cost:income 46%, CET1 13.2% (after Evelyn), assets £745.4bn, loans £435.9bn, deposits £448.6bn. FY2025 operating profit £7.7bn, RoTE 19.2%. 2026 guidance RoTE >19%; 2028 targets RoTE >18%, cost:income <45%, CET1 ~13%.
- Ownership: 2008 rescue (£45.5bn; up to 84%); Treasury's last shares sold 30 May 2025.
- Segments: Retail Banking (19m customers; £219bn mortgages, third in the UK), Private Banking & Wealth Management (Coutts, Evelyn; £130.6bn client assets), Commercial & Institutional (business banking, mid-market, corporates, NatWest Markets, RBS International).
- Leadership: Paul Thwaite (CEO), Rick Haythornthwaite (Chair), Katie Murray (CFO), Solange Chamberlain (Retail), Robert Begbie (C&I), Emma Crystal (PBWM), Mark Brant (chief payments officer).
- Deals: Metro Bank mortgages (2020, 2024), Sainsbury's Bank (2025), Evelyn Partners (£2.2bn, closed 30 Jun 2026); Cushon sold (2026). Ulster Bank left the Republic of Ireland (2023).
- Technology and AI: £1.2bn in 2025; OpenAI (Mar 2025, first UK bank), AWS, Accenture, Google; Cora (12.9m conversations in 2025), Cora+ and Spending Chat (OpenAI models, ~25,000 customers); mortgage app in ChatGPT (Apr 2026); five C&I agents live. 336 branches kept open to 2029; 11.4m app users.

## 2. NatWest × Mastercard
- Debit: ~16m personal and business debit cards (NatWest, RBS, Ulster, Coutts) moved from Visa to Mastercard (announced Feb 2021); Nilson: 2019 debit spend $148bn, third-largest debit issuer in Europe. Term not public.
- Credit: core range mostly Mastercard. Visa: Travel Reward card (Aug 2024, Team GB); Sainsbury's Nectar cards ⚠ reportedly moving from Mastercard to Visa; Rooster Card prepaid on Visa.
- Consumer Fraud Risk: early adopter (2023); PSR 2024 data ranks NatWest best of 20 for scam money received, fourth for refunds.
- Approval2Buy: commercial virtual cards on Mastercard's mobile virtual card app (Nov 2024).
- Tyl: built 2019 with Pollinate (RBS and Mastercard invested) on First Data processing.
- Open banking: Payit (NatWest's own A2A, VRPs); joined Mastercard's Pay by Bank app (Aug 2021).
- Agentic: live on Visa's agentic payments with 30+ European issuers (Jul 2026); co-published principles for trusted agentic commerce with Bank of America, Capital One, CBA, ING and ASB (22 Sep 2026), no network named. No Mastercard Agent Pay transaction announced.
- People: Salim Secretary, head of scheme partnerships.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay (Visa went first); merchant enrichment for Cora and Spending Chat; open-banking payments and VRPs alongside Payit; win back Travel Reward and Nectar; Consumer Clarity in the app; carbon on card spend next to the transition-finance targets; the 2021 debit agreement's renewal.

# Commercial Bank of Ceylon: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Commercial_Bank_of_Ceylon_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and ComBank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (H1 2026 results release, 13 Aug 2026; FY2025 results, 26 Feb 2026; 2025 integrated annual report) unless marked. Market data are from the CBSL Payments Bulletin for Q1 2026. ComBank does not publish cards by network; networks come from its card pages. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are due in November 2026.

## 1. The company
- Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC (CSE-listed; founded 1920). Sri Lanka's largest private bank and the only private higher-tier D-SIB. 4m+ customers, 272 branches, 5,726 staff; Fitch AA-(lka). Widely held: two private investors and DFCC Bank (about 9.6–9.9% each), EPF 7.3%, IFC funds about 14%. Market cap USD 1.06bn at end-2025.
- H1 2026 (group): PAT Rs 35.4bn (+13.7%), ROE 20.3%, deposits Rs 3.02tn, loans Rs 2.36tn, assets Rs 3.74tn, Tier 1 13.2%. Q2 impairments almost tripled. FY2025 (bank): PAT Rs 58.5bn; market share of loans 15.4%, deposits 13.3%, exports 22%.
- Segments: Personal Banking (37% of group PBT), Corporate Banking, International Operations (31% of group PBT, mostly Bangladesh), Dealing/Treasury, NBFI. Entities: CBC Bangladesh, Commercial Bank of Maldives (55%), CBC Myanmar Microfinance, CBC Finance, Orysys, DIFC office.
- Leadership: Chairman Sharhan Muhseen; MD/CEO Sanath Manatunge; COO Hasrath Munasinghe (from 27 Apr 2026, ex-cards and retail); CIO Sumudu Gunawardhana; CDO Randil Boteju.
- Technology: Euronet switch, IDEMIA cards and tokens, Axonect API layer, Bancstac gateway technology (Apr 2026); AI SME underwriting with BCG (Jul 2025); retail transformation with McKinsey.

## 2. ComBank × Mastercard
- Multi-network issuer: classic debit and Gold/Silver/Platinum credit on Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay and LankaPay/JCB; No. 1 in debit issuance and usage. Split not published ⚠.
- Mastercard: World Mastercard; all co-brand and affinity cards (Anagi, school affinity, NEGC, Mobitel); corporate fuel card; SME credit ⚠; corporate cards alongside Visa.
- Visa: Infinite, Signature, Signature Elite debit (Feb 2025), BIZ Club debit, travel and prepaid, corporate tiers (Feb 2024), first Google Pay (Nov 2025), FIFA World Cup campaign (2026).
- UnionPay: Asia Prestige with LoungeKey (Sep 2025), SplendorPlus (Jan 2026), 3DS 2.x (May 2026). LankaPay/JCB: first credit issuer (Mar 2023), first 3DS (Mar 2026), fee-waiver drive to Dec 2026.
- Mastercard joint work: MPGS since 2007; online DCC on MPGS with FEXCO and Euronet (30 Dec 2025); Google Pay for Mastercard (9 Feb 2026); Soundbox with Payable, "powered by Mastercard" (15 Oct 2026); Priceless Rugby World Cup trips (2019, 2023). Mastercard leads: Sandun Hapugoda (Group Country Manager from Jun 2026), Mahesha Amarasuriya (Country Manager Sri Lanka).
- Bancstac IPG agreement (17 Apr 2026): ⚠ unclear whether it replaces MPGS.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Click to Pay and card-on-file tokens for the gateway and Q+; keeping e-commerce on MPGS; AI fraud scoring (2026 plan, no vendor named); commercial virtual cards for the top trade and SME bank; Mastercard Move for remittances and GIG+; card-linked offers for Max Rewards; World Elite and Priceless for Prestige; Goldnet's eight client issuers.

## 4. Market
- CBSL Q1 2026: 2.23m credit and 20.9m debit cards; 144,046 POS terminals; 473,214 LANKAQR merchants but only Rs 1.1bn of LANKAQR payments in the quarter; CEFTS Rs 6.8tn in the quarter. National QR launched April 2026 with small-payment fees waived ⚠. Google Pay at ComBank, Seylan and Sampath; BOC Flex Tap & Pay with Mastercard (Jul 2026).

# Bank Central Asia (BCA) — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Bank_Central_Asia_BCA_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BCA × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are BCA's own (1H26 analyst presentation, 28 Jul 2026; FY25 presentation, Feb 2026) and BCA's product pages unless marked; market data from Bank Indonesia via press. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results were not out on 17 October 2026.

## 1. The company
- PT Bank Central Asia Tbk (IDX: BBCA), Menara BCA, Jakarta. Market value Rp769tn (27 Jul 2026), the largest Indonesian bank by value. PT Dwimuria Investama Andalan 54.94%; Robert Budi Hartono ultimate shareholder after Bambang Hartono's death. Fitch BBB, outlook negative ⚠; Pefindo idAAA. About 25,000 staff ⚠ (inferred from 860 leavers = 3.4% turnover).
- 1H26: net profit Rp29.5tn (+1.8%), assets Rp1,661tn, loans Rp1,036tn (+8.0%), CASA Rp1,082tn (85.2% of funding), ROE 24.1%, NIM 5.3%, NPL 1.9%. FY25: net profit Rp57.5tn (+4.9%).
- Customers: 34.3m (FY25 deck) vs 32.9m (1H26 deck) ⚠. 1,272 branches, 20,300 ATMs; 99.8% of transaction volume digital.
- Loans: corporate Rp513tn (+13.6%), commercial Rp154tn, SME Rp135tn, consumer Rp221tn (mortgages Rp144tn; vehicles Rp51tn, -21.5%).
- Subsidiaries: BCA Digital (blu), BCA Syariah, BCA Finance (absorbed BCA Multi Finance, 2024 ⚠), BCA Sekuritas, BCA Insurance, BCA Life, Central Capital Ventura.
- Leadership: Gregory Hendra Lembong (President Director from 1 Jun 2025), Jahja Setiaatmadja (President Commissioner), Armand Wahyudi Hartono and John Kosasih (Deputy Presidents), Santoso (director; cards).
- Strategy: the transaction bank for whole supply chains; myBCA (users x24 in four years); on-premise LLM, ISO 42001; AI in onboarding, fraud (complaints -47%), credit and collateral.

## 2. BCA × Mastercard
- Debit: Paspor BCA in a GPN variant or a Mastercard variant (launched 21 Nov 2017, Jahja Setiaatmadja and Mastercard's Safdar Khan). No Visa debit found.
- Credit (about 4.9m cards, June 2025 ⚠): BCA Card (own network); Visa (KrisFlyer Signature/Infinite, PPS Club, Black, Batman); Mastercard (Black, World, Globe, Blibli Tiket); JCB Black; UnionPay; Amex Platinum ⚠.
- BCA Blibli Tiket Mastercard launched 12 Sep 2026, merging the Blibli and tiket.com (2021) co-brands: about 360,000 cards, nearly Rp3tn a year.
- blu: bluDebit, Garuda x bluDebit and bluVirtual all on Mastercard.
- Visa: FIFA World Cup top-spender (Mar–Apr 2026) and BTS ticket draw (Aug–Nov 2026 ⚠) for BCA Visa holders.
- Domestic rails: GPN debit and Business Debit Card; QRIS and QRIS Tap; BI-FAST; Flazz; KKI (BCA one of eight issuers, 17 Aug 2026); Paylater BCA (196,000 users).
- Not found: tokenisation programmes, Click to Pay, Send/Move, commercial cards, Agent Pay with Mastercard ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Travel spending moving to bilateral QR (six countries); tokenised credentials in myBCA; commercial and virtual cards for the corporate book; fraud and identity scoring (Mastercard already scores GPN debit through Artajasa ⚠); card-linked offers for Reward BCA and the Affin deal; Agent Pay.

## 4. Market
- QRIS: 65.77m users, 44.86m merchants (Jun 2026); July 2026 volume +82.42%. BI-FAST 546m transactions in July 2026. Industry credit cards about 18.75m (Apr 2026) ⚠.
- US named QRIS and GPN as trade barriers (2025); the 19 Feb 2026 trade deal promises a level playing field for US payment firms ⚠.

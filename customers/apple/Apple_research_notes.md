# Apple: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Apple_Platform.html`. One research thread (Apple's payments business and Apple × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Apple's own (Q3 FY2026 results and statements, 30 Jul 2026; Q4 FY2025 statements, 30 Oct 2025) unless marked, with Apple's newsroom, product, support and developer pages, JPMorgan Chase, the European Commission and the CFPB. SEC EDGAR refused automated fetching, so the 10-K and 10-Q were not read directly. ⚠ marks items that rest on prior knowledge or secondary sources. Q4 FY2026 results are due at the end of October.

## 1. The company
- Apple Inc. (Nasdaq: AAPL), Cupertino. Market value about $4tn (Apple, Apr 2026). Employees about 166,000 ⚠.
- FY2025 (to 27 Sep 2025): $416.2bn of sales; Services $109.2bn; iPhone $209.6bn; net income $112.0bn. Segments: Americas $178.4bn, Europe $111.0bn, Greater China $64.4bn, Japan $28.7bn, Rest of Asia Pacific $33.7bn.
- Q3 FY2026 (to 27 Jun 2026): $109.4bn (+16%); Services $30.7bn (+12%); net income $29.8bn; EPS $2.02.
- Payments numbers: none published. Apple Pay is in 101 countries and regions (30 Sep 2026). Active users grew at a double-digit rate in Q4 FY2025 ⚠.
- Leadership: John Ternus, CEO from 1 Sep 2026; Tim Cook, Executive Chairman; Arthur Levinson, Lead Independent Director; Kevan Parekh, CFO; Jennifer Bailey, VP Apple Pay and Apple Wallet; Eddy Cue, SVP Services ⚠.
- Entities: Apple Payments Services LLC serves Goldman Sachs (Apple Card, Savings) and Green Dot (Apple Cash). Apple Financing LLC lent Apple Pay Later. Apple holds no bank licence.
- Products:
  - Apple Pay (2014), Apple Card (2019), Apple Cash, Savings, Apple Card Family and Monthly Installments.
  - Instalments and rewards from partners in Apple Pay: iOS 18 online; in store from iOS 26.
  - Tap to Pay on iPhone (2022): 59 countries, 100+ PSPs.
  - In Wallet: Digital ID (Nov 2025), and licences in 12 states and Puerto Rico.
  - iOS 27: a new checkout sheet, Tap to Share, Create a Pass and bill splitting ⚠.
- Deals: Mobeewave (2020) ⚠, Credit Kudos (2022) ⚠, Chase to replace Goldman Sachs as Apple Card issuer (7 Jan 2026, about 24 months, $20bn+ of balances).
- Rules:
  - EU: NFC commitments binding from 11 Jul 2024 for ten years (free HCE access, default wallet choice).
  - Elsewhere: a paid NFC & SE Platform in 49 more territories.
  - US: the DOJ suit (Mar 2024) names wallets; the motion to dismiss was denied (Jun 2025); talks were reported in Jul 2026.
  - CFPB Apple Card order (Oct 2024; Apple $25m), terminated Sep 2025.

## 2. Apple × Mastercard
- Network split:
  - Apple Card: Mastercard, staying through the move to Chase.
  - Apple Pay Later (Mar 2023 to Jun 2024): ran on Mastercard Installments, with a Goldman-issued Mastercard credential.
  - Apple Cash: Visa (virtual card "anywhere Visa is accepted").
  - Apple Pay and Tap to Pay: every network.
  - Apple Pay India (30 Sep 2026): Axis Bank on Visa and Mastercard; not RuPay or UPI.
- Mastercard tokens behind Mastercard cards in Apple Pay ⚠ (not re-verified).
- Named executives: Ajay Banga (Apple Card launch, 2019); Linda Kirkpatrick (Chase move, 2026).
- Not found: Click to Pay, Agent Pay, loyalty, open finance or fraud tools with Apple. The Apple Pay sheet competes with Click to Pay.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- The Apple Card conversion to Chase: tokens, benefits, disputes and notifications.
- Mastercard Installments and rewards through issuers on the iOS 27 checkout sheet.
- Loyalty and offers at Tap to Share.
- Credentials for HCE wallets in the EEA and on the NFC & SE Platform.
- More Indian issuers.
- Agentic tokens if Apple moves ⚠.
- Apple Cash only if re-tendered ⚠.

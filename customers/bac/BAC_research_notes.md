# BAC — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `BAC_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BAC × Mastercard), condensed. One customer covers all six BAC markets. Figures are BAC's own (IIQ 2026 corporate presentation, results to 30 June 2026, published September 2026) unless marked. BAC publishes little in English and no network split; card networks are read from BAC's country catalogues, which count products, not volumes. The web-search budget ran out early, so Mastercard and Visa newsrooms were not searched directly. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- BAC Holding International Corp. (Panama; listed in Bogotá and Panama) → BAC International Corp. → BAC International Bank, Inc. (Panama-regulated) → six local banks. Spun off from Grupo Aval/Banco de Bogotá in 2022; Sarmiento Angulo family companies bought Banco de Bogotá's 25% in Dec 2022 and control BHI ⚠ (current %).
- H1 2026: assets US$46.9bn, loans US$33.2bn, deposits US$35.1bn (each about +20% with Multibank); net income US$357m (−8.2% on a colón FX loss; underlying +11.6%); ROAE 18.0%; total capital 12.5%. FY2025 net income US$789m.
- Ratings: S&P BBB-, Fitch BBB- (investment grade, Sep 2026), Moody's Ba1.
- 6.3m clients (4.1m digital), 21,000+ staff, 300,000+ businesses. 96% of monetary transactions digital.
- Loans: corporate 49%, consumer 36%, mortgage 16%; Panama 30%, Costa Rica 27%, Guatemala 15%, Honduras 12%, El Salvador 9%, Nicaragua 6%.
- Six markets, H1 2026 net income: Costa Rica US$90m, Honduras US$86m, Panama US$64m, Guatemala US$63m, Nicaragua US$56m, El Salvador US$27m.
- Multibank: 99.57% of Multi Financial Group bought 18 Mar 2026 (US$26.8611/share; about US$5bn assets); merged into BAC Panamá 9 Jun 2026. Panama rank: 2nd by loans; 2nd or 3rd by assets ⚠.
- Leadership: Rodolfo Tabash Espinach (President and CEO), Ramón Chiari Brin (BAC Panamá). Other executives not found ⚠.
- Payments through BAC about US$226bn in 2025 (55% of Central American GDP); US$122bn H1 2026.

## 2. BAC × Mastercard
- Long-term agreements with Visa, Mastercard and American Express "among others". About 41% credit issuer share and 54% acquirer share; debit about 43% ⚠ (read from chart).
- Costa Rica catalogue: credit 27 Amex, 20 Visa, 16 Mastercard; debit 4 Visa, 2 Mastercard. Nicaragua mostly Mastercard ⚠; Walmart co-brand on Mastercard in Guatemala and El Salvador.
- Mastercard Digital First certification, first in the region (Dec 2021). Unlimited Mastercard in Panama (Nov 2022). Google Wallet for Visa and Mastercard (Jun 2024).
- Visa: operational-excellence award (Feb 2020); Apple Pay on Compra Click for Visa and Amex only (Jul 2024). Amex: largest brand in the Costa Rica catalogue; metal Platinum card ⚠ date. Discover acceptance since 2006.
- BAC's own rails: Tasa Cero instalments (3–24 months), Compra Click, HIT Tap on Phone, Mi Remesa, Ameritransfer ⚠, SINPE Móvil (Costa Rica), ACH Xpress (Panama).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Click to Pay, passkeys and merchant risk at the region's largest acquirer; Mastercard Move for remittances into the northern markets; the Multibank card reissue in Panama; commercial cards for supplier payments; spending insights alongside the Índice de Consumo BAC; Agent Pay for a future app assistant.

## 4. How the page reads BAC
- Five discs: Foundation (balance sheet, six markets, owners and heritage, strategy and Multibank) · Rails (credit, premium and co-brands, debit and Digital First, Tasa Cero, acquiring and HIT, Compra Click, wallets, SINPE and ACH, remittances) · Services (fraud, data, loyalty, treasury APIs) · Business lines (consumer, mortgages, corporate and SME, pensions and insurance) · Access (app, branches and Rapibac, partners). Six kinds of user. Each market is a brand/entity (DIV) with its own product in the foundation.
- Gaps: volume split by network; agreement terms and renewal dates; Multibank's networks; remittance partners; fraud vendors; branch and ATM totals; market cap.

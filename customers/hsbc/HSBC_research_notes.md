## 1. The company
- HSBC Holdings plc (LSE: HSBA; HKEX: 5; NYSE ADS: HSBC). Founded 1865 in Hong Kong and Shanghai. c.41m customers, 56 markets, 208,720 FTE (end-2025). Market value c.$323bn on 16 Oct 2026 ⚠ (third-party).
- 1H26: revenue $37.7bn, PBT $19.5bn, RoTE 18.2%, CET1 14.1%, assets $3.438tn, loans $1.022tn, deposits $1.828tn. FY2025: revenue $68.3bn, PBT $29.9bn, RoTE 13.3% (17.2% ex notable items).
- Four businesses since 1 Jan 2025: Hong Kong (incl. Hang Seng), UK (HSBC UK, first direct, M&S Bank, HSBC Innovation Bank), CIB, IWPB; plus Corporate Centre.
- Leadership: Brendan Nelson (Chairman), Georges Elhedery (CEO), Pam Kaur (CFO), Stuart Riley (CIO), Michael Roberts (CIB), Barry O'Byrne (IWPB), David Lindberg (HSBC UK), Maggie Ng (HK). Andy Rankin is Chief Payments Officer, HSBC UK.
- Hang Seng privatised (HK$106bn; effective 26 Jan 2026). Exits: Canada, Argentina, French retail, UK life, Uruguay, Australian loans, Egypt retail, Singapore life; US, Greece and Oman dates not re-verified ⚠.
- Targets 2026–28: RoTE ≥17% ex notable items; CET1 14–14.5%; 2026 banking NII ≥$46bn.
- AI: Google Cloud partnership (Jun 2026), c.1bn transactions a month monitored for financial crime; AI Centre of Excellence in Singapore; HSBCnio (Sep 2026) with MCP access for clients' AI.
- Digital money: Tokenised Deposit Service in six markets; HKD stablecoin licence (10 Apr 2026); HSBC RedCoin named 30 Sep 2026, due 2H26; PayMe >3.3m users.

## 2. HSBC × Mastercard
- UK credit on Mastercard (Premier World Elite, Oct 2024; Rewards with Pay with Rewards and Priceless). Other UK credit cards assumed Mastercard ⚠.
- UK debit Visa ⚠ (inferred); Global Money on Visa; first direct and M&S Bank networks not verified ⚠.
- Hong Kong: debit on Mastercard; credit split between Mastercard (Premier, Privé World Legend, May 2026), Visa (Signature, Gold, EveryMile ⚠) and UnionPay. Hang Seng MMPOWER and commercial debit on Mastercard.
- Mexico split: Mastercard premium and VIVA ⚠, Visa One+ ⚠. Gulf consumer cards Visa-led (exclusive debit renewal 2013).
- Commercial: Track Card-to-Account (UK, Mar 2022), HK virtual corporate card on In Control (Jun 2025), UAE mobile virtual cards (Jun 2026), Middle East Wholesale Program (May 2024).
- Other: card recycling pilot (Jun 2023), City Possible (Sep 2020), Live Nation Priceless (HK, Jun 2025). Last public group-level renewal: Premier credit to 2015 (May 2010).
- Not found: Click to Pay, instalments, Move/Send, Open Finance, Ethoca, Consumer Fraud Risk, Agent Pay, Multi-Token Network, acquiring.
- Visa ran HSBC UK's first live agentic payment (14 Jul 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay on the UK credit book; UK scam and fraud tools; In Control virtual cards across GPS markets; gateway and tokens for Digital Merchant Services' six new markets; on and off ramps for RedCoin and tokenised deposits; Premier and Wealth Centre loyalty; Move for cross-border money (currently Visa).

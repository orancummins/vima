# PrivatBank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `PrivatBank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and PrivatBank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (H1 2026 results, 27 Jul 2026; Q1 2026, 29 Apr 2026; 2025 financial statements and management report) unless marked. Mastercard's newsroom blocked automated reads; the Agent Pay release was read through PrivatBank's copy. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources, inference or data that could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- JSC CB PrivatBank, Kyiv. Operating since 1992; nationalised 21 Dec 2016; 100% owned by the state through the Cabinet of Ministers. Not listed; NBU floated an IPO (Warsaw or Kyiv, 2027 ⚠) in Jun 2026. Fitch IDR CCC, Moody's deposits Caa3. About 17,000 staff.
- H1 2026: profit before tax UAH 49.5bn, net profit UAH 24.6bn, assets UAH 924.7bn, net loans UAH 188.5bn (+45%), customer funds UAH 785bn. 18m individuals, 13.7m Privat24 users, 950,000 business clients.
- FY2025: profit before tax UAH 88bn, after tax UAH 29.1bn; UAH 59bn to the budget. Commission income UAH 50.2bn (acquiring UAH 15.8bn); UAH 21.7bn paid to international payment systems.
- Segments: Retail; Corporate business (incl. POS acquiring); Capital markets; Separate portfolio (former owners, Cyprus); Capital management.
- Leadership: Mikael Björknert (CEO since Jan 2025), Larysa Chernyshova (CFO), Dmytro Musiienko (retail), Yevhen Zaihraiev (corporate/SME, acquiring), Marc Krabbenborg (digital), Oleksii Filipov (risk); chair Nils Melngailis.
- Legacy: High Court of England win and NPLs cut to 10% (2025); PCA award vs Russia over Crimea, USD 327m+ (6 Oct 2026).
- Scale in payments: 31.97m active cards (1 Apr 2025, about 54% of Ukraine ⚠ computed); Nilson's largest European Visa/Mastercard issuer (2024); 345,000+ POS, UAH 1.06trn acquired in 2024; LiqPay about UAH 103bn; Terminal app UAH 15bn.
- 2025–26 launches: Diia.Card, Visa Request to Pay, Instant Instalments for Business, SEP 4.1 instant, Katka24 (Visa/NAVI, Apr 2026), Subscriptions (Sep 2026), document-fraud data link with the Interior Ministry (Sep 2026).

## 2. PrivatBank × Mastercard
- Member of Visa (Mar 2006) and Mastercard (Jul 2006); also PROSTIR (2017) and UnionPay (2019). Mass cards on either network; no split published ⚠.
- Ukraine (NBU, 1 Apr 2025): Visa 50.4% of cards in circulation vs Mastercard 49.3%; Mastercard 50.3% of active cards vs Visa 49.3%; PROSTIR about 0.3%.
- Mastercard: international P2P sending only from Mastercard currency cards; transfer-cashback campaigns (Mar 2025–Apr 2026); free card delivery to 60+ countries (Sep 2025, extended to end-2026 ⚠); Superhumans fundraiser and business schools; Agent Pay, Ukraine's first agentic payment (23 Jun 2026, test environment ⚠; Inga Andreieva and Dmytro Musiienko).
- Visa: Visa Infinite premium flagship; FacePay24 (2019); Digital Authentication Framework (Aug 2023, world first); Request to Pay (2025); NAVI ecosystem (Dec 2025) and Katka24 card (Apr 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Scale Agent Pay in Privat24 and Subscriptions; cross-border transfers for Ukrainians abroad; identity and scam tools for P2P and onboarding; card-linked offers for Pryvit; scheme instalments next to Payment in Parts; Tap on Phone and risk tools for Terminal and LiqPay merchants; commercial cards for a corporate book that quadrupled; a network deal priced against UAH 21.7bn of scheme fees.

# Scotiabank: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Scotiabank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Scotiabank × Mastercard), condensed.

Figures are the bank's own unless marked: the Q3 2026 results, presentations and fact sheet (25 August 2026), and the 2025 Annual Report and results (2 December 2025). Fiscal years end 31 October.

Web search was capped part-way through, so the Mastercard and Visa newsrooms were not checked. Card networks come from Scotiabank, Tangerine and subsidiary product pages and from network releases. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources, an inference, or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- **Identity:** The Bank of Nova Scotia (TSX and NYSE: BNS), founded in Halifax in 1832, run from Toronto. Market value about C$150bn (31 Jul 2026). About 81,700 FTE; 1,984 branches and offices. Rated Aa2 by Moody's and A+ by S&P on legacy senior debt.
- **Q3 2026:**
  - revenue C$10.5bn and record net income C$2,953m;
  - ROE 14.1%, above the 14% target for the first time;
  - CET1 13.1%; assets C$1,548bn.
- **FY2025:** net income C$7,758m reported, C$9,510m adjusted.
- **Segments:** Canadian Banking (11m+ customers, including Tangerine's 2m+), International Banking (8m+ clients; fourth in Mexico, Peru and Chile), Global Wealth Management (AUM C$474bn) and Global Banking and Markets.
- **Leadership:** Aaron Regent (Chair), Scott Thomson (CEO), Raj Viswanathan (CFO), Aris Bogdaneris, Francisco Aristeguieta, Travis Machen, Tim Clark and Phil Thomas. Simona Salter is EVP Cards, Loyalty, Payments; Ralph Hamers joined the board in September 2026.
- **Strategy:** the North American corridor. Deals:
  - KeyCorp 14.9% (US$2.8bn, completed Dec 2024);
  - Colombia, Costa Rica and Panama to Davivienda for 20.3% (closed 1 Dec 2025);
  - CrediScotia sold (Feb 2025);
  - MapleMark Bank (agreed May 2026 ⚠ pending);
  - Scotia Group Jamaica buy-out (⚠ due Q4 2026);
  - Tangerine moving to Engine by Starling ⚠.
- **AI:** Scotia Intelligence (Apr 2026) gives assistive AI to more than 71,000 staff; Predictive UX; Lightworks AI Consortium (Jul 2026).
- **Digital money:** six-bank CAD tokenised deposit (Sep 2026); Project Agorá (Sep 2026).

## 2. Scotiabank × Mastercard
- **Scotiabank Canada credit:** Visa (Scene+, Passport incl. Infinite Privilege launched with Visa Canada in Mar 2025, Momentum, Value, ScotiaGold) and Amex (Gold, Platinum, no-fee). Business cards are all Visa; a new no-fee card launched in Aug 2026. No commercial card programme is public ⚠.
- **Debit:** Interac with Visa Debit, at both Scotiabank and Tangerine.
- **Tangerine credit:** Mastercard. Money-Back (since 2016), Money-Back World, and the Rewards World Elite Mastercard (revealed 22 Apr 2026, earns Scene+).
- **Legacy portfolio:** JPMorgan Chase Canada Mastercard and private-label portfolio bought in Oct 2015 (C$1.7bn, 2m accounts) ⚠ size today unknown.
- **Mexico:** Visa-led, with a World Elite tier on Scotia Travel, Singular and AAdvantage ⚠ inferred Mastercard.
- **Peru:** both networks in full, including AAdvantage, and both debit networks.
- **Chile:** Visa.
- **Caribbean:** Mastercard debit under a seven-year deal from Mar 2020 (Bahamas, Barbados, Dominican Republic, Jamaica; ends about 2027 ⚠). Visa has a seven-year deal from Dec 2019 for Central America and the Caribbean. Jamaica sells both credit networks.
- **Other joint work:** Masterpass (Nov 2017); Click to Pay is explained mainly for Visa and Amex. Not found: Installments, Send, Finicity, Agent Pay, fraud tools ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Card-linked offers and data for Scene+ (15m+ members, network-neutral).
- Tangerine's rebuild: tokens, instalments and Click to Pay.
- Scam scoring as the Real-Time Rail launches in Q4 2026.
- Renewing Caribbean debit around 2027.
- Commercial and virtual cards for GTB along the corridor.
- Agent Pay for Predictive UX; the Multi-Token Network for tokenised deposits.
- **Watch-outs:** Visa wins the Scotiabank-brand launches, and Interac, PLIN and bank tokens move flows off cards.

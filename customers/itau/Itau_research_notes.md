# Itaú Unibanco — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Itau_Platform.html`. Three research threads (group profile; products and channels; Itaú × Mastercard), condensed. Amounts in reais (R$). The shared web-search budget ran out partway through, and itau.com.br blocks automated fetching, so SEC and MZiQ mirrors of the investor documents were used; ⚠ marks items resting on press or not confirmed. 3Q26 results are due in early November.

## 1. The group
- Itaú Unibanco Holding S.A. (incorporated 27 Sep 1924), São Paulo; B3 (ITUB3, ITUB4) and NYSE (ITUB). Controlled through IUPAR (51.71% of voting shares), jointly held by Itaúsa (Setubal and Villela families) and Cia. E. Johnston (Moreira Salles family). Co-chairs Pedro Moreira Salles and Roberto Setubal.
- Heritage: Casa Moreira Salles licence 1924 (Unibanco); Banco Central de Crédito founded 1943, first branch 1945, Banco Itaú 1973; Itaúsa 1974; Itaú BBA 2002; BankBoston Brazil/Chile/Uruguay 2006; Itaú–Unibanco merger agreed Nov 2008 (approved Feb 2009); Itaú CorpBanca 2016 → Banco Itaú Chile ⚠ (renamed 2023); Argentina sold to Banco Macro (Nov 2023, ~R$1.2bn loss).
- Scale: recurring managerial result R$41.4bn (2024), R$46.8bn (2025, record), R$12.4bn (2Q26); ROE 22.2%, 23.4%, 24.3% (2Q26; Brazil 25.7%); total assets R$3,227bn; credit R$1,522bn (+9.6%); deposits R$1,134bn; CET1 12.3%; efficiency 37.4%; 90,429 employees; 2,210 branches and service branches (from 2,738). Market value ~US$96bn (Jul 2026) ⚠ no October figure.
- Ratings ⚠ (IR page via search): holding Ba2 (Moody's), BB+ (Fitch); AAA national scale.
- Segments: Retail Business (R$5.5bn in 2Q26), Wholesale Business (R$5.6bn), Activities with the Market + Corporation (R$1.3bn). Brazil ~95% of the result.
- Leadership: CEO Milton Maluhy Filho (2021; Febraban chair Apr 2026); CFO Gabriel Amado de Moura; André Rodrigues (retail individuals); Carlos Vanzo (SMEs and Rede); Flávio Souza (Itaú BBA); Ricardo Guerra (CIO); Carlos Constantini (wealth); Matias Granata (CRO).
- Strategy: One Itaú (single SuperApp), "AI First", conversational banking; 2026 guidance credit +5.5–9.5%, fees and insurance cut to 2–5% (4 Aug 2026); payout 72% for 2025 plus R$23.4bn brought forward (Nov 2025); sustainable finance R$605bn of R$1tn by 2030.
- Technology: AWS ten-year deal (Nov 2020); ~70% of services in public cloud (Jan 2026), 100% target 2028; tech spend R$11.7bn (2025, +18%); 750+ gen-AI initiatives; Cognition's Devin across ~75% of tech teams; ia.i assistant (27 Jul 2026).

## 2. Products and channels
- Cards: issuer volume R$245.7bn in 2Q26 (credit +10.4%, debit −0.2%); balances R$150.4bn; card fees R$3.3bn a quarter; #1 issuer with 24% of purchase volume. Networks: Mastercard and Visa; Hipercard's own network closed and its ~15m cards moved to Mastercard by 30 Jun 2025; no Elo or Amex issuing ⚠. Premium: World Legend (Mastercard, 2026), The One Mastercard Black, Visa Infinite Privilege (R$15k). Co-brands: LATAM Pass and Azul (both networks), Pão de Açúcar (Mastercard), Passaí (Visa), Mercado Livre (Visa), Luizacred (50% with Magalu; R$20.2bn); FIC/Investcred buyout agreed Dec 2025 ⚠ closing.
- Pix: Pix by tap (Google Wallet Nov 2024; in-app on Android Jan 2026; iPhone excluded); Pix Automático for businesses from Jan 2025; Pix on WhatsApp; voice Pix in ia.i. No Itaú Pix volumes disclosed.
- Rede: R$1.03tn in 2025, R$298.7bn in 2Q26 (+23.5%); #1 by volume (22.7%); 50+ brands accepted; Laranjinha+ with gen-AI (May 2026); own agentic gateway (30 Sep 2026; protocols and token partners not named).
- Lending: payroll R$81.3bn (private-sector Crédito do Trabalhador +90% y/y; Minas Gerais payroll won); vehicles R$35.1bn; personal and overdraft R$68.1bn; microcredit R$1.5bn; mortgages R$152.2bn (2nd to Caixa); SMEs R$307.4bn (1.6m+ clients); Itaú BBA corporate R$474.9bn (#1 DCM and M&A by deals).
- Wealth and insurance: AUM R$3.82tn; Itaú Asset R$1.24tn (largest private-bank manager), Kinea; Íon, Itaú Corretora; Private Bank 31.1% share (Miami, Zurich, Portugal, Bahamas); Itaú Bank, N.A. OCC preliminary approval (17 Aug 2026); Avenue control (30 Jan 2026); pensions 19.9%; insurance #4 incl. 30.7% of Porto Seguro; consórcio R$37bn sales.
- Services: ia.i; Inteligência Itaú on WhatsApp; fraud engines, Alerta Pix, central-bank anti-fraud rules (IN 766: tracing from 10 Aug 2026, 80-day MED window from 1 Sep 2026); open finance (2023: ~25% of data receptions ⚠); Minhas Vantagens and Mais Vantagens; non-expiring points; Itaú Shop, Itaú Live.
- Access: SuperApp (~15m clients from six apps, done ~Jan 2026; iti retired); 4.6m digital account openings (68%); 11,822 own ATMs plus 14,196 Banco24Horas; WhatsApp and digital managers; Itaú Empresas app, Bankline.
- Latin America: loans R$253bn; Chile (67.4%, 11.5% share), Paraguay (largest private bank, ROE 21.5%), Uruguay (largest private bank, ROE 34.1%, OCA), Colombia corporate-only after retail moved to Banco de Bogotá (31 Jul 2026).

## 3. Itaú × Mastercard
- 16 Mar 2015: 20-year agreement to create a new Brazilian network run by Mastercard on which Itaú would issue; CADE (11 May 2016) approved for seven years, banned either name on the new brand and flagged a Hipercard–Banknet processing contract. ⚠ No evidence the new brand launched; Hipercard's 2025 migration to Mastercard looks like the eventual outcome.
- Hipercard Banco Múltiplo merged into Itaú (Jan 2025); ~15m Hipercard cards migrated to Hipercard Mastercard Platinum by 30 Jun 2025.
- Click Digital virtual Mastercard Platinum (Sep 2022); World Legend launch issuer (Oct 2025; Rodrigo Carneiro); Agent Pay live transactions among 17 LAC issuers and processors (24 Mar 2026; Andrea Scerch); Candido Bracher (ex-Itaú CEO) on Mastercard's board (2021) ⚠ current.
- Not found: MDES or Click to Pay announcements (Itaú not in Brazil's 2024 Click to Pay launch cohort), Installments, Send/Move, open finance, Decision Intelligence/Brighterion, Ethoca, Priceless Planet, crypto or Drex tie-ups (Mastercard was in the MB Drex consortium; Itaú bid alone).
- Visa: Uniclass Visa Signature, Visa Infinite co-brands, Mercado Livre and other retail co-brands; Visa ran Brazil's first agentic transactions with BB and Santander, about ten days before Mastercard's with Itaú and Santander.
- Market: card spend R$4.5tn in 2025 (credit +14.5%, debit +0.2%); Pix R$35.4tn (+34%); Itaú 24.3% of credit-card volume vs Nubank 15.2% (growing ~2x); network shares ⚠ last solid figure 2017 (Mastercard 46%, Visa 36%, Elo ~14%); debit interchange capped; credit-interchange cap consultation announced May 2025 ⚠ outcome; Elo pushing into premium.
- Where it could land: Pix scam and mule intelligence; Agent Pay in Rede's gateway; Click to Pay and passkeys; SME and commercial cards; merchant-funded offers against rising rewards costs; installments against "Pix no crédito"; cross-border for Brazilians abroad.

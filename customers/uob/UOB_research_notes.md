## 1. The company
- United Overseas Bank Limited (SGX: U11), Singapore; founded 1935; built by acquisition (OUB 2001, Bank of Asia 2004, Bank Buana 2005). About 430 offices in 19 markets; subsidiaries in Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam and China. Headcount 31,222 (end-2025). Aa1/AA-/AA-. Strategic shareholders, including the Wee family, 28%.
- 2Q26: net profit S$1.48bn (+10%); 1H26 S$2.92bn, ROE 11.6%, CET1 15.4%, NIM 1.78%, loans S$361bn, deposits S$437bn. FY2025: net profit S$4.68bn (−23%, pre-emptive allowance), total assets S$572bn, market cap S$57.9bn.
- Segments: Group Retail (credit card income +13% in 1H26; gross card fees S$572m; about S$15bn card billings a quarter), Group Wholesale Banking (transaction banking about half), Global Markets.
- Leadership: Wong Kan Seng (Chairman), Wee Ee Cheong (Deputy Chairman and CEO), Leong Yung Chee (CFO), Susan Hwee (Group Retail), Frederick Chin (Wholesale and Markets), Lawrence Goh (Technology), Tan Choon Hin (Head of ASEAN and Greater China from 1 Sep 2026), Pratik Bhattacharjee (Group Cards and Payment Products).
- Citi ASEAN consumer businesses: agreed 14 Jan 2022 (net assets plus S$915m premium, about S$4.9bn ⚠); completed Malaysia/Thailand Nov 2022, Vietnam Mar 2023, Indonesia Nov 2023; more than 8.5m retail customers after integration.
- 2026: UOBAM sold to Allianz GI for about S$555m (Aug); AWS cloud and AI MoU (Oct); live tokenised-deposit transactions on Swift's ledger with HSBC (Aug) and DBS and OCBC (Sep). AI: Program AURA, 20,000+ staff trained.

## 2. UOB × Mastercard
- Top consumer credit card issuer in ASEAN by billings across Visa and Mastercard (three years); "Visa's largest card issuer in ASEAN"; top in Visa consumer billings in Singapore.
- Singapore credit: Visa (Visa Infinite Metal, Reserve, Privilege Banking, Singtel-UOB, Visa Signature, EVOL ⚠, Lazada-UOB ⚠, One Card ⚠); Mastercard (Lady's World Mastercard, Lady's Solitaire ⚠, PRVI Miles Mastercard); Amex (Absolute Cashback, PRVI Miles Amex).
- Regional: Visa Infinite tiers relaunch for 300,000+ cardholders in five markets (22 Jul 2026); Malaysia mixed with World Card ⚠ and Platinum Business on Mastercard; Indonesia Visa ⚠; Thailand credit networks unknown ⚠.
- Debit: One Debit on Visa and Mastercard (NETS on Visa only); KrisFlyer debit and FX+ ⚠ on Mastercard; Malaysia, Thailand (with PromptCard) and Vietnam debit on Mastercard.
- Joint work: Regal Business Metal, APAC's first World Elite Mastercard Business metal card (28 May 2019); Woori Card reciprocal privileges, Mastercard first (Jul 2024); first live Agent Pay transaction in Singapore with DBS (6 Mar 2026); UOB as Mastercard's ASEAN agentic testing partner (6 Apr 2026); Mastercard Easy Savings on commercial cards.
- Rivals: Visa (premium, Visa Commercial Offers, 2015 Visa Token Service ⚠), Amex, PayNow (about 11m registrations), NETS, DuitNow and PromptPay links, TMRW Scan to Pay on national QR, Project Nexus (2027), Swift ledger.

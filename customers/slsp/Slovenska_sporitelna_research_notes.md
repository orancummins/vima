Companion to `Slovenska_sporitelna_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Slovenská sporiteľňa × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (H1 2026 results and half-year report, 30 Jul 2026; Q1 2026 investor presentation; 2025 audited accounts) unless marked. Most product pages are in Slovak and were translated. Mastercard's newsroom was not searched. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. ## 1. The company
- Slovenská sporiteľňa, a.s., Bratislava; 100% Erste Group Bank AG since 2001; roots in 1825; not listed. Moody's A1 deposits, A3 issuer, Aaa covered bonds.
- H1 2026: net profit €176.8m (+18.9%), operating result €304.8m, ROE 15.3%, cost/income 38.9%, capital adequacy 20.4%, NPL 2.3%. Assets €27.6bn, loans €20.7bn, deposits €19.8bn. FY2025: net profit €310.3m.
- 1.77m customers (June 2026; the bank also says "around 2 million" ⚠), 1.94m payment cards, 1.4m George users, 128 branches, 733 ATMs, 3,589 employees.
- No. 1 in assets, loans and deposits; 24.2% of retail loans (May 2026), nearly 28% of retail deposits, about 22.5% of corporate loans. Rivals VÚB and Tatra banka.
- Segments: Retail (€123.6m net profit H1), Corporates (€34.5m), Group Markets (€11.7m), ALM & Local Corporate Center.
- Stakes: Prvá stavebná sporiteľňa (9.98%, 35% of votes), SLSP Social Finance, Seed Starter, Slovak Banking Credit Bureau, Monilogi (cash), Holding Card Service (linked to the acquiring JV ⚠).
- Leadership: Michaela Bauer, CEO from 1 Mar 2026 (ex-ČSOB/KBC), succeeding Peter Krutil; Pavel Cetkovský (factsheet says Peter ⚠), Milan Hain (IT and payments), Juraj Barta (retail), Marek Sásik (corporate), Slavomír Seemann (CFO).
- Strategy: Slovakia's largest digital bank; George AI on OpenAI (Sep 2026); investing from €20 in George; fee reset Jan 2026 rewards mobile and credit-card payments and charges €10 for physical debit cards. No numeric targets of its own ⚠.

## 2. Slovenská sporiteľňa × Mastercard
- Visa since 1994; exclusive Visa issuing agreement July 2021 (all new cards Visa; recycled cards). Length and renewal date not public ⚠.
- Every issued card is Visa: debit (Electron, Standard, Platinum, Junior, virtual), credit (Classic, Gold), business debit. Apple Pay (Jun 2019) and other wallets on Visa tokens.
- Mastercard World and Mass sold until about 2019–20 ⚠; no Mastercard in disclosures from June 2021.
- Acceptance through Global Payments s.r.o. (JV of Global Payments, CaixaBank, Erste, 2016): terminals and GP webpay take Visa, Mastercard and Diners.
- No joint Mastercard initiative found after 2019 ⚠.
- Account rails: SEPA Instant (mandatory Oct 2025), Payme (2021), national QR payments (May 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Network-free services: spending insight and fraud alerts for George AI; card-linked offers against Dateio's Moneyback; open banking and scam checks beside QR and instant payments; commercial and virtual cards (none advertised); a second network if the Visa agreement is renegotiated ⚠. Erste Austria's Debit Mastercard and Miles & More show the group is open to Mastercard.

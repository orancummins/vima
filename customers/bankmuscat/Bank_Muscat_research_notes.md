## 1. The company
- Bank Muscat SAOG, Muscat; founded 1982; MSX-listed since 1993, also London and Bahrain. Royal Court Affairs 27.31%; market value about RO 3.65bn (Mar 2026 ⚠). Moody's Baa3, S&P BBB-, Fitch BBB- (all stable); D-SIB.
- About 4.0m retail customers; 4,462 employees (end-2025); 200 branches and service centres; a Saudi branch (since Apr 2007); representative offices in Dubai, Singapore and Iran; Kuwait branch closed in 2025.
- FY2025: net profit RO 255.5m (+13.3%), total assets RO 15.13bn, net loans RO 10.73bn, deposits RO 10.43bn, ROE 13.6%, cost:income 38.0%, CAR 19.66%. H1 2026: net profit RO 137.5m (+9.3%), deposits +13.2%, loans +6.3%.
- About 32% of Omani banking assets (Mar 2026); more than 46% of merchant-acquiring volume; leading e-commerce gateway.
- Segments: Corporate, Personal, Wholesale, International, Islamic (Meethaq: 32 branches, RO 2.09bn assets). The central bank requires Islamic windows to be spun off ⚠.
- Leadership: Sheikh Khalid Al Mashani (Chairman), Sheikh Waleed Al Hashar (CEO), Ahmed Al Bulushi, Sheikha Al Farsi, T. Ganesh (deputy CEOs), Abdulnasir Al Raisi (Personal Banking), Abdullah Al Jufaili (Digital).
- Digital: instant digital debit and then credit cards in the app (Sep 2026), Apple Pay and Samsung Pay, Tap'N'Go soft POS (Jul 2025 ⚠); AI and RPA used internally ⚠.

## 2. Bank Muscat × Mastercard
- Network split: debit, prepaid (incl. Shukran with Landmark, Nov 2023), credit (Classic to Infinite, Oman Air), Meethaq and corporate (Visa Commercial Pay) all Visa. One Mastercard: LuLu Titanium co-brand (Mar 2015). Maal national debit card issued free on OmanNet.
- Visa partnership renewed Nov 2021 (CEO Al Hashar; Visa's Dr Saeeda Jaffar); term not disclosed ⚠.
- Mastercard named the LuLu card Oman's largest co-branded credit card (Jan 2024; Gina Petersen-Skyrme, Mastercard).
- Acquiring: Mastercard accepted across the bank's 46% acquiring share; no Mastercard Gateway deal found ⚠.
- Competition: Visa; the central bank's Maal (Sep 2025) and MPCSS instant payments (RO 8.2bn in H1 2026, now ahead of OmanNet by value; free retail transfers from Jul 2026). BankDhofar signed with Mastercard (Sep 2025 ⚠); Sohar International uses Mastercard Send ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Acquirer risk, tokenised checkout and Click to Pay for the biggest acquirer; Mastercard Move for expatriate remittances; scam and identity scoring as instant payments grow; tokens and controls for instant digital cards; card-linked offers for Bank Muscat Rewards; premium and co-brand credit if the Visa deal is re-tendered ⚠.

## 1. The company
- Erste Group Bank AG (Vienna, Prague, Bucharest; market cap €48.1bn at 30 Jun 2026) and Erste Bank der oesterreichischen Sparkassen AG. ERSTE Foundation controls 26.65%; free float 73.35%. Moody's Aa3, S&P A+, Fitch A.
- Heritage: Erste oesterreichische Spar-Casse, 1819; IPO 1997; holding split 2008; 48 Austrian Sparkassen in a cross-guarantee system.
- H1 2026: net profit €1,975m (+18.6%; +7.6% like for like), ROTE 19.6%, C/I 44.4%, CET1 15.2%, loans €282.7bn, deposits €323.7bn, 55,231 staff, 23.2m customers in eight markets. FY2025: net profit €3.5bn.
- Austria: 4.4m customers, 714 branches (4.3m and 732 at end-2025), 2.6m George users; EBOe net result €333m in H1 2026 (+27.2%), payment fees up. Retail shares about 22.4% of loans, 24.7% of deposits ⚠ read from a chart.
- Segments: EBOe & Subsidiaries, Savings Banks, Other Austria, seven CEE markets, Poland.
- Leadership: Peter Bosek (CEO), Stefan Dörfler (CFO), Alexandra Habeler-Drabek, Maurizio Poletto ⚠ portfolios; Gerda Holzinger-Burgstaller (CEO, Erste Bank Oesterreich).
- Deals: Santander Bank Polska 49% + 50% of TFI for ~€7.0bn, completed 9 Jan 2026, renamed Erste Bank Polska 24 Apr 2026; Salzburger Sparkasse merged Aug 2025; Erste Card Club (Croatia) sold H1 2026 (€116m gain, buyer ⚠).
- Strategy: EPS above €15 by 2030, ROTE above 20%; George in seven markets (11.8m+ users), 73% digital sales; Hey George with OpenAI in CZ and RO ⚠ Austria.

## 2. Erste × Mastercard
- Debit: Maestro replaced by Debit Mastercard from Apr 2019 (~1.7m issued by Nov 2019; done mid-2020). Mastercard Identity Check on all debit online payments. Business Debit Mastercard.
- Credit: Smartcard and Premiumcard each on Mastercard and Visa (Split Now instalments, Umsatzbonus). GÖD union cards on Visa. Business Smartcard on Visa; premium business and virtual cards' networks not stated ⚠.
- Austrian Miles & More: Erste won the tender (Aug 2025), took the cards from card complete in early 2026; Mastercard stays.
- Acceptance: Global Payments joint venture since 2016, all schemes.
- Wero: Erste Bank Oesterreich became an EPI shareholder on 22 Jun 2026; no launch date.
- No group-wide network agreement found ⚠. Erste Bank Polska and other CEE card networks not researched ⚠.

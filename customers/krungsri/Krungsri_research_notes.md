# Krungsri — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Krungsri_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Krungsri × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (1H 2026 results, 20 Jul 2026; analyst briefings, 5 Feb and 3 Aug 2026; 2025 One Report) unless marked; Krungsri Consumer figures are from Thairath Money (Apr and Aug 2026). Mastercard's and Visa's newsrooms had nothing Krungsri-specific for 2025–26, and some Thai news sites were blocked. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are due in late October ⚠.

## 1. The company
- Bank of Ayudhya PCL (SET: BAY), founded 1945, listed 1977; MUFG Bank 76.88% since 2013–15, Ratanarak group and others 23.12%. D-SIB; fifth-largest Thai bank by assets. Rated A3 / BBB+ / BBB+.
- 1H 2026: net profit ฿16.9bn (+6.8%), NIM 4.60%, cost-to-income 45.5%, loans ฿1.94trn, deposits ฿1.69trn, NPL 3.08%, bank CET1 15.95%. FY2025: net profit ฿31.7bn, loans +1.7%.
- Loan mix (Jun 2026): retail 48% (auto HP and title 24%, mortgage 11%, cards and personal loans 8%, ASEAN 5%), Thai and MNC corporate 27%, SME 15%, Japanese corporate 10%.
- #1 in credit cards by number (15%), personal loans (19%) and auto HP (30%), Sept 2025 data.
- 555 branches, 5,100 ATMs, 50,127 EDC terminals; 54,746 group employees (end-2025).
- Leadership: Noriaki Goto (Chairman), Kenichi Yamato (CEO), Duangdao Wongpanitkrit (CFO and strategy), Sayam Prasitsirigul (retail and consumer), Atis Rujirawat (Krungsri Consumer), Saisunee Hanprathueangsil (CIO), Ninlawan Jeeraboon (transaction banking).
- MTBP 2024–26: "The Leading Sustainable and Regional Bank", ROE 9%. IT spend about ฿12bn; Jupiter core; BaaS for 1,300+ partners.
- Deals: TIDLOR to 46.51% and consolidated (Aug 2025); Home Credit Indonesia deconsolidated (Jun 2026); ASEAN subsidiaries in Cambodia, Laos, the Philippines and Vietnam (about 19m customers).

## 2. Krungsri × Mastercard
- Krungsri Consumer: 2.4m card customers; 1H26 card spend ฿196.7bn; 2026 target ฿420bn; new credit cards planned for 2026 (network not announced).
- Central The 1 (The Black, Black, Luxe, REDZ; issued by General Card Services): Mastercard, about 960,000 cards (5.8% share), Priority Pass, Mastercard Travel & Lifestyle, Mastercard Friday at Central Retail (25 Sep–31 Dec 2026).
- Krungsri Platinum Mastercard: the one own-brand Mastercard.
- Visa: Krungsri Signature and Exclusive Signature, Private Banking (Visa Infinite), Visa Platinum, Man Utd, HomePro, AIA; First Choice cash card; debit ⚠; Boarding Card (16 currencies); Hattha Bank debit. Visa Paris campaign (Mar 2024).
- JCB: Krungsri JCB, Siam Takashimaya JCB. Amex acceptance on Krungsri EDC from 8 Sep 2026.
- Lotus's cards (50% JV): terms name Mastercard and Visa ⚠ network of the card face unconfirmed.
- PromptPay: 2.38bn transactions, ฿4.70trn in Dec 2025; Mastercard's Vocalink supplied the technology ⚠.
- No public network agreement, tokenisation, Click to Pay or fraud-tool deal found.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- The new cards planned for 2026; the Lotus's programme; ASEAN card launches (SHB Finance, Home Credit Philippines co-brands); merchant data for Krungsri Consumer's segment offers; scam and alert tools under the BoT's fraud rules; acquirer-risk tools for 50,000 terminals; travel FX against the Visa Boarding Card; SME commercial cards (2,000 of 80,000 SME clients).

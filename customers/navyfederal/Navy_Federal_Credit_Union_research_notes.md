## 1. The company
- Navy Federal Credit Union, Vienna, Virginia; federal charter 5536, NCUA-insured; founded 1933 by seven Navy Department employees; the world's largest credit union. Field of membership: DoD and Coast Guard personnel, veterans, DoD civilians and contractors, and families.
- Q2 2026: assets $204.4bn, loans $147.7bn, deposits $173.4bn, members' equity $20.0bn, 15,517,448 members. H1 2026 net income $1,412m; net interchange $528m.
- FY2025: assets $197.2bn, net income $1,910.9m, net worth ratio 11.36%, net interchange $975.7m (net of rewards). Card book $33.2bn, >$50bn spend, 803,000 new cards; 3.7bn debit transactions; 21.4m RTP/FedNow payments ($7.7bn); 11m active checking accounts; 12.2m digital users.
- Lending: real estate $107.8bn, consumer $43.3bn (auto $33.1bn), student $1.3bn; Business Solutions 638,700 members; NFIS $7.4bn.
- Access: 392 locations (178 on or near installations, 27 international), 30,000+ ATMs, DoD Overseas Military Banking Program on about 60 installations since 2024; 25,450 staff.
- Leadership: Dietrich Kuhlmann (President/CEO since Mar 2024), Edward Cochrane Jr. (Chair), John Collins (CFO), Aaron Aggerwal (CLO), Meghan Gound (SVP Digital).
- Technology: rebuilt app; Databricks on Azure for generative and agentic AI ⚠ vendor-stated; Dark Matter Empower ⚠; Curql Fund II (Mar 2025); Cube AI scam bots (Oct 2026).
- Conduct: CFPB overdraft order Nov 2024 ($80m+ refunds, $15m penalty), terminated mid-2025 ⚠ date; fair-lending class action (filed Dec 2023) continues on disparate impact.

- Visa: debit for every checking account, GO Prepaid, gift cards, Flagship Premier Visa Signature (Sep 2026, first metal card). Visa renewed credit and debit and added prepaid and small business in Feb 2009. Visa B shares exchanged in 2024; Class C sold for $169.6m.
- Visa or Mastercard: cashRewards (Visa / Mastercard) and cashRewards Plus (Visa Signature / World Mastercard), GO REWARDS, Platinum, cashRewards Secured (Apr 2025), GO BIZ Rewards. Split not published ⚠.

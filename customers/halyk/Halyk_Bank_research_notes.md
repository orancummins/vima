# Halyk Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Halyk_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Halyk × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (1H 2026 results, presentation and call, 18 Aug 2026; annual report 2025) unless marked. Product networks come from halykbank.kz card pages and the 1 Aug 2026 retail tariffs. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources, on inference, or could not be confirmed. 9M 2026 results are due in the second decade of November.

## 1. The company
- JSC Halyk Bank of Kazakhstan, Almaty. KASE (1998), LSE GDRs (2006, HSBK), AIX (2019). ALMEX 61.9%, GDR holders 35% (30 Jun 2026). Founded 1923; KKB merged 2017–18; Tenge Bank (Uzbekistan) 2019; Halyk Bank Georgia 2008. Market cap about US$6.8bn (Jul 2025) ⚠. Over 16,700 group employees.
- 1H 2026: net income KZT 447.6bn (−15.3%), RoAE 24.8%, NIM 6.8%, assets KZT 22,036bn, gross loans KZT 13,958bn, deposits KZT 14,871bn, k2 19.0%. FY2025: net income KZT 1,058bn, RoAE 32.6%.
- About 30% of Kazakh loans and deposits; 47.6% of real-economy lending; 26.9% of retail deposits. 11.5m active retail clients; Super-App MAU 8.6m; 514k active B2B clients; Onlinebank MAU 317k.
- Ratings: Baa1 / BBB- / BBB- (S&P outlook positive, Sep 2026).
- Leadership: Chairman Arman Dunayev; CEO Umut Shayakhmetova; First Deputy CEO Dauren Sartayev; CFO Murat Koshenov; Mikhail Khasin (IT, Jan 2026); Kiril Bachvarov (B2C, Apr 2026).
- Strategy 2025–2027: ecosystems, Uzbekistan, AI. 2026 targets: net income about KZT 1.0trn, RoAE about 29%; fee guidance cut to about −10% (Aug 2026) on BNPL curbs and VAT.
- Deals: re:Kassa (Comrun) owned from 3 Jun 2026; 49% of Click agreed Jul 2025, lapsed ⚠; Halyk Kyrgyzstan sold 2024.

## 2. Halyk × Mastercard
- Multi-network issuer: Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, Amex. No card count by network is published.
- Mastercard: World Elite and World Elite Imperial (Lounge Key, Fast Track, Cashback without borders); MasterCard World in the basic debit pack; one choice for the VIP card; Black Premium credit labelled MasterCard ⚠; wallet tokens; acceptance on Halyk POS.
- CEO on the Mastercard Europe Advisory Board (since 2019) and EEMEA Advisory Board (since Jul 2022) ⚠; earlier chaired UnionPay's Eastern Europe regional council (2016–22).
- Visa: digital card tariffed as Visa Gold ⚠; Visa Signature, Infinite Ultra, Infinite Credit; first digital-tenge card (Nov 2023); Visa Direct for Alipay/WeChat transfers; She's Next lending (Oct 2025); Visa speaker at OIY Tech (29 Oct 2026).
- No Halyk-specific Mastercard deal, Click to Pay, Send/Move, Installments, Agent Pay or fraud-tool adoption found.

## 3. Market
- NBK at 1 Jun 2026: 83.9m cards — Visa 40.6m, Mastercard 14.1m, UnionPay 0.76m, local systems (Kaspi.kz) 28.3m; active cards Visa 21.1m, local 17.4m, Mastercard 3.4m.
- Kaspi.kz 2Q 2026: Payments TPV KZT 12.7trn, take rate 1.00%, pay-by-palm, Kasper AI assistant.
- National instant payments (2022), unified QR (Nov 2025), nationwide interbank instant payments and QR from 19 Jul 2026. 80% of non-cash transactions via mobile and internet banking (May 2026).
- Mastercard Almaty hub and Digital Country Partnership (Sep 2023): digital tenge, instant payments, open banking.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Mastercard Move for cross-border (Uzbekistan, China) against Visa Direct; fraud scoring for A2A and QR; Agent Pay for Halyk's AI agent platform; card-linked offers for Halyk+; commercial and fuel cards; Tap on Phone for Halyk Pos and re:Kassa.

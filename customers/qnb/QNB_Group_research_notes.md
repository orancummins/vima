# QNB Group — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `QNB_Group_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and QNB × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own as reported by QNA, Gulf Times and The Peninsula (nine-month 2026 results, 8 Oct 2026; H1 2026, 8 Jul 2026; 2025 results, 13 Jan 2026). QNB's own sites and Mastercard's newsroom returned 403; QNB product pages were read from 2025 archive copies. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Qatar National Bank (Q.P.S.C.), Doha; listed on the Qatar Stock Exchange; QIA the anchor shareholder (stake ⚠). Chair Ali Ahmed Al-Kuwari (Minister of Finance), elected for 2025–27. Group CEO Abdulla Mubarak Al-Khalifa; Group CBO Yousef Al-Neama; retail and cards Adel Ali Al-Malki.
- 9M 2026: net profit QR13.3bn (+4%; QR16.4bn before Türkiye hyperinflation), assets QR1.46tn, loans QR1.06tn, deposits QR979bn, cost:income 24.3%, NPL 2.7%, CAR 19.7%. FY2025: QR17.0bn, assets QR1.39tn.
- Ratings (2025): Moody's Aa2, S&P A+, Fitch A+. More than 28 countries and 31,000+ staff (2025); 4,800+ ATMs (2023). Market value about QR160bn ⚠ (computed from a QR17.40 share price, July 2026).
- Subsidiaries: QNB Türkiye (ex-Finansbank, 2016; renamed Oct 2024; 417 branches), Enpara Bank (separated Aug 2025), QNB Egypt (9M 2026 profit EGP28.1bn), plus Indonesia, Tunisia, Syria, Switzerland. Two branches in Saudi Arabia (2017; Jeddah 2023).
- Regional conflict from late February 2026 shut branches until 24 March ⚠.

## 2. QNB × Mastercard
- Commercial cards: long-term agreement, 18 Apr 2023 (Al-Neama; J.K. Khalil). QNB Mastercard World corporate cards, virtual cards, Smart Data, Corporate Liability Waiver, Airport Pass.
- myPOS tap on phone with Mastercard, Mar 2023. QNB Simplify on Mastercard Simplify Commerce ⚠ (inferred from domain).
- Private: World Elite Exclusive metal card, Jan 2021; earlier diamond World Elite ⚠ undated.
- Debit: QNB Mastercard debit campaign, Apr–Jun 2025 (Al-Malki; Erdem Çakar). Share of debit on Mastercard ⚠.
- Sudan: first international Mastercard transaction and issuing licence, Jun 2020.

## 3. Visa, NAPS and Fawran
- Visa: Life Rewards Platinum/Infinite, Ooredoo Nojoom, Qatar Airways (Feb 2024; one Mastercard Platinum tier listed ⚠), Harrods (Apr–May 2025), invite-only UHNW card. Visa award, Apr 2024: fastest-growing credit portfolio in Qatar.
- NAPS: QCB's domestic switch; Himyan national card (Nov 2022 / Mar 2023), Qatar and riyals only; QNB issues debit and prepaid.
- Fawran (QCB, Mar 2024): 3.19m payments, QR5.09bn, 3.61m accounts, 25% of transactions in Dec 2025. QMP wallets 2%.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Scam scoring and request-to-pay around Fawran; tokenised debit and Click to Pay; Mastercard Move for expatriate corridors; payroll and prepaid beyond Himyan; card-linked offers for Life Rewards; commercial cards across Saudi Arabia, Türkiye and Egypt.

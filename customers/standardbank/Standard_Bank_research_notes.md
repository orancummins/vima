## 1. The company
- Standard Bank Group Limited (JSE: SBK), Johannesburg; main bank The Standard Bank of South Africa (SBSA). Africa's largest bank by assets; 21 African countries; 51,200 employees; 163-year heritage (founded 1862 ⚠).
- Owners: ICBC 19.7%, GEPF/PIC 14.3%; the group holds 40% of ICBC Standard Bank. Market cap R532bn (US$32bn) at 30 Jun 2026.
- H1 2026: headline earnings R26.1bn (+10%), ROE 19.8%, CET1 13.6%, assets R3.81trn, deposits R2.5trn, 19.5m active clients. FY2025: HE R49.2bn, ROE 19.3%. Earnings: SA 51%, Africa Regions 40%.
- Units: PPB (Montjane), BCB (Blackie), CIB (Masinda; HE R13.8bn), IAM (Maharaj; Liberty, STANLIB). Chair Nyembezi, CEO Tshabalala, CFO Daehnke, COO Nienaber (tech, AI, payments), SBSA CEO Hodnett.
- 2028 targets: HEPS +8–12% a year, ROE 18–22%, CET1 >12.5%. Pillars: technology (R23.5bn spend 2025, 78% cloud, card modernisation), AI (72% of staff on GenAI), payments.
- Deals: Liberty minority buyout (2022); Tanzania capital (Jul 2026); Angola stake increase (H2 2026); Cairo office.

## 2. Payments and cards
- R164trn of payments processed in 2025 (2.3bn payments); SA cross-border share 30%. Card acquiring R129bn in H1 2026 (SA R98bn; new merchants +28%).
- Personal credit (Blue, Gold, Titanium, Platinum): Mastercard. World Citizen: World Mastercard. Card book R33.3bn.
- MyMo, Achieva and Consolidator debit: Mastercard ⚠ card art. Private Banking and Professional cards: Visa. Offshore debit: Visa.
- Corporate, travel, procurement and aviation cards: Mastercard or Visa. Fleet: Visa ⚠. PayCard prepaid: Mastercard or Visa ⚠.
- Diners Club SA is a wholly owned SBSA subsidiary.
- In-app tap-to-pay: Mastercard cardholders only. Apple Pay, Samsung Pay (Mastercard debit since Aug 2019) and Garmin Pay are also offered. Masterpass is still listed.
- SimplyBlu (Jun 2019) runs on Mastercard Payment Gateway Services; Mastercard and Google SME offer in Oct 2020.
- PayShap launch bank (Mar 2023); low-value payments auto-routed to PayShap in 2026. Instant Money R23.4bn turnover in H1. FlexiPay (Uganda) up 99%.
- Shyft is built on Visa Cross-Border Solutions (Currencycloud); its physical cards appear as Mastercard ⚠, plus a UnionPay card.
- UnionPay: partnership since about 2010; renewed Apr 2025; e-commerce acceptance in nine more markets Aug 2026 ⚠.
- Digital assets: ZARU rand stablecoin; Aroko blockchain rails (>R1trn); CIPS link to China.
- Mastercard deal: five-year Africa agreement, Aug 2013; nothing public since ⚠. H1 2026 cites higher US-dollar Visa and Mastercard processing costs.

## 3. Market
- No domestic card scheme. The SARB studied one in 2021, citing offshore processing of domestic card payments.
- PASA's functions moved to the SARB (card interoperability) and PayInc (EFT, DebiCheck, RTC, PayShap) from 11 Aug 2026. The SARB holds 50% of PayInc (formerly BankservAfrica).
- No current rule routes card debit domestically ⚠.
- SA 2025 fraud: digital banking about R2.4bn (+29%); card fraud about R1.75bn (+18%), led by card-not-present.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Fraud and identity scoring; Click to Pay; Agent Pay for agentic e-commerce; acquirer risk and SoftPOS for a growing acquiring book.
- Remittances and Blue-to-Blue on Mastercard Move; card-linked offers for UCount (1.64m members).
- Commercial and fleet cards against Visa; card modernisation; stablecoin-linked cards.

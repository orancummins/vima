# BRAC Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `BRAC_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BRAC Bank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (H1 2026 earnings disclosure, call 13 Aug 2026; 2025 annual report, updated 9 Jul 2026) unless marked. 1 crore (Cr) = 10 million. Several Daily Star, Dhaka Tribune and Mastercard newsroom pages were blocked. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- BRAC Bank PLC (DSE/CSE: BRACBANK), Dhaka; opened 4 Jul 2001 by Sir Fazle Hasan Abed's BRAC to bank SMEs. BRAC holds 46.2%. Market cap BDT 125.6bn at end-2025, about USD 1bn and the highest among Bangladeshi banks. S&P B+, Moody's B2, CRAB AAA. 10,308 staff; 2.3m customers (2025).
- H1 2026 (consolidated): PAT BDT 1,423 Cr (+57%), assets BDT 145,270 Cr, loans BDT 76,189 Cr (+18%), deposits BDT 109,491 Cr (+25%), ROE 21.5%, NPL 2.03%, CRAR 19.95%. FY2025: PAT BDT 2,251 Cr (+57%).
- Segments: SME, Retail, Wholesale; subsidiaries bKash (51% ordinary, ~36% economic), BRAC Saajan (UK remittance, ~98%), BRAC EPL Investments and Stock Brokerage.
- Leadership: Meheriar M. Hasan (Chair), Tareq Refat Ullah Khan (MD & CEO, after Selim R. F. Hussain resigned in May 2025), M Masud Rana (CFO), A.S.M. Wasi Noman (CIO from Mar 2026), Md. Mahiul Islam (Retail).
- Strategy: "Missing Majority", moving from access-led to engagement-led inclusion. Astha has 1.2m users and handles BDT 200bn a month; TARA has 465,340 women; 2m+ SMEs served since 2001.
- bKash: 84m customers (50m active), 950k merchants, 369k agents, BDT 1,363bn a month (H1 2026). Initial approval for bKash Digital Bank on 24 Sep 2026.

## 2. BRAC Bank × Mastercard
- Credit: Visa Infinite/Signature/Platinum Flexi/Cashback/TARA Platinum; Mastercard World (Jun 2023), TARA World (Sep 2021), Millennial Titanium (Oct 2021); Classic, Gold and Platinum on Visa or Mastercard; Diners Club Emerald (Dec 2025).
- Debit: mostly Visa; Mastercard Platinum Multicurrency and TARA debit since 23 Nov 2025 (Khan, Syed Mohammad Kamal, Gautam Aggarwal); TakaPay on NPSB.
- Google Pay for Visa credit only (Feb 2026); CyberSource token management; Mastercard MDES status unknown ⚠.
- Acquiring: 11,000+ merchants on MPGS (Mastercard) and CyberSource (Visa) gateways.
- Visa Direct for Account outbound remittance (Jun 2025); Visa SME multicurrency cards (Jan 2026).
- bKash: Mastercard Send with Western Union and BRAC Bank (Apr 2016); Add Money from Mastercard cards (Mar 2019 ⚠); Mastercard Move for wallet remittances (Jun 2026; Zakia Sultana, Mastercard country manager).
- Three Mastercard Excellence Awards (credit) in 2025. No exclusive network deal found ⚠.
- Market: Visa 71.8%, Mastercard 17.5%, Amex 10.4% of domestic credit-card spending (Mar 2025, Bangladesh Bank).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Tokens and phone payments for Mastercard cards; Move for the bank's own USD 2bn+ remittances; SME commercial cards; acquirer risk as Bangla QR becomes mandatory; TARA as a Mastercard women's proposition; cards and tokens for bKash Digital Bank; fraud and scam tools.

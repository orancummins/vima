## 1. The company
- Bank Audi sal: founded in Sidon in 1830, incorporated 1962; shares and GDRs listed in Beirut (588.5m shares; $1.71 on 9 Oct 2026, about $1.0bn ⚠ thin trading). More than 1,500 holders incl. the Audi family, FRH Investment Holding, IFC and EBRD. No current international ratings found ⚠.
- FY2025: assets $15.12bn, deposits $12.89bn, loans $1.14bn, equity $1.16bn, net profit $83m (2024: nil), about half from Lebanon. Q1 2026: profit $19.4m, deposits $12.62bn. 1,612 staff, 43 branches.
- Two dollars: $3.4bn of fresh foreign-currency deposits (of which $973m in Lebanon) against $9.4bn of restricted "lollar" deposits; Lebanese loans $163m, $146m in fresh dollars.
- Footprint: Lebanon; Banque Audi (Suisse); Audi Capital (KSA), the group's Gulf wealth arm ($635m AuM); Bank Audi France; Bank Audi LLC (Qatar); Abu Dhabi office. Sold: Egypt to FAB (2021), Odea Bank to ADQ (Mar 2025). IFC settlement (Apr 2025). FINMA censure of the Swiss unit (2024).
- Leadership: Sherine R. Audi (Chair from 13 Jan 2026, after Samir Hanna), Khalil El Debs (Group CEO), Hassan Saleh (Deputy CEO; CEO of neo), Elias Abousleiman (CFO).
- Strategy: readiness for restructuring under Law 23/2025 and the pending Gap law (Cabinet draft 27 Dec 2025; deposits up to $100,000 repaid in phases); selective fresh lending; digital leadership through neo. War resumed in March 2026; partial ceasefire from 16 April.
- neo by Bank Audi (2024): multi-currency accounts and cards, QR and mobile-number transfers, digital loans, neo teens, neo Business; profitable in 2025. Customer numbers not disclosed ⚠. Platform: Codebase Technologies ⚠.

## 3. The market: fresh dollars and BDL's payments push
- BDL Circular 150 (Apr 2020) keeps fresh dollars free for card use; Circular 158 pays withdrawals partly onto cards; Announcement 980 (4 Jun 2026) makes local dollar cards payable in lira at POS at LBP 89,500.
- BDL's push: April 2024 work with Mastercard and Visa on card costs; merchant fees capped at 1.25% (2026); card transactions up about 130% in Jan–Aug 2026; Mastercard–BDL acceptance programme (Sep 2026).
- "Instant payments": BDL-RTGS (2012) and BDL-CLEAR, fees reset 18 Feb 2026; licensed wallets (Circular 69) carry instant P2P. No national retail instant-payment scheme found ⚠.

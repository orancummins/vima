## 1. The company
- Nexi S.p.A. (Euronext Milan: NEXI; FTSE MIB), Milan. From ICBPI (1939) and CartaSi; renamed Nexi 2017; IPO Apr 2019 at €9; Intesa acquiring book (2020), Nets (Jul 2021), SIA (Jan 2022). c.9,600 staff, 25+ countries, c.3m terminals, c.140m cards, c.250 bank and corporate issuing clients.
- FY2025: revenue €3,585m (+2.1%), EBITDA €1,904m (53.1%), normalised net profit €783m, reported loss €3,377m after €3.7bn impairment, net debt €4,942m (2.6x). 1H 2026: revenue €1,736m (+1.0%), EBITDA €870m, normalised net profit €354m, net debt €5,098m (2.7x).
- Segments (FY2025): Merchant Solutions €2,038m (57%), Issuing Solutions €1,157m (32%), Digital Banking Solutions €391m (11%). Geography (9M25): Italy 59%, Nordics 18%, CSEE 15%, DACH 9%.
- Volumes 2025: merchant 20.1bn transactions / €837bn; issuing 22.0bn / €952bn.
- Owners: H&F 22.23%, CDP Equity 19.14% (May 2026), CDP cleared to 29.9% ⚠ not verified. TPG's DBS offer rejected (Dec 2025); CVC reported weighing €9bn (Apr 2026); golden power. Market cap c.€5.1–5.2bn ⚠ derived. Investment grade (Fitch, S&P) ⚠ letters not stated.
- Leadership: Chair Marcello Sala; CEO Bernardo Mingrone (25 Mar 2026); CFO Piergiorgio Pedron (1 Apr 2026). Catanzaro and Segersven roles unclear after the Jul 2026 simplification ⚠.
- CMD (5 Mar 2026): 2026 transition year; mid-single-digit growth by 2028; c.€2.4bn excess cash 2026–28; SmartPay, Nexi Unified, Nexi Checkout, Nexi Ready, AI.

## 2. Nexi × Mastercard
- Accepts Mastercard and Maestro everywhere; SoftPOS prices Mastercard at 1.89%, the same as Visa and PagoBANCOMAT.
- Mastercard bought Nets' A2A business for €2.85bn (5 Mar 2021); Nets × Ethoca (7 Dec 2021); Mastercard Open Banking in Nexi gateways (11 Mar 2024; Bart Willaert, Omar Haque); Mastercard Move and Visa Direct for Italian businesses (29 Oct 2025); named as exploring Mastercard Agent Connect (9 Sep 2026) ⚠ secondary.
- Not found: scheme or volume agreement, Click to Pay, Installments, MDES tokens, Brighterion. Network split not disclosed.
- Visa: partner for Nexi Ready in Germany (23 Apr 2026; Tobias Czekalla), Visa Direct, Eurowag fleet cards (Feb 2025), Luna Rossa sponsorship (30 Sep 2026).
- Domestic and A2A: Bancomat hub (Apr 2024), Dankort ⚠ operator role; EPI founder, Wero acquirer in Germany (Feb 2025) and live in e-commerce (Apr 2026); Zippay (Mar 2026); digital-euro pilot PSP (Jul 2026). Pay-later via PayPal, Klarna, Compass, Younited (2027).

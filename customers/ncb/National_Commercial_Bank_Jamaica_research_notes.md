## 1. The company
- National Commercial Bank Jamaica Ltd (NCBJ), subsidiary of NCB Financial Group Ltd (NCBFG; JSE and TTSE). AIC (Barbados) owns 46.99% (Mar 2026); Portland Holdings and Chairman Michael Lee-Chin control it. Heritage from 1837 (Colonial Bank), NCB from 1977, NCBFG from 2016.
- NCBJ: 27 branches, 300+ ABMs, 2,000+ staff. Group: J$2.43tn assets, J$844bn deposits, J$604.5bn loans, NPL ratio 4.17% (31 Mar 2026).
- H1 FY2026: net profit J$10.3bn (−53%; +46% excluding the prior-year J$15.1bn Dutch brokerage gain), annualised ROE 7.15%, cost-to-income 72.7%. FY2025: net profit J$36.9bn, ROE 12.46%.
- Seven segments: Consumer & SME; Payment Services (FY2025 revenue J$39.0bn, net fees J$12.0bn); Corporate & Commercial; Treasury & Correspondent; Wealth, Asset Management & Investment Banking; Life & Health and Pensions; General Insurance.
- Entities: NCBJ, NCB Capital Markets, NCB Insurance Agency & Fund Managers, TFOB (Lynk), Guardian Holdings (21 markets), Clarien (Bermuda), NCB Cayman (moving to Clarien), NCB Merchant Bank T&T (moving to Guardian).
- Leadership: Michael Lee-Chin (Chairman), Robert Almeida (Group CEO), Sheree Martin (NCBJ CEO from 1 Sep 2026; interim since 19 Jan 2026), Danielle Cameron Duncan (VP Payments and Digital Channels).
- Hurricane Melissa (28–31 Oct 2025): relief packages, lower fees and volumes in Q1 FY2026, about US$129m of reinsured claims, about J$2bn of repossessed property listed (Jun 2026). SME Growth Fund II J$2bn (Apr 2026). High-value cheque clearing ends 1 Sep 2026; all cheques by 1 Mar 2028.

## 2. NCB × Mastercard
- Debit: Visa (Classic, Platinum). Keycard (1981) retired from Nov 2020; Multilink Midas debit converted to Visa and switched off 31 Mar 2025.
- Personal credit: Mastercard Standard USD, Platinum Travel JMD/USD, Black ⚠ (form only); Visa Classic and Platinum.
- Business credit: Business Elite and Executive Travel Business (Mastercard); Visa Platinum Business; Business Edge ⚠ network. FlexiPay Biz instalments.
- Prepaid: NCB Mastercard Prepaid (Apr 2021; Mastercard country manager Dalton Fowles at launch).
- Acquiring: about 70% of POS, all networks incl. Amex; ePOS Tap on Phone (Jun 2024, Fasstap); Mastercard phones for unbanked MSMEs (Feb 2025).
- Wallets: NCB Pay (Mar 2023; Visa and Mastercard cards, Android ⚠ status); Lynk (Dec 2021; ~300k users; Jam-Dex); Lynk Virtual Card on Visa (Apr 2026).
- No public network agreement with either scheme found ⚠. No Mastercard tokenisation, Click to Pay, fraud tools or Move programme found.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Tokens for Apple Pay and Google Pay (CIBC launched Google Pay Jun 2026; Scotia and Sagicor plan Apple Pay); Mastercard Move for remittances into cards and Lynk; scaling Tap on Phone and open-loop tolling; commercial cards as cheques end; fraud and identity scoring amid post-Melissa credit stress; card-linked offers for NCB Miles.

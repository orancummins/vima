# Société Générale — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Societe_Generale_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Société Générale × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (Q2 2026 results, 30 Jul 2026; Capital Markets Day, 21 Sep 2026; 2026 URD, 13 Mar 2026) unless marked. The Mastercard and Visa newsrooms were not reachable; ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are due 29 October 2026.

## 1. The company
- Société Générale S.A. (Euronext Paris). Founded 1864, nationalised 1945, privatised 1987. About 110,000 staff, 27m clients, 58 countries. Market value €52.7bn at end-2025; share €64.47 on 17 Oct 2026 (no October market-cap figure ⚠).
- FY2025: revenues €27.3bn, Group net income €6.0bn, ROTE 10.2%. Q2 26: revenues €7.1bn, record net income €1.79bn, ROTE 12.2%; H1 26 €3.49bn; CET1 13.2%; loans €476bn, deposits €626bn. Ratings Fitch A+, Moody's A1, S&P A (positive).
- Pillars: French Retail, Private Banking and Insurance (SG network 8.5m clients and ~1,400 branches; BoursoBank 9.1m clients; Private Banking €145bn; life insurance €167bn); Global Banking and Investor Solutions (Global Markets, SGSS, Financing & Advisory, GTPS, SG-Forge); Mobility, International Retail Banking and Financial Services (KB, BRD, Africa, Ayvens 3.2m vehicles, consumer finance €23bn).
- Leadership: Chair William Connelly (from May 2026), CEO Slawomir Krupa, Deputy CEO Pierre Palmieri, Alexis Kohler, Lubomira Rochet, CFO Leopoldo Alvear, Cécile Bartenieff; Benoit Grisoni (BoursoBank) also runs French individual and professional clients from 1 Oct 2026.
- 2029 roadmap (21 Sep 2026): ROTE 13–14% in 2029, >15% from 2030; C/I <55%; costs <€16.3bn; BoursoBank >14m clients; strategic agreement with Anthropic; AI savings €500–600m.
- Deals: Rosbank sold (2022); Ayvens formed (2023); PayXpert stake (2023); Bernstein JV (2024); SGEF, UBP and African sales (2025); SGSS retail MoU with Arkéa (Mar 2026); Treezor sold to Shares (6 Oct 2026). 15 disposals since 2023.

## 2. Société Générale × Mastercard
- SG France retail and professional cards: CB + Visa only. CB Mastercard and CB Gold Mastercard no longer sold from June 2025; holders moved to Visa in Sep–Oct 2025 (MoneyVox). The CB Service Éclair Cirrus withdrawal card remains.
- SG corporate cards: CB Visa Affaires and Gold Affaires alongside CB Corporate Mastercard and Corporate Executive Mastercard.
- BoursoBank: Visa (Welcome, Ultim, Metal, Business) ⚠ CB co-badging not stated. Banxup teen card: Mastercard issued by Treezor ⚠ future after the Treezor sale not stated.
- KB: Visa debit (Platinum, Infinite, virtual Visa in KB+ from Mar 2026); Mastercard World Elite credit since Jul 2021; voted Mastercard Bank of the Year 2025. 4U and A card networks not verified ⚠.
- BRD: Mastercard-led — Gold, Platinum, Noir (World Elite), Priceless; Visa alongside on debit.
- Consumer finance: Fiditalia Carta Bianca on Mastercard (Identity Check, Click to Pay); Hanseatic Bank on Visa. Ayvens fleet-card network not found ⚠.
- Mastercard bought 5% of Treezor in 2022 ("industrial partnership"); ended with the sale. League of Legends Mastercard (Sep 2019) presumed ended ⚠.
- Wero: SG a founding EPI member; live from Oct 2024 replacing Paylib; e-commerce next; BoursoBank by end-2026. No public network contract or renewal date.

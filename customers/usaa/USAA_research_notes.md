# USAA — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `USAA_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and USAA × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are USAA's own (2025 Annual Report to Members, spring 2026; newsroom) or FDIC call reports for USAA Federal Savings Bank to 30 June 2026. USAA is member-owned and publishes no 10-K; 2025 revenue and net income were not disclosed. Several usaa.com pages sit behind a bot gate and the web-search budget ran out, so credit ratings were not checked. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- United Services Automobile Association, San Antonio; founded 1922 by 25 Army officers; a reciprocal inter-insurance exchange owned by its members. Not listed.
- Members 14.3m (end-2025), 14.5m+ (Jul 2026). Employees 36,000+ (Jul 2026), down from 38,000+ (Jun 2025). Offices in seven US cities and four overseas.
- 2025: assets $235.8bn, net worth $38.6bn (+20%), record $3.8bn returned to members. 2024: revenue $48.6bn, net income $3.9bn. 2022: net loss $1.3bn.
- USAA FSB (OCC-regulated, cert 32188), 30 Jun 2026: assets $106.2bn, deposits $92.0bn, net loans $44.3bn (cards $18.6bn, auto $17.4bn), H1 net income $484m, 11,883 staff.
- Lines: Property & Casualty (fifth-largest US auto insurer), Banking, Life Insurance. Investments sold to Victory Capital ⚠ and Schwab ($1.8bn, 2020).
- Leadership: Chairman David F. Melcher (Aug 2026, after James Zortman); CEO Juan C. Andrade (Apr 2025); CFO Brett Seybold; USAA Bank president Michael Moran (Jan 2025); CIO Dan Griffiths (Feb 2026, ex-Santander US); CRO Brian Goldman (May 2025).
- OCC cease-and-desist order (Dec 2024) replacing the 2019 and 2022 orders ($85m and $140m penalties); new products need 90 days' notice. No termination found ⚠.
- Strategy: member value, auto premium cuts, better card rewards and savings, more base branches and ATMs, Honor Through Action ($500m over five years). 3,000+ patents (240 in AI); 8bn cyberthreats blocked in 2025; RDC patent licences and a BofA cross-licence (Aug 2026).

## 2. USAA × Mastercard
- Mastercard carried USAA's credit and debit cards for "roughly 30 years"; USAA announced a move to Visa on 26 Oct 2015 and converted cards through 2016. Mastercard: economics "did not make sense"; it kept "certain processing and other services" ⚠ current status unknown.
- Today: debit on Visa (Visa Secure, contactless, Apple/Google/Samsung Pay). Credit: six Visa cards (Eagle Adapt, Eagle Navigator, Preferred Cash Rewards, Rewards, Rate Advantage, Secured) and three American Express (Cashback Rewards Plus, Eagle Ascend, Rewards). 4m+ cardholders.
- Jul 2026: Eagle Adapt Visa and Eagle Ascend Amex launched; Cashback Rewards Plus Amex refreshed. Amex partnership about 20 years ⚠.
- Mastercard touchpoints left: account funding by outside Visa or Mastercard cards; legacy processing ⚠. No joint initiative found since 2015.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Network-neutral services: scam and identity scoring for Zelle and external transfers; card-linked offers beside USAA Perks; Agent Pay for an AI-heavy insurer-bank; open finance as Section 1033 is rewritten; cross-border for members overseas. A future network re-tender has no public date ⚠.

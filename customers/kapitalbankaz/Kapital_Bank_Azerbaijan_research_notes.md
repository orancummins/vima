# Kapital Bank (Birbank), Azerbaijan — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Kapital_Bank_Azerbaijan_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Kapital Bank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own: statutory indicators to 30 Sep 2026 (published 13 Oct 2026), the 2025 consolidated IFRS statements (22 Apr 2026) and the 2024 Sustainability Report, plus birbank.az, Trend, PashaPay and Report.az. Not Kapitalbank of Uzbekistan. The bank publishes little on card networks; several networks are read from card artwork on birbank.az. ⚠ marks items that rest on inference or secondary sources.

## 1. The company
- Kapital Bank OJSC, Baku (Port Baku Tower II). Licence No. 244 (2000, reissued 2025). Heir to the Savings Bank of Azerbaijan (1874). Sells everything as Birbank since 2025; branches converting over about two years.
- Owner: PASHA Holding 99.89% at end-2025 (IFRS) ⚠ About page shows 81.08% PASHA Holding, 7.52% PASHA Sigorta, 11.40% others. Ultimately controlled by Leyla Aliyeva, Arzu Aliyeva, Arif Pashayev and Mir Jamal Pashayev. Unlisted. In Bir ecosystem LLC under PASHA's new financial holding from Jan 2026.
- Ratings: S&P BB/B stable; Moody's ba3 BCA, Ba2 deposits, outlook positive.
- 9M 2026 (statutory): net profit AZN 297.0m (+30%); assets AZN 13.92bn; gross loans AZN 6.39bn (consumer 3.54bn, business 2.27bn, real estate 0.59bn); deposits AZN 10.31bn; total capital ratio 13.64%.
- FY2025 (IFRS): net profit AZN 274.6m (+15%); NII AZN 797m; fees AZN 572m; assets AZN 11.57bn. Segments: Retail AZN 548m profit, Treasury 101m, Corporate 35m, Ecosystem −87m.
- Customers: 5m+ individuals, 22,000+ legal entities; Birbank app 3.5m users; 106,406 micro and 16,716 SME clients; 7.2m debit and 975k credit cards (end-2024); 34.1% of consumer loans.
- Subsidiaries: BirKart NBCO, PashaPay (m10 wallet 829k users; 6,500 MilliÖn terminals), Competo (Birmarket, Bir Bonus). ⚠ An unauthorised-transactions fraud at a subsidiary in 2026, possibly material, unquantified.
- Leadership: Farid Mammadov (Chair), Farid Huseynov (CEO since May 2023), Javid Mirzayev, Jalal Orujov, Rauf Atakishiyev (CFO), Farid Hidayatzada, Agharza Bakhshiyev (CRO). 5,765 employees; 115–116 branches; about 1,300 ATMs.
- Second-largest bank by assets (AZN 12.7bn, June 2025) after ABB; about a fifth of sector assets ⚠ own calculation.

## 2. Kapital Bank × Mastercard
- Both networks; collateral posted for Mastercard and Visa (IFRS). Site: Birbank cards "support Visa and MasterCard".
- Visa: Birbank Cashback/Taksit ⚠ artwork, Star, Platinum, Miles Debit ⚠, Visa Infinite Cashback and Miles, TələbəPlus (2024), all business cards; Disneyland campaign (Nov 2025); metro and bus with BakıKart (2025).
- Mastercard: Miles Taksit (World; LoungeKey, Airport Experiences), World Elite Miles (Private), TəhsilPlus for about 450,000 education staff with Priceless Specials (6 Mar 2026; Engin Yashar, Mastercard VP Business Development).
- PashaPay–Mastercard MoU, 3 Jun 2025: identity, fraud, MoneySend, Cross-Border Send, Alias Directory, m10 loyalty ⚠ live use unknown.
- Debit card network not stated ⚠. No network agreement or switch public.

## 3. Market and where Mastercard could land
- 22.5m cards, 160k POS (May 2026); CBAR IPS at about 1m transactions a day (Oct 2026), lower fees from Apr 2027; caps on incoming card transfers in 2026; Visa says 10 local issuers on Click to Pay.
- Openings: outbound sends via Birbank and PashaPay; fraud and identity; Click to Pay on Birmarket; offers into Bir Bonus; subscription and merchant clarity; commercial cards for SMEs.

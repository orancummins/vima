# Nordea — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Nordea_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Nordea × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (Q3 2026 report, 15 Oct 2026; 2025 results, 29 Jan 2026; 2030 strategy, 5 Nov 2025) unless marked. The web-search budget ran out part-way, and Nordea's fact sheet sits behind a cookie wall. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Nordea Bank Abp, Helsinki (re-domiciled from Sweden 1 Oct 2018; ECB-supervised). Listed in Helsinki, Stockholm and Copenhagen; about 580,000 private shareholders. Roots back to 1820; one Nordea brand since 2001.
- Q3 2026: income EUR 2,968m, net profit EUR 1,181m, ROE 15.1%, cost-to-income 45.0%, CET1 15.8%, loans EUR 399.6bn, deposits EUR 259.0bn, AuM EUR 509bn, 28,376 FTEs. Payments and card fees EUR 163m (+4%).
- FY2025: net profit EUR 4,840m, ROE 15.5%, RoTE 17.8%, dividend EUR 0.96.
- Ratings: Moody's Aa3 (senior unsecured, Oct 2026), S&P AA-, Fitch AA (May 2026).
- Business areas: Personal Banking, Business Banking (incl. Transaction Banking, Nordea Finance), Large Corporates & Institutions, Asset & Wealth Management.
- Leadership: Sir Stephen Hester (Chair), Frank Vang-Jensen (CEO), Ian Smith (CFO); Per Långsved heads Personal Banking from 1 Nov 2026.
- 2030 strategy: ROE >15%, C/I 40–42%, EPS ~EUR 2, >EUR 20bn distributions; Nordic scale in mortgages, corporate lending, savings and payments; EUR 190m restructuring (Q1 2026), ~1,500 roles.
- Digital: one platform replacing 22 codebases; 5m digitally active users; 135m monthly log-ins; 1.6bn ML insights (2025).
- Deals: Danske Bank Norway personal business (Nov 2024, 235,000 customers); Gjensidige Bank (2019); acquiring sold to Nets (2015).
- Total customer count not published in 2025–26 sources ⚠; market shares not sourced ⚠.

## 2. Nordea × Mastercard
- Debit on Visa everywhere: Nordea Debit (Sweden, Finland), Visa/Dankort and Nordea Pay (Denmark), BankAxept + Visa (Norway ⚠ inferred from fee table), Business Visa Debit.
- Credit on Mastercard everywhere: Nordea Gold, Credit, Premium/Platinum; Stockmann Mastercard (2008); Nordea Business Mastercard. First Card network not stated ⚠.
- Premium: Mastercard LoungeKey and Airport Experiences.
- Click to Pay with Visa and Mastercard; Mastercard Open Banking connects to Nordea accounts.
- Agent Pay: Finland's first live agentic payment, 5 Jun 2026 (Kirsi Wiitala, Nordea; Erik Gutwasser, Mastercard).
- P27 chose Mastercard (Jun 2019); P27 later abandoned ⚠.
- Processing: TietoEVRY (NO, SE; Dec 2020 ⚠), Nets (DK, FI; renewed Jun 2021). No network agreement or switch announced 2015–26 ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay across four countries; SME cards and spend controls for 50,000+ new small businesses; the payments scale programme; scam and fraud scoring; card-linked offers on the credit book; open finance; debit if ever tendered ⚠.

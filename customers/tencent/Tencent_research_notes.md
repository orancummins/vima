# Tencent: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Tencent_Platform.html`. One research thread (Tencent as a payments player: Weixin Pay, Tenpay, TenPay Global, WeChat Pay HK; and Tencent × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Tencent's own (Q2 2026 results and presentation, 12 Aug 2026; 2025 annual results, 18 Mar 2026) unless marked, with Tencent's newsroom, Visa, trade press and Wikipedia. Mastercard's newsroom blocked fetching (Tencent's copy of the Dec 2025 release was used). Tencent does not report payments revenue, payment volume or Weixin Pay users. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or prior knowledge. Q3 2026 results are due 18 Nov 2026.

## 1. The company
- Tencent Holdings Limited (HKEX: 0700 / 80700), Shenzhen; founded Nov 1998, listed Jun 2004. Prosus ≈25–26% ⚠; Pony Ma ≈8% ⚠. Market value ≈US$476bn (Oct 2026) ⚠. 115,927 staff (30 Jun 2026). On the US DoD list since Jan 2025 ⚠.
- FY2025: revenue RMB751.8bn (+14%); FinTech & Business Services RMB229.4bn (+8%, 31%), gross margin 51%.
- Q2 2026: revenue RMB204.8bn (+11%); FinTech & Business Services RMB60.3bn (+9%); FinTech growth from commercial payment, wealth and consumer loans; payment transactions up, ticket-size decline narrowing. Weixin & WeChat MAU 1,439m.
- People: Pony Ma (Chairman, CEO), Martin Lau (President), John Lo (CFO), Allen Zhang (Weixin Group), Davis Lin (SVP; FiT head ⚠). Royal Chen and Daniel Hong (VPs, FiT); Wenhui Yang (CEO, TenPay Global); Celine Zhang (Weixin Pay business management).
- Regulation: PBOC fined Tenpay ≈RMB2.99bn (Jul 2023). WeChat Pay HK holds an HKMA SVF licence (Aug 2016).
- Strategy: AI (Hy3, WorkBuddy, capex RMB84.7bn H1 2026); Xiaowei agent prototype in Weixin; SkillPay + Weixin Pay in WorkBuddy.

## 2. Payments products
- Weixin Pay (Aug 2013): QR, in-app, Mini Program; funded by balances and bound mainland cards (UnionPay ⚠). WeChat Pay ≈42% vs Alipay ≈53% ⚠. QR interoperability with Alipay and UnionPay (2021).
- Foreign cards for visitors (Jul–Aug 2023): Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB, Discover, Diners, overseas UnionPay ⚠; ≈US$5k / US$50k limits ⚠; +80% Jan–Apr 2026; 90-day 3% fee waiver (May 2026).
- CPG "Pay with Your Home E-Wallet" (Sep 2025): 40+ wallets, 36 live, 13 markets; Eldik Bank, HUMO Pay (2026). PayPal World QR (May 2026).
- Outbound: Golden Week 2025 +21%; Korea +46%, Singapore +32%, Malaysia >3× via PayNet; Japan via PayPay etc.
- Remittances in: Visa Direct (16 Nov 2023); Mastercard Move (8 Dec 2025); 30+ remittance partners (2023).
- WeChat Pay HK: HKD wallet; Payment Connect (IBPS–FPS) with HSBC (Mar 2026); tax refunds in Shenzhen.
- Licaitong (since 2014; >RMB300bn in 2018 ⚠ current not disclosed); consumer loans via WeBank (≈30% ⚠) and Fenfu ⚠; WeSure.

## 3. Tencent × Mastercard
- 2019 partnership ⚠ (cited by Mastercard in 2023). 16 Aug 2023: Mastercard cardholders can bind cards to Weixin Pay. 8 Dec 2025: Mastercard Move to Weixin Pay (Anouska Ladds; Wenhui Yang).
- Visa was named first for card binding (Jun 2023) and on remittances (Nov 2023).
- Mastercard NUCC holds a China bank-card clearing licence (Nov 2023) ⚠ live scale unknown.
- Not found: tokenisation, Click to Pay, Agent Pay, offers or fraud tools with Tencent.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Risk, token and identity tools for visitor card binding; Agent Pay for Xiaowei and WorkBuddy; Move volume against Visa Direct; tax refunds to cards; acquirer links for outbound acceptance; RMB cards through NUCC ⚠.

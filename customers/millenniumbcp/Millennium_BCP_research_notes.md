## 1. The company
- Banco Comercial Português, S.A. (Euronext Lisbon: BCP), founded 1985, one Millennium brand since the early 2000s. Fosun 20.5%, Sonangol 19.9%. About €14bn market value in May 2026 ⚠ derived.
- H1 2026: net income €565.8m (+12.7%), ROE 14.6%, CET1 15.1%, loans €65.2bn, customer funds €116.7bn, cost of risk 32 bp. 7.4m active customers, 75% mobile. Q1 2026: €305.8m.
- FY2025: net income €1,018.6m (+12.4%), ROE 14.1%; card and transfer commissions €264.5m (+2.6%; down in Portugal). 15,747 staff.
- Portugal: second-largest bank (~17% of business volume); 16.5% of loans, 18.5% of deposits (Jul 2026); 2.9m customers; 385 branches.
- Group: Bank Millennium (Poland, 50.01%; 3.35m active retail clients; H1 net PLN 708m); Millennium bim (Mozambique, 66.67%); ActivoBank; Millenniumbcp Ageas; Macau branch; stake in Banco Millennium Atlântico (Angola).
- Leadership: Nuno Amado (Chair), Miguel Maya (CEO), both reappointed 7 May 2026 for 2026–29; Miguel Bragança and João Nuno Palma, executive vice-chairs.
- Ratings: DBRS senior A (Sep 2026); Fitch deposits A (May 2026); Moody's deposits A2 (May 2025). S&P not checked ⚠.
- 2025-28 plan: >8m customers, >80% mobile, C/I <40%, ROE and CET1 >13.5%; payout up to 90%; €407.5m buyback from June 2026. GenAI at scale, cloud data platform, AI Academy with Nova SBE (6,500+ staff, Nov 2025).

## 2. Millennium bcp × Mastercard
- Portugal is Visa: all adult debit (with Multibanco) and credit cards sold are Visa, including the TAP Miles&Go co-brand. Mastercard Débito, GO! Débito, Maestro and Mastercard credit cards, and all Amex cards, are listed as no longer sold ⚠ (switch dates unpublished).
- Mastercard in Portugal: Mastercard Débito Empresas (branch only); junior debit listed under Mastercard and Visa ⚠; acceptance on TPA terminals and the P@y.me gateway.
- UnionPay: Europe's first issuer (deal Nov 2017, cards Jan 2019).
- Poland is dual-network: Impresja and Gold on Visa or Mastercard, WWF Mastercard, Mastercard Voyager and Prepaid, Visa Raz and Visa Global; Mastercard Click to Pay since Sep 2026; BLIK 2.45m users. Debit split by network unknown ⚠.
- Mozambique: Millennium bim cards on Visa ⚠.
- No public Mastercard services deal (fraud, tokens, open finance, Agent Pay) found ⚠.

## 3. Competitors and context
- Visa (all new Portuguese consumer cards; Visa Luxury Hotel Collection on Prestige); SIBS Multibanco and MB WAY, built into the app; EuroPA with Bizum, Bancomat, Vipps MobilePay and EPI (P2P across 13 countries by end-2026, shop payments by end-2027) aimed at displacing Visa and Mastercard; digital euro not before 2029.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Network-agnostic fraud and scam tools for the plan's real-time fraud push; merchant data and acquirer risk for TPA, TPA Analytics and P@y.me; open finance for Cash-on-Time; card-linked offers beside Millennium Rewards; business cards and spend controls; Click to Pay and tokens in Portugal, as in Poland.

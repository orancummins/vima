## 1. The company
- U.S. Bancorp (NYSE: USB), Minneapolis; charter No. 24 (1863); fifth-largest US commercial bank; 15m clients; ~70,000 staff; market value ~$91.5bn (16 Oct 2026).
- Q3 2026: record revenue $7.97bn (+8.7%), net income $2.20bn, EPS $1.34, ROTCE 18.5%, CET1 10.8%, assets $703bn. FY2025: revenue $28.7bn, net income $7.57bn, EPS $4.62.
- Segments: WCCIB ($1.60bn Q3 net income); Consumer & Business Banking ($633m); Payment Services ($175m, −31% on the Amazon portfolio provision); Treasury & Corporate Support.
- Payments split (2025) into PMI (Elavon, corporate payments, Payments Europe; Mark Runkel) and PCS (cards, Elan, co-brand; Courtney Kelso, ex-Amex).
- Leadership: Gunjan Kedia (CEO Apr 2025, Chair Apr 2026), Stephen Philipson (WCCIB), John Stern (CFO ⚠), Wally Mlynarski (Elavon CEO, Mar 2026), Jamie Walker (digital assets), Toby Clements (COO ops).
- Deals: MUFG Union Bank (Dec 2022); Salucro (Aug 2024); BTIG (completed 1 Jun 2026, target $725m); Amazon small-business card portfolio (Aug 2026).
- Targets 2026–27: ROA 1.15–1.35%, ROTCE high teens, positive operating leverage. Digital assets: USBDC stablecoin pilot on Stellar (Sep 2026).

## 2. U.S. Bank × Mastercard
- Visa: debit; all bank-brand consumer credit (Smartly, Cash+, Altitude, Shield); small-business cards (Business Essentials Sep 2026, Shield, Triple Cash, Altitude Connect — launched 2023 as World Elite Mastercard ⚠); State Farm, AAA (Mastercard in 2015 ⚠), H-D, SKYPASS; most Elan cards ⚠.
- Mastercard: Amazon Prime Business / Amazon Business (from Amex; live 13 May 2026, conversion 14 Aug 2026); Edward Jones (3 cards, Nov 2025); BMW/MINI/Motorrad (relaunch 13 Oct 2026); Split World Mastercard (Nov 2025); Voyager Mastercard (2021); part of commercial cards ⚠.
- Moving away: Kroger co-brand to Smartly Visa (announced 29 Jul 2026; earlier network ⚠).
- Services: subscription management and digital receipts via Ethoca (Oct 2025). Elavon accepts all networks; Paze and Zelle are bank-owned rivals.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Elavon acceptance (Click to Pay, tokenisation, merchant risk) as merchant revenue slips; Agent Pay for Amazon business buyers; identity and scam tools next to Protect 360; commercial virtual cards and freight/healthcare B2B data; token settlement beside USBDC; Elan's network choice ⚠.

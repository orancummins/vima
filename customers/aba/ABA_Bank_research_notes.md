# ABA Bank: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `ABA_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread covering the company and ABA × Mastercard, condensed. Figures come from ABA's audited 2025 results (released 24 Apr 2026, via Cambodia Investment Review) and National Bank of Canada's Q3 FY2026 filings (26 Aug 2026), unless marked.

ababank.com returned a bot challenge to every fetch, so ABA pages seen only in search results are marked ⚠, as are items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Advanced Bank of Asia Limited, Phnom Penh, founded 1996. Not listed. National Bank of Canada bought 10% (2014), 42% (2015) and 90% (2016, US$103m), then the rest in Oct 2019: US$320m in all, 99.99% ⚠.
- 2025: assets US$16.2bn (+17%), deposits nearly US$13bn, gross loans US$9.6bn, net profit US$377.5m (+26%), equity US$2.7bn.
- Size: about 16% of system assets and about 20% of deposits ⚠ (derived). 104 branches, 1,900+ machines, 10,300+ staff, about 6m customers, 6m+ ABA Mobile users.
- Q3 FY2026 (to 31 Jul 2026): net income C$125m (+5%; about +1% at constant currency), revenue C$279m; provisions rising. FY2025 (parent year): net income US$323m (+12%).
- Leadership: Askhat Azhikhanov (President & CEO), Zhiger Atchabarov (Deputy CEO & COO), Zokhir Rasulov (CDO), Sanzhar Abdullayev (Chief Card & E-Payment Officer), Huot Chanthida (Corporate Banking), Romny Theam (External Affairs). Board chair not found ⚠. No credit rating found ⚠.
- Strategy: become a universal bank (retail plus corporate and investment banking) and a financial ecosystem (Etiqa insurance in-app, Jul 2026). Digital: FacePass Secure, Navi AI, Analytics PFM (Aug 2025), PaySound (Jun 2026), Document Verification (Sep 2026).
- Market: a US$100bn system, NPLs 8.3%, IMF growth forecast of 3% for 2026, Chief Bank under administration (Sep 2026).

## 2. ABA × Mastercard
- Debit on Visa and Mastercard (plus UnionPay since 2015 ⚠ and CSS), issued from Card Machines since Nov 2022 (Compass Plus). Virtual Visa or Mastercard cards in ABA Mobile ⚠.
- Mastercard awards for 2023 (prepaid, debit, cross-border volume) ⚠, and accolades at the 2024 Cambodia customer event ⚠.
- Card-to-card transfers with Mastercard and UnionPay, up to US$20,000 a month ⚠.
- Visa: 8 leadership awards for 2024 and 10 for 2025, including affluent debit and credit spend. PayWay Plus is powered by Visa CyberSource (Grab, Nov 2024).
- Everyday payments run on ABA PAY and KHQR (in ABA PAY since Mar 2022 ⚠) over Bakong: 1.33bn transactions in 2025.
- No public network agreement or switch found ⚠. Mastercard's country manager is Sharad Jain (Sep 2025). Canadia Bank is previewing a Mastercard World credit card (Sep 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Affluent and travel credit against Visa's affluent book.
- Identity Check and fraud scoring, as Visa pushes its security roadmap.
- Cross-border services for remittances.
- Commercial and virtual cards for ABA Business.
- Tap on Phone beside KHQR.
- Merchant-data enrichment for Analytics.

# SBI Card — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `SBI_Card_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and SBI Card × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the company's own unless marked: Q1 FY27 investor presentation (24 Jul 2026), Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, and the Q4 FY26 (27 Apr 2026) and Q3 FY26 (28 Jan 2026) earnings calls. SBI Card's card pages render by script and could not be read, so most networks come from launch coverage. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q2 FY27 results are due late October 2026 ⚠.

## 1. The company
- SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited (NSE/BSE: SBICARD), an NBFC that only issues credit cards; incorporated 1998 ⚠; listed 2020. SBI owns 68.58% (Jun 2026). Rated AAA/Stable (CRISIL, ICRA). Market cap about ₹54,000 crore (13 Oct 2026) ⚠. 4,218 employees.
- Q1 FY27: 2.26 crore cards (+7%), 10.23 lakh new accounts (+17%), spends ₹1,18,475 crore (+27%; retail ₹94,033 crore, corporate ₹24,442 crore), receivables ₹58,269 crore, PAT ₹664 crore (+20%), ROAE 16.5%, credit cost 6.5%, GNPA 2.04%, CAR 25.6%. Shares: 18.6% of cards, 19.5% of spends.
- FY26: total income ₹20,708 crore, PAT ₹2,167 crore, 35.9 lakh new accounts, spends ₹4.30 lakh crore (corporate ₹76,595 crore, +210%).
- Sourcing: SBI branches 47%, open market 53%; YONO 2.0 for digital acquisition. 22 co-brand partners.
- Leadership: Chairman C. S. Setty (also SBI Chairman); MD & CEO Salila Pande (since 1 Apr 2025); CFO Rashmi Mohanty; CRO Krishna Kant Bishnoi; COO Saurav Ghosh; CSMO Girish Budhiraja; CIDO Pradeep Khurana.
- Technology: in-house AI in underwriting, fraud and collections; SPRINT digital onboarding; Oracle personalisation; Ask ILA; a "Network Selection In Card Renewal" build under way at Mar 2026.

## 2. SBI Card × Mastercard
- Four networks: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express. No split published ⚠.
- Mastercard: ELITE on Mastercard World (Jul 2016; Vijay Jasuja and Vikas Varma); app contactless on MDES tokens (Nov 2020) ⚠ current use; KrisFlyer (Amex and Mastercard, Sep 2024); Apollo SELECT (RuPay and Mastercard, May 2025); Flipkart (Mastercard and Visa, Aug 2025); IndiGo (Mastercard and RuPay, Sep 2025) ⚠; Eternal (Mastercard and RuPay, Oct 2026).
- Visa: Tata Neu (Apr 2025), PhonePe (Jul 2025), Google Pay Flex (Jul 2026), each with RuPay; Flipkart. RuPay: partner since 2019, on UPI since 2023; 60%+ of RuPay base UPI-linked; UPI spends +126% in FY26.
- Not found: Click to Pay, Agent Pay, Mastercard fraud/identity, commercial-card or loyalty services at SBI Card ⚠. Corporate, partner-bank, BPCL and Reliance card networks undisclosed ⚠.
- Regulation: RBI barred Mastercard from new cardholders Jul 2021 – Jun 2022; network choice at issue and renewal, no exclusivity, since 6 Sep 2024.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Every renewal and new card under network choice; the international variant on the next platform co-brand; tokens and Click to Pay for 63% online spend; Agent Pay with Flipkart, Zomato and Google; identity and fraud for SPRINT and partner-app onboarding; controls and data for a corporate book that tripled; travel benefits on KrisFlyer, IndiGo and AURUM.

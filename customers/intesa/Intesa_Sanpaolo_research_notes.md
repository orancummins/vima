## 1. The company
- Intesa Sanpaolo S.p.A. (Euronext Milan: ISP), Turin and Milan. Formed in 2007 from Banca Intesa and Sanpaolo IMI; UBI Banca bought in 2020 and merged in 2021. About €107bn market value at €6.148 (16 Oct 2026) ⚠ derived. Main holders: BlackRock 6.7%, Compagnia di San Paolo 6.7%, Fondazione Cariplo 5.5%. Rated A3/BBB+/A-/A.
- About 13.9m customers in Italy and 7.5m abroad; 3,567 branches (2,644 in Italy); 89,725 staff (30 Jun 2026).
- H1 2026: net income €5,554m (+6.5%); loans €437bn; banking deposits €612bn; customer financial assets €1,511bn; CET1 13.1%; 2026 outlook above €10bn. FY2025: record €9,321m; ROTE 22%; cost/income 42.2%.
- Divisions: Banca dei Territori (with Isybank and Mooney), IMI CIB, International Banks (12 countries), Private Banking (Fideuram), Asset Management (Eurizon), Insurance, Corporate Centre.
- Leadership: Gian Maria Gros-Pietro (Chair), Carlo Messina (CEO; board 2025–2027), S. Barrese, M. Micillo, P. Papanicolaou, T. Corcos (division heads), M. Proverbio (data, AI and technology).
- 2026–2029 plan: net income above €11.5bn and ROTE 27% in 2029; about €50bn distributed; cost/income 36.8%; €4.6bn for technology; isytech fully in the cloud by 2029; +2.5m customers mainly through Isybank; isywealth Europe from 2027. AI: 153 apps (2025); 19 agentic and GenAI projects (H1 2026); 80% of requests by AI by 2029 ⚠ (press).
- Isybank: about 1.2m customers and 1.05m accounts opened by new customers; the AGCM halted the migration of 2.4m accounts in 2023.

## 2. Intesa Sanpaolo × Mastercard
- Debit (XME Debit, XME Card Plus): co-badged BANCOMAT plus Visa **or** Mastercard, chosen by the customer.
- Credit (XME Credit Card, XME Credit Icon): Mastercard or Visa. Exclusive credit (May 2021) and Exclusive debit (Nov 2021): **Mastercard only**.
- Prepaid: mostly Mastercard (Superflash, Pensione, university cards). Flash comes in either network.
- Isybank cards: Mastercard ⚠ (from Isybank's own promotion).
- Business: Debit Plus on Visa or Mastercard; Visa Business Solutions on commercial Visa cards ⚠ split unknown.
- Joint history: MasterPass partnership (Jul 2014, led by Stefano Barrese); biometric Mastercard pilot with Gemalto (c.2019 ⚠); Caritas ATM donations (16 Jul 2020, three years; Michele Centemero of Mastercard). Bank of Alexandria × Mastercard (Mar 2024).
- Visa: multi-year strategic partnership renewed on 4 Nov 2024 (Stefano M. Stoppani, Claudia Vassena); my Visa on XME Credit Icon. No Mastercard renewal was found ⚠.
- Nexi: processing since the 2016 Setefi sale (ten-year contract ⚠ renewal), and exclusive acceptance since 2020 (9.9% stake ⚠ current).

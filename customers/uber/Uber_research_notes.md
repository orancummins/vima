## 1. The company
- Uber Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: UBER), San Francisco; ~34,000 staff; 70+ countries, 15,000+ cities. Market value not re-verified ⚠.
- FY2025: Gross Bookings $193.5bn (+19%); revenue $52.0bn; GAAP net income $10.1bn; Adj. EBITDA $8.7bn; FCF $9.8bn; 13.6bn trips; buybacks $6.5bn.
- Q2 2026: GB $58.0bn (+24%) — Mobility $29.0bn, Delivery $27.5bn, Freight $1.6bn; revenue $14.2bn; MAPCs 208m; 3.9bn trips; TTM FCF >$10bn. Non-GAAP operating income replaced Adj. EBITDA from Q1 2026.
- Uber One: 46m members end-2025, 50m in Q1 2026, driving half of Mobility and Delivery GB. Earners: 8.5m+ (Jul 2025). Uber for Business: 300,000+ businesses.
- Payments facts: >75% of 2025 GB paid by card or wallet; cash ~6%. Uber Payments B.V. is an EEA e-money institution; similar licences in the UK and Mexico.
- Leadership: Dara Khosrowshahi (CEO); Balaji Krishnamurthy (CFO from 16 Feb 2026); Jill Hazelbaker (President & Chief Corporate Affairs Officer, May 2026); Karl Hébert (VP Global Commerce); Andrew Macdonald ⚠.
- Deals: Trendyol Go (Jun 2025, ~$700m ⚠); Getir food $335m + 15% of grocery for $100m (Feb 2026); Blacklane ~$550m (Mar 2026); Delivery Hero offer at €41.50/share with a €14.2bn bridge (16 Jul 2026, close H2 2027); €4.5bn notes (Sep 2026); Lime IPO (Jun 2026).

## 2. Uber × Mastercard
- Uber Pro Card: Mastercard business debit, Evolve Bank & Trust + Branch (US, in market by Dec 2022), with Easy Savings +1% at fuel; Canada renewed Jul 2025 (issuer ⚠); UK — Transact Payments + Griffin Bank, announced Jul 2025.
- 24 Jul 2025 agreement: Mastercard Move (payouts), One Credential (Pro Card), Gateway (collections), Offers Platform (cardholders). Sherri Haymond, co-president Global Partnerships. Share of payouts and collections not disclosed ⚠.
- Switch: Uber Money's 2019 debit card was a Green Dot Visa; replaced by the Branch/Evolve Mastercard by 2022 ⚠ exact date.
- Others: Adyen processor since 2012 (local acquiring in Japan, Mexico, NZ, Australia from Feb 2026; kiosks Dec 2025); Amex Platinum/Gold Uber Cash ($200/$120) and Uber One credits; Uber Visa with Barclays (2017, relaunched 2019, discontinued ⚠); Capital One Savor 10% (2022–24 ⚠, Mastercard ⚠); Pix via EBANX (2020); UPI; PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna ⚠.
- No Uber One co-brand found ⚠; no Uber agentic-payments, Click to Pay or commercial-card announcement with Mastercard found.

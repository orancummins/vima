## 1. Saudi National Bank — the group

- Ratings: Fitch A (8 Dec 2025), S&P A (18 Mar 2025), Moody's upgrade Nov 2024.

- Customers ~15m (Q1 2026); 478–481 branches, 21 retail service centres, 93 QuickPay remittance centres; overseas branches Bahrain, UAE, Qatar, Singapore; ~15–16k employees; ATMs 3,088 / POS 306k (Apr 2023).
- Chairman Saeed Al-Ghamdi (since 27 Mar 2023); Vice Chair Yazeed Al-Humied (PIF); CEO Tareq Al-Sadhan (1 May 2024; a member of a Mastercard advisory board, per Forbes Middle East); CFO Hussein Eid; Retail head Saud Bajbair; GTB head Ahmed Abu Khamsin; COO/CTO Saleh Saleh; Shariah Board chair Saad Al-Shithri; NEO Head of Products & Innovation Mohammed Al-Faifi.

- SNB Capital 100%: #1 asset manager AUM SAR 274.5bn / 22.1% share (Q4 2025); #1 broker; 100 IB deals since 2021; market maker for Almarai, Al Rajhi Bank and SABIC from 5 Oct 2026, adding to earlier mandates (ANB, BAAN, Sadr from Aug 2026).

## 2. NEO — the digital bank
- NEO / نيو; "NEO Digital Banking"; app subtitle "Lifestyle Digital Banking"; investor decks "SNB NEO". Brand/channel under SNB's banking licence (not a SAMA-licensed digital bank); CEO said (Sep 2024) NEO plans to apply for a digital-bank licence.
- Operating entity: Digital Ventures and Payments Company (SNB-DV), SNB subsidiary. CEO Dr Saleh Ibrahim Alfuraih (appointed Mar 2024; ex-Cenomi, noon KSA, Bank Albilad, Al Rajhi). Head of Products & Innovation: Mohammed Al-Faifi (ex-Alinma cards). Vice-Chair NEO Ventures Fund (Dec 2025).
- Launch: SAMA approval by 9 Apr 2024; invite beta ~24 Aug 2024; official launch 3 Sep 2024 at Fintech24, Riyadh (SAMA Governor Al-Sayari, Chairman Al-Ghamdi present); public ~31 Oct 2024. App Store record 8 Dec 2023.
- Positioning: "lifestyle digital banking and financial ecosystem"; Shariah-compliant; youth, freelancers (>2M registered), domestic workers, expats (EN/AR/Hindi); Gen-Z via esports (Team Falcons 5-yr sponsorship, Nov 2024).
- Onboarding: account "in under a minute" with instant card; Nafath e-KYC (ID/Iqama); eligibility 15+; Family Banking for 15–18 (v2.0.0, 12 Nov 2025); micro-financing for freelancers planned with the Ministry of Human Resources.
- Products: current account w/ IBAN (IBAN in Apple Wallet); multi-currency account 20 currencies, fixed FX rates; cards: mada debit badged Mastercard — neo.sa calls it 'your NEO Platinum Mastercard' and the fee schedule lists 'Mada Card (Platinum) free, (World) SAR 350'; an Easy card is also listed; NEO SKY prepaid travel card (Mastercard Platinum prepaid, Nov 2025; no issuance/annual/international fees, 10 NEONS/SAR, lounge access via Mastercard Travel Pass); NEO Gamers prepaid card (late Aug 2025, v1.5.3); Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay, mada Pay; local transfers, by phone number, international via QuickPay (free) or MoneyGram (up to SAR 50) per the Feb 2026 fee schedule; corridors not itemised, QR beneficiary; bill & government payments (SADAD); Qattah split/request and "Tone Gattah" (split by sound); emergency cash withdrawal; card controls; expense categorisation; Advance Salary finance (Nov 2025); NEONS loyalty (points per riyal, cashback, partner discounts, gift cards, bookings, store; NEONS exchange, leaderboards, NEO Moments Aug 2026; gamified earning); donations, Zakat calculator and NEO Places (v2.1.0, Jan 2026), Quran reader, wellness; referrals.
- Not found: credit cards, BNPL, investments, AI chatbot. Financing to come via NEO financing subsidiary (initial SAMA approval, ops before end-2026).
- B2B2C: NEO infrastructure powers Nusuk Wallet (Ministry of Hajj & Umrah; embedded open-loop wallet + international card; SDK/API; SAMA trial approval; 3 Jun 2024).
- Scale: 1.0M (2024) → >1.2M (Q3 2025) → 1.4M (Q1 2026) → ~1.9M (Q2 2026, Aug 2026); target >5M by 2027; ambition 10M addressable. Google Play 1M+ downloads, 4.6★; App Store SA 4.6★, 3.8K ratings; iOS v2.3.2 (18 Sep 2026).

- Competitors: STC Bank (>3M, ops Jan 2025), D360 (>2M, ops Dec 2024), Vision Bank (Sep 2025), ezbank (licensed Oct 2025, not live); incumbent brands Bouki (Riyad), Iz (Alinma), Meem (GIB); wallets Barq, Hala.

## 3. Saudi rails, open banking and the Mastercard footprint
- E-payments 85% of retail transactions in 2025 (79% 2024, 36% 2019); 14.6bn electronic txns 2025 (SAMA, 12 Apr 2026, SPA N2558262).
- mada: co-badging with Visa/Mastercard mandatory; 47.7m mada cards; >90% of cards, >95% of txns; 96% POS contactless; phone wallets 50.6% of POS value 2025. POS 2025 SAR 707.2bn, 11.55bn txns, 2.48m terminals (Jul 2026). mada e-commerce ≈SAR 325bn 2025 (Q4 90.9bn); Oct 2025 record SAR 30.7bn.
- sarie instant payments launched 20 Feb 2021, built by IBM with Mastercard's real-time payments technology (ISO 20022); SAR 20,000 cap; alias quick transfer SAR 2,500; ~784m txns 2025 (+32%); Aug 2026 93.7m txns / SAR 96.9bn. Open Banking PIS runs on sarie.
- SADAD (2004): 364m bills / SAR 817bn 2024.
- Wallets: Apple Pay 19 Feb 2019 (NCB a first Mastercard issuer via MDES); Samsung Pay 2024; Google Pay 15 Sep 2025 via mada; Mastercard on Google Pay 18 Feb 2026; Alipay+ due 2026; STC Pay → STC Bank Jan 2025.
- Gateways: Mastercard Gateway SAMA-certified for new e-commerce interface 30 Nov 2025 (on-soil since Oct 2024; >1bn KSA txns 2024); Visa Acceptance Platform local Sep 2025; PSPs Geidea, Moyasar, HyperPay, PayTabs, Checkout.com.
- Open banking: Framework AIS Nov 2022; Lab Jan 2023; PIS release Sep 2024; 26 Mar 2026 formal licensing framework, sandbox closed; Lean first licensee (>1m accounts connected, >1bn txns analysed; clients Tabby, Tamara); Drahim (Al Rajhi) licensed Mar 2026; Tarabut Riyadh RHQ Sep 2025, SNB a signed partner, >5bn API calls, $50m round Sep 2026. SNB SME POS lending with Tarabut & Geidea (22 Sep 2025, subject to SAMA approval); SNB Capital led Malaa SAR 65m Series A (Sep 2024). Mastercard–SingleView agreement (7 May 2024) for data-powered corporate solutions.
- FSDP: fintechs 525 by 2030; 371 at end-Aug 2026; SME share of lending 11% (2025) → 20% (2030); cashless 80% aim. Digital banks: D360 >2m, STC Bank >3m, Vision Bank Sep 2025, EZ Bank licensed. barq >10m users. BNPL: Tabby licensed Oct/Nov 2025, Tamara earlier, Daftar Jul 2026.
- Mastercard KSA: Saud Swar, Country Manager KSA, Bahrain, Jordan & Levant (May 2025); Adam Jones EVP West Arabia; Cyber Resilience Center Riyadh 21 May 2025 (Riyad Bank first partner).
- SNB/NCB–Mastercard timeline: 19 Feb 2019 Apple Pay (MDES); 10 Oct 2019 NCB–Mastercard exclusive long-term partnership (debit, credit, prepaid, commercial); 24 Feb 2021 Corporate World card (Priceless, LoungeKey); 22 Jan 2023 SNB–Mastercard–Saudi Esports Federation; 6 Oct 2023 UEFA CL credit (cashback) + prepaid (LAK points); 17 Apr 2024 Flexi (Mastercard Installments debut); 19 Aug 2026 World Legend Mastercard, first in MENA (Private Banking, Mastercard Collection); 15 Sep 2026 Gateway / Merchant Solutions expansion (GTB head Ahmed Abu Khamsin).

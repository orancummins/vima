## 1. The company
- Bank of Valletta p.l.c. (MSE: BOV), Valletta; founded 1974, listed December 1992. Malta's largest bank, larger than HSBC Malta, APS and Lombard combined (Business Now, Sep 2026). Government of Malta about 25%; UniCredit about 10% ⚠ dated profile; Slingshot Global SICAV 5.04% (Aug 2026). Market value about €1.35bn in early 2026. Fitch BBB (Mar 2025), S&P BBB.
- H1 2026: profit before tax €119.8m (−11.3%), record operating income €251.3m, NII €207.0m, fees €39.1m (cards €8.2m, payments €4.4m), cost:income 51.4%, total assets €17.6bn, deposits €14.5bn, net loans €8.6bn, CET1 19.32%, NPE 1.49%. FY2025: PBT €260.4m, pre-tax RoAE 17.9%. 2026 guidance €215–250m PBT.
- Segments: Retail Banking; Wealth Management; Business Banking; Treasury; Others. Associates Mapfre MSV Life and Mapfre Malta; fund management and fund services subsidiaries ⚠ names.
- Leadership: Dr Gordon Cordina (Chair), Kenneth Farrugia (CEO), Kevin Cardona (CFO, 2024). Strategy and data, customer experience and people chiefs leaving in 2026; the proposed CRO was withdrawn on 9 Oct 2026 ⚠ interim.
- Strategy: 2024–26 cycle ends; 2027–29 strategy due for Board approval late 2026. Omnichannel Banking Platform rolling out, CRM programme, phased AI, DORA. €300m senior preferred notes (May 2026); second buyback from 19 Oct 2026.
- Employees about 1,690 ⚠ third-party estimate; about 28 branches and 5 investment centres ⚠ undated; no customer count found ⚠.

## 2. BOV × Mastercard
- BOV's only Mastercard product, a credit card held by "thousands", closed on 1 Dec 2021; holders moved to Visa Classic. BOV called it "purely a business and strategic" decision (Business Now; The Shift, Oct 2021).
- Since then all cards are Visa: Cashlink Visa debit, Visa Classic and Gold credit (Platinum, Skypass ⚠), and the June 2025 corporate suite (Business Debit, Corporate Credit, Corporate Premium).
- Wallets: Apple Pay (by Apr 2021) and BOV Pay (Android, 2019) carry Visa tokens; Google Pay ⚠ unconfirmed.
- Mastercard's live touchpoint: acceptance through BOV SmartPay (ePOS, e-commerce gateway, MobilePOS) ⚠ processor not named. Mastercard prepaid gift cards in 2011 ⚠.
- BOV holds shares in "a global payments technology company", most likely Visa ⚠.

## 3. Market and where Mastercard could land
- Rival BNF Bank signed a Mastercard partnership on 25 Feb 2026 (Panagiotis Polydoros, Mastercard Greece, Cyprus and Malta). HSBC Malta is being sold to CrediaBank (close about Q2 2027). SEPA Instant mandatory since Oct 2025; the central bank is studying a national mobile-number look-up.
- Openings: scam scoring beside Verification of Payee for instant payments; enrichment and open finance for the CRM and omnichannel programmes; merchant risk and offers for SmartPay; carbon data for the CSRD agenda; a second-network premium or commercial card at the 2027–29 strategy.

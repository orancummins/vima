# OTP Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `OTP_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and OTP × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (first-half 2026 report, 5 Aug 2026; 2Q 2026 investor presentation; 2025 results, 6 Mar 2026) unless marked. Card networks come from OTP's own card pages; no split by network is published. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are due in November 2026 ⚠.

## 1. The company
- OTP Bank Nyrt. (BSE: OTP), Budapest; listed since 1995, no strategic investor; market value €36.2bn (30 Jun 2026). Ratings BBB (S&P), Baa1 deposits (Moody's), BBB+ (Scope).
- c.17.5m active customers in 11 countries; almost 4m in Hungary, over half digitally active; 1,177 branches, 5,619 ATMs, 287,188 POS terminals, 41,089 staff.
- H1 2026: profit after tax HUF 482.7bn (HUF 580.3bn with special taxes spread evenly), NIM 4.61%, CET1 17.6%, assets HUF 46.7tn. FY2025: profit HUF 1,146bn, ROE 21.6%.
- Hungary: c.30% of assets, 35% of retail loans, c.40% of retail deposits, 22% of corporate loans, 31% of asset management.
- Segments: OTP Core (Hungary); other Hungarian subsidiaries (Merkantil, OTP Fund Management, OTP Mobil); foreign subsidiaries (DSK Bulgaria, OTP banka Slovenia, OBH Croatia, Serbia, Ipoteka Uzbekistan, Ukraine, CKB Montenegro, Albania, Moldova, Russia).
- Leadership: Dr Sándor Csányi (Chairman), Péter Csányi (CEO since 1 May 2025); Deputy CEOs László Wolf, András Becsei, László Bencsik, György Kiss-Haypál, András Sebők.
- Deals: Nova KBM and Ipoteka (2023), Romania sold (Jul 2024), Luminor agreed 20 Jul 2026 (below book, awaiting approvals). AI: 80–90% of consumer-loan decisions automated; own deep-learning fraud model.

## 2. OTP × Mastercard
- Hungary debit: Mastercard Online, Standard, OKÉ, Gold, Prémium, EUR, webKÁRTYA; Visa Online and Visa Classic also sold. Private banking: Mastercard World Elite debit; Visa Infinite credit.
- Credit: OTP Bonus and BonusGold on Mastercard ⚠; Telekom and Otthon cards' network not stated ⚠.
- Wallets: Apple Pay first in Hungary (May 2019); Google Pay, Garmin Pay, Xiaomi Pay (Sep 2023), both networks.
- History: NFC alliance with Mastercard (2011); OTPay, Europe's first bank wallet with Masterpass (Mar 2014).
- Mastercard Bank of the Year in Hungary: grand prize for 2020 and 2025 (Feb 2026; first in cybersecurity).
- Bulgaria: DSK Bank replacing all Mastercards with Visa under a strategic partnership ⚠ start date.
- Uzbekistan: Humo, Uzcard, Humo-Visa, Humo-Mastercard, Mastercard and Visa Gold. Ukraine, Moldova, Serbia: Mastercard awards and campaigns ⚠ dates.
- Competition: qvik QR/NFC instant payments (Sep 2024), AFR instant payments (225m in 2024), foreign fintech cards (2.1m), SimplePay vs Barion ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Scam and identity scoring beside OTP's own model; card-linked offers for the c.100-merchant cashback programme; acquirer risk and merchant data for 150,000 terminals and SimplePay; Agent Pay for OTP's AI; commercial cards; open finance as qvik grows; Luminor's card portfolio after closing.

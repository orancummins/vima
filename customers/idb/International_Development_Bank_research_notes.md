## 1. The company
- International Development Bank for Investment and Finance, an Iraqi private commercial bank founded 2011 (capital IQD 100bn, now IQD 250bn paid up); listed on the Iraq Stock Exchange 2018 (ticker not confirmed ⚠).
- FY2024 ⚠ (IR page via search extract): total assets c.USD 2.38bn (2023: 1.82bn), net profit c.USD 45.8m (2023: 31.3m), equity c.USD 350.9m. Claims to be Iraq's largest private bank by assets and deposits ⚠.
- Network (May 2024): 27 branches (Erbil the 27th), c.400 ATMs, c.5,000 POS, c.7,000 "POC" outlets, correspondents in 20+ countries. Dubai branch since 9 Nov 2022 (first Iraqi private bank licensed by the UAE Central Bank). Beirut representative office ⚠. 600+ staff (c.2021 ⚠).
- Leadership: Chairman Dr Ziad (Zead) Khalaf Abdul Karim (co-founder, chair since 2015; ZK Holding). CEO Fadi Al-Faqih (2025 ⚠). MD Saad Faik (May 2024 ⚠).
- Segments: retail, corporate, treasury and investment banking (custody), e-IDB. Partners: IFC MoU (May 2019), AD Ports (2022 ⚠), Aramex JV (2024 ⚠), Arab Tourism Organization (Sep 2023).
- US-dollar bar: reported 8 Sep 2025 when the KRG removed IDB from MyAccount; barred banks cannot open dollar accounts or send dollar transfers and saw international card use restricted. Date of the bar and current status unknown ⚠. IDB not on the named Feb 2024 or Feb 2025 lists.

## 2. IDB × Mastercard
- Says it was the first Iraqi bank with issuing and acquiring licences from both Mastercard and Visa (from 2016 ⚠).
- Cards (IDB cards page ⚠): Visa Classic debit, Visa Platinum debit (lounges, purchase protection), Mastercard Gold credit (IQD 6–12m / USD 4,000–8,000).
- Prepaid: 2023 ATO deal described a Visa prepaid travel card; the Arab Tourist Card (May 2025) names Visa and Mastercard ⚠ sources differ.
- Promotions: "IDB x Visa – AFC Elite" (Apr 2025) and Paris 2024 Olympics, titles only ⚠.
- No public tokenisation, Click to Pay, fraud tools, World cards or Mastercard executive tie. Mastercard's Iraqi deals are with the CBI: MoU 7 Aug 2018, partnership 19 Apr 2021 (Khalid Elgibali).

## 3. Market
- Cash 90–95% of transactions (Mar 2026). CBI building a national switch, instant payments, a government gateway and a national card scheme (Aug 2025; go-live unknown ⚠). State bodies cash-free by Jul 2026 ⚠. Apple Pay targeted Q1 2026 ⚠.
- Qi Card (International Smart Card, with Rafidain Bank): 11m cardholders (Aug 2025), 16m people and 50,000+ merchants (Oct 2025); Mastercard, Visa, UnionPay; Turkish Airlines World Mastercard debit (Aug 2025). Visa: KRG MyAccount partner (Jan 2025).

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Mastercard debit for salary customers; premium credit; tokenisation ahead of Apple Pay; acquirer-risk tools; fraud and identity; tourism offers on the Arab Tourist Card; compliance advisory around the dollar bar.

## 1. The company
- Nova Ljubljanska banka d.d., Ljubljana; listed in Ljubljana with London GDRs since Nov 2018; Republic of Slovenia 25% + 1 share. Market value about EUR 3.65bn (end-2025). Moody's A2 (Mar 2026), S&P BBB+ (positive).
- H1 2026: profit after tax EUR 252.4m (Q2 EUR 133.1m), assets EUR 31.85bn, gross loans EUR 20.06bn (+12% YoY), deposits EUR 25.08bn, CET1 14.8%, ROTE 14.5%, CIR 46.5%. FY2025 profit EUR 503.1m.
- 7 banks, 371 branches, 8,064 employees, 2.9m active clients (Slovenia 738k; Serbia 1.0m; Skopje 470k; Banja Luka 220k; Sarajevo 138k; Prishtina 246k; Podgorica 99k). SEE banks gave 57% of the H1 result.
- Segments: retail banking, CIB and financial markets in Slovenia; strategic foreign markets; other and non-core. NLB Skladi 43.3% of Slovenian fund assets; NLB Lease&Go 37% of leasing; 46% of Bankart.
- Leadership: Blaž Brodnjak (CEO), Archibald Kremser (CFO), Andreas Burkhardt (CRO), Reinhard Höll (CTO), Hedvika Usenik, Andrej Lasič, Antonio Argir; Primož Karpe chairs the Supervisory Board.
- Deals: Komercijalna banka (Dec 2020, EUR 394.7m), N Banka (2022), Summit Leasing and Mobil Leasing (2024). Addiko bid (May 2026, EUR 29 a share, later raised ⚠) failed on 3 Aug 2026 with 31.22% tendered; Raiffeisen took control. **Addiko is not part of NLB.** Agreement for >55% of Deželna banka Slovenije (18 Sep 2026; 65 branches, EUR 1.6bn assets; closing H2 2027).
- Strategy 2030 (May 2024, updated May 2026): EUR 45bn assets organic (EUR 50bn+ with M&A), revenue >EUR 1.8bn, net income >EUR 0.85bn, CIR low 40s, payout towards 60%, digital penetration >80%, payments revenue EUR 236.5m → >EUR 320m, NLB Pay wallet share 25.9% → >40%.

## 2. NLB × Mastercard
- Slovenia personal debit: all Mastercard (standard, World, World Elite) after Maestro was discontinued from April 2025; digital-first debit card into NLB Pay (late 2025).
- Slovenia credit: 3v1 revolving Mastercard (standard, World, World Elite), Zlati Mastercard, NLB Mastercard; plus NLB Visa and Premium Visa. Concierge, Priority Pass, LoungeKey (2025).
- Business debit migrated to Visa in June 2025 ⚠ (help pages still name a business debit Mastercard); prepaid CeKR card is Visa.
- Serbia: DinaCard, Visa and Mastercard. Kosovo: Visa and Mastercard ⚠. Skopje, Bosnia, Podgorica: not confirmed ⚠.
- Wallets: NLB Pay (cards + Flik), Apple Pay (Slovenia Feb 2024; every NLB bank by Jul 2025), Google Pay, Garmin Pay. Tokenised share of card transactions 2.9% (2023) → 22.2% (2025) → 28.9% (H1 2026).
- Flik (Bankart, bank-owned) P2P and at every POS; SEPA Instant via TIPS; SEPA reaching Montenegro and North Macedonia.
- No joint initiative or network agreement with Mastercard found in public ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Token services and Click to Pay for NLB Pay; instant issuance across seven banks; acceptance and onboarding for Smart POS; card-linked offers for the loyalty model under design; fraud scoring and identity; commercial and virtual cards to win back business spend from Visa; converting DBS customers after 2027.

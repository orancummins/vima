# Banco República (BROU): research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Banco_Republica_BROU_Platform.html`. This is one research thread, covering the company and BROU × Mastercard, condensed.

Figures are BROU's own unless marked: the Financial Synthesis for June 2026 and December 2025, the 2024 Annual Report (English) and the card pages on brou.com.uy. Amounts are in US dollars. Most sources are in Spanish and were translated. Mastercard's and Visa's newsrooms could not be reached, and the web-search budget ran out part-way through, so network deals rest on BROU's own pages. ⚠ marks items that rest on one source or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- **Ownership and history.** Banco de la República Oriental del Uruguay, founded 1896 and 100% state-owned. It is a commercial and development bank ("Banco País"), supervised by the BCU, with 130 years marked in 2026. Moody's rates its deposits Baa1/P-2 and raised the BCA to baa1 on 23 April 2026.
- **Scale (H1 2026).**
  - Assets USD 25.9bn, 41% of the system; deposits USD 22.2bn, a 45% share; gross loans USD 8.9bn, a 32% share.
  - Net income USD 375m, ROE 22.4%.
  - 2025: net income USD 475m, down from USD 774m in 2024, which had an FX gain; ROE 16.0%.
- **Customers and staff.** 2.14m deposit customers, 1.61m e-banking customers, 1.78m debit cards, 3,429 staff, 129 branches plus 2 abroad (New York among them).
- **Group.**
  - República AFISA, trusts (100%);
  - República Microfinanzas (100%);
  - República AFAP, pensions (51%);
  - Sistarbanc, card processing for Visa and Mastercard (about 64%);
  - tuapp trust (50%).
- **Leadership.**
  - Board: Álvaro García (President since March 2025), Adriana Rodríguez, Leandro Francolino, Ignacio Fessler, Elbio Fernández.
  - Management: Mariela Espino (General Manager), Diego Neira (Digital Banking), Gustavo Galli (Personas), Juan Llosa (cards and digital business product manager).
- **Strategy.**
  - credit growth;
  - the card "repositioning" through BROU Recompensa and the national-team sponsorship;
  - replacing Maestro;
  - instant payments and wallets;
  - SMEs.
  - The Strategic Plan is not public ⚠. No generative-AI programme was found ⚠.

## 2. BROU × Mastercard
- **Agreement.** December 2019 renewal of a long alliance, covering credit, debit and digital, under President Jorge Polgar. Its first product was BROU Recompensa Mastercard credit (2020). Its term is not public ⚠.
- **Debit.**
  - Debit Mastercard was launched publicly in 2024.
  - By December 2024 the Maestro migration had passed 50% of customers, and Debit Mastercard was 60% of debit purchases.
  - RedBROU Maestro is switched off for residents on 1 November 2026.
  - Foreign e-commerce on Debit Mastercard is limited to seven whitelisted sites.
- **Other Mastercard programmes.**
  - Mastercard credit tiers up to Black (LoungeKey);
  - Recompensa debit;
  - BROU Pyme Mastercard and Pyme Platinum (10 June 2026), which BROU calls Uruguay's first with SME-only benefits;
  - corporate debit;
  - Identity Check (2024).
- **Visa.**
  - Visa Débito and MI BROU youth debit;
  - Visa credit up to Infinite, now inside Recompensa ⚠ launch date;
  - Platinum with ANCAPuntos and Priority Pass (August 2025);
  - Agro Visa;
  - corporate credit on Visa or Mastercard.
- **Shared and not Mastercard.**
  - Google Pay and the BROU a Mano wallet (credit and prepaid tokens on both networks; no debit);
  - Apple Pay built by Sistarbanc ⚠ live status;
  - SPI instant transfers, mobile aliases and Toke QR;
  - tuapp.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- **Debit and wallets.** Finish the debit migration well: debit tokens in BROU a Mano, Apple Pay, Click to Pay, and wider foreign e-commerce with risk scoring.
- **Fraud.** Scam and fraud scoring as instant transfers boom: 34m in H2 2025, 88% of retail payment value.
- **Open finance.** Open-finance and A2A tools under the BCU's 2026–2030 roadmap.
- **Recompensa.** Card-linked offers.
- **SMEs.** Tap on Phone and commercial cards around Paquete Pyme.
- **Cross-border.** Cross-border flows via the New York branch.

## 1. JPMorgan Chase — the company
- JPMorgan Chase & Co., 270 Park Avenue, New York; NYSE: JPM. 322,964 employees. Market value $878.6bn (30 Sep 2026). Parent A1/A/AA-.
- 2025: managed revenue $185.6bn, net income $57.0bn, ROTCE 20%. 3Q26: revenue $50.9bn, net income $15.3bn, EPS $5.57, CET1 13.9%, assets $5.09tn, deposits $2.78tn, loans $1.58tn. Card sales (debit + credit) $1.94tn in 2025.
- Segments: CCB ($76.0bn revenue 2025; 94m customers; 5,164 branches; card loans $252.7bn), CIB ($78.5bn; payments $19.3bn; #1 IB fees and markets; $44.1tn AUC), AWM ($24.1bn; $5.21tn AUM).
- Leadership: Jamie Dimon (Chair and CEO); co-presidents Doug Petno (CIB) and Troy Rohrbaugh (CCB) from 25 Jun 2026; Marianne Lake retiring; Mary Erdoes, Jennifer Piepszak, Jeremy Barnum; Allison Beer (Card & Connected Commerce); Naveen Mallela (Kinexys).
- Strategy: ROTCE 17% through the cycle; Security & Resiliency Initiative ($1.5tn, ten years); 160+ new branches in 2026; Chase Germany (20 May 2026); $19.8bn tech budget; LLM Suite.
- Kinexys (>$7bn a day) and JPMD (live on Base 12 Nov 2025).

## 2. JPMorgan Chase × Mastercard
- Chase's catalogue: 35 Visa cards, 8 Mastercard (Freedom Flex since 31 Aug 2020, IHG ×4 incl. business, Aeroplan, DoorDash, Instacart). Debit Visa ⚠.
- Visa: letter of intent for a ten-year renewable partnership and ChaseNet (26 Feb 2013); no public 2020–21 network-wide renewal found; United extended to 2029 (Feb 2020).
- Apple Card: Chase to replace Goldman Sachs (7 Jan 2026); $20bn+ balances; ~24 months; Mastercard stays the network; $2.2bn 4Q25 reserve (Allison Beer, Linda Kirkpatrick, Jennifer Bailey).
- Commercial cards: J.P. Morgan is the largest US commercial issuer on both networks; split not disclosed.
- Digital assets: Mastercard Multi-Token Network linked with Kinexys (Nov 2024); Mastercard in JPMD proof of concept and planning to add JPMD to its token network (Nov 2025; Raj Dhamodharan).
- Not found: Agent Pay with Chase or J.P. Morgan Payments (Mirakl agentic deal, Mar 2026, is network-agnostic); a Finicity data-access agreement (Chase charges aggregators since 2025; Plaid signed Sep 2025).

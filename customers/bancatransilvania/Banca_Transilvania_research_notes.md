## 1. The company
- Banca Transilvania S.A. (BVB: TLV), Cluj-Napoca, founded 1993. No controlling shareholder: NN Group 9.29%, EBRD 5.16%, Romanian companies 44.4%. Fitch BBB (May 2026), one notch above the sovereign. Market cap about RON 30–36bn ⚠.
- Romania's largest bank: 21.98% of system assets in 2025 ⚠ (BCR 13.81%, CEC 11.24%).
- H1 2026: group net profit RON 2.5bn (+26.8%), group assets RON 233.3bn, bank deposits RON 172.4bn, gross loans RON 113.7bn, ROE 21.98%, CAR 21.96%, NPL 2.41%. FY2025: group profit RON 4.66bn, ROE 25.23%.
- Nearly 5m active customers (end-2025); 250,000+ new in H1 2026; about 600,000 companies. 13,565 group staff; 42 branches and 463 agencies.
- Segments: Large Corporate, Mid Corporate, SME, Micro, Retail, Treasury, Leasing & NBFI.
- Group: Salt Bank (digital, Mastercard), Victoriabank (Moldova, 44.63%), BT Leasing, BT Direct, BT Mic, BT Asset Management, BT Capital Partners, BT Pensii (ex-BRD Pensii), Microinvest.
- Deals: OTP Bank Romania (€347.5m, merged 28 Feb 2025); BRD Pensii and Microinvest (2025); Idea::Bank (2021) ⚠.
- Leadership: Horia Ciorcilă (Chairman), Ömer Tetik (CEO, renewed to 2030), deputy CEOs George Călinescu (Finance), Oana Ilaș (Retail), Leontin Toderici (Operations), Cosmin Călin (Large Corporate), Andrzej Dominiak (Technology).
- Strategy: one ecosystem of banking, investments, microfinance and pensions; diaspora growth through BT Pay; no published multi-year targets found ⚠.

## 2. BT × Mastercard
- Dual-scheme issuer, 8m+ cards (70% in BT Pay; 5.7m unique cards enrolled). Split not published ⚠.
- Mastercard: Star Forte credit, Mondo, Direct, Omnipass, Mastercard Business, Salt Bank debit, McLaren F1 Team Debit and Debit Gold (Jul 2025; about 80,000; physical euro card Mar 2026; partnership extended for 2026). Named: Oana Ilaș (BT), Cosmin Vladimirescu (Mastercard Romania and Croatia).
- Visa: BT Visa UNTOLD (200,000+; animated 2023, OLED 2025), BT Visa Flying Blue (35,000), EU Digital Identity Wallet payments (Jul 2025) and WE BUILD assembly in Bucharest (3–4 Nov 2026).
- 2015 Western Union–Mastercard ATM remittances ⚠ status. Scheme income RON 41.8m in H1 2026; BT holds Visa and Mastercard equity (RON 417m).
- Domestic rival rail: RoPay in BT Pay (Oct 2025; at POS and checkout from Jan 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay and tokens behind Chat BT and BT Pay (4.6m users); identity against Visa's EU wallet lead; scam tools as RoPay grows; card-linked offers for STAR (40,000 merchants); commercial cards for 600,000 firms; cross-border for the diaspora; open finance (no BT product found).

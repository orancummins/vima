## 1. The company
- ANZ Group Holdings Limited (ASX: ANZ), Melbourne; main bank ANZBGL. History from 1828. 29 markets, more than 11m customers. Market value A$108.4bn (31 Mar 2026). AA band at all three agencies.
- 1H26: cash profit A$3,780m (+14% ex-one-offs), statutory A$3,650m, cash RoTE 11.6%, CTI 49.4%, CET1 12.39%; assets A$1,314bn, loans A$822bn, deposits A$771bn; dividend 83c. 3Q26 cash A$1.90bn, CET1 12.51%, NIM 1.54%, NZ$125m class-action provision. FY25 cash ex-one-offs A$6,896m; one-offs A$1,109m.
- Divisions: Australia Retail (6.5m customers, A$348bn home loans, 13.2% share ex-Suncorp); Business & Private Bank (580k); Institutional (No.1 relationship bank in AU and NZ); New Zealand (2.69m; MFI 33.3%); Suncorp Bank (1.26m; A$74bn loans); Pacific & Group Centre.
- Leadership: Paul O'Sullivan (Chair), Nuno Matos (CEO), Farhan Faruqui (CFO), Christine Palmer (CRO), Pedro Rodeia (Retail), Tammy Medard (B&PB), Mark Whelan (Institutional), Ben Kelleher (ANZ NZ CEO from 1 Oct 2026), Bruce Rush (Suncorp Bank).
- ANZ 2030 (13 Oct 2025): RoTE 12% FY28 and 13% FY30 ⚠ (secondary), CTI mid-40s by FY28, 3,500 roles cut (84% by Jun 2026), Suncorp onto ANZ by Jun 2027, ANZ Plus as single front end for 8m customers by Sep 2027.
- Deals: Suncorp Bank (31 Jul 2024; A$500m synergies by FY29); ANZ Worldline 51% bought (EV A$89m), completed 31 Jul 2026.
- Tech/AI: Agentforce CRM (Feb 2026); Falcon fraud; tokenised deposits on Swift's ledger with BHP and Citi (Sep 2026).

## 2. ANZ × Mastercard
- Consumer debit: ANZ Access and ANZ Plus Visa Debit; savings/cheque on eftpos ⚠. Visa Debit exclusive deal from 2010.
- Consumer credit: seven cards, all Visa; Amex dropped 2017; points caps and fee rises from 28 Oct 2026, insurance cuts Dec 2026 and Mar 2027.
- Business cards Visa ⚠ (ANZ page names no network); Australian corporate/purchasing network unconfirmed ⚠.
- Suncorp Bank: Visa Debit; Clear Options credit cards (NAB-issued) moved to NAB's MyCard on 24 Nov 2025, network ⚠.
- ANZ NZ: all cards Visa; Mastercard credit cards dropped April 2019; ANZ Loop on Visa Offers Exchange (30 Apr 2026).
- No ANZ–Mastercard initiative found 2020–2026 ⚠. Mastercard reaches ANZ only through ANZ Worldline acquiring (all networks).

## 3. Market context
- RBA Conclusions Paper (31 Mar 2026): surcharging removed on eftpos, Mastercard and Visa from 1 Oct 2026; debit cap 8c/0.16%; consumer credit cap 0.3% (from 0.8%); commercial stays 0.8%; foreign cards 1.0% from 1 Apr 2027; wallets, BNPL and three-party schemes next.
- AP+ (eftpos, NPP, BPAY): zero-surcharge limit for eftpos from 1 Oct 2026; least-cost routing cuts debit acceptance costs nearly 20%. PayTo and PayID on ANZ channels ⚠.
- Scams: ANZ customer losses down 24% (Oct 2025–Jun 2026); >A$100m prevented and recovered; 476,000 payments abandoned after Confirmation of Payee warnings.

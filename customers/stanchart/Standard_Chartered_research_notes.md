## 1. The company
- Standard Chartered PLC, London HQ, listed in London and Hong Kong; 55 markets across Asia, Africa and the Middle East; about 170 years of history (1853/1862, merged 1969 ⚠). Market value about $61bn ⚠ (third party). SCB rated AA- (Fitch), A+ (S&P), A1 (Moody's). Employee count not found ⚠.
- H1 2026: income $11.6bn (+6%), record PBT $4.8bn, RoTE 17.6%, CET1 14.2%, total assets $993.4bn, loans $299.3bn, customer accounts $552.6bn. CIB $6.9bn; WRB $4.9bn; Wealth Solutions $2.1bn (+39%); affluent NNM $33bn. FY 2025: income $20.9bn, underlying RoTE 14.7%.
- Segments from 1 Jan 2026: CIB; WRB (now including Mox and Trust); Central & other items (now including SC Ventures).
- Leadership: Maria Ramos (Chair), Bill Winters (CEO), Manus Costello (CFO, confirmed Jul 2026), Tanuj Kapilashrami (COO), Roberto Hoornweg (CIB), Judy Hsu (WRB), Noelle Eder (technology), Alex Manson (SC Ventures).
- Targets: RoTE >15% 2028, ~18% 2030; 5–7% income CAGR; $200bn NNM by 2028.
- Retail exits: Zimbabwe, Angola, Sierra Leone, Cameroon, Gambia; Tanzania retail to Access Bank (Jun 2025); Uganda retail to Absa (Oct 2025); Botswana and Zambia retail being sold ⚠ buyers.
- Digital banks: Mox (HK, 2020, ~750k customers, takes part of SC HK retail from 2026); Trust (SG, 2022, >1m customers, with FairPrice).
- Digital assets: custody (HK, SG, Luxembourg, DIFC), BTC/ETH trading (UK 2025, UAE 2026, UAE affluent Oct 2026), tokenised deposits (Dec 2025; HSBC on Swift Aug 2026), Anchorpoint HKDAP (licence Apr 2026; distributor Aug 2026), USDC with Circle (Jul 2026), Zodia Custody deal (May 2026), RLUSD custody (Oct 2026).

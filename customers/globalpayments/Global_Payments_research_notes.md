## 1. Global Payments — the company
- Global Payments Inc., Atlanta; NYSE: GPN. Market value $22.1bn (16 Oct 2026). About 26,000 employees at end-2025 (pre-Worldpay ⚠).
- Worldpay acquired and Issuer Solutions (TSYS) sold to FIS, closed 9 Jan 2026 (announced 12 Jan). Net Worldpay price $22.7bn; Issuer Solutions $13.5bn (now FIS Total Issuing Solutions). Combined: 6m+ locations, ~$3.7tn volume, ~94bn transactions, 175+ countries.
- Segments from Q2 2026: Enterprise ($838m adjusted revenue in Q2), Platforms ($628m), SMB ($1,513m), Other ($181m). Q2 adjusted net revenue $3.16bn (~+4% normalised), adjusted EPS $3.46. 2026 outlook cut on 5 Aug to 4–5% and $13.60–13.80 (Middle East travel). Leverage 3.5x at close, target 3.0x.
- Leadership: Cameron Bready (CEO), Bob Cortopassi (President and COO), Josh Whipple (CFO), Cindy Turner (CPO), David Rumph (SMB), Matt Downs (Integrated & Platforms), Troy Woods (Chair).
- Divestitures: Netspend consumer and gaming (2023), AdvancedMD (2024), payroll to Acrisure (2025), Issuer Solutions (2026).
- Products: Worldpay enterprise acquiring; Heartland/EVO SMB acquiring; online gateway and payment methods; payouts (Push-to-Card, BVNK); Link2Gov (IRS preferred partner 2026); Ravelin; Vault, credential management, Revenue Boost, routing; Disputes Defender; Genius POS (2025; AI handheld May 2026; CKE and Subway); Global Payments for Platforms (Payrix); Worldpay MCP (Nov 2025) and Google UCP (Jan 2026).

## 2. Global Payments × Mastercard
- Mastercard and Visa appear in filings only as risk factors (network rules, registration, sponsorship).
- Worldpay Push-to-Card on Mastercard Move in the UAE (18–19 Aug 2025; Mete Güney, Tausif Ahmed); Worldpay listed as a Move partner.
- Thrive Street 2025 with Mastercard and Mary Portas (UK SMB; Chris Wood, Simon Forbes).
- Verifiable Intent (Mar 2026): Worldpay participation ⚠ unverified. No Agent Pay announcement; Agent Pay for Machines (Jun 2026) did not name Worldpay. Both on the Solana Developer Platform (⚠ Mar 2026).
- Visa: Click to Pay and Visa Token Service with Worldpay (10 Dec 2020); Trusted Agent Protocol with Visa and Cloudflare (14 Oct 2025; Cindy Turner); stablecoin settlement pilot (2023).
- TSYS issuer processing of Mastercard portfolios now sits with FIS.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land

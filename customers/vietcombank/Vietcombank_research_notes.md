# Vietcombank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Vietcombank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Vietcombank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (H1 2026 results, reported 31 Jul 2026; 2025 results at the AGM, 24 Apr 2026; 2024 annual report) or from Vietcombank's card pages, NAPAS, the State Bank of Vietnam and Vietnamese press, translated. Vietcombank publishes little in English. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are not yet out.

## 1. The company
- Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (HOSE: VCB), Hanoi. Founded 1 Apr 1963; equitised 2008; listed 30 Jun 2009. State (SBV) 74.8%, Mizuho 15.0% (end-2024). Market value about USD 18bn at end-2025. Ratings BB+ / Ba2 (positive) / BB ⚠ agencies not labelled.
- H1 2026: pre-tax profit VND 29,222bn (+33.5%; Q2 VND 17,420bn, a record); assets about VND 2.66 quadrillion; loans over VND 1.76 quadrillion; deposits nearly VND 1.73 quadrillion; NPL 0.61%; Q2 net fee income flat at VND 860bn.
- FY2025: pre-tax profit VND 44,020bn (+4%), the system's highest; assets over VND 2.4 quadrillion (+17%); charter capital VND 83,557bn. 2026 plan: credit about +10% (13% cap), profit about +5% ⚠.
- Network: 131 branches, 536 offices, 3,090 ATMs, 121,035 acceptance points, about 24,000 staff, 1,194 correspondents.
- Subsidiaries: VCBS, VCB Leasing, VCBR and VCB Money (remittances), Vinafico HK, Vietcombank Laos (licensed Aug 2024), VCBNeo (former Construction Bank, mandatory transfer Oct 2024, renamed Jan 2025). Holds 1.83% of NAPAS. VIFC bank approved Apr 2026.
- Leadership: Nguyễn Thanh Tùng (Chairman), Lê Quang Vinh (CEO from 7 Mar 2025), Shojiro Mizoguchi (Deputy CEO, Mizuho), Đoàn Hồng Nhung (Retail Director).
- Strategy to 2030: top 200 financial institutions, lead digital transformation, more fee income, data platform; VCB Corporate DigiBank with Virtusa (Aug 2026), AI-native.

## 2. Vietcombank × Mastercard
- Live cards: Mastercard credit and World Mastercard (Mastercard); Mastercard Debit (⚠ no apply button). About 13 Visa, 4 Amex, 2 JCB and 3 NAPAS-only products alongside. UnionPay discontinued.
- Visa first everywhere new: Google Wallet (Nov 2022), Apple Pay (Aug 2023), Click to Pay on Visa Token Service (31 Mar 2026), DigiCard, Snack, Vibe, MeGO. Eight Visa awards in Oct 2025.
- Mastercard Customer Forum 2025 (Dec 2025): Debit Growth Trailblazer, Leadership in Merchant Acceptance Volume, Leading Acquirer for Global Travelers. 2015: Mastercard debit issuance and payment volume awards.
- Offers: Mastercard abroad (Jun 2025), Traveloka x Mastercard. No public network agreement, tokenisation, Send/Move, fraud or commercial-card work with Mastercard ⚠.
- Domestic competition: NAPAS Connect24 on every account; NAPAS >32m transactions a day in 2025; VietQR value +85% in ten months; 90m accounts scanning VietQR; cross-border VietQR with Thailand, Laos, Cambodia, Singapore, Korea, UnionPay, Alipay. NAPAS tokenising with IDEMIA. 164m cards in Vietnam at end-2025.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Tokens and Click to Pay for Mastercard cards; acquirer tools for visitor acceptance; Mastercard Move for VCB Money and VCBR; commercial and virtual cards in Corporate DigiBank; fraud and identity under SBV biometric rules; card-linked offers; Carbon Calculator against Visa MeGO.

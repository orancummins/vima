## 1. The company
- Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group, Inc. (Tokyo, Nagoya, NYSE). Formed 2005; about 2,300 locations in 50+ countries, about 160,000 staff. Market value about ¥41.9tn (13 Jul 2026), Japan's most valuable company. Ratings A1/A-/A- (holding company).
- FY2025: net profit ¥2,427.2bn (+31.8%), ROE 11.3%. Q1 FY2026: net income ¥809.4bn (+48%, record), NOP ¥809.0bn, ROE 14.4%, Morgan Stanley ¥222.2bn; loans ¥135.7tn, deposits ¥236.9tn; CET1 12.95%. Card settlement ¥63.4bn and consumer finance ¥83.1bn in the quarter.
- Business groups: Retail & Digital, CWM, JCIB, GCB, AM/IS, GCIB, Global Markets.
- Leadership: Junichi Hanzawa (President & Group CEO from Apr 2026), Hironori Kamezawa (Chairman), Masakazu Osawa (MUFG Bank CEO); group heads Yamamoto, Tanaka, Takase, Komoriya ⚠.
- Strategy: FY26 ROE target raised to about 12%; net profit target ¥2.7tn. Emut retail brand (Jun 2025); MUFG Emut Bank licensed 24 Sep 2026, opening Jan–Mar 2027 on Google Cloud; eSmart Securities + WealthNavi merge FY27; Emut Excellent Club with an AuM-linked platinum card ⚠ network; AI with OpenAI, Google (May 2026) and Sakana AI.
- Deals: Union Bank sold (Dec 2022); WealthNavi (Jan 2025); Jaccs 40% votes (Mar 2025); Zenhoren (NICOS, Apr 2025); Moneytree (2025); Shriram Finance 20% (Apr 2026); Bank MUFG Danamon (Oct 2026); Krungsri's SFinance (Oct 2026).
- Mitsubishi UFJ NICOS (Nippon Shinpan 1951; NICOS 2007): FY to Mar 2026 revenue ¥329.2bn, card shopping ¥219.6bn, ordinary loss ¥23.0bn after ¥31.1bn system-integration cost (DC Card merged into MUFG Card system 9 Dec 2025). Member count not published ⚠.

## 2. MUFG × Mastercard
- No network split published. Mitsubishi UFJ Card and Gold Prestige: Mastercard, Visa, JCB or Amex; Platinum: Amex.
- ACOM AC Mastercard: Mastercard only, about 1.05m holders, ¥158.4bn receivables (Jun 2026).
- JAL Mastercard CLUB-A co-brand; NICOS business cards on Visa and Mastercard; NICOS acquiring including Recruit's Airペイ.
- MUFG Bank debit: Visa and JCB only. Global Point Wallet prepaid: Visa. Apple Pay in store via QUICPay ⚠.
- MUFG Bank × JCB ASEAN strategic alliance (6 Jul 2026), incl. JCB's top-tier card in Indonesia. Sanwa Bank and Nippon Shinpan founded JCB (1961) ⚠ shareholding.
- MUFG Union Bank once ran a Mastercard commercial-card programme ⚠ date. No MUFG–Mastercard agreement found 2015–2026.

## 3. Market context
- METI: 58.0% cashless in 2025 (¥162.7tn); credit 82.7%, code 10.2%, e-money 3.7%, debit 3.4%; 65% by 2030.
- PayPay 75m users (Aug 2026). Visa about 50.8% and JCB about 28% of cards ⚠ undated. JFTC accepted a Visa commitment plan (Jul 2025). Card fraud ¥51.05bn in 2025; EMV 3-DS mandatory since Mar 2025.
- Cotra (MUFG Bank, Mizuho, SMBC, Resona) for small transfers; megabank stablecoin PoC (Nov 2025).

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Commercial and virtual cards for NICOS's corporate cashless push and JCIB's payments plans; a debit card for Emut Bank; Agent Pay for OpenAI/Google agents; tokens and fraud scores; a premium card for Emut Excellent Club; cross-border flows with Asian partner banks and wallets.

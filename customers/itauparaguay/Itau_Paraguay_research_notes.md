## 1. The bank
- Banco Itaú Paraguay S.A., Asunción; unlisted; 99.99688% ITB Holding Brasil (Itaú Unibanco). Incorporated 1976, Interbanco from 1978, Unibanco 1995, renamed July 2010. AAApy stable (FIX SCR).
- 2025: assets Gs 39.8tn (USD 6.1bn), loans Gs 27.1tn (+17.3%), profit Gs 1,258.6bn (~USD 191m), ROAE 20.3%, NPL 1.57%, capital ratio 19.3%. 726k+ customers; 1,387 staff (FIX: 1,279 ⚠).
- 30 Sep 2026: assets Gs 40.7tn; nine-month profit Gs 1,014.4bn (+~9%); non-financial deposits Gs 28.8tn; ROE 19.3% for nine months.
- Rank (Dec 2025): #1 equity (20%), #2 deposits (14.5%), #3 assets (15.1%) and loans (15.2%); housing 27.1%; consumer 21.1%. Continental passed Itaú in 2025 profit (USD ~200m vs ~191m).
- Segments: Banca Personal (Agencias, Personal Bank, Empresas, Private) and Banca de Empresas (Itaú Campo, IBBA), plus Institutional Treasury (24% spot FX, 47% forwards). Sister companies: Itaú Invest, Itaú Asset Management, Itaú Seguros (2021), Pont S.A. (2022: Pik, Tienda Naranja).
- Leadership (Dec 2025 ⚠): chair César Blaquier; GM José Brítez; Carrón (risk), Vasconsellos (personal), Arana (business), Luraghi (markets), Torrents (tech/ops/data), Prantte (people), Geraci (finance).
- Strategy: customer experience, growth in high-income retail and SMEs, a payments and collections ecosystem for companies, AI in internal processes; Open Banking APIs (Jun 2025). No numeric targets ⚠.
- Stakes: 9.09% of Bancard (main processor); minority Visa shares.

## 2. Itaú Paraguay × Mastercard
- Debit: Mastercard Debit on every account (Maestro, Cirrus, Infonet).
- Credit: 496,360 active cards, Gs 9.3tn spend; 35.6% of balances (#1), 16.2% by number (#3); 21 types on Visa, Mastercard, Amex. No network split ⚠.
- Mastercard: Black; AAdvantage and Centenario on Visa or Mastercard. Visa: Infinite, LATAM, Touring, Interventajas Platinum, 24 HS IN, Joven prepaid, Pro Campo ⚠ inferred. Amex: sole issuer. Tigo Itaú and Tarjeta Empresarial networks not stated ⚠.
- Wallets: Apple Pay/Google Pay; report says Visa credit + Mastercard debit, FAQ says Visa only ⚠. Mastercard announced Apple Pay for Itaú Mastercard holders, 5 Nov 2024 ⚠ not fetched.
- Click to Pay: Visa only. 3D Secure on Visa and Mastercard.
- No Paraguay network agreement public; parent's 2015 Mastercard deal and 2026 Agent Pay are Brazil only.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Click to Pay and tokens for the Mastercard debit book; fraud and scam scoring (transfers +49%); Pik acceptance and risk tools; commercial and agribusiness cards; card-linked offers for Interventajas and Tienda Naranja; cross-border payouts; a World Elite tier; Agent Pay after the parent.

# Banco Santander — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Santander_Platform.html`. Two research threads (company profile; Santander × Mastercard), condensed. ## 1. Santander — the company
- Banco Santander, S.A., founded 1857; corporate centre at Boadilla del Monte (Madrid). Listed in Spain, NYSE (SAN), London, Mexico and Warsaw. Market value €177.5bn at 30 Jun 2026, largest in the eurozone. 185,279 employees; 6,496 branches.
- H1 2026: attributable profit €8,973m (incl. €1,895m Poland gain), underlying €7,328m (+15%), RoTE 17.4% reported / 15.6% underlying, efficiency 42.8%, CET1 14.0%, assets €1,954.5bn, 182.5m customers (105.7m active). FY2025 profit €14.1bn (record).
- Global businesses: Retail & Commercial (52% of profit), Openbank (renamed from Digital Consumer Bank, Feb 2026), CIB (22%), Wealth Management & Insurance (14%), Payment Solutions (Getnet, Getnet Platforms, Ebury; cards moved to Retail in Jan 2026).
- Deals: Poland sold to Erste (9 Jan 2026), TSB (30 Apr 2026, ~£2.9bn), Webster ($12.2bn, announced 3 Feb 2026, closed 19 Aug 2026 ⚠ single source); Openbank and SCF merged in Europe.
- Leadership: Ana Botín (executive chair), Héctor Grisi (CEO), José Antonio García Cantera (CFO), Juan Olaizola (COO/CTO), Matías Sánchez and Borja Oyarzábal (Retail co-heads, Jun 2026), Nitin Prabhu (Openbank), Mahesh Aditya (Santander UK).
- Strategy: ONE Transformation, Gravity core, One App (53% of retail customers), OpenAI agreement (Aug 2025); 2028 targets: profit >€20bn, RoTE >20%, efficiency ~36%, >210m customers.

## 2. Santander × Mastercard
- UK: eight-year Mastercard debit deal (May 2018; >9m cards; reissue from 2019); credit Mastercard. Term ends around 2026–27 ⚠. TSB's cards believed Visa ⚠.
- Mexico: all debit and LikeU credit reissued as Mastercard Touch Cards (2023); LikeU credit also Visa; Amex co-brand.
- Brazil: Unique as Mastercard Black and Visa Infinite. Spain, US, Portugal, Chile: split not public. No group-wide network deal found.
- Agentic: Europe's first live agent payment with Mastercard Agent Pay and PayOS (Spain, 2 Mar 2026; Matías Sánchez, Kelly Devine); Agent Pay live at Santander Brasil (24 Mar 2026; Gustavo Santos, Andrea Scerch); Getnet an early LAC Agent Pay partner (Dec 2025), live in Mexico with Neivor (Q2 2026), launch partner of Agent Pay for Machines (11 Jun 2026; Jorn Lambert).
- Visa: agentic pilot in five LatAm markets (Mar 2026); Santander first bank on Visa Destinations (30 Jun 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay from pilot to product; TSB migration and the UK renewal; Webster's cards; Agent Pay for Machines with stablecoin settlement; Move for Ebury and Brazil Pay-in; tokens and passkeys on Plard; Installments beside Openbank Pay (weak fit); account-to-account fraud as Bizum/Wero grows (thin evidence).

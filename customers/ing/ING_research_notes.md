## 1. The company
- ING Groep N.V. (Euronext Amsterdam: INGA; NYSE ADR: ING). Incorporated 4 Mar 1991 ⚠ (merger of Nationale-Nederlanden and NMB Postbank is general knowledge). >41m customers; nearly 16m mobile primary; 62,770 FTE. Market value about €79bn at 30 Jun 2026 ⚠ derived.
- 2Q26: income €6,284m, net result €1,947m, RoTE 17.0%, CET1 13.1%. FY2025: income €23,035m, net result €6,327m.
- Segments: Retail Netherlands, Retail Belgium (incl. Luxembourg), Retail Germany, Retail Other (Spain, Italy, Poland, Romania, Türkiye, Australia), Wholesale Banking.
- Leadership: Steven van Rijswijk (CEO), Ida Lerner (CFO), Andrea Cesaroni (CRO), Ljiljana Čortan (Wholesale), Pinar Abay (Retail; EPI board), Hans Overeem (payments ⚠ title varies), Lars Stoy (Germany), Karl Guha (Supervisory Board chair).
- Outlook raised 30 Jul 2026: 2026 income >€24.5bn, RoTE >15%; 2027 income >€26bn, RoTE >16%.
- Deals: Goldman Sachs TFI (Apr 2026), ~40% of Singular Bank (Jul 2026), TTB cut to 11.6% (Aug 2026), Russia sale terminated (Apr 2026).
- AI: unified data/AI/agentic environment; AI Risk Committee; GenAI chatbots in seven markets; agentic mortgage assistant (NL); inbound contacts −43% (2025).
- Subscription plans Go/More/Extra/Max in nine markets, 3m customers (NL from 10 Jun 2026).

## 2. ING × Mastercard
- NL debit: Maestro and V PAY split at random since 2018; ~10m cards being reissued as Visa Debit and Debit Mastercard since Nov 2025, split undisclosed ⚠. NL credit believed Mastercard ⚠ (unverified).
- BE debit: Bancontact + Debit Mastercard since the 2023 Maestro migration. BE credit: data missing.
- DE: Visa debit free; girocard optional (€1.49/month); first credit card planned for 2026, network not decided.
- PL: Visa main debit; Mastercard for euro account, prepaid and Platinum credit; standard credit in both; Click to Pay supported.
- RO, ES, IT, AU card networks not stated ⚠.
- Agentic payment pilot with Mastercard and Worldline (2 Jun 2026, Money20/20) ⚠ Agent Pay not named; principles with NatWest, ASB, BofA, Capital One and CBA (22 Sep 2026), networks not signatories.
- No ING network contract found; no evidence of joint instalments, Move, open banking, fraud or commercial-card programmes.
- Wero: ING is an EPI shareholder. BE P2P Nov 2024, e-commerce Jan 2026; DE P2P Aug 2025, online Jun 2026, POS next; NL iDEAL to Wero by end-2027. Qivalis euro stablecoin (37 banks) licensed by DNB 14 Oct 2026.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Network for ING Germany's first credit card; a larger share of the NL reissue; card benefits in subscription plans; agentic pilot to production; fraud and identity across Wero and A2A; commercial and virtual cards for Business Banking in Italy and Spain; multi-token interoperability with Qivalis.

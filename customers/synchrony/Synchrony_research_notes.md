# Synchrony — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Synchrony_Platform.html`. Two research threads (company profile; Synchrony × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Synchrony's own (2Q26 results and 10-Q, July 2026; FY2025 10-K, 6 Feb 2026) unless marked. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. 3Q26 results are due 20 October 2026.

## 1. Synchrony — the company
- Synchrony Financial (NYSE: SYF), Stamford, Connecticut; Synchrony Bank is a federal savings association (OCC). Formerly GE Capital Retail Finance ⚠ (IPO 2014, separated 2015). More than 20,000 employees; Atlanta hub opened Oct 2026.
- Scale: $103.8bn receivables, $182.3bn purchase volume and 70.7m active accounts in 2025; 2Q26 receivables $102.2bn, record $49.8bn purchase volume (+8%), deposits $82.8bn (83% of funding), NIM 15.08%, NCO 5.43%, CET1 13.2%, net earnings $885m. 2026 EPS guidance $9.25–9.50.
- Five platforms (2025 interest and fees): Digital 30% (Amazon, PayPal/Venmo, Verizon, QVC, Virgin Red); Home & Auto 26% (Lowe's, Ashley, Floor & Decor, Car Care); Diversified & Value 22% (Walmart/OnePay, Sam's Club, TJX, JCPenney, Belk); Health & Wellness 17% (CareCredit, Walgreens); Lifestyle 5% (J.Crew, DICK'S, Polaris).
- Top five (Amazon, Lowe's, PayPal, Sam's, TJX): 54% of interest and fees; agreements to 2030–35.
- Deals: Ally Lending (2024), Pets Best sold (2024), Versatile Credit (Oct 2025), Lowe's commercial portfolio (Apr 2026, Amex network).
- Leadership: Brian Doubles (CEO), Brian Wenzel (CFO), Carol Juel (Digital), Florin Arghirescu (CTO), Nimrod Barak (Chief AI Officer), Curtis Howse, Courtney Gentleman, Beto Casellas, Mike Storiale (innovation, payments and AI).

## 2. Synchrony × Mastercard
- Network chosen programme by programme; no enterprise network agreement public. Consumer co-brand is 34% of receivables; the rest is private label and instalment credit off-network.
- Mastercard: PayPal Cashback, Sam's Club, OnePay CashRewards (Walmart, live Sep 2025), myWalgreens (2021), J.Crew (2023), Virgin Red World Elite (2024, exclusive), Belk, CareCredit Rewards, Synchrony Premier; TJX ⚠ likely.
- Visa: Venmo, Verizon; Synchrony holds Visa Class B shares ($30m exchange gain in 2Q26). Amex: MyLowe's Pro Rewards (Apr 2026).
- Agentic: frameworks with Mastercard and Visa, AP2 contributor (Oct 2025); pilot with Mastercard, Visa and Google; "still in testing" (Aug 2026).
- People: Linda Kirkpatrick (Walgreens, OnePay), Chiro Aikat (J.Crew, Virgin Red).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay at scale; network tokens and Click to Pay for Dual and private-label cards; identity and fraud at instant point-of-sale credit (operational losses rising); Mastercard Installments for Synchrony Pay Later; open finance in underwriting; merchant-funded offers against rising loyalty costs; commercial cards and the Visa/Amex programmes at renewal.

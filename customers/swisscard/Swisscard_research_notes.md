# Swisscard AECS — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Swisscard_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Swisscard × Mastercard), condensed. Swisscard is unlisted and publishes no accounts, card counts or volumes; figures come from swisscard.ch and its brand sites (read 17 Oct 2026), its press releases, Swiss press and the Swiss Payment Monitor. The web-search budget ran out after about eight searches, so Fitch's January 2026 release, ABS status and Mastercard newsroom items were not confirmed. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Swisscard AECS GmbH, Neugasse 18, 8810 Horgen. Founded 1998 as a 50:50 American Express–Credit Suisse joint venture; took over Credit Suisse's card issuing in 2015.
- Ownership: JV not renewed (3 Jun 2024); UBS agreed to sell its inherited half to American Express Swiss Holdings (21 Oct 2024); completed 21 Jan 2026. Amex is now sole owner. Credit Suisse-branded cards (Amex, Mastercard, Visa) moved to UBS, which began issuing in H1 2025.
- Scale: >650 staff (about 600 in a June 2026 release ⚠); >400,000 app users; >149,000 Amex acceptance points; "over 1.5 million cards" before the Credit Suisse exit ⚠; 31 private and 23 corporate products (Oct 2024).
- Ratings: Fitch A- / F1; support rating a- anchored to Amex from Jan 2026 ⚠. ABS via Swiss Credit Card Issuance 2022-1 and 2023-1 ⚠ current status.
- Leadership: Guido Müller (CEO since Jan 2021; Mastercard Country Manager Switzerland 2012–17, Mastercard Data & Services SE Asia 2018–20), Linda de Winter (CIO), Roman Binder (CFO), Sabine Mantoiu (CCO since May 2026), Alex Friedli (COO). Armin Czapla (CGO from Aug 2023) no longer listed ⚠.
- Strategy: no published targets. Premium Amex and lifestyle (Caminada, Jun 2026; Globus), free cards with instant digital issuing, Amex acceptance.

## 2. Swisscard × Mastercard
- Amex: proprietary Platinum, Gold, Card, Centurion; corporate, purchasing, vPayment, BTA; the lead card in every duo; acceptance is Amex-only.
- Mastercard: Business Standard, Gold, Euro, Corporate Dollar; Picture Card; SDG2 data; spending bonus; World Mastercard Standard/Gold/Platinum and Gold EUR/USD for private banks; the Mastercard option in SWISS Miles & More and Cashback duos; SWISS SME packages (Amex + Mastercard); Priceless Cities.
- Visa: poinz Cards (Amex + Visa, 2022); swisscard Visa Credit Card (2025 ⚠); the Visa option in Miles & More and Cashback.
- Shared: Apple Pay and Samsung Pay (2016), Swatch Pay (2018, certified by Mastercard and Visa), Google Pay (Jul 2020), Click to Pay for all three networks (date ⚠); 3-D Secure pilot for Mastercard and Visa (2010).
- No public network agreements, renewals or Mastercard fraud, open-finance or agentic deals found.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- The second-card slot in new free cards and poinz (both went to Visa); scam and identity tools (phishing warnings, LINDA); virtual and purchasing cards against Amex vPayment; Click to Pay enrolment through the app; FX tools for EUR/USD private-bank cards; Agent Pay as the CIO builds the AI strategy ⚠.

## 4. Market
- Swiss Payment Monitor (19 Feb 2026, autumn 2025 data): mobile 31.4% of transactions, debit 23.8%, cash 23.0%, credit 17.2%; TWINT 10.6% of in-store payments. Spring 2026: credit fell to 14.5% ⚠.
- Rivals: Viseca (private cards on Mastercard or Visa, business on Mastercard), UBS, Cembra, PostFinance, Raiffeisen, Cornèrcard ⚠ no reliable share data.

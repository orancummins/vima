## 1. The company
- PayPal Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: PYPL), San Jose; founded 1998, eBay 2002–15. ~200 markets, 439m active accounts, ~23,800 staff. Market cap ~$48.5bn (16 Oct 2026) ⚠ conflicting figure on the same page.
- FY2025: revenue $33.17bn (+4%), TM$ $15.47bn, TPV $1.794tn (+7%), non-GAAP EPS $5.31. Q2 2026: revenue $8.68bn (+5%), TPV $486.4bn (+10%), losses $397m; FY26 guidance raised.
- Mix (Q4 2025): branded checkout 31% of TPV (+1–2%), PSP 43%, US 63%; Venmo revenue ~$1.7bn, 67m MAA; Pay Later TPV >$40bn; PayPal debit TPV +50%+.
- One reported segment; three businesses since 29 Apr 2026: Checkout Solutions & PayPal (Frank Keller); Consumer Financial Services & Venmo (Alexis Sowa, interim); Payment Services & Crypto (Jeff Pomeroy, interim).
- Leadership: Enrique Lores CEO from 1 Mar 2026 (Alex Chriss out); David Dorman chair; Jamie Miller CFO/COO ⚠.
- Strategy: $1.5bn gross savings over 2–3 years; agentic commerce (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Claude); PayPal Bank ILC applied 15 Dec 2025, seeking "direct membership in U.S. card networks"; Cymbio bought (Feb 2026).
- Stripe/Advent reported $60.50/share (~$53bn) bid, 15 Jul 2026; board reportedly found it inadequate; no status after early Aug ⚠.

## 2. PayPal × Mastercard
- Mastercard: PayPal Debit (US, The Bancorp), UK debit (relaunched 12 Nov 2025), Venmo Debit, Business Debit, PayPal Cashback (Synchrony), PayPal Credit card (Synchrony, 3 Jun 2025); Germany in-store tap on Mastercard contactless (May 2025).
- Agreements: Mastercard 6 Sep 2016 (equal wallet option, tokens, cash-out, no wallet operator fee, no ACH steering), global 5 Oct 2017. Agent Pay in the PayPal wallet (27 Oct 2025; Sherri Haymond). One Credential co-development (Jun 2025 ⚠ day).
- Visa: 21 Jul 2016 deal (VDEP, Visa Direct cash-out); Venmo Credit Card on Visa (Synchrony), upgraded to 4% from 15 Oct 2026.
- Off-network: Pay Later (KKR, Blue Owl funding), PYUSD (Paxos, 70 markets), Pay with Crypto, PayPal World (UPI, Weixin Pay), Pix. Not found: Click to Pay, Mastercard Move/Send, joint PYUSD work; a Mar 2026 blockchain report is unverified ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay across every AI channel; One Credential instalments on debit; Move for Xoom, Venmo international and PayPal World; fraud and identity; Click to Pay beside Fastlane; offers for PayPal+ and Venmo Stash; stablecoin settlement for PYUSD; issuer services for PayPal Bank. At risk: Venmo credit, Visa Direct, A2A and stablecoins, direct membership, the bid.

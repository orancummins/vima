# Revolut — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Revolut_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Revolut × Mastercard), condensed. Revolut is private: figures are its 2025 annual report (24 Mar 2026), regulators and press; revolut.com blocked fetching, so some newsroom items come via press. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. Revolut — the company
- Revolut Group Holdings Ltd, London; founded 1 Jul 2015 by Nik Storonsky (CEO) and Vlad Yatsenko (stepped down as CTO 1 Jul 2026; Donato Lucia succeeds). CFO Victor Stinga; UK CEO Francesca Carlesi. ~10,000 staff.
- 2025: revenue $6.0bn (+46%), profit before tax $2.3bn (38% margin), net profit $1.7bn; cards $1.3bn, subscriptions $936m, wealth $876m, FX $800m, interest $1.3bn; 68.3m retail customers, 767k businesses, $1.7tn volume, $67.5bn balances, $2.9bn lending. 80m+ customers in 40 countries by Sep 2026 ⚠.
- Licences: Revolut Bank UAB (EEA); UK bank out of mobilisation 11–12 Mar 2026; Mexico bank live 27 Jan 2026; UAE (Jun 2026); Australia (Jul 2026 ⚠); France (Aug 2026); US OCC conditional approval 3 Sep 2026. Fines: €3.5m Lithuania AML (Apr 2025), €11.5m Italy (Apr 2026, appealed); ECB curbs reported ⚠.
- Valuation: $75bn (Nov 2025), reported $115bn secondary (Jul 2026); IPO at least two years away, US primary listing preferred (Oct 2026).
- Strategy: 100m customers by mid-2027, 30 new markets by 2030, £10bn investment over five years; Banco Cetelem Argentina (approved Sep 2026 ⚠).
- Products: cards (Mastercard and Visa), Visa Direct payouts, FX, Revolut Pay, Terminal/Reader/Gateway (Amex accepted), Wero ⚠, UPI (India beta), crypto and Revolut X, EURR stablecoin with Bridge (26 Aug 2026), Customer Protection Platform, Street Mode, AIR (agentic shopping tests Dec 2026), RevPoints (17m users), plans, Revolut Mobile, Business, trading/ISA, loans, mortgages, Revolut <18.

## 2. Revolut × Mastercard
- Mastercard launch partner (2015); Start Path (2016).
- Visa lead issuing partner for global expansion (30 Sep 2019); India, Canada, US credit card on Visa ⚠.
- Mastercard deal (22 Oct 2019; Michael Miebach): first US cards, ≥50% of existing and future European cards, Mastercard Send first.
- Visa Direct for P2P (2023) and Revolut Business payouts (Aug 2024).
- No Revolut × Mastercard announcement found since 2019; both named among Google AP2 contributors ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- US credit and debit after the 2027 charter; Agent Pay for AIR; Consumer Fraud Risk for the UK bank; Move against Visa Direct; new-market issuing (Mexico, UAE, Argentina, Australia); stablecoin-linked cards; Click to Pay against Revolut Pay.

## 1. Fiserv — the company
- Fiserv, Inc., Milwaukee; Nasdaq: FISV (moved back from NYSE "FI" in Nov 2025). More than 38,000 employees. Market value $25.3bn (16 Oct 2026), from $116.9bn at end-2024; Moody's Baa2 stable.
- 2025: GAAP revenue $21.2bn, adjusted $19.8bn (+4%), adjusted EPS $8.64. 2Q26: adjusted revenue $4.96bn (−4%), adjusted EPS $1.84 (−26%). 2026 guidance cut on 6 Aug 2026 to organic −1% to 0%, EPS $7.20–7.40. 2029 targets: 4–6% growth, margin >37%, EPS >$12.
- Segments: Merchant Solutions (Clover, enterprise/Commerce Hub/Carat, processing for FIs and ISOs; FY25 $10.1bn) and Financial Solutions (digital payments incl. STAR, Accel, Zelle, CheckFree; issuing incl. Optis, FirstVision, VisionPLUS; banking cores; FY25 $9.7bn).
- Scale: $4.6tn merchant volume (2025), ~a third of US merchant GPV, 6m+ locations, ~900k Clover merchants, 1.6bn issuing accounts, ~10,000 FI clients; claims #1 US acquirer (Nilson, 2025 data ⚠ self-reported).
- Leadership: Takis Georgakopoulos (CEO from 15 Jun 2026; Mike Lyons left for Truist), Paul Todd (CFO), Gordon Nixon (Chair), Sanjay Saraf (merchant product and technology), Lia Cao (merchant CRO), Sunil Sachdev (embedded finance and digital assets).
- Deals and exits: Payfare, CCV, Pinch, CardFree, AIB Merchant Services, part of TD Merchant Canada, StoneCastle (2025); MoneyPass JV with Bridgepoint (Aug 2026); student-loan and India exits; portfolio review includes STAR/Accel.
- AI and digital assets: agentOS (beta), OpenAI collaboration, Project Elevate ($500m savings); FIUSD (Jun 2025); Digital Asset Platform live 1 Oct 2026 with Bank of North Dakota's Roughrider Coin.

## 2. Fiserv × Mastercard
- Acquirer and issuer processor for Mastercard volume at scale.
- FIUSD integration by Mastercard (24 Jun 2025: Multi-Token Network, settlement, FIUSD cards; ⚠ not live).
- Agent Pay acceptance framework, Secure Card on File, network token requestor (22 Dec 2025; Saraf and Chiro Aikat); Visa Intelligent Commerce and TAP announced the same day.
- Verifiable Intent supporter (5 Mar 2026; Pablo Fourez).
- Mastercard Merchant Cloud integrated into Commerce Hub (4 Aug 2026; Saraf, Cao, Aikat).
- Competition: STAR and Accel (dual-routed debit; sale talks with JPMorgan, BofA, Wells, PNC reported Jul 2026); Affirm pay-over-time on debit; Zelle.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Named services through Merchant Cloud (Click to Pay, passkeys, Ethoca, fraud scores); live Agent Pay on Clover and Carat; issuer-side tokens, Click to Pay and Installments across Fiserv's banks; Finicity for verification and pay-by-bank; Move for payouts and community-bank cross-border; FIUSD-linked cards; the debit landscape if STAR or Accel is sold.

## 1. The company
- Powszechna Kasa Oszczędności Bank Polski S.A., Warsaw; WSE-listed since Nov 2004; market cap PLN 129bn (30 Jun 2026). State Treasury 29.43%, NN OFE 7.03%, Allianz OFE 5.90%. Moody's A2 deposits. Founded 1919 ⚠.
- 1H 2026: net profit PLN 5.29bn, ROE 18.4%, C/I 31.6%, CoR 30 bp, CET1 15.55%, assets PLN 608bn. FY2025: net profit PLN 10.7bn, ROE 19.5%.
- 12.6m customers (11.9m retail, 679k small firms, 32k corporates); 9.9m accounts; 11.1m cards (965k credit); 8.7m active IKO apps; 954 branches, 3,138 ATMs; 26.6k FTEs.
- Segments: Retail; Corporate & Investment; Transfer centre & other. Subsidiaries: PKO Bank Hipoteczny, PKO Leasing (PKO Masterlease, PKO Faktoring), PKO TFI, PKO Życie, PKO TU, Bankowy PTE, KREDOBANK (Ukraine), PKO BP Finat.
- Board: Szymon Midera (President), Dresler, Mazur, Falak-Cyniak, Papierak, Pol, Sobolewski, Stolarczyk, Zarzycki. Supervisory Board chair Tomasz Siemiątkowski.
- Strategy "The Number 1, full stop": 15m individual customers, 2.5m via ecosystems, ROE >18%, C/I <35% by 2027; expansion to nine more EU markets.
- AI: 13.8m bot conversations in 1H 2026, first LLM deployment, Document Intelligence, Google Cloud data platform.

## 2. PKO × Mastercard
- Debit on Visa (account card, Junior card, business cards); Visa Infinite and Visa Mobile; Żappka Pay card with Visa and Planet Pay (Apr 2026).
- Mastercard: PKO Mastercard Platinum (Priceless points); Przejrzysta card in Visa and Mastercard versions; PKO Euro Biznes network ⚠ not stated.
- Contactless BLIK runs at Mastercard terminals at Mastercard FX rates: 516.6m transactions to 30 Jun 2026, 104.6m in 1H 2026.
- Mastercard became a shareholder of Polski Standard Płatności (BLIK) in Apr 2020; PKO's own stake ⚠ not confirmed.
- Acceptance via eService (EVO bought 66% in 2013, 20-year alliance) ⚠ current ownership; terminals take Visa, Mastercard and BLIK.
- Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay and others for Visa and Mastercard cards. No named Mastercard executives found ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Contactless BLIK abroad; scam and identity scores beside PKO ID and interbank behavioural checks; card-linked offers for Allegro Klik and Kropka; fleet and commercial cards for PKO Masterlease (45.8k vehicles); cards and acceptance for EU expansion.

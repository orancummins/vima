## 1. The company
- BAI – Banco Angolano de Investimentos, S.A. | Sociedade Aberta, Luanda. Founded 14 Nov 1996, first private bank with wholly Angolan capital; 30 years old in 2026. Listed on BODIVA since June 2022 (10% IPO), the exchange's most valuable company; market value about Kz 1.9trn ⚠ own calc. 2,329 shareholders, none qualifying; CEO holds 6.33%. Fitch B-, Moody's B3, both stable.
- Scale (individual): assets Kz 5,334bn, loans Kz 1,366bn, customer funds Kz 4,105bn at 30 Jun 2026; H1 profit Kz 117bn (−34%); 2025 profit Kz 296bn (record, sector's largest per Deloitte). Consolidated assets Kz 6,482bn. 3,088,238 active customers; 1,024,978 BAI Directo users; 2,057 staff.
- Segments: retail, commercial (corporate), private, investment and correspondent banking, insurance (NOSSA). Group: BAI Europa (Portugal; police search 14 Oct 2026 ⚠), BAI Cabo Verde 81.63%, BISTP 25%, NOSSA 72.24%, ÁUREA, Pay4all (é-Kwanza wallet, 1.14m clients).
- Leadership (2026–29): Mário Alberto Barber (Chair), Luís Filipe Rodrigues Lélis (CEO), nine executive directors incl. Irisolange Verdades. Card and digital portfolios not published ⚠.
- Strategy: Geração BAI 2022–27 (five axes, 58% done end-2025); Finastra Essence core in execution since Aug 2025; LUENA AI assistant; sustainability bond Kz 57.5bn (Sep 2026); Bank of China RMB/CIPS MoU (Sep 2026); first SIBOS stand (Oct 2026).

## 2. BAI × Mastercard
- Active cards at June 2026: Multicaixa debit 2,084,773; Kamba Visa prepaid 169,755 (−15%); Multicaixa prepaid 109,019; Visa credit 6,142; Multicaixa credit 5,207 (incl. TAAG co-brand); Mastercard corporate prepaid 796. About 92% domestic, 7.4% Visa, 0.03% Mastercard ⚠ own calc.
- EMIS–Mastercard MoU for multi-brand Multicaixa/Mastercard debit cards (2 Aug 2023). BAI launched its multi-brand debit card on 20 Aug 2026 (Multicaixa in Angola, Mastercard abroad); take-up and fees not disclosed ⚠.
- Visa: Kamba, Classic/Business credit, e-BAI online acceptance, apparent sponsorship of local banks ⚠; strategic agreement 23 Sep 2026 (Lélis; Visa's Michael Berner). Visa also launched a rival kwanza co-badge debit with Banco Millennium Atlântico (Jul 2026).
- April 2024 FX squeeze: Visa credit limits −25%, Kamba loads USD 1,000 → 500, Mastercard corporate USD 1,500/month ⚠.
- No public evidence of Mastercard tokenisation, Click to Pay, Move, fraud tools, commercial platforms or acquiring at BAI.

## 3. Market
- 21 banks, about Kz 26trn of assets; BAI first by assets and 2025 profit, ahead of BFA. EMIS runs Multicaixa (3.4bn operations in 2025; Multicaixa Express about 62%, nearly 2.5m users ⚠) and KWiK instant payments (BNA, mandatory since 2025). Dollar scarcity limits international cards.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Scale the multi-brand card across 2 million debit holders; commercial and fleet cards (FLEX, 3,251 corporates); fraud scoring and tokens (Kz 10.8bn op-risk losses, 3.9m cyber attempts in 2025); remittances in place of Kamba loads; Mastercard online acceptance beside Visa-only e-BAI; a network for the INFOTUR tourism card.

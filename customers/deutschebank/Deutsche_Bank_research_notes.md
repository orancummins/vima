## 1. The company
- Deutsche Bank AG, Frankfurt (XETRA: DBGn.DB; NYSE: DB). Founded 1870. Market value about US$61.2bn (16 Oct 2026). Rated A1 (Moody's, stable) / A (S&P, positive) / A+ (Fitch, positive). 89,879 FTE; revenue 39% Germany, 28% EMEA, 21% Americas, 12% APAC (2025).
- Q2 2026: net revenues €8.48bn, record Q2 net profit €1.91bn, RoTE 11.0%, cost/income 63.0%, CET1 13.9%. Loans €491bn, deposits €698bn, AuM €1,922bn. FY2025: revenues €32.1bn, net profit €7.1bn, RoTE 10.3%; all 2025 targets met.
- Divisions (2025 revenue): Corporate Bank €7.4bn, Investment Bank €11.5bn, Private Bank €9.7bn, Asset Management (DWS) €3.1bn. About 19m private clients in Germany; 800k SMEs; 13k+ large corporates.
- Brands: Deutsche Bank, Postbank (a branch of DB AG), BHW, DWS, Fyrst ⚠ (2026 status unverified), Vert (with Fiserv).
- Leadership: Christian Sewing (CEO), Fabrizio Campelli (President), Raja Akram (CFO from 15 Mar 2026), Marie-Jeanne Deverdun (CTDIO), Claudio de Sanctis (Private Bank); Alexander Wynaendts chairs the supervisory board. Payments: Gerald Podobnik, Ole Matthiessen, Kilian Thalhammer.
- Global Hausbank 2028: RoTE >13%, cost/income <60%, revenue ~€37bn, 60% payout. Corporate Bank: 100% API access, card issuing "B2B2C with PB", DLT and stablecoins. Private Bank: 15→2 core platforms, AI voice agents.
- Deals: Vert JV (2021–22), Better Payment (2021), DataMesh stake (2023), Private Bank India sale (2026). Numis and other moves not verified ⚠.

## 2. Deutsche Bank × Mastercard
- Every DB and Postbank issuing programme found is on Mastercard: girocard co-badged with Debit Mastercard; virtual debit; all consumer credit; business debit and credit. Exceptions: Amex corporate cards; some legacy Postbank Visa ⚠.
- Miles & More: won with Mastercard (10 Jul 2023); DB issues from 1 Oct 2025, migrated from DKB by spring 2026. MilesPay runs on Mastercard.
- Feb 2021 wider payments partnership for business clients. Jun 2025 Request to Pay on Mastercard open banking in Merchant Solutions ⚠ (search summary).
- Click to Pay and wallet tokens on DB and Postbank Mastercards. No dated master network agreement found; Maestro → Debit Mastercard switch date not found.
- Not found: Agent Pay, Mastercard Move, commercial virtual cards, stablecoin settlement or Mastercard fraud tools at DB.

## 3. Competition and where Mastercard could land
- girocard keeps domestic debit; Wero (DB is an EPI shareholder) replaced giropay; Visa Cybersource screens merchant fraud (Sep 2022); Amex holds corporate T&E; Vert and Merchant Solutions accept every network; AllUnity (DWS JV) and digital-asset custody (not confirmed live ⚠) are non-card rails.
- Openings: commercial and virtual cards and B2B2C issuing; Agent Pay for Private Bank voice agents; merchant risk against Cybersource; Mastercard Move for the cross-border push; stablecoin and tokenised settlement; loyalty data around Miles & More; acceptance services for Merchant Solutions and Vert.

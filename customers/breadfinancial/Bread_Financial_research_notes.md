Companion to `Bread_Financial_Platform.html`. Two research threads (company profile; Bread Financial × Mastercard), condensed. ## 1. Bread Financial — the company
- Bread Financial Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: BFH), Columbus, Ohio. Formerly Alliance Data Systems (1996); Epsilon sold 2019, Bread bought Dec 2020, LoyaltyOne spun off Nov 2021, renamed Mar 2022. About 6,000 staff. Market value about $4.3bn ⚠ (Aug 2026).
- Banks: Comenity Bank (Delaware) merging into Comenity Capital Bank (Utah); approved 31 Jul 2026, expected around 1 Oct 2026 ⚠ completion not confirmed.
- Scale: 2025 revenue $3.85bn, diluted EPS $10.96, credit sales $27.8bn, net loss rate 7.7%; about 34m open accounts. 2Q26: revenue $993m (+7%), EPS $3.55, credit sales $7.5bn (+11%), loans $18.5bn, DTC deposits $9.4bn (50% of funding), NLR 6.98%, NIM 18.5%, CET1 12.9%.
- Mix: co-brand about 52% of 2025 credit sales, private label about 43%, the rest Bread's own cards and Bread Pay ⚠ slide pairing unclear.
- Concentration: top five programmes 49% of 2025 revenue; Signet, Ulta Beauty and Victoria's Secret each 10%+. Top ten run to at least 2028.
- Deals: Saks won (Jun 2024; Saks in Chapter 11 Jan 2026); Crypto.com, Cricket, Vivint, Raymour & Flanigan, Bed Bath & Beyond (2025); Caesars renewed (2025); Ford and Ethan Allen (1Q26); Academy full suite (1Q26); Signet renewed (Sep 2026).
- Technology: Fiserv processing since Jun 2022; AWS and Microsoft cloud; Enhanced Digital Suite, APIs, SDKs; AI investment "ramping up".
- Leadership: Ralph Andretta (CEO), Perry Beberman (CFO), Val Greer (CCO, retiring Feb 2027), Dennis McCarthy (CRO from Sep 2026), Rick Cunningham (SVP Strategy).

## 2. Bread Financial × Mastercard
- Network chosen programme by programme; no enterprise network agreement is public. Co-brand cards run on Amex, Mastercard or Visa; Bread's own cards on Amex.
- Mastercard: Victoria's Secret (Mar 2022, John Levitsky for Mastercard), Ulta Beauty (2016, renewed 2022), Saks World Elite (Aug 2024), Unity by Hard Rock, PENN, myAcademy, Ross, Petco, Yankees, Packers, Ann Taylor/LOFT, Talbots.
- Visa: AAA, Caesars, Ford (Mar 2026), Crypto.com (2025), Toyota/Lexus, NFL, IKEA, Sephora ⚠, PlayStation.
- Amex: Bread Cashback (Apr 2022) and Bread Rewards. No network: private label and Bread Pay.
- By CFPB count, about 18 Mastercard and 17 Visa programmes ⚠ count, not volume; some may be closed.
- No Bread-specific tokenisation, Click to Pay, Installments, fraud or Agent Pay work with Mastercard is public.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay for the co-brands (Bread is building agentic use cases with partners); tokens and Click to Pay for store cards; identity and fraud at instant credit ($65m fraud losses a year); Mastercard Installments for Bread Pay; merchant-funded offers against rising partner payments; open finance in underwriting; and the Visa programmes at renewal.

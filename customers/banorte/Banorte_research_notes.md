## 1. The company
- Grupo Financiero Banorte, S.A.B. de C.V. (BMV: GFNORTEO; OTCQX: GBOOY; Latibex: XNOR); bank Banco Mercantil del Norte (98.26%). HQ San Pedro Garza García, Nuevo León. Roots: Banco Mercantil de Monterrey (1899); privatised 1992; Ixe 2011; Interacciones 2018. Only large Mexican bank not foreign-controlled.
- Market cap US$29.7bn (30 Jun 2026). Ratings: S&P BBB (negative, May 2026), Moody's Baa2 (stable), Fitch BBB- (positive). 31,376 employees (2025).
- 2Q26: net income Ps 15.55bn (+6%); performing loans Ps 1,273bn (+8%); deposits Ps 1,199bn; ROE 25.7%; efficiency 36.2%; NPL 1.50%; CET1 12.55%. FY2025 net income Ps 58.8bn. 2026 guidance Ps 62–64bn.
- Clients: 13.5m banking, 8.0m Afore; RappiCard ~1.3m cards. 1,229 branches, 12,318 ATMs, 46,191 correspondents (OXXO from Dec 2025), 268,877 POS. Banorte Móvil 7.38m users (2025).
- Market (CNBV, May 2026): loans 15.1% (2nd), cards 11.5% (3rd), mortgages 19.9%, auto 20.6%, payroll 22.0%, government 27.4%.
- Leadership: Carlos Hank González (Chairman), Marcos Ramírez (CEO), Rafael Arana (COO; transformation, technology, AI), Tomás Lozano (CFO from 21 Jul 2026), José Francisco Martha (digital businesses).
- Deals: RappiCard (TDF) fully bought, closed 17 Dec 2025 (US$50m, 15-year Rappi exclusivity); Bineo being sold to Klar's Clearscope (agreed Sep 2025); Trans Union de México stake sold (Mar 2026); 19.73% of Controladora Prosa.
- Technology: Google Cloud AI alliance, Red Hat OpenShift AI, Azure, Copilot Chat for 18,000+; Maya assistant (2m+ messages a month); own acquiring engine.

## 2. Banorte × Mastercard
- Visa: Clásica and Oro credit (2024), Infinite ⚠, RappiCard (2021), Visa Platinum debit, ATMs on Visa/Plus; Visa agreed to buy 51% of Prosa (Dec 2023) ⚠ completion.
- Mastercard: Marriott Bonvoy and Bonvoy Inspire (Apr 2021 ⚠), Mujer Banorte debit with Priceless (600,513 accounts), World Elite debit, Amazon Recargable (Mar 2018), Socio 7 with 7-Eleven (2018 ⚠).
- American Express: corporate cards (2008 ⚠ current) and OptBlue acceptance.
- Unconfirmed networks: Platinum, OneUp, Banorte Conmigo, United co-brands ⚠.
- No public network agreement, switch, volume split or named Mastercard executive found ⚠.

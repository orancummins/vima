# Kaspi.kz — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Kaspi_kz_Platform.html`. This is one research thread, condensed: the company, and Kaspi.kz × Mastercard. Unless marked, figures are Kaspi.kz's own: the Q2 2026 results and presentation (10 Aug 2026), the 4Q and FY 2025 results (2 Mar 2026) and the Form 20-F for 2025 (16 Mar 2026, read from ir.kaspi.kz because SEC EDGAR was not reachable). Kaspi reports Kazakhstan without Türkiye unless stated. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or inference. Q3 2026 results are due on 9 Nov 2026.

## 1. The company
- **Listing and value.** Joint Stock Company Kaspi.kz, Almaty, is listed on Nasdaq (KSPI, Jan 2024). Its market cap was about $17.7bn on 16 Oct 2026.
- **Owners.** Lomtadze holds 22.6% and Kim 20.8%; Baring-related funds hold about 17% (Jan 2026). Tencent bought in about Apr 2026 ⚠ (the release is undated).
- **Ratings and capital.** The holding is rated Fitch BBB- and Moody's Baa3. Kaspi Bank is a systemically important bank with Tier 1 of 19.6%.
- **Staff.** 14,008 at end-2025, 3,611 of them in Türkiye.
- **2025 results.** Consolidated revenue KZT4,046bn ($8.0bn) and net income KZT1,068bn ($2.1bn).
  - Payments: TPV KZT44.2tn (+19%). QR and card were 69%, bills 16%, P2P 8% and B2B 7%.
  - Marketplace: GMV KZT6.7tn. e-Commerce KZT3.2tn and Travel KZT538bn.
  - Fintech: TFV KZT11.7tn (BNPL 41%, merchant finance 18%) and cost of risk 2.2%.
  - Users and merchants: 15.7m MAU, 10.7m DAU and 764k active merchants.
- **Q2 2026.** Revenue KZT1.1tn (+15%) and net income KZT259bn (flat).
  - TPV KZT12.7tn ($26.3bn, +13%) at a take rate of 1.00%.
  - The loan book was KZT7.3tn (+18%), with funding costs of 14.5%.
  - e-Commerce GMV was KZT1.3tn (+28%), including Türkiye. Active consumers were 26.6m, including Türkiye.
  - The dividend rose to KZT1,000 per ADS.
- **Leaders.** Chair Vyacheslav Kim; CEO Mikheil Lomtadze; CFO Tengiz Mosidze; Yuri Didenko (capital markets); Pavel Mironov (COO).
- **Deals.**
  - Hepsiburada: control in Jan 2025 (86.7%).
  - Rabobank A.Ş.: agreed Mar 2025, BDDK approval 24 Jun 2026, completed 15 Jul 2026 and renamed Hepsi Bank, with about $300m of capital.
  - Portmone in Ukraine: 2021, sale agreed 2025.
  - Humo bid dropped ⚠ date.
  - $600m Eurobond (Apr 2026).
- **New products.**
  - Kaspi Alaqan pay-by-palm (Dec 2025; 511k registered in three months).
  - Kasper AI shopping assistant (Jul 2026).
  - Hepsicredit shopping loan pilot.
- **Headwinds.** Bank tax rose to 25% in 2026, with higher reserve requirements.

## 2. Kaspi.kz × Mastercard
- **Issuing.** Kaspi Gold is a Visa card (Kaspi representative, Jan 2026), and top-ups by phone number run "through the Visa service". Kaspi Red is probably on Kaspi's own local system ⚠. No Mastercard programme is public.
- **Own rails.** Kaspi QR, Alaqan, P2P and B2B run on Kaspi's proprietary network. Merchants pay 0.95% on Kaspi Gold QR payments.
- **Acceptance.** Kaspi POS takes other banks' cards at 1.3–1.35%, but those volumes are "significantly lower". Kaspi Magazin has accepted other banks' Visa and Mastercard cards since Sep 2025.
- **Transfers.** Kaspi sends to Visa and Mastercard cards abroad (except Russia), and to Elkart, Uzcard, Humo and UnionPay (Jul 2026); which rails it uses is not public ⚠. Alipay+ QR payments in China started in Apr 2024.
- **Interbank.** Kaspi joined the National Bank's interbank QR and phone transfers on 17 Jul 2026; the unified QR had launched in Nov 2025.
- **Mastercard nationally.** It signed a Digital Country Partnership with Kazakhstan's government on 18 Sep 2023, with an Almaty hub ⚠.
- **Market (NBK, 1 Sep 2026).** 84.3m cards: Visa 41.0m, local systems 28.4m, Mastercard 14.1m and UnionPay 0.75m.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- **Hepsi Bank cards in Türkiye.** Hepsi Bank has no network chosen; fintech starts in 2027.
- **Transfers.** Mastercard Move for transfers to Central Asia and Türkiye.
- **Merchant tools.** Acquirer risk and merchant data for an open Kaspi QR network.
- **Agentic payments.** Agent Pay and tokens for Kasper.
- **Other.** Virtual cards for B2B, and identity alongside Kaspi ID.

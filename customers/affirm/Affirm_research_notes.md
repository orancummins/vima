## 1. The company
- Affirm Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: AFRM), San Francisco; founded 2012 by Max Levchin and others; IPO Jan 2021; reincorporated in Nevada 1 Jul 2025. Class B shares carry 15 votes. 2,358 staff. Market cap $24.97bn (16 Oct 2026).
- FY2026: GMV $50.2bn (+~37%), revenue $4.26bn, operating income $147m, net income $1.93bn (boosted by a $1.45bn tax benefit). Q4 FY2026: GMV $14.1bn (+36%), revenue ~$1.17bn (+33%), RLTC $589m (+39%), GAAP operating margin 13%.
- 27.8m active consumers (+21%), ~571k merchants (+50%+); Affirm Card 5.2m active users (+125%), $2.8bn Q4 GMV. GMV mix: interest-bearing 70%, 0% monthly 14%, Pay-in-X 16%.
- Funding capacity $30bn; CPP Investments $1.7–2.2bn renewal (Jun 2026); New York Life (Oct 2025).
- Partner banks: Celtic and Lead originate; Cross River originates some and holds Affirm Money; Evolve and Stride issue the card; Cross River, Sutton and Celtic issue virtual cards.
- Leadership: Levchin chair and CEO; Michael Linford President from 27 Aug 2026; Libor Michalek President; Rob O'Hare CFO; Pat Suh global markets ⚠.
- Strategy: "next generation payment network"; card and 0% growth; Fiserv debit BNPL (26 Jan 2026); Affirm Edge pilots; Affirm Bank (Nevada ILC filed 23 Jan 2026, pending); UK (Nov 2024), Australia (Q1 FY2027), then NL, FR, DE.
- Partners: Amazon (extended five years, late 2025), Shopify (exclusive US and Canada, renewed Feb 2025; UK; Australia Aug 2026), Apple Pay (in store Sep 2025), Google Pay, Costco (May 2025), Stripe, Worldpay, J.P. Morgan Payments. Walmart went to Klarna/OnePay (Mar 2025, ~5% of GMV).

## 2. Affirm × Mastercard
- Visa: the Affirm Card is a Visa debit card; first US Visa Flexible Credential partner (12 Nov 2024); one-time virtual cards on Visa USA and Visa Canada BINs.
- Mastercard: Mastercard Gateway lists Affirm as a payment method ⚠ (date unknown); both support Google's AP2 (Oct 2025); Fiserv bank-debit plans attach to banks' own debit cards, networks not named ⚠.
- Not found: Mastercard issuing, Mastercard Installments with Affirm, Click to Pay, Agent Pay, Move, tokenisation or fraud deals. Mastercard was not mentioned on the Q4 FY2026 call.
- Competition: Mastercard Installments targets the same bank-debit space; Klarna, PayPal, Afterpay, Sezzle, Zip; card issuers.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Mastercard Installments and issuer tools on Fiserv bank debit and Affirm Edge; a network for Affirm Bank if approved; Agent Pay for Affirm plans; UK and European debit; fraud, identity and open finance for underwriting; merchant data for 571k merchants; offers for merchant-funded 0%.

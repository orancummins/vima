## 1. The company
- Banco Nacional de Bolivia S.A., incorporated 4 March 1872 (decree 1871), legal seat Sucre, multiple bank supervised by ASFI, listed on the Bolivian stock exchange (BNB). Bank-level shareholders not public ⚠; Bedoya family chairs the board.
- 2025: assets USD5,388m, gross loans USD3,421m (11.43% share, third), deposits USD3,903m (12.4%, third), equity USD388m, net profit USD50m (+51.5%), ROE 14.6%, NPL 3.4%. H1 2026: net profit Bs216.5m (+52%), assets Bs37.7bn, deposits Bs28.5bn.
- Loan mix: mortgages 38.4%, corporate 32%, SME 14.4%, consumer 8.2%, microcredit 6.9%. Card receivables about 1% of loans.
- Staff 1,972 (Dec 2025), 2,013 (Mar 2026). 663 service points, 428 ATMs, 135 agents. Customer numbers not published ⚠.
- Group: BNB Corporación S.A. controls BNB SAFI, BNB Valores, BNB Leasing, BNB Valores Perú. Bank owns 25% of card processor ATC.
- Leadership: Pablo Bedoya Sáenz (Chair), Gonzalo Abastoflor Sauma (Executive VP), Erick Grundner (Finance, Sep 2025), Ramiro Argandoña (Corporate), Rolando Achá (Mass Banking), Carlos Mojica (Innovation), Freddy Ramírez (Electronic Payment Instruments).
- Strategy: Mobile First (97% digital, 83% in the app), new core (first stage 2025), AI (FELABAN CLAB 2025 award), open-banking APIs, Cuenta Cripto BNB for USDT (2026). Ratings AAA local (Moody's Local, AESA).

## 2. BNB × Mastercard
- Consumer credit cards Visa (Classic, Platinum, Signature, Excellence); Visa Empresarial; Presto BNPL card on Visa, issued by BNB (May 2023).
- Debit network not stated; Visa ⚠ inferred from 2025 "Paga con Visa y gana" debit and credit campaign.
- Mastercard corporate range launched September 2025: Corporate Black, Corporate, T&E, B2B, CTA (travel). Still in the October 2026 tariff. No named executives or Mastercard release found ⚠.
- BNB Cash, Banca Joven Plus and SOBOCE cards: network not stated ⚠.
- Competing rails: QR Simple, Bille wallet, USDT via Cuenta Cripto, SWIFT and remittance firms.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Spend controls and virtual cards on the corporate range; cross-border payouts and FX against a floating boliviano and USDT bridges; card-linked offers for Cashback BNB (100 merchants, 84,000 users); acquiring risk and QR interoperability; fraud, tokens and alerts; a consumer card choice in a Visa book.

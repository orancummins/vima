# BBVA — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `BBVA_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BBVA × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (January–June 2026 report, 30 Jul 2026; BBVA in 2025 and the 2025 annual report) or BBVA México's (2T26 report, Aug 2026) unless marked. bbva.es, bbva.it and bbva.de product pages blocked automated reading, so Spanish and digital-bank card networks rest on BBVA's newsroom and secondary sources. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. ## 1. The company
- Banco Bilbao Vizcaya Argentaria, S.A. (1857; BBV 1988–89; Argentaria 1999). Registered in Bilbao, run from Ciudad BBVA, Madrid. Listed in Madrid, New York and Mexico; market value €122.1bn (30 Jun 2026). Rated A+ / A2 / A (upgrades in late 2025).
- H1 2026: profit €6,051m (+11.1%), ROTE 22.2%, efficiency 37.8%, CET1 12.90%; assets €965bn, loans €522.5bn, deposits €533bn; 82.8m active customers, 126,994 employees, 5,555 branches. FY2025: record €10.5bn; 11.5m new customers, 66% digital; about 79% of units sold digitally.
- Areas (H1 2026 profit): Mexico €2,979m, Spain €2,172m, South America €556m, Turkey €532m, Rest of Business €508m (CIB outside Spain, digital banks in Italy and Germany).
- Spain: loans €201bn, efficiency 33.6%, 2025 profit €4.18bn; loan share about 14% ⚠ chart.
- Mexico: 34.5m customers (82% digital), 26.2% of loans, 24.2% of deposits, 31.4% of credit cards, Ps 232bn card book, more than a quarter of new mortgages, 1.1m SMEs, Banco de Barrio with 411,000 terminals.
- Leadership: Carlos Torres Vila (Chair), Onur Genç (CEO), María Luisa Gómez Bravo (Finance), Peio Belausteguigoitia (Spain), José Luis Elechiguerra (BBVA México Director General from 1 Sep 2026; Eduardo Osuna proposed as chairman ⚠), Murat Kalkan (Digital Banks).
- Strategy 2025–29: six priorities; ROTE about 22%, cost:income about 35%, about €48bn cumulative profit 2025–28. AI: 'The 8', Blue on OpenAI models, ChatGPT Enterprise for 120,000 staff (Dec 2025), AI Transformation unit (2026).
- Deals: BBVA USA to PNC (US$11.6bn, 2020–21); Garanti to about 86% (2022); Sabadell bid failed at 25.33% (16 Oct 2025); Garanti Romania to Raiffeisen (about €591m, Mar 2026); Qivalis euro-stablecoin consortium (Feb 2026).

## 2. BBVA × Mastercard
- Visa: BBVA México credit (11 cards) and debit; World Cup alliance (Jun 2025). Spain Aqua debit ⚠ third party; premium Visa Infinite, Platinum and Aqua Oro (16 Mar 2026). First AI-agent payment on Visa Intelligent Commerce (2 Jul 2026). Card heritage: Spain's first card with BankAmericard (1971).
- Mastercard: Renfe Mastercard via BBVA Consumer Finance (Jun 2022); BBVA Italy credit (Nov 2024, "strategic agreement"); BBVA Germany credit (17 Jun 2026; Nikolaus Trzeschan); Mexico travel-agency virtual cards with Sabre Direct Pay (30 Sep 2025; Chiara Quaia).
- Unknown ⚠: Aqua credit network, digital-bank debit, Mexican business cards, South America. No group network agreement found.
- Competition: Bizum (30.6m users, 1.24bn operations, €67.8bn in 2025; in-store NFC 2026; Wero link agreed Feb 2026). SPEI (7.3bn transfers in 2025, expected to pass cards by end-2026); CoDi and DiMo low adoption ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Digital-bank debit, tokens and Installments as Italy targets 1m customers; Agent Pay beside Visa's pilot and BBVA's ChatGPT plans; virtual cards as the Sabre product moves to South America and Europe; A2A scam scoring for Bizum and SPEI ⚠ thin evidence; the Aqua credit range if it is contestable; merchant data from 411,000 Banco de Barrio terminals.

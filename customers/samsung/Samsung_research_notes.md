## 1. The company
- Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. (KRX: 005930). Market value ≈US$1.05tn ⚠ third-party. Board: 3 executive, 5 independent directors; Chair Je-Yoon Shin. HQ, ratings, headcount and ownership percentages not verified ⚠.
- Q3 2026 guidance: sales ≈KRW 195tn, operating profit ≈KRW 107.4tn. Q2 2026: KRW 171.5tn / 89.5tn (DS 127.5tn / 89.2tn; MX and Networks 33.2tn, −0.7tn). Q1 2026: 133.9tn / 57.2tn. FY2025: 333.6tn / 43.6tn.

- Samsung Wallet: launched as Samsung Pay in Korea, Aug 2015 (NFC + MST; LoopPay); Samsung Pass merged 2022; Samsung Wallet name in Korea 2024. 61 countries (Aug 2025), 62 markets (Feb 2026).
- Korea: 18.66m users (2025; 1.6m in 2015), 16.6m launches a day, payments KRW 88.6tn in 2024 (3.6tn in 2016), ≈KRW 430tn cumulative; about 19m users (Nov 2025). Wallet Money and Points (Oct 2025) passed 1m subscribers by Dec 2025.

- Strategy: "life hub" — AI-automated online payment, digital assets and Web3; no public targets ⚠.

## 2. Samsung × Mastercard
- MDES tokens for Samsung Pay since 2015 (MWC Mar 2015; Europe 31 Jul 2015; South Africa Aug 2018). Mastercard Processing documents Samsung Pay provisioning.
- Samsung Money by SoFi: Mastercard debit, announced 27 May 2020, opened 23 Jul 2020 ⚠ current status.
- Wallet Express (UK, 6 Dec 2023): Mastercard programme for issuers to offer Samsung Wallet. DZ BANK and 600+ German cooperative banks add Mastercard and Visa cards (21 Sep 2026).
- Visa: Tap to Transfer (US, 21 May 2025; Visa Tap to P2P + Visa Direct, Green Dot; Samsung says Mastercard debit also accepted); Galaxy Card (Barclays, Visa; 21 Jul 2026).
- Others: Amex abroad for Samsung Card holders (4 Feb 2026); UnionPay + BOCHK NFC/QR (27 Feb 2026); Maybank QR (24 Jul 2026, scheme ⚠); USDC with Coinbase (US, late Oct 2026, 60+ payout countries); Korean tap transfer (Woori, Oct 2024); mobile cash card with Bank of Korea and KFTC (Sep 2024); Hana Card travel co-brand MOU (Nov 2025, network ⚠).
- Not found: Agent Pay, Click to Pay, Move/Send or Mastercard offers with Samsung Wallet.

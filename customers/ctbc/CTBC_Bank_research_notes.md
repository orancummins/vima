# CTBC Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `CTBC_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and CTBC × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are CTBC's own (2Q26 results presentation, 18 Aug 2026; monthly results to September 2026; CTBC Holding press releases) unless marked. Many CTBC Bank card pages sit behind a bot check, so several co-brand networks rest on CTBC's tier names or comparison sites. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources, inference or my own romanisation.

## 1. The company
- CTBC Bank Co., Ltd., wholly owned by CTBC Financial Holding (TWSE: 2891); HQ Nangang, Taipei. Founded 1966; Taiwan's first credit card in 1974. Rated A1 (Moody's) and A (S&P).
- Bank: assets NT$7.55tn, loans NT$4.73tn (+17.5%), deposits NT$6.23tn (Jun 2026). Profit NT$31.2bn in 1H26 (+12.3%), NT$49.5bn Jan–Sep 2026, NT$57.3bn in 2025. NIM 1.67%, cost:income 50.7%, NPL 0.54%.
- Fee income 1H26 NT$34.3bn: wealth 51%, cards 12%, lottery 14%, corporate 14%.
- Group: Taiwan Life, CTBC Securities, Taiwan Lottery; group profit NT$63.2bn Jan–Sep 2026.
- Overseas: 14 countries and regions, 370+ outlets; Tokyo Star Bank (owned since 2014 ⚠), LH Financial Group 46.6% (2021); US, Canada, Philippines, Indonesia; India GIFT City branch (May 2026).
- Deals: Shin Kong tender (Aug 2024) rejected by the FSC; Taishin merged with Shin Kong in 2025.
- Leadership: holding chair Wen-Long Yen, president Rachael Kao; bank chair James Chen, president Yang Ming-hsiang ⚠ romanisation.
- Market cap and headcount not checked ⚠.

## 2. Cards and payments
- 2025: NT$890bn credit plus NT$130bn debit spend (over NT$1tn), NT$930bn+ acquired; first in cards in force and active cards. FSC, Nov 2025: about 18% of market spend, 27% of new cards.
- Networks: Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express all issued.
- LINE Pay card (4m+ by 2021): Visa and JCB; debit on Visa.
- uniopen co-brand (Aug 2025; 1m+ cards by Aug 2026): World and Titanium tiers, so Mastercard ⚠.
- Marriott Bonvoy (Sep 2026) and foodpanda (Dec 2023): Visa. Smart Pay commercial virtual card (Jul 2026): Visa.
- Ding Ji premium: Visa Infinite, World Mastercard, JCB Ultimate. CPC, China Airlines, Agoda, SOGO, Hotai cards: network not confirmed ⚠.
- Apple Pay first wave (2017); Tap to Pay on iPhone (2023); open banking phase 3 live Nov 2025.

## 3. CTBC × Mastercard
- In: uniopen ⚠, Ding Ji World tier, part of the card book, acceptance, wallet tokens. CTBC cited Mastercard's survey for its recurring-payment detector (Jan 2025).
- Not found: tokenisation, Click to Pay, Agent Pay, fraud or commercial deals with CTBC ⚠.
- Market: Mastercard's first Taiwan Agent Pay was with DBS Taiwan (Mar 2026); Click to Pay with Union Bank (planned early 2026); Mastercard–NCCC security summit (Apr 2026).

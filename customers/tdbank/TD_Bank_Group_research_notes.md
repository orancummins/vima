## 1. The company
- **Identity:** The Toronto-Dominion Bank (TSX/NYSE: TD), Toronto; founded 1855.
- **Scale:** sixth-largest bank in North America by assets, C$2.11tn; 28.2m clients; more than 14m active mobile users; more than 100,000 colleagues.
- **Q3 2026:** net income C$4.6bn reported, adjusted ROE 16.0%, CET1 14.3%.
- **Fiscal 2025:** adjusted net income C$15.0bn. The Schwab stake was sold for C$21.0bn, a gain of C$8.6bn.
- **Segments:**
  - Canadian Personal & Commercial Banking: Q3 net income C$2.1bn; 1,037 branches.
  - U.S. Banking: US$771m; 1,048 stores; about 10m clients.
  - Wealth Management & Insurance: C$841m.
  - Wholesale Banking (TD Securities, TD Cowen): C$743m.
- **Leadership:** Chair John MacIntyre (since Sep 2025); CEO Raymond Chun (since Feb 2025); CFO Kelvin Tran; CRO Ajai Bambawale; Leo Salom (U.S. Banking); Sona Mehta (Canadian Personal Banking); Barbara Hooper (Canadian Commercial Banking); Paul Clark (Wealth & Insurance); Tim Wiggan (Wholesale).
  - From Sep 2026, Renu Gupta, Chief Strategy and Commercial Officer, owns payments and digital assets, and Vlad Shpilsky runs technology. COO Taylan Turan left.
- **US AML:**
  - Guilty plea on 10 Oct 2024; US$3.09bn paid in all, including a US$450m OCC penalty.
  - OCC asset cap of US$434bn on TD Bank, N.A. and TD Bank USA, N.A., still in force ⚠.
  - Remediation about US$550m in fiscal 2026; the SAR look-back runs into 2027. FINTRAC is reviewing the Canadian programme.
- **Strategy:**
  - Adjusted ROE about 16% by fiscal 2029; EPS growth of 7–10%; C$2.0–2.5bn of savings; C$1bn of AI value ⚠.
  - US point-of-sale finance (US$3bn) in run-off.
  - Part of TD Merchant Solutions sold to Fiserv, which now provides the technology and Clover (Jul 2025).
- **Digital money:**
  - CAD tokenised deposits with five other banks (22 Sep 2026).
  - USD stablecoin consortium (Sep 2026).
  - Project Agorá test (31 Aug 2026).
  - QCAD reserve custody (Jul 2026).

## 2. TD × Mastercard
- **On Visa:**
  - TD credit cards in Canada: Aeroplan (primary issuer since Jan 2014), First Class Travel, Cash Back, US Dollar, business.
  - TD Bank US credit, debit, business and gift cards.
  - Nordstrom cards (servicing moved to TD in Feb 2026).
- **Interac:** in-person debit on the TD Access Card, which adds Visa Debit; e-Transfer; identity verification.
- **On Mastercard:**
  - MBNA in Canada: True Line, Rewards, Smart Cash and the Amazon.ca Rewards Mastercard. MBNA was the largest Canadian Mastercard issuer when TD bought it in 2011. The MBNA site also lists Visa benefits ⚠.
  - The Target Mastercard, issued by TD Bank USA. It was Visa when TD bought the portfolio in 2013; the date of the switch is not found ⚠.
  - Maestro has carried US PIN debit and prepaid since 2016, under a July 2015 deal (Ryan Bailey, TD; Chris McWilton, Mastercard). Current status ⚠.
- No other joint initiatives with Mastercard from 2020 to 2026 were found (Click to Pay, Agent Pay, Move, fraud tools) ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- AML and scam intelligence for a bank under an asset cap.
- Issuer, offers and subscription tools for MBNA, Amazon.ca and Target.
- Agent Pay as TD scales AI.
- Mastercard Move for TD Global Transfer.
- Multi-token links for CAD deposit tokens and the stablecoin.
- Commercial and virtual cards, where TD is Visa and BMO launched with Mastercard in Oct 2026.

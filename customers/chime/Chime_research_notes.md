# Chime — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Chime_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Chime × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Chime's own (Q2 2026 results, 5 Aug 2026; Q1 2026, 6 May 2026; FY2025, 26 Feb 2026; IPO prospectus, 11 Jun 2025) unless marked. Chime's investor site and parts of its help centre could not be read. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are due 4 November 2026.

## 1. The company
- Chime Financial, Inc. (Nasdaq: CHYM; IPO at $27, 12 Jun 2025). San Francisco. Founded 2012 as 1debit by Chris Britt and Ryan King. Not a bank: The Bancorp Bank, N.A. and Stride Bank, N.A. hold deposits and issue cards. Market value about $11.2bn ⚠; about 1,500 staff ⚠; no public credit ratings found ⚠.
- Q2 2026: revenue $670m (+27%; payments $430m, platform-related $240m), net income $28m, adj. EBITDA $102m, 10.4m active members (+20%), ARPAM $260, purchase volume $38bn. FY2025: revenue $2.2bn, net loss $1.0bn (IPO stock comp), 9.5m members, PV $134bn. FY2026 guidance raised to $2.76–2.77bn (Sep 2026).
- Leadership: Chris Britt (Chair, CEO), Mark Troughton (President, interim CFO from 7 Aug 2026), Janelle Sallenave (COO), Vineet Mehra (CGO), Adam Frankel (GC). CFO Matt Newcomb left; search under way. 10% job cut, 31 Jul 2026.
- Own stack: ChimeCore processor and ledger; Galileo exited Q4 2025 (about 60% lower processing cost). 84% AI-assisted code (Q1 2026).
- Products: Visa debit, Chime Card (secured Visa credit; 27% of PV), SpotMe ($200), MyPay (up to $750; $4.5bn originated in Q2 2026), Instant Loans ($300m in Q2 2026), Chime Plus and Chime Prime (Apr 2026; 5% cash back, Visa Signature perks), Chime Invest (Jul 2026, with Atomic), Chime Workplace (Mar 2025, after Salt Labs), OIT (1.75% fee ⚠).
- Stride Bank: $590m cash deal (8 Sep 2026) to become Chime Bank, N.A.; OCC and Fed approval; close 1H 2027; assets kept under $10bn (Durbin-exempt).
- Oversight: CFPB order May 2024 ($3.25m penalty, $1.3m+ redress); breach class actions from Apr 2026, disputed by Chime.

## 2. Chime × Mastercard
- Debit, credit, Prime perks and ATMs (Visa Plus Alliance, Allpoint) are all Visa. Network pricing is "favourable" and undisclosed; STAR named as an example debit network ⚠.
- No Chime–Mastercard programme, pilot or switch found, 2015–2026.
- Edges only: funding from linked Mastercard debit cards ⚠; OIT rail not disclosed ⚠ (pushes to Mastercard cards would use Mastercard Send).
- US market: Visa about 70% of combined Visa and Mastercard US card volume in 2025 (Nilson).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- The network decision at Chime Bank (2027), with Chime owning issuer and processor.
- Mastercard Move for OIT, MyPay and Chime Workplace payouts.
- Identity and scam tools after the 2026 outage and breach claims.
- Open finance for cash-flow underwriting of MyPay and Instant Loans.
- World Elite and Priceless benefits for Chime Prime; Agent Pay for an AI-first company.

# Banco Pichincha — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Banco_Pichincha_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Pichincha × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (audited 2025 annual financial report, February 2026; BankWatch rating extract with March 2026 data, May 2026) unless marked. The bank is unlisted, has no investor-relations site, publishes no quarterly deck or segment results, and publishes almost nothing in English; sources are Spanish and translated. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary or older sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Banco Pichincha C.A., Quito, founded 1906; universal bank since 2017. Unlisted; controlled by the family of Fidel Egas Grijalva ⚠. Local AAA (PCR, BankWatch); Fitch raised it from CCC+ to B in June 2026.
- Bank 2025: assets $21.5bn, deposits $17.7bn, net loans $12.3bn, net profit $240.5m (2024: $150.3m), solvency 16.5%. March 2026: assets $22.3bn, about 28% of the banking system; Q1 profit $62.4m. Group (with Spain, Colombia, Miami): assets $27.0bn, profit $236.9m.
- About 6.5m customers (over 36% of Ecuadorians), 5.5m active digital users, 83% of monetary transactions digital, 8,500+ Mi Vecino agents, branches in every canton.
- Subsidiaries: Deuna (payments operator), Pague Ya, Vaserum, Almesa, Teuno; Banco Pichincha España (78.9%), Banco Pichincha Colombia (91.1%, loss-making; Pibank ⚠), Agencia Miami. Associations with Diners Club (to 2029), BGR and Banco de Loja.
- Leadership: Alejandro Ribadeneira Jaramillo, general manager from 27 Feb 2026 (Santiago Bayas to the board); chair Antonio Acosta Espinosa ⚠; VP Finance Xavier Donoso; payments and remittances manager Gandhi Trujillo.
- No published targets or AI programme ⚠. Agricultural book $2.5bn with Rabobank to 2028.

## 2. Pichincha × Mastercard
- Debit: all Visa, including the digital debit card in Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- Consumer credit: six Visa tiers (Infinite down) and five Mastercard tiers (Black, Platinum, Gold, Premium, Joven); Quantum on either. The bank holds both licences as issuer, processor and administrator.
- Corporate: Visa Empresarial, Corporativa, CTA, Prepago; Mastercard Corporativa, Black Corporate, Business Black, Black Corporativa. Microfinance cards on both.
- Acquiring 2025 merchant commissions: Visa $40.8m, Mastercard $12.6m (about 76/24), Discover $26k. Processor not published ⚠.
- 2026 campaigns: Mastercard Father's Day with Priceless experiences; Spend And Get Mastercard (31 Jul–6 Oct); Visa World Cup sticker album for new Visa credit cards.
- Visa Direct international transfers in the app from 24 Sep 2026 (up to $15,000).
- No public Mastercard tokenisation, Click to Pay, Send/Move, fraud or open-finance initiative found ⚠; no network switch 2015–26 found ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- A second remittance rail beside Visa Direct; a Mastercard digital debit option; virtual corporate cards and spend controls; card-linked offers for Pichincha Miles; QR and instant-payment services as the central bank's real-time standard lands (30 Nov 2026); fraud scoring for a bank with 83% digital transactions.

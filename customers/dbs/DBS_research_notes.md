## 1. The company
- DBS Group Holdings Ltd (SGX: D05), Singapore; founded 1968 as the Development Bank of Singapore; POSB is the mass-market brand. Present in 19 markets; about 39,900 staff.
- 2Q26: record net profit S$3.08bn (+9%), income S$6.09bn, ROE 17.9%, CET1 16.6%, cost-income 39%; deposits S$638bn, loans S$469bn, assets S$965.6bn; wealth assets S$516bn. FY2025: net profit S$11.0bn (−3%, global minimum tax), ROE 16.2%; card fees S$1,225m (−1%). Market cap US$124bn (end-2025).
- Segments: Consumer Banking/Wealth Management, Institutional Banking, Markets Trading. Geography: Singapore, Hong Kong, rest of Greater China, South and Southeast Asia.
- Leadership: Tan Su Shan (CEO since 28 Mar 2025; on Mastercard's APAC Advisory Board), Peter Seah (chairman; succession reported Aug 2026), Chng Sok Hui (CFO), Shee Tse Koon (consumer and wealth), Lim Soon Chong (GTS). Country heads change Dec 2026–Mar 2027.
- Strategy: ROE 15–17%; S$8bn capital return 2025–27; >S$1tn wealth and retail assets ambition; about S$1bn of AI value in 2025.
- Deals: Citi Taiwan consumer (Aug 2023; 3m+ card accounts); Shenzhen Rural Commercial Bank to 19.9%; India card partnerships exited (2025).
- MAS: capital multiplier 1.5x (2022), 1.8x (May 2023, about S$1.6bn); pause ended Apr 2024 ⚠ current status unknown.

## 2. DBS × Mastercard
- Singapore credit: 5 Visa, 5 Mastercard, 3 Amex. Visa: Altitude, Vantage, Chromo (replaced Live Fresh ⚠). Mastercard: Woman's and Woman's World, POSB Everyday (⚠ possible switch from Visa), Esso, SAFRA.
- Singapore debit: 3 Visa (DBS Visa Debit, TravelLah!, Takashimaya), 2 Mastercard (PAssion, SAFRA), 1 UnionPay.
- Hong Kong: Black World and Live Fresh on Mastercard; Eminent and COMPASS on Visa. Taiwan: World Elite travel card ⚠ Mastercard, Visa Signature and Infinite tiers ⚠.
- Joint work: Mastercard Installments with Pine Labs (Mar 2022 ⚠ status); Gamer Exchange (2025); Agent Pay live in Singapore (4 Mar 2026) and Hong Kong (27 Mar 2026); Black World hotel conversion (27 Apr 2026).
- Visa: first APAC bank on Visa Intelligent Commerce (16 Feb 2026). Account-to-account: PayLah! (3m+ users, 60% of hawker QR), PayNow (60% of cross-border transfers), Alipay+ and Weixin Pay links. Tokens: Token Services, Kinexys framework, Partior, DDEx (RLUSD), tokenised gold.

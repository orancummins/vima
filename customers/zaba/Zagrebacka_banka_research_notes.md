## 1. The company
- Zagrebačka banka d.d., Trg bana Josipa Jelačića 10, Zagreb. Listed in Zagreb (ZABA) since 1995. Part of UniCredit since March 2002; UniCredit holds about 96.2% ⚠ (registry aggregator). Fitch A- (stable), viability bbb.
- Heritage: Gradska štedionica, 1914; first joint-stock bank in former Yugoslavia (1989); London GDRs (1996); first euro-denominated GDRs (1998).
- H1 2026 (bank): net profit €251m (−2.7%), operating income €417m, NII €278m, net fees €109m (+16.0%), cost/income 28.3%; assets €23.6bn, loans €13.7bn (+6.6% in six months), deposits €19.1bn; 3,145 staff. Group H1 2026: €301m.
- FY2025 (group): net profit €572m (+2.9%), operating income €1,042m, assets €28.4bn; about €448m of dividends paid. Bank alone €510m ⚠ (snippet).
- Market: 25.8% of assets, 26.2% of resident deposits, 29.0% of income (31 Mar 2026). 680,000+ m-zaba and 690,000+ e-zaba users; 100+ locations, 700+ ATMs; 1.3m+ account holders (Nov 2024). Total customer count not published ⚠.
- Group: UniCredit Bank Mostar (Bosnia), UniCredit Invest (ex-ZB Invest, renamed 30 Apr 2026, ~32% fund share), UniCredit Leasing Croatia, ZANE; Allianz ZB pensions (~38%).
- Board: Dalibor Ćubela (CEO since Jul 2024), Slaven Rukavina (Retail), Aleksandra Rašić (Corporate), Jasna Mandac (Finance), Ivana Lonjak Dam (Risk), Stefano Gison (Banking support). Jana Novina Cukrov, Director of Payments.
- 2026 launches: single-use virtual card (Jan), mini bonds (~€60m, nine issues), DealSync (Apr), Prime (May), Bitcoin ETF certificate (Jun), Nekretninski puls. Euromoney best bank in Croatia 2026. No Zaba-specific targets; UniCredit Unlimited is the frame ⚠.

## 2. Zaba × Mastercard
- Debit: about two million debit cards moved from Visa to Mastercard, announced May 2024; private customers through early 2025, business from June ⚠. Mastercard to be the only brand on Zaba cards during 2025. Mastercard's Croatia manager named as Gea Kariž ⚠.
- Credit: Standard, Platinum, Student, Gold and go!card (revolving) — all Mastercard. Business Mastercard, go! business and Business Mastercard Plus.
- Single-use virtual card on the Mastercard debit card (27 Jan 2026). Apple Pay, Google Pay, Garmin Pay (first in Croatia ⚠ date); m-novčanik phone wallet on Mastercard and Maestro from Feb 2019 ⚠ current status.
- Priceless ("Mastercard. Vrijedi više."), LoungeKey lounges on Platinum and Business (Gold dropped 1 Sep 2026), Zaba Mastercard merchant offers, MultiPlusCard points.
- Own instalments: 2–36 interest-free at 25,000+ merchants; split-later in m-zaba.
- Acquiring is network-neutral: Mastercard, Visa (plus Maestro, Diners on Fiskal POS); SoftPOS, Fiskal POS, gateways Corvus Pay, Monri, HT PayWay, mstart.
- Frame: UniCredit–Mastercard multi-market deal, 26 May 2023 (13 banks, ~20m cards) ⚠ Zaba not named in the source.
- Not found (not proof of absence ⚠): Click to Pay, Send/Move, Agent Pay, Mastercard fraud tools, virtual commercial cards.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Scam scoring for IziPay and SEPA Instant (mandatory since 9 Oct 2025), backed by Zaba's own fraud campaign (55% failed its scam quiz; €20m+ Croatian cyber-fraud losses in 2025). Click to Pay beside the single-use virtual card. Installments online. Gateway, MATCH and tap-on-phone tools for a tourism-heavy acquiring book. Virtual commercial cards and Business Payment Controls. Card-linked offers. Agent Pay later.

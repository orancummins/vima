## 1. The company
- Industrial and Commercial Bank of China Limited (SSE: 601398; HKEX: 1398). Founded 1984, listed Oct 2006. Central Huijin 34.79%, Ministry of Finance c.31%. Market cap c.US$345bn (Oct 2026 ⚠ third-party). S&P A, Moody's A1. G-SIB Bucket 3; first in The Banker's Top 1000 for 13 years.
- H1 2026: operating income RMB446.2bn (+9.1%), net profit RMB176.5bn (+4.5%), total assets RMB57.07trn, loans RMB31.99trn, deposits RMB39.17trn, NIM 1.29%, ROE 8.63%, NPL 1.29%, CET1 13.21%. FY2025: net profit RMB370.8bn, assets RMB53.48trn. ⚠ China Daily quotes "operating revenue" of RMB465.86bn; the report says RMB446.2bn.
- 780m+ personal and 15m+ corporate customers; 402,548 employees; 15,426 outlets; 398 overseas institutions in 49 countries; 19.74% of Standard Bank.
- Segments: Corporate banking (48.6% of H1 income), Personal banking (36.8%), Treasury operations (14.3%), Others.
- Leadership: Liao Lin (Chairman), Liu Jun (Vice Chairman and President), Wang Jingwu (CRO), Yao Mingde (CFO), Zhang Weiwu, Zhang Shouchuan, Zhao Guide (senior EVPs).
- AI-ICBC: LLMs in 600+ scenarios, two super agents, AI doing the work of c.55,000 people (2025); credit-card approval assistant used 2.82m times in H1 2026. FinTech spend RMB28.6bn (2025).
- Cards: 1,338m (1,196m debit, 143m credit) at 30 Jun 2026; credit balances fell from RMB698bn to RMB597bn in the half. Mobile banking: 649m customers, 300m+ MAUs.

## 2. ICBC × Mastercard
- 1995: first Mastercard co-brand issuer in China (Ling Hai, Mastercard, 2017).
- Feb 2017: ICBC Sands Lifestyle Mastercard (ICBC (Asia) and ICBC (Macau)); first Pay with Rewards in Asia Pacific.
- Nov 2023: Mastercard NUCC licensed for domestic clearing; opened 9 May 2024. ICBC launched a China Mastercard RMB/USD card (10 foreign currencies) at opening.
- 2025: Mastercard "10+1" multi-currency domestic debit card, "first of its kind" (Annual Report).
- 3 Feb 2026: ICBC European Travel co-branded credit card with the European Travel Commission and Mastercard (Ailish Campbell, Eduardo Santander). Details ⚠ from press summary.
- Peony International Debit Card on Visa and Mastercard. ICBC (Asia): Platinum Mastercard, Mastercard Corporate Card, virtual cards, Click to Pay; debit UnionPay. ICBC (Macau): MARTS Mastercard. ICBC (Argentina): Visa and Mastercard. ICBC USA (2016): UnionPay and Visa.
- Domestic debit and most credit on UnionPay ⚠ inferred. Visa planned terminals with ICBC in the Beijing Visa–UnionPay zone (Mar 2025 ⚠ plan). ICBC supports Weixin Pay for overseas UnionPay cards (Nov 2024).
- Not found: tokenisation, fraud tools, Send/Move, open finance or Agent Pay with ICBC.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- More outbound travel cards and Priceless content inside ICBC Travel; acceptance for inbound visitors; data and offers for a shrinking credit book; scoring on cross-border card fraud; commercial cards for 15m firms; Agent Pay for ICBC's agents; deeper issuing at ICBC (Asia).

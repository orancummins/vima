## 1. The company
- Nu Holdings Ltd. (Cayman; NYSE: NU; B3: ROXO34), operating from São Paulo; founded May 2013 by David Vélez, Cristina Junqueira and Edward Wible; first card transaction 1 Apr 2014; IPO 9 Dec 2021. Market cap $70.33bn at $14.56 (16 Oct 2026). S&P BB- / Moody's Ba2 (holding) ⚠ undated. About 10,000 staff ⚠ Wikipedia.
- Q2 2026: 139m customers (Brazil c.118m, Mexico 15.8m, Colombia 5m+); revenue c.$5.9bn; net income $1.06bn (first $1bn+ quarter); ROE 33%; credit book $39.4bn (cards $26.0bn); deposits $45.3bn; NPL 90+ 6.9%; gross profit mix credit 41%, fees 25%, float 34%. FY2025: revenue $16.3bn, net income $2.9bn.
- Licences: Mexico a bank from 6 Aug 2026; Brazil buying Banco Porto Real (Jul 2026, pending BCB); Colombia financing company (2024); US: OCC conditional approval for Nubank, N.A. (29 Jan 2026), Lead Bank meanwhile. Argentina licence gone from the 2025 20-F ⚠.
- Leadership: Vélez (CEO), Junqueira (Nu US), Livia Chanes (LatAm, Jul 2026), Rob Livingston (CFO from 13 Jul 2026; 12+ years at Visa), Armando Herrera (Mexico), Marcela Torres (Colombia), Eric Young (CTO).
- Strategy: Brazil first (R$45bn in 2026), up-market with Croma and Ultravioleta, US spend capped at 100bp of efficiency ratio, nuFormer AI models, AI Private Banker (15m+ MAU), $1bn buyback, sponsorships (Nu Stadium, Mercedes-AMG F1, Nubank Parque).

## 2. Nu × Mastercard
- Mastercard was the only network willing to engage in 2013; card shipped in about six months (NeoFeed, 2019).
- Network by programme: Brazil credit, debit (Mastercard), Ultravioleta (Mastercard Black, 2021), Croma (Mastercard Platinum, 28 Jul 2026); Mexico credit (2020) and debit (May 2023) Mastercard; Colombia Mastercard Gold/Black (licence Apr 2020), debit via Mastercard ATM network ⚠; US Nu Credit Card, World Elite, 'exclusive relationship with Mastercard' (10 Sep 2026); Nu Global virtual Mastercard over USDC/EURC. Nubank+, SME, under-18 and US debit networks not stated ⚠.
- No Visa, Elo or Amex programme found ⚠ limited search. Agreements found are trademark licences (Brazil 2014, Mexico 2019/2022, Colombia 2020) ⚠ exhibits not read; commercial terms missing.
- Not found: Click to Pay, Mastercard Installments, Send/Move, fraud, open finance or acquiring services; Nu's role in Agent Pay LAC (Dec 2025) unconfirmed ⚠. Pay by Tap tokenises a virtual card but names no token service ⚠.
- Substitutes: Pix (179.7m users; Nu c.20% of originations ⚠), Pix financing (12x), Pay by Tap, NuPay (via PPRO, EBANX, Uber), SPEI, Bre-B, open-finance initiation (#1 in 2025 ⚠ varies by quarter), stablecoins and crypto (Fireblocks).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay under Nu's AI; Mastercard tokens under Pay by Tap and NuPay; scam and A2A fraud tools for Pix, SPEI and Bre-B; Move and stablecoin settlement for Nu Global; open finance in Mexico, Colombia and the US; Tap on Phone and SME cards; Priceless and World benefits for Croma, Ultravioleta and US customers; debit conversion from cash in Mexico and Colombia.

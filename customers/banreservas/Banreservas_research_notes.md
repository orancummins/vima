# Banreservas — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Banreservas_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Banreservas × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (Memoria Institucional 2025, the bilingual annual report with the 2025 accounts) and the live card catalogue on banreservas.com unless marked. No 2026 half-year report or English investor material was found; 2026 figures come from the Dominican press. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Banco de Reservas de la República Dominicana – Banco de Servicios Múltiples. Founded 1941; wholly state-owned under Organic Law 13-24 (April 2024). Not listed. Moody's deposits Ba2 (upgraded 2025); local AAA ⚠.
- FY2025: assets RD$1,281.9bn (35.2% of multiple-bank assets), gross loans RD$628.1bn (31% share), deposits RD$1,045.9bn, net profit RD$25.45bn (−7.7%), ROE 20.8%, solvency 19.85%, NPLs 1.29%. Assets RD$1,352.8bn at Sep 2025; no 2026 balance sheet confirmed ⚠.
- About 8m customers; 1.467m active credit cards (33.4% share); 3.85m active debit cards (47.3%); app 13.7m downloads, 1.9m transacting users; 458 locations, 971 ATMs, 1,300+ sub-agents. More than 10,000 staff ⚠ no exact headcount.
- Business areas: personal and SME, corporate and investment, government, tourism (about 44% of system tourism lending), treasury. Subsidiaries: Seguros, ARS, AFP, AFI, Inversiones & Reservas, Fiduciaria, Inmobiliaria Reservas, Fundación Reservas del País.
- Leadership: Leonardo Aguilera Batista, Executive President since 17 Aug 2025 (replaced Samuel Pereyra); board chaired ex officio by Finance and Economy Minister Magín Díaz; vice-chair Jean Antonio Haché.
- Strategy: productive credit, inclusion (Bancarizar es Patria, a million banked since 2021), digital and AI (Alma assistant, 15 bots, 88.3% digital share). No numeric targets published ⚠.

## 2. Banreservas × Mastercard
- Debit: all personal and business debit cards in the live catalogue are Mastercard (Standard, Platinum, USD, Negocios). A Visa/Electron licence remains in the accounts ⚠.
- Credit: Visa and Mastercard on most tiers (Classic/Standard, Gold, Platinum); Visa Infinite, Platinum Universe and SER; Mastercard Black. Network split not published ⚠. Preserva's network not stated ⚠.
- Commercial: Visa Corporativa, Empresarial, Negocios and Flotilla; Mastercard Comercial launched 5 Oct 2026 by Leonardo Aguilera and Tomás Alonso (Mastercard country manager, DR and Haiti) with Easy Savings, Entrepreneur's Odyssey and My Cyber Risk.
- MIO Banreservas e-wallet: Visa at launch (Nov 2022); Mastercard credential for new clients from mid-2025.
- Mastercard named among Bancarizar es Patria partners; Girls 4 Tech; promotions and the 2021 FEDOGOLF alliance.
- Not found: Click to Pay, Send/Move, fraud tools, Agent Pay, open-finance products ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Fleet and corporate cards (Visa-held); fraud and identity on 700,000+ new wallet tokens; Move for US$1.65bn of remittances and prepaid for RD$21.8bn of state grants; acceptance as the central bank's QR instant payments arrive (H1 2027) and Banco Popular's Toke competes with cash; card-linked offers for Puntos Banreservas; Agent Pay for Alma.

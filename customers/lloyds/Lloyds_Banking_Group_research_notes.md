## 1. The company
- Lloyds Banking Group plc (LSE: LLOY; NYSE ADR: LYG). c.28m customers (c.50% of UK adults), c.1m business relationships, c.22m app users. H1 2026: statutory profit after tax £3.1bn, RoTE 17.1%, CET1 13.6%, loans £491.5bn, deposits £500.9bn. FY2025: profit after tax £4.76bn, RoTE 12.9%.
- Segments: Retail; Commercial Banking (BCB and CIB); Insurance, Pensions & Investments; Equity Investments & Central Items.
- Brands: Lloyds, Halifax (retiring into Lloyds from 2027 ⚠), Bank of Scotland, MBNA, Scottish Widows, Lloyds Wealth, Lloyds Auto (Black Horse, Lex Autolease, Tusker; Oct 2026), LDC, Lloyds Living, Curve.
- Leadership: Robin Budenberg (Chair), Charlie Nunn (CEO), William Chalmers (CFO), Jas Singh (Consumer), Nick Williams, Amanda Murphy, John Langley.
- Accelerate 2030: RoTE >18% 2028, c.20% 2030, cost:income <45%. AI: £50m value 2025, >£100m 2026; agentic AI assistant.
- Motor finance redress provision £1.95bn.

## 2. Lloyds × Mastercard
- Debit on Visa (~40 years; 30m+ accounts). June 2024 Visa deal: ~10m credit cards (Lloyds, Halifax, Bank of Scotland, MBNA; consumer and commercial) to move from Mastercard to Visa by end-2026.
- Curve (Mastercard issuer; 6m+ customers) acquired, completed May 2026; Smart Wallet launching H2 2026.
- PayFrom Bank on Mastercard Open Banking Connect (Jul 2021) ⚠ status.
- Consumer Fraud Risk launch bank (Jul 2023) ⚠ current use.
- Visa USDC cross-border settlement pilot (30 Sep 2026).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Curve-based Smart Wallet; stablecoin and cross-border settlement; Agent Pay; expanded scam and identity tools; card-linked offers for Connected Commerce; fleet and virtual cards for Lloyds Auto; open banking for SMEs.

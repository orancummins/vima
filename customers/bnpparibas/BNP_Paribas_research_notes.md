# BNP Paribas — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `BNP_Paribas_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BNP Paribas × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (2Q26 results, 23 Jul 2026; 4Q25 results, 5 Feb 2026; 2025 URD, 19 Mar 2026) unless marked. group.bnpparibas, mabanque.bnpparibas, hellobank.fr and mastercard.com were blocked; statements that rest on search snippets are marked ⚠. 3Q26 results not yet published.

## 1. The company
- BNP Paribas SA (Euronext Paris; CAC 40). Created 23 May 2000 from BNP and Paribas; BNL (2006), Fortis and BGL (2009), Nickel (2017), Bank of the West sold (2023), AXA IM (2025). >180,000 FTE in 64 countries. Market cap €90.2bn at end-2025 (no Oct 2026 figure ⚠).
- 2Q26: revenue €14.1bn, net income €4.3bn (incl. €858m Ageas/AGI gain), RoTE 13.3%, CET1 13.0%; loans €939.4bn, deposits €1,161bn. FY2025: revenue €51.2bn, net income €12.2bn.
- Divisions: CIB; CPBS (France, BNL, Belgium, Luxembourg, Europe-Mediterranean, Personal Finance, Arval and Leasing, Nickel, Floa, Lyf, Personal Investors); IPS (Cardif, Asset Management incl. AXA IM, Wealth).
- Leadership: Jean Lemierre (Chair), Jean-Laurent Bonnafé (CEO), Yann Gérardin and Thierry Laborde (COOs; Laborde on the EPI board), Lars Machenil (CFO), Marc Camus (CIO). Later changes not verified ⚠.
- Targets: RoTE 12% (2026), >13% (2028); cost:income <56% (2028); 2027–30 plan due 2 Feb 2027.
- AI: Mistral AI partner; 7,500+ developers on GenAI; "My companion"; aiming at "the agentic era". 2,068 APIs.
- Deals: Estreem JV with BPCE (Feb 2025); Athlon to Arval ⚠ completion; BMCI sale 4Q26; MiCAR euro stablecoin consortium ⚠ name.

## 2. BNP Paribas × Mastercard
- France: all cards CB + Visa; Mastercard Cirrus card discontinued 12 Dec 2025. Hello bank! France believed Visa ⚠.
- Belgium: Fortis, Fintro and Hello bank! debit Bancontact + Visa Debit since 2021. Fortis credit network not verified ⚠.
- Mastercard: Nickel (since 2014; UCL designs), Cpay (2019 ⚠), Consors Finanz, Floa, BNL debit and credit (Amex alongside), BGL premium credit, Bank Polska (Mastercard-led, Priceless "Bezcenne Chwile"), BGL "Assurance retour colis". TEB mixed ⚠.
- Estreem processes V, MC and CB (17bn transactions a year, c.30% of French card payments).
- No group network contract found; no public BNP Paribas announcement on MDES, Click to Pay, Installments, Move, Open Banking, fraud tools, Agent Pay, commercial cards or acquiring (no evidence, not proof of absence).
- Wero: 57m users; e-commerce for all French customers Sep 2026; Fortis e-commerce Feb 2026; BGL Payconiq off 30 Sep 2026; point of sale next.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay and tokens (only 23% of Belgians trust AI payments); services built into Estreem ⚠ hypothesis; fraud scoring for instant payments; instalments on Mastercard credentials (Personal Finance, Floa); Business debit for Nickel Compte Pro; fleet cards for Arval; Move and virtual cards for cash management. Watch Fortis debit, without assuming a renewal window.

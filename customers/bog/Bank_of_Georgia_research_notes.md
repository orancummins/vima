Figures are Lion Finance Group's own (2Q26 and 1H26 results, 11 Aug 2026; 4Q25 and FY25 results, 25 Feb 2026) unless marked. ## 1. The company
- JSC Bank of Georgia, Tbilisi; parent Lion Finance Group PLC (LSE: BGEO, FTSE 100), renamed from Bank of Georgia Group PLC on 6 Feb 2025. Roots in 1903; bank formed 1994; LSE IPO 2007; Georgia Capital demerged 2018.
- 1H26: profit GEL 1,203.8m (+17.3%; ROAE 27.2%), Georgia GEL 925.1m, Armenia GEL 272.5m; loans GEL 44.4bn, deposits GEL 43.7bn, assets GEL 65.8bn. FY25 profit before one-offs GEL 2.19bn (ROAE 28.4%).
- About 40% of Georgian banking assets; systemically important; ranked second by assets after TBC on Wikipedia ⚠.
- Customers (June 2026): 2.28m active retail, 1.92m digital MAU, 1.05m daily users, 73% of retail products sold digitally; 136,400 active businesses; 206 branches; about 8,600 staff (13,300 in the group).
- Payments: 1.71m retail Payment MAU; 56.7% of Georgian acquiring volume; 28,800 merchants; 2Q26 acquiring volume GEL 6.5bn (POS 4.2bn, e-commerce 2.4bn).
- Segments: Retail (mass retail, SOLO premium), SME, CIB (with Galt & Taggart); Ameriabank (Armenia, #1 in loans at 22.9%); BNB (Belarus); Digital Area (e-commerce and SaaS).
- Leadership: Mel Carvill (Chair), Archil Gachechiladze (CEO of group and bank), Giorgi Shagidze (CFO from Mar 2026), Giorgi Gureshidze (mass retail), Levan Kobakhidze (Head of Payments from Jan 2026), Valerian Gabunia (premium from Jul 2026).
- Targets: about 15% loan growth, ROAE above 20%, 30–50% payout. Deals: Ameriabank (2024, about USD 303.6m), HSBC Malta bid (Jul 2025, no deal announced ⚠), Fina ERP (2025).
- AI: World's Best AI Bank 2026 (Global Finance, 15 Oct 2026); 99% of unsecured loans paid in under 15 seconds; AI assistant resolves 80%+ of queries at 91% satisfaction; generative AI since 2025.
- Funding: USD 300m 6.50% notes due 2031 (Ba2, May 2026); IFC USD 118m for MSMEs (Oct 2026).

## 2. Bank of Georgia × Mastercard
- Dual-network issuer: everyday and digital cards on Visa or Mastercard at the customer's choice ⚠; no split by network is public.
- SOLO Premium and International include both a Visa Platinum and a Mastercard World Elite (Feb 2025).
- American Express: exclusive issuing and acquiring partner since Dec 2008 ⚠ current exclusivity.
- Mastercard Corporate Card launched with the bank in 2025, the region's first premium business card ⚠ exact date.
- Business Mastercard software offers (20+ brands, to end-2025) ⚠ launch date; Priceless and cashback campaigns with Mastercard in 2025.
- Apple Pay since Sep 2019 on Visa and Mastercard. Google Pay status unconfirmed ⚠.
- 2025: better terms negotiated with the "international payment systems", cutting fee expense 17.7% y-o-y in 4Q25.
- Market: Visa has 1.9m active cards in Georgia (Jun 2026). The National Bank is building cardless, SEPA-compatible instant payments (due by end-2026) and a National Switch to reduce Visa's and Mastercard's role in lari transfers (Mar 2025); open banking APIs mandated. Mastercard's Georgia lead: David Zgudadze.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Merchant risk and data tools for the country's largest acquirer; Agent Pay for the AI assistant; account-to-account and open-banking services beside national instant payments; fraud and scam scoring; virtual and expense cards for SMEs; issuer tools at Ameriabank; cross-border and tourism flows.

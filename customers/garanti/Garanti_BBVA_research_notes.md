## 1. The company
- Türkiye Garanti Bankası A.Ş. (BIST: GARAN), founded in Ankara in 1946; Turkey's second-largest private bank. BBVA 85.97% since May 2022. Market value TL 581bn (about US$12.5bn) at end-June 2026. Fitch BB-/Stable, Moody's Ba2/Stable.
- 1H26: net income TL 64.4bn (+20%), ROAE 28.1%, assets TL 5.2tn, deposits TL 3.5tn, CAR 15.9%, NPL 3.5%. FY2025: net income TL 111.3bn, ROAE 29%.
- 31.2m customers, 18.3m mobile, 23,288 staff, 796 branches, 6,562 ATMs, 932,287 POS. 14.0m credit-card customers, 20.6m credit cards, 24.2m debit cards.
- #1 private bank in TL loans, consumer loans, mortgages, auto loans, card customers, issuing (17.4%) and acquiring (15.4%) volume. Payment systems are about two-thirds of fees.
- Leadership: Süleyman Sözen (Chair), Mahmut Akten (CEO), Ceren Acer Kezik (Retail; cards), Kerem Orbay (GM, Garanti BBVA Payment Systems), Cemal Onaran (Commercial), Sinem Edige (CIB).
- Romanian unit sold to Raiffeisen for €591m (Mar 2026; closing Q4 2026). TAMİ e-money institution live Nov 2024. Garanti BBVA Kripto in the app since Aug 2025.
- Strategy: BBVA 2025–29 plan, radical client perspective, AI (UGİ on an OpenAI-powered LLM, 9.1m users), TL 3.5tn sustainable-finance target.
- ⚠ Reported IFRS segment names not checked; the page uses the management structure.

## 2. Garanti BBVA × Mastercard
- Credit: Bonus core range (Classic, Gold, Platinum, Flexi, virtual), BJK and Trabzonspor co-brands and Miles&Smiles Platinum on Mastercard; Bonus Trink and Fenerbahçe on Visa ⚠; Troy versions of the main Bonus cards.
- Debit: Paracard range on Visa; Paracard Bonus Troy.
- Travel: Shop&Fly on Visa and Amex; Visa World Cup 2026 tie-in (Nov 2025).
- American Express: Garanti is Amex's licensed institution in Turkey, 20 years in Jul 2026.
- Joint firsts: tokenisation in BonusFlaş (Jul 2021), biometric card (Feb 2024 ⚠ network inferred), Masterpass merchant view (May 2024), e-export package for Mastercard commercial cards (May 2025), Bonus at 25 (Aug 2025), AI collections (Dec 2025; new NPL inflows down up to 15%), agentic e-commerce on Agent Pay principles (Dec 2025, not yet live for customers).
- Partnership described as more than 30 years old by Mastercard Turkey (Avşar Gürdal, Aug 2025; now Onur Faydacı).
- ⚠ No network split by count or volume, and no network contract or renewal dates are public.

## 3. Market and where Mastercard could land
- Troy: 90m cards and 25.3% of card spending in 2025 (18.3% in 2024), target 30%. FAST: 5.4bn transactions in 2025; limits TL 300,000 from 26 Aug 2026; Ödeme İste request-to-pay API (May 2026).
- Openings: Agent Pay for customers; card-linked offers on the eight-bank Bonus programme; debit now on Visa; acquirer and merchant data for the #1 private acquirer and TAMİ; commercial cards for SMEs; A2A tools beside FAST.

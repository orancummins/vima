# National Bank of Egypt — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `National_Bank_of_Egypt_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and NBE × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own as reported in the Egyptian press (2025 results, released after the general assembly and reported 5 October 2026, with figures to end-August 2026) unless marked. NBE's site (nbe.com.eg) is behind a firewall and could not be read; Mastercard's newsroom refused automated access. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- National Bank of Egypt, Cairo. Founded 25 June 1898; Egypt's central bank until 1960; nationalised 1960; wholly state-owned, not listed. Ratings track the sovereign (Fitch B stable ⚠; Moody's Caa1 ⚠). Staff 29,162 (2024); Forbes says 35,000+ ⚠.
- Scale (FY2025): net profit EGP 175.5bn (2024: 133.3bn), PBT EGP 312.5bn; assets EGP 9.2tn (+13%); deposits EGP 5.7tn; loans EGP 4.8tn (corporate 4.2tn, retail 438bn, SME 195bn); equity EGP 690bn; NPL 0.8%. End-Aug 2026: assets ~EGP 10.6tn, deposits 6.4tn, loans 5.8tn.
- Customers 21.7m (23.5m cited Nov 2025 ⚠); 8.4m debit and 1.9m credit cards; 7,572 ATMs; 758,000 POS ⚠ scale; 700+ branches and units.
- Structure: NBE; NBE (UK) (1982, PRA/FCA); branches in New York, Shanghai and Saudi Arabia (opened 16 Oct 2025); offices in Dubai (DIFC), Johannesburg, Addis Ababa; Al Ahly Capital Holding with Tamkeen and 75% of Momken (Mar 2021), now Al Ahly Momkn ⚠.
- Leadership: CEO Mohamed El-Etreby (Sep 2024, swap with Hisham Okasha); non-executive chairman Mohamed Barakat ⚠ (Wikipedia: Mohamed Kamal Al Din); deputy CEO Soha El-Turky; executive deputy chairman Yehia Abou El Fotouh.
- Strategy: digital transformation and regional expansion (DIFC, Iraq under study); AI across channels; a digital bank proposed but not pursued yet; EBRD US$100m MSME line (Dec 2025). No numerical targets published ⚠.

## 2. NBE × Mastercard
- Credit: parallel Visa and Mastercard ranges (Visa Classic to Infinite; Mastercard Standard, Titanium, Platinum, World, World Elite); FX limits raised Mar and May 2024. Network split not published ⚠.
- Mastercard co-brands: UEFA Champions League credit card (Feb 2024; trophy tour Feb 2026 with Mohamed Assem and Mohamed El-Etreby; draws Mar 2026), EgyptAir Mastercard.
- USD corporate debit card with Mastercard, 27 Jul 2026 (Soha El-Turky; Mohamed Assem).
- Mastercard Gateway: nbe.gateway.mastercard.com live (checked 17 Oct 2026); start date unknown ⚠.
- Debit: Meeza (mass, domestic), Visa debit (2026), Mastercard accepted in Phone Cash ⚠ no product page.
- Visa: debit range, Signature and Infinite, a USD credit card for dollar-certificate holders ⚠ about 2024.
- Not found: Click to Pay, Send/Move, instalments, fraud tools, Agent Pay, open finance.

## 3. Market
- InstaPay (Apr 2022): ~1.5bn transactions, EGP 2.9tn, 12.5m users in 2024; 0.1% fee since 1 Apr 2025; 1.47bn transactions worth EGP 4.33tn and 22m customers by end Q2 2026 ⚠ period basis. NBE carried 717m InstaPay transfers worth EGP 2.87tn in 2025.
- Meeza: national scheme; 17m cards and 22m wallets on its site ⚠ undated. Apple Pay since 10 Dec 2024 (NBE, Banque Misr, CIB). Gulf remittances via InstaPay approved Nov 2024.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Commercial and virtual cards for the EGP 4tn+ corporate and SME books; acquirer risk and merchant data on the POS estate and gateway; Move for Gulf remittances (NBE now in Saudi Arabia); tokens across wallets; scam and fraud scoring on instant payments.

Companion to `Ceska_sporitelna_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and ČS × Mastercard), condensed. ## 1. The company
- Česká spořitelna, a.s., Prague; founded 30 Dec 1991 as successor of the Czech State Savings Bank; savings-bank roots from 1825. Controlled by Erste Group Bank AG since 2000; not listed; Erste's exact stake not stated ⚠. Ratings not found ⚠.
- H1 2026: net profit CZK 14.3bn (12.8bn), ROE 19.4%, cost/income 41.5%, loans CZK 1,259bn (+9.5%), deposits CZK 1,655bn, total capital 19.5%, NPL 1.6%. 4.58m clients, almost 2.7m digital users, 4.53m active cards (239,760 credit), 332 branches, 1,501 ATMs, 9,175 staff. 28% of new mortgages. FY2025 figures not retrieved ⚠.
- Segments: Retail (H1 net profit CZK 7.6bn), Corporates (CZK 4.5bn), Group Markets (CZK 1.5bn), ALM & Local Corporate Center.
- Entities: George and George Business; Stavební spořitelna ČS; ČS penzijní společnost; Leasing and Factoring ČS; Dostupné bydlení ČS (695 affordable flats); Seed Starter fund.
- Board: Tomáš Salomon (CEO since 2016), Karel Mourek (risk), Daniela Pešková (retail), Martin Kobza (operations and IT), Attila Sánta (CFO, 2025), Norbert Hovančák (2025). Supervisory board chaired by Peter Bosek (Erste CEO).
- Strategy: clients' financial health; Hey George on OpenAI GPT-4o (Dec 2024); George Business migration by end-2026; crypto ETFs, direct bitcoin purchase being prepared ⚠. No ČS-level numeric targets found ⚠.
- Awards: Mastercard Banka roku 2024 and 2025; Euromoney Best Bank 2025 and Best Retail Bank 2026 in Czechia.

## 2. ČS × Mastercard
- Visa-only since 2023: announced 6 Apr 2023; about 15% of card clients were on Mastercard/Maestro; personal cards ended 31 Aug 2023, Premier, Private Banking and corporate cards 30 Nov 2023. Reason given: one network simplifies innovation, loyalty and discounts (spokesman Filip Hrubý).
- Visa Awards, 27 Apr 2023: ČS #1 Visa issuer in Czechia and #1 digital proposition (virtual cards); Daniela Pešková #1 woman in payments.
- Credit card network not named on its page; Visa inferred ⚠. Wallet tokens and Click to Pay presumably Visa ⚠.
- Acquiring: carved out in June 2016 into Global Payments s.r.o. (Global Payments and CaixaBank 51%, Erste 49% ⚠ current stake); accepts every scheme.
- No joint Mastercard programme found. Mastercard's name appears only on the Banka roku award.
- Group contrast: Erste Austria issues Debit Mastercard to everyone and the Miles & More Mastercard.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Open finance and pay-by-bank for George Business; identity and scam tools around Bank iD and instant transfers; merchant enrichment and Agent Pay for Hey George (ČSOB tested an AI purchase with Mastercard in May 2026 ⚠); merchant offers for Moneyback; acquirer risk tools via Global Payments; a bid at the next Visa renewal (date unknown ⚠). Card fee expense rose 17% in H1 2026.

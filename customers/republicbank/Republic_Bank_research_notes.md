# Republic Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Republic_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Republic × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Republic Financial Holdings' own (2025 Annual Report, year to 30 September 2025; quarterly releases to 30 June 2026) in TT$ unless marked. The shared web-search budget ran out part-way, so several items rest on one source. ⚠ marks items that could not be confirmed. FY2026 results are due in November 2026.

## 1. The company
- Republic Financial Holdings Limited (TTSE: RFHL), Port of Spain; Republic Bank Limited is the T&T bank. Market value about TT$18.4bn (16 Oct 2026). 7,293 staff (3,631 at Republic Bank); 16 countries.
- FY2025: profit TT$2.2bn (+9.8%), assets TT$127.1bn, ROE 15.0%, CAR 14.9% (Republic Bank 16.0%), credit card fees TT$372m. 9M FY2026: profit TT$1.52bn (+1.6%), assets TT$134.6bn, loans TT$77.4bn, deposits TT$105.3bn.
- Republic Bank: 38 branches, 130 ATMs at 83 locations, "the nation's largest credit card operator". Customer count not published ⚠.
- Group banks: Barbados, Guyana, Grenada, Eastern Caribbean, Suriname (from RBC, 2015), BVI, Cayman National (74.99% in 2019; 86.91% from Aug 2025), Ghana. Scotiabank Caribbean deal agreed Nov 2018 (US$123m).
- Leadership: Yashmid Karamath (Chairman, from early 2026), Nigel Baptiste (Group President and CEO; President and MD of Republic Bank), Marsha Mc Leod-Marshall (CFO), Houston Ross (Chief Information and Digital Transformation Officer), Karen Tom Yew (CRO).
- Strategy: digital transformation (cloud-first, digital onboarding across most banks by Jul 2026), regional connectivity, M&A; US$750m sustainable-finance goal (Sep 2026). No public AI programme ⚠.

## 2. Republic × Mastercard
- Credit: about 7 Visa programmes (Classic, Gold, Platinum, Signature, AAdvantage Visa Infinite, PriceSmart Visa Platinum, Visa Business) and 5 Mastercard (Classic, Platinum, AAdvantage, AAdvantage Gold, AAdvantage Corporate).
- Debit: Visa co-badged with LINX, domestic use only. Prepaid and gift: Visa (VTM network ⚠).
- Acquiring: terminals take Visa, Mastercard and LINX; Republic also signs Amex merchants; EPay gateway.
- Priceless Carnival with Mastercard and Tribe (Feb 2024; Danielle Dumas, Mastercard T&T). BVI Mastercard Black (Apr 2024; Dalton Fowles, Mastercard).
- Visa: PCI DSS listing on Visa's registry (May 2025; Jorge Salum). Barbados and Ghana credit on Visa.
- FY2025: "VISA/Mastercard incentives booked" lifted T&T other income. No network agreement published ⚠.
- Endcash (Mar 2021) is loaded from Visa or Mastercard cards; suspended for a time ⚠ date and status. Apple Pay/Google Pay in T&T unconfirmed ⚠; first card-in-wallet went live in Guyana.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Tokenised phone wallets and tokens behind Endcash; identity for RepublicOnboard; virtual and fleet commercial cards; acquirer risk tools; card-linked offers beyond Priceless; cross-border spend under US$ limits (halved Sep 2023); A2A tools as T&T builds a UPI-based instant-payment platform with NPCI International.

## 4. Market
- LINX carries domestic debit; cash still widely used; Payment Systems and Services Bill consultation (to Jun 2026); e-money issuers WamNow (Nov 2025) and Convenience Pay (Jun 2026). Competitors: First Citizens (Google Pay ⚠ date), Scotiabank, RBC. Market share not confirmed ⚠.

## 1. The company
- Al Rajhi Banking & Investment Corp. (Tadawul: 1120), Riyadh. Exchange house 1957, bank 1988; world's largest Islamic bank, 100% Shariah-compliant. Market cap SAR 395.4bn (30 Jun 2026); Moody's Aa3, S&P A, Fitch A; 25,000+ staff.
- H1 2026: net income SAR 13.76bn (+14.2%); assets SAR 1,055bn; financing SAR 762bn; deposits SAR 688bn; ROE 23.3%; C/I 22.7%; CASA 64%. FY2025: record SAR 24.8bn (+25.7%).
- About 22m customers (20.6m end-2025); 513 branches, 4,273 ATMs, 135 remittance centres; digital:manual 96:4; 22.6m instant payments a month.
- Market shares: mortgages 37.7%, personal finance 38.5%, auto lease 34.4%, credit cards 38.4% (2025, from 35.4%), POS terminals 43.5%, corporate lending 14.5%.
- Group: neoleap (payments, urpay 8m+ users), Emkan, Al Rajhi Capital (#1 broker), neotek (first final open-banking licence, 2026), Drahim (65%), Ejada, Atmaal, Tawtheeq; Malaysia, Jordan, Kuwait.
- Leadership: Abdullah bin Sulaiman Al Rajhi (Chairman), Waleed Al-Mogbel (MD & CEO), Abdulrahman Al-Fadda (CFO), Majed Al-Rajhi (Retail), Abdullah Alibrahim (neoleap CEO).
- Strategy: Harmonize the Group (2024–26), final year; new strategy to the Board by end-2026. 100% of applications cloud-ready; 409 APIs.

## 2. Al Rajhi × Mastercard
- Debit: mada on every account; premium debit (Signature, Mada Infinite) and business debit carry Visa ⚠.
- Credit, charge, cashback, AlFursan, flynas, Marriott Bonvoy (Dec 2024), prepaid, business and corporate cards: Visa on every page checked. No Mastercard-branded card found ⚠.
- Acquiring: POS licensed by mada, Visa and Mastercard; e-commerce accepts mada, Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay; MPGS is one of four gateways (with neoleap, Cybersource, Moyasar).
- urpay × Mastercard Move (24 Jun 2024; Abdullah Alibrahim and Adam Jones): transfers to 180+ countries.
- Visa: Visa Direct (2023; multi-year deal 2025 ⚠ date), AFC Asian Cup 2027 partnership (Jun 2026), Malaysia Visa credit cards (Jul 2026). Amex: POS acceptance and 4,500+ ATMs (Jan 2024).
- Market: Mastercard and mada brought Apple Pay to the Kingdom in 2019 ⚠; which token service carries Al Rajhi cards is not public.

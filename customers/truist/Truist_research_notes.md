## 1. The company
- Truist Financial Corporation (NYSE: TFC), Charlotte NC. Formed late 2019 from BB&T and SunTrust; Truist Bank chartered 1872. Top-10 US commercial bank; market value about $56bn (16 Oct 2026 ⚠ third-party).
- 3Q26: total assets $554bn, loans $323.9bn, deposits $413.2bn, CET1 10.7%; EPS $0.56 reported / $1.16 adjusted (RAC sale and $1.4bn securities loss); adjusted ROTCE 14.6%. FY2025: net income to common $4.97bn, EPS $3.82, revenue $20.5bn, ROTCE 12.7%.
- Card and treasury management fees $1.36bn (2025); card spend $32bn in 3Q26; credit card loans about $4.9bn.
- Segments: Consumer & Small Business Banking; Wholesale Banking (commercial, corporate, Truist Securities, CRE, Wholesale Payments, Wealth); Other, Treasury & Corporate.
- 38,711 teammates (end-2025); 1,912 branches and 2,790 ATMs (3Q26); 5.4m active mobile users (2Q26). Total client count not published ⚠.
- Leadership: Mike Lyons CEO from 1 Sep 2026 (ex-Fiserv CEO, ex-PNC president); Bill Rogers executive chair to April 2027; CFO Mike Maguire; Kristin Lesher (wholesale). Oct 2026: Dontá Wilson leaves; Shimna Sameer (consumer), Jeff Warner (small business), Steve Boland (vice chair) named.
- Strategy: 15% ROTCE in 2027, 16–18% long term; franchise review; RAC auto book sold (3Q26); LightStream loans ($7.7bn) to Carlyle (agreed 14 Oct 2026). Earlier: Truist Insurance Holdings sold (May 2024), Sterling Capital (Jul 2024).
- AI: Truist Insights (2bn insights since 2023), Truist Assist (2.0m chats in 3Q26), Client Pulse pilot; chief AI & data officer Pascal Belaud (Nov 2025).

## 2. Truist × Mastercard
- Consumer debit: Visa. Delta SkyMiles debit: no new cards since 10 Nov 2025, ends 31 Oct 2026; network not stated ⚠.
- Consumer credit (Enjoy Cash, Beyond, Travel, Future, Secured): Visa. Small business: Business Cash Rewards and Business Premium Visa Infinite (Oct 2025). Gift cards: Visa.
- Commercial cards (One Card, Corporate, Purchasing, ePayables): Visa and Mastercard benefit guides; split not published ⚠.
- 2020: pre-conversion credit book was Visa and Mastercard (BB&T almost all Visa), so the SunTrust part likely carried Mastercard ⚠. TSYS processes credit cards (May 2020 deal).
- Open banking on Mastercard's open finance platform, Truist's first direct API integration (12 Feb 2026; Sherry Graziano, Bart Willaert).
- Truist Merchant Engage (Jul 2025) runs on Pollinate, which Mastercard invested in (2019) and partnered with (2020) ⚠ no Mastercard role in Truist's deployment is public.
- Truist co-owns Early Warning (Zelle, Paze; ZelleUSD stablecoin unveiled Jun 2026 ⚠). First alias-based Request for Payment on RTP (Apr 2025).
- No public network agreement, renewal or switch found for 2015–2026 ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Open finance beyond data sharing (income and account verification for thin-file lending, pay-by-bank); commercial and virtual cards as Enterprise Payments grows; acquirer risk and SMB tools in Merchant Engage; scam scoring for Zelle and RTP; Agent Pay for Truist Assist; cross-border against ZelleUSD; card-linked offers for Truist Deals.

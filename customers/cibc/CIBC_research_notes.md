## 1. The company
- Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce, a Schedule I bank headquartered in Toronto; TSX and NYSE: CM. Market value C$150.8bn (31 Jul 2026). Rated AA / AA / Aa2 / A+ on deposits (Morningstar DBRS, Fitch, Moody's, S&P).
- Q3 2026: revenue C$8.37bn; net income C$2.41bn reported, C$2.65bn adjusted (after a C$232m after-tax charge on the Caribbean sale); adjusted ROE 16.8%; CET1 13.4%; assets C$1,177.8bn. FY2025: revenue C$29.1bn, net income C$8.45bn (a record).
- About 15m clients; 51,711 FTE; 10m digitally registered clients. Branch count not found ⚠.
- Segments: Canadian Personal and Business Banking (with Simplii from 2025); Canadian Commercial Banking and Wealth Management (with Investor's Edge); U.S. Commercial Banking and Wealth Management (CIBC Bank USA); Capital Markets; Corporate and Other (CIBC Caribbean).
- Leadership: Harry Culham (CEO from 1 Nov 2025, after Victor Dodig), Katharine Stevenson (Chair), Robert Sedran (CFO), Hratch Panossian (Personal and Business Banking), Susan Rimmer (Commercial and Wealth), Kevin Li (U.S.), Christian Exshaw (Capital Markets), Jaime Tatis (Chief Data & AI Officer).
- Strategy: four priorities (mass affluent and private wealth, digital-first personal banking, connectivity, simplify and protect). Investor Day on 9 Dec 2026 will set new objectives.
- Deals: Costco card portfolio from Capital One (closed 4 Mar 2022; more than C$3bn of balances ⚠ secondary); Pollinate stake for Tyl by CIBC (Feb 2022; status ⚠); &Partners 19.15% (Jul 2026); CIBC Caribbean to Butterfield for about US$1.6bn (agreed May 2026, closing H1 2027).
- AI and tech: CAI bank-wide (May 2025), CAI 2.0 agentic workspace (Q3 2026), AdvisorAssist, DocuMind, CRTeX personalisation (Oct 2025); Microsoft Azure (2021), nCino (2022).
- Digital money: six-bank CAD tokenised-deposit project (22 Sep 2026); Project Agorá (29 Sep 2026).

## 2. CIBC × Mastercard
- Visa: about 20 personal credit cards (Aventura, Aeroplan, Dividend, Select, Classic), the business line (Aventura and Aeroplan for business, bizline, Corporate Classic Plus), Simplii Cash Back Visa, prepaid travel cards (2016 ⚠ status), Visa Debit on every CIBC debit card, Visa Direct in Global Money Transfer (Dec 2021), Visa Installments (Jun 2021; launch ⚠).
- Mastercard: CIBC Costco Mastercard and Costco Business Mastercard (since Mar 2022); Adapta Mastercard with Touch Card (1 May 2025; Mastercard Canada's Diane Miquelon); Simplii debit (Interac + Debit Mastercard; launch date ⚠); CIBC Bank USA debit. CARDS II trust opened to Mastercard accounts (Apr 2024).
- Click to Pay: from 21 Oct 2026 CIBC enrols credit cards with each card's network on activation, on both the Mastercard and Visa agreements.
- Interac: domestic debit and e-Transfer. Acquiring: referral to Global Payments.
- No public network agreement, renewal or switch found ⚠. Not found: Mastercard Installments, Move/Send, Finicity, Ethoca, Decision Intelligence, Agent Pay, Multi-Token Network, World Elite products, commercial cards. Token services and fraud vendors not named ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Loyalty, offers and data on the Costco book; enrichment for Adapta's category engine and CRTeX; Consumer Clarity and Ethoca in the automated disputes flow; Agent Pay on CIBC's Click to Pay and wallet tokens; Move beside Visa Direct in Global Money Transfer; a commercial Mastercard beside the Visa business line; Multi-Token Network for the CAD deposit token.
- Watch-outs: Visa holds the big own-brand lines (Aventura, Aeroplan, Dividend) and CIBC debit; RTR (Q4 2026) and tokenised deposits could move flows off card rails.

## 1. The company
- Wells Fargo & Company (NYSE: WFC), San Francisco; principal bank Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. Founded by Henry Wells and William G. Fargo (1852 ⚠); the current company is the former Norwest and absorbed Wachovia (2008 ⚠).
- Q3 2026: revenue $21.9bn, net income $5.95bn, ROTCE 16.3%, CET1 10.0%; assets $2,253bn, loans $1,048bn, deposits $1,503bn. FY2025: revenue $83.7bn, net income $21.3bn, ROTCE 14.6%. Market cap about $252bn (16 Oct 2026). About 194,000 staff (−8%).
- Segments: Consumer Banking & Lending ($10.66bn revenue in Q3), Commercial Banking ($3.24bn), Corporate & Investment Banking ($5.01bn), Wealth & Investment Management ($4.10bn; client assets $2.45tn).
- Leadership: Charles Scharf (chair and CEO), Mike Santomassimo (CFO), Scott Powell (COO, CRO from Jan 2027), Kleber Santos and Saul Van Beurden (co-CEOs of CBL; Van Beurden also runs enterprise AI), Ed Olebe (cards and merchant services since Jan 2025, formerly at Mastercard).
- Regulation: Fed asset cap lifted 3 Jun 2025; 2018 Fed consent order ended 5 Mar 2026. New ROTCE target 17–18%; $15bn of gross expense cuts over five years.
- Deals: Merchant Services joint venture bought out from Fiserv (Apr 2025; Fiserv stays as processor); rail leasing sold (closed Q1 2026); Volkswagen and Audi finance deal (May 2025).
- Channels: 34.0m mobile-active and 38.2m digital-active customers; 4,068 branches; Vantage for companies; Advisor Gateway (May 2026). Fargo has passed 1bn interactions.

## 2. Wells Fargo × Mastercard
- Own-brand consumer credit on Visa: Active Cash (Jun 2021), Reflect (Oct 2021), Autograph (Jun 2022, "solidifies" the Visa partnership), Autograph Journey (Mar 2024). Debit on Visa ⚠ inferred from the account agreement.
- Mastercard: Choice Privileges cards (Feb 2023, exclusive network), One Key and One Key+ with Expedia (Jul 2024), Signify Business Cash (May 2024), Attune (Jun 2024 ⚠ possibly closed).
- Bilt Mastercard (Mar 2022) left Wells Fargo for Cardless on 7 Feb 2026, still on Mastercard. From Q1 2026, card metrics exclude co-brands.
- WellsOne commercial and virtual cards: network not found. Finicity data-exchange deal (Apr 2017 ⚠).
- Merchant Services accepts every network; Wells Fargo also sponsors an unnamed payment company on the networks ($322bn chargeback exposure at 30 Jun 2026 ⚠).
- Bank-owned rails: Zelle and Paze (Early Warning; Paze on all cards since Oct 2024), RTP and FedNow, tokenized deposits (Aug 2026), a 21-bank stablecoin venture (Sep 2026) and ZelleUSD (Jun 2026). Big banks reportedly explored buying STAR and Accel (Jul 2026 ⚠).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Risk, token and data services for the wholly owned acquirer; Premier cards with no network named; business and commercial cards as Business Banking is rebuilt; Agent Pay for Fargo and the agentic programme; cross-border and stablecoin interoperability; fraud and identity for account-to-account payments.

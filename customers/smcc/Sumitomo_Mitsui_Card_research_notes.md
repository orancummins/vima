# Sumitomo Mitsui Card — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Sumitomo_Mitsui_Card_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and SMCC × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are SMBC Group's own unless marked: the FY3/26 results presentation and data book (May 2026) and the 1Q FY3/27 overview (31 July 2026). ## 1. The company
- Sumitomo Mitsui Card Company, Limited (SMCC); registered office Osaka, Tokyo head office in Toyosu. Wholly owned by SMFG; Docomo's 34% bought out in April 2019 ⚠. Unlisted.
- Heritage: Sumitomo Credit Service (1967); BankAmericard 1968; Japan's first Visa card and founder of Visa Japan (VJA), 1980; Mastercard from 1989; renamed 2001 ⚠.
- Consolidation: SMBC Mobit (Jul 2023), SMBC Finance Service / Cedyna and OMC (Apr 2024), SMBC Consumer Finance (Promise) wholly owned (Oct 2024). V POINT MARKETING (formerly CCC MK HD) a subsidiary from 31 Mar 2026.
- Scale: 43.35m card holders (Mar 2026); shopping sales handled ¥41.95tn FY3/26 (from ¥30.18tn FY3/23) and ¥10.1tn in 1Q FY3/27. With SMBCCF: operating revenue ¥1,034.7bn, net income ¥105.7bn FY3/26. About 30% of Japanese credit card spend by this map's arithmetic ⚠.
- Leadership: 佐々木丈也 (Sasaki) president from 30 Jun 2026 (reading unconfirmed ⚠); Yukihiko Onishi chairman; SMFG CEO Toru Nakashima.
- Strategy (SMFG plan, May 2026): Olive 7.5m → 15m accounts by FY3/29 (8.0m Jun 2026); card sales ¥38tn → ¥55tn; Trunk 90k accounts (Jul 2026), 300k target; PayPay integration and AI-Olive; ¥100bn AI; stablecoin and tokenised-deposit infrastructure.
- Acquiring: stera with GMO-PG, GMO-FG and Visa (2019), 300k terminals by Mar 2024, 1m target by 2030 ⚠; stera transit at 200+ operators in 45 prefectures (Jan 2026); Fiserv Clover for small merchants from late 2026.
- Alliances: SoftBank and PayPay (May 2025); Infcurion (Sep 2024) ⚠; Money Forward Home (Dec 2024) ⚠; Expo 2025 cashless partner.

## 2. SMCC × Mastercard
- Mastercard issued since April 1989. NL, Gold and Platinum cards: Visa or Mastercard by choice. Mastercard line promoted less than Visa ⚠.
- Amazon Mastercard / Prime Mastercard: issued by SMCC since Feb 2014, renewed Nov ⚠2021, Mastercard only.
- Apple Pay with Mastercard contactless from Oct 2016, five years before Visa ⚠. Mastercard touch not available in Google Pay or Samsung Pay (Jun 2025).
- stera transit: SMCC provides Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay touch; JCB handles JCB, Amex, Diners, Discover.
- Visa is the house brand: Olive (Visa flexible credential, 2023), Platinum Preferred, Visa Infinite (Sep 2025), stera co-built with Visa, Visa Agentic Ready issuer (May 2026).
- Mastercard's first live agentic transaction in Japan (20 May 2026) used MUFG NICOS and other cards; SMCC not named. No Mastercard–SMCC announcement found for 2015–26 ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Mastercard tokens in Google Pay and Samsung Pay, where phone-touch rewards drive campaigns.
- Agent Pay next to Visa's agentic work.
- Tokens and passkeys against number theft (¥51bn of fraud in 2025).
- Acquirer risk and merchant data for stera and Clover; transit for inbound travellers.
- Commercial cards for Trunk and corporate cards, whose networks are not stated ⚠.
- Issuer tools for the Amazon co-brand.

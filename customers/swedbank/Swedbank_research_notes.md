Companion to `Swedbank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Swedbank × Mastercard), condensed. One customer covers four home markets: Sweden, Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania. Figures are the group's own (Q2 2026 interim report and Factbook, 17 Jul 2026; Q1 2026 report, 29 Apr 2026; 2025 full-year report, 29 Jan 2026) unless marked. ## 1. The company
- Swedbank AB (publ), Sundbyberg; Nasdaq Stockholm. Roots in Sweden's first savings bank (1820); Hansabank bought 2005; Swedbank since 2006. Market cap not captured ⚠.
- H1 2026: profit SEK 14.5bn, RoE 13.6% (14.3% adjusted), CET1 17.4%, loans SEK 1,934bn, deposits SEK 1,349bn. FY2025: profit SEK 32.8bn, RoE 15.2%, dividend SEK 29.80.
- Business areas: Swedish Banking, Baltic Banking, Corporates and Institutions, Wealth Management; Group Functions & Other holds Entercard and PayEx.
- Markets: Sweden 3.8m private / 246k corporate customers, 4.6m cards; Estonia 0.8m / 130k, 1.1m cards; Latvia 1.0m / 79k, 1.1m cards; Lithuania 1.7m / 93k, 1.9m cards. Baltic profit H1 2026: EE SEK 1.3bn, LV 0.7bn, LT 2.3bn.
- Brands: Swedbank, Swedbank Robur, Swedbank Försäkring, Swedbank Hypotek, Swedbank Pay, Entercard (re:member), Stabelo, PayEx (for sale).
- Leadership: Jens Henriksson (CEO), Jon Lidefelt (CFO), Göran Persson (Chair from Mar 2026), Henrik Liljeblad (Group Business Support). CCO and C&I head being replaced (Sep 2026). Baltic heads not captured ⚠.
- Swedbank 15/27: RoE ≥15%, C/I ≤0.40, payout 60–70%. 2026 restructuring: SEK 1.3bn one-off, SEK 1bn savings by end-2028, ~16,800 FTE by end-2027. Oken AI for all staff.
- Deals: Entercard wholly owned 1 Dec 2025 (SEK 2.76bn); Stabelo Nov 2025 (SEK 349m); Entercard loan book and PayEx for sale (2026). DoJ closed (Jan 2026), DFS USD 50m (Jul 2026).

## 2. Swedbank × Mastercard
- Sweden: Bankkort Mastercard debit (Maestro retired; earlier Visa ⚠ undated); Mastercard Standard/Guld/Platinum credit issued by Entercard; Click to Pay; Priceless.
- Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania: Mastercard debit and credit, Identity Check, Click to Pay, Priceless (LV, LT), LoungeKey and Flexiroam; Apple/Google/Garmin Pay. Some Visa cards in Latvia ⚠ and a Visa Business Debit in Lithuania ⚠.
- Entercard re:member and partner cards: network not confirmed ⚠. Swedish company cards: network not stated ⚠.
- No public Swedbank–Mastercard agreement found ⚠. Visa Inc. C shares held (SEK 138m).
- Swedbank Pay acquires all networks (983m transactions in Q2 2026; Nordics −5%, Baltics +5%); UnionPay since 2016.

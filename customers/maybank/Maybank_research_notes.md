# Maybank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Maybank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Maybank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (2Q FY26 results, 27 Aug 2026; FY2025 results, 26 Feb 2026; ROAR30, 21 Jan 2026) unless marked. Maybank's Malaysian and Singapore retail sites (maybank2u.com.my, maybank2u.com.sg) timed out, so card networks there come from RinggitPlus, SingSaver and MileLion; maybank.com and maybank.co.id were read directly. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are not yet out.

## 1. The company
- Malayan Banking Berhad (Bursa Malaysia: 1155), opened 12 Sep 1960; head office now in Merdeka 118 ⚠. Home markets Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia; all ten ASEAN countries; over 43,000 staff and about 2,600 branches ⚠. Market cap and ratings not confirmed ⚠.
- 2Q FY26: net profit RM2.69bn (+2.4%), ROE 12.0%, NIM 2.10%, CI 49.1%, loans RM695.9bn, CASA 41.5%, CET1 14.92%. 1H FY26 cross-border payments RM465.2bn (+15.8%), wealth fees +48.4%. FY2025: net profit RM10.51bn, ROE 11.7%.
- Segments: Community Financial Services, Global Banking, Insurance & Takaful (Etiqa), Head Office. Entities: Maybank Islamic (global top five), Maybank Singapore, Maybank Indonesia (ex-BII, 2008), Maybank Investment Bank (ex-Kim Eng, 2011), Etiqa.
- Leadership: Zamzamzairani Mohd Isa (Chairman), Khairussaleh Ramli (President & Group CEO), Shafiq Abdul Jabbar (CFO), Syed Ahmad Taufik Albar (CFS), John Chong (Global Banking), Giorgio Migliarina (technology), Alvin Lee (Singapore), Steffano Ridwan (Indonesia).
- ROAR30: ROE 13–14% by 2030, CI ≤47%; lead in Islamic finance, regional wealth, regional transactions and payments, and CIB; RM10bn for technology, data and AI; a next-gen app from MAE.
- 10.68m active MAE and Maybank2u users (9M FY25); over 8m MAE users and 700,000+ QRPay merchants ⚠ date.

## 2. Maybank × Mastercard
- Malaysia credit on three networks. Mastercard: World Elite (2026 perks: Malaysia Airlines, Agoda), Cash Back Platinum, Ikhwan Mastercard range (World Elite Ikhwan from Oct 2024), Nadi Mastercard Card-i (Jul 2026 ⚠). Visa: Petronas, Shopee, Manchester United, FC Barcelona, Visa Signature/Infinite. Amex: Maybank is Amex's Malaysian issuer since 2006; Maybank 2 Cards pair Amex with Visa or Mastercard.
- Grab Mastercard Platinum (Aug 2020, with Mastercard's Ari Sarker) discontinued 7 Sep 2026; holders moved to a cashback card keeping the same number ⚠ network not stated.
- Debit: Visa and Mastercard debit, working on MyDebit at home; MyDebit-only Kawanku card. Volume split not public ⚠.
- Singapore: World Mastercard, Family & Friends and XL cards on Mastercard ⚠; Horizon, Platinum and Infinite on Visa.
- Indonesia: debit on Mastercard and GPN; credit mostly Visa with one Mastercard Platinum and JCB; Mastercard Travel Pass for World Premier debit.
- No public Maybank–Mastercard tokenisation, Click to Pay, Send/Move, fraud or Agent Pay deal found ⚠.

## 3. Competition and market
- Visa: Visa Checkout (2015–16), Payband (2015), biggest co-brands; Maybank Marathon 2026 in Indonesia. Amex: exclusive issuer. PayNet: MyDebit and DuitNow (8.44bn transactions in 2025). BNM: 18.4bn e-payments in 2025, DuitNow QR doubled to 3bn, contactless 2bn.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Cross-border flows (RM465bn a half; Mastercard Move for remittances), a new co-brand for the former Grab base, Agent Pay in the next MAE, scam and identity tools, acquirer risk and Tap on Phone, Priceless for wealth clients.

Companion to `Credicorp_BCP_Platform.html`. ## 1. The company
- Credicorp Ltd. (NYSE and BVL: BAP), Lima; more than 40,000 staff in Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Colombia, Panama and the US. BCP founded 1889 as Banco Italiano. Chair Luis Enrique Romero ⚠. Market value about US$22bn at end-2025 ⚠; ratings not confirmed ⚠.
- 2Q26: net income S/1,981.9m, ROE 20.3%; loans +13.1%, deposits +17.7%; NPL 4.1%, cost of risk 1.9% (S/106m El Niño provisions). BCP ROE 29.2%, Mibanco 22.9% ⚠. FY2025: S/6,925.4m, ROE 19.0%.
- BCP (end-2025 ⚠): assets S/203bn; 33.4% of loans, 36.2% of deposits, 34% of card revolving balances, 32.7% of mortgages; 16m+ clients, 13.7m retail, 2.4m SME; 305 branches, 10,400 agents, 2,400 ATMs.
- Yape: 16.7m MAU, 69 transactions per MAU (2Q26 ⚠); payments 47% of revenue (1Q26); 4.1m borrowers; Yape Bolivia about 4m users.
- Leadership: Gianfranco Ferrari (CEO), Diego Cavero (BCP), Raimundo Morales (neobank unit: Yape, iO, Tenpo, Yape Bolivia, from 1 Apr 2026), Alejandro Pérez-Reyes (Mibanco from 1 Oct 2026, replacing Javier Ichazo), Ignacio Belaunde (CFO from 1 Oct 2026).
- Deals: Helm Bank USA (US$180m, Dec 2025; SBS approval May 2026; US approvals pending ⚠); BCP Miami branch (Jul 2026); Banmédica buy-out (2025); Tenpo bank licence (Jan 2026); Krealo's 17 investments.
- AI: about 250 gen-AI use cases, 10,000 Copilot licences; agents next (Cavero, Aug 2026).

## 2. Credicorp × Mastercard
- BCP credit: Visa (Light, iO, Clásica, LATAM Pass, Signature, Infinite, Qore) and Amex LATAM Pass; no Mastercard found. BCP debit Visa ⚠; Mibanco debit Visa; BCP Bolivia Visa.
- Tenpo (Chile): Prepago Tenpo Mastercard; credit network not shown ⚠.
- Yape: account to account, no network; interoperable with Plin and banks since 2023.
- CCE interbank real-time hub built on Mastercard's real-time payments infrastructure (c. 2021) ⚠.
- Acceptance: Culqi (Credicorp) and Niubiz (ex-VisaNet; Mastercard since 2020); BCP's Niubiz stake not confirmed ⚠.
- No joint BCP–Mastercard announcements found for 2015–2026 (tokenisation, Click to Pay, Send, Agent Pay) ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Cross-border rails for Yape remittances (now Western Union, Ria, Remitly and others) and BCP's US push; Agent Pay for BCP's AI agents; scam scoring on account-to-account payments; SME cards and transaction data; merchant data and risk at Culqi; Tenpo's network across the neobank unit.

## 4. How the page reads Credicorp
- Five discs: Foundation · Rails (credit cards, debit, Yape payments, cross-border and digital assets, acquiring, Bolivia and Chile) · Services (fraud and security, AI and agents, loyalty) · Business lines (retail, Yape lending, small business, wholesale and US, microfinance, insurance and wealth) · Access (BCP app and wallets, Yape app, branches and agents, WhatsApp and portals). Six kinds of user. Lens areas read from the inventory.

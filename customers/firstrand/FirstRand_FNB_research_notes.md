# FirstRand (FNB) — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `FirstRand_FNB_Platform.html`. One research thread (company, and FirstRand × Mastercard), condensed. ## 1. The company
- FirstRand Limited (JSE: FSR), Sandton; main bank FirstRand Bank (divisions FNB, RMB, WesBank). Market cap R545.6bn at 30 Jun 2026 (+28%). No controlling shareholder: PIC 15.7%, BEE partners 4.9% (June 2025). 51,712 employees. FNB's roots 1838 ⚠.
- FY2026: continuing normalised earnings R44.5bn (+13%), ROE 24.9%; total R39.7bn (−5%, ROE 18.3%) after a £518m (R11.3bn) UK motor-finance provision. CET1 13.9%; record dividend 539c. New ROE target 21–26%.
- Franchises: FNB R26.4bn (ROE 40.5%), RMB R12.3bn (ROE 23.0%), WesBank R2.3bn. FNB active customers 12.77m; 19m platform users; 8.16m digitally active.
- Leadership: Chair Johan Burger; CEO Mary Vilakazi; CFO Markos Davias; FNB CEO Lytania Johnson from 1 Apr 2026 ⚠ (succeeded Harry Kellan); RMB CEO Emrie Brown.
- Deals: HSBC SA clients to RMB (approved Jun 2025 ⚠; integrated, 444 entities); Optasia 26.1% (Mar 2026 ⚠); UK exit announced Apr 2026, Aldermore and MotoNovo for sale, binding bids due end-Dec 2026 ⚠ bidder names from press.

## 2. Payments and cards
- Card issuing 1.39bn transactions (+7%); card-issuing income +15%, offset by higher association charges. FNB cards on Visa ⚠ (relationship since 1966, renewed 30 Oct 2025; FNB Namibia disclosure cites Visa).
- Virtual cards 6.3m (+7%), R101bn (+47%). Wallets: Garmin/Fitbit (2018), Samsung Pay (2019 ⚠), Apple Pay (Mar 2021), Google Pay (2022 ⚠). Over 60% of card-present payments contactless.
- Acquiring 1.16bn transactions (+5%); devices +9%; commission and rental NIR −7%. SpeedeeApp SoftPOS ⚠; upgraded SpeedPoint with PayShap (Mar 2026 ⚠).
- PayShap made FNB's default instant payment; volumes more than 8x. Instant Pay (2022) on Visa Direct. eWallet about 68m sends a year.
- Mastercard: Globba on Mastercard Move (11 Nov 2025; 120+ countries; Africa roll-out planned); Scan to Pay QR on Masterpass (Jan 2019 ⚠ current status). No Mastercard issuing found.
- Visa: World Cup 2026 sponsorship with FNB (Jul 2025).

## 3. Market
- PASA wound down; SARB took card interoperability (11 Aug 2026 ⚠) and is taking 50% of PayInc (formerly BankservAfrica), which runs PayShap, EFT and DebiCheck.
- PayShap: R100bn+ across 136m payments; about 45m a month by late 2025, 80% under R500. No domestic card scheme (SARB study 2021).
- Card-not-present fraud leads SA card losses ⚠.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Globba corridors across FNB's eight African markets; fraud and identity scoring; acquirer risk and data for a squeezed acquiring book; Agent Pay with NAVi; card-linked offers for eBucks (Pick n Pay); commercial and fleet cards for RMB's HSBC clients and WesBank.

# Attijariwafa bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Attijariwafa_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Attijariwafa × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (H1 2026 results, 27 Jul 2026; 2025 results, 23 Feb 2026; Financial Report 2025; investor presentation, Sep 2026) unless marked. The bank's main website sits behind a bot challenge, so card networks rest on press and the independent Wafir guides. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Attijariwafa bank SA, Casablanca (Casablanca SE; market value c.USD 17bn at end-2025). Formed 2004 from BCM (1911) and Wafabank (1904). Al Mada 46.5%, Santander (Santusa) 5.1%. Fitch BB+, S&P BB+, Moody's Ba1.
- 12m+ customers, 22,052 employees, 27 countries, 8,000+ branches (5,593 in Morocco).
- H1 2026: assets MAD 825.3bn, NBI MAD 18.4bn (+4.0%), net income group share MAD 5.9bn (+1.1%), RoaTE 22.9%, cost:income 36.8%. FY2025: NBI MAD 34.9bn, group share MAD 10.6bn (+16.2%).
- Segments: Banking in Morocco, Europe & offshore; Specialised financial subsidiaries (Wafasalaf 28.8% share, Wafabail, Wafa Immobilier, Wafacash); International retail banking (Tunisia, Egypt, Mauritania, WAEMU, CEMAC; 32.8% of 2025 NBI); Insurance & real estate (Wafa Assurance).
- Leadership: Mohamed El Kettani (Chairman & CEO); DGDs incl. Rachid Kettani (retail, Sep 2025) and Hassan El Bedraoui; Mohammed Hamlili (finance); Ghyzlaine Alami Marrouni (retail market).
- GOAL 2030 (2026): digital payments and embedded finance, pan-African leadership, AI, one group. AI: NBOA, PFM, real-time fraud ML, GenAI assistants. Attijariwafa Ventures (USD 30m, 2023).

## 2. Attijariwafa × Mastercard
- Simple neobank (25 May 2026): Go (Standard Mastercard), Plus (Mastercard Gold), Prime (Mastercard World ⚠ or Gold), Metal (network not stated ⚠). Simple Pay QR; Apple/Google Pay timing ⚠.
- AWB Black on Mastercard World Elite ⚠; UEFA Champions League Mastercard (Sep 2019; Khalid Elgibali, Mastercard MEA) ⚠ current status.
- Visa: Wafa Visa Classic/Gold/Platinum ⚠; Royal Air Maroc co-brand (Feb 2024 ⚠); strategic partnership (Dec 2024; Hassan El Bedraoui, Leila Serhan).
- Processing: CMI (domestic switch) and Attijari Payment Processing (UnionPay 2018, JCB 2021). Acquiring: Attijari Payment licensed 13 Mar 2025, live 1 May 2025; schemes accepted ⚠.
- No Mastercard master agreement, Click to Pay, Send, instalments, fraud or commercial-card deal found ⚠.

## 3. Market
- Competition Council decision 152/D/2024 (31 Oct 2024) ended CMI's acquiring monopoly; contracts moved by 31 Jan / 30 Apr 2026. Interchange cap 0.65% (Oct 2024), 0.50% from 1 Oct 2026 (0.15% for government and neighbourhood shops).
- Instant transfers since 1 Jun 2023; mobile payment used by c.10% (May 2024). Mastercard deals with CIH/Lana Cash (Apr 2026), Crédit du Maroc (Jul 2024), Citi Maghreb (Mar 2024), CAM (Mar 2022).

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Acquirer risk and SoftPOS for Attijari Payment; instalments, tokenisation and PFM for Simple; Agent Pay for Simple AI; cross-border send for Wafacash and the diaspora; commercial cards; a group platform for the African banks; offers before the 2030 World Cup.

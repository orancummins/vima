# DNB — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `DNB_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and DNB × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are DNB's own (Q2 2026 report, 14 Jul 2026; debt-investor presentation, Aug 2026; 2025 annual report, Mar 2026) unless marked. Q3 2026 results are due 21 October 2026 and a Capital Markets Day is set for 10 November 2026. Many sources are Norwegian; translations are ours. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- DNB Bank ASA (Oslo Børs: DNB), about 34% owned by the Norwegian state; Moody's Aa2, S&P AA-. Roots going back to 1822. About 25% of the Norwegian market; 2.4m personal customers, about 244,000 corporate customers, about 10,800 FTEs. Market value not checked ⚠.
- Q2 2026: profit NOK 9.8bn, ROE 14.6%, cost/income 40.3%, CET1 17.4%; loans NOK 2,332bn, deposits NOK 1,605bn; AUM a record NOK 1,782bn. FY2025: profit NOK 43.6bn, ROE 15.9%, dividend NOK 18.00.
- Segments: Personal customers; Corporate customers Norway; Large corporates & international; Other operations (associates Luminor, Vipps, Fremtind).
- Brands and entities: DNB, Sbanken (bought 2022), DNB Carnegie (DNB Markets and Carnegie, completed Mar 2025, about SEK 12bn), DNB Livsforsikring, DNB Asset Management, DNB Eiendom, DNB Finans (own subsidiary Jan 2026), DNB Luxembourg. 47.3% of Vipps Holding; 28.5% of Fremtind; 20% of Luminor, agreed for sale to OTP (Jul 2026).
- Leadership: Olaug Svarva (Chair), Kjerstin R. Braathen (CEO; chairs Vipps MobilePay), Rasmus T. Figenschou (CFO), Maria Ervik Løvold, Marianne Wik Sætre, Harald Serck-Hanssen, Alexander Opstad, Per Kristian Næss-Fladset (products, data and innovation; chairs Bits).
- Targets 2025–27: ROE >14%, cost/income <40%, CET1 >16.4%, payout >50%. Critical payment systems moved to cloud (2025); agentic AI in KYC and coding, about 400 FTE cut in Technology & Services (Oct 2026).
- Fraud: NOK 3.3bn attempted against customers in 2025 (+30%), NOK 3.0bn stopped.

## 2. DNB × Mastercard
- Personal debit: BankAxept + Visa in every package. Business debit: Visa Business, Visa-only since Oct 2022.
- Consumer credit: all Mastercard (DNB, Pluss, Ung, SAGA); Private Banking World Elite Mastercard with Priceless and Fast Track; DNB-funded Circle K and EV-charging discounts paid on DNB Mastercard; Priceless Live Nation presales.
- DNB Corporate Mastercard issued by SEB Kort (terms Apr 2020), for firms up to 250 staff. Virtual purchase card network not stated ⚠.
- Mastercard clears NICS for Bits (renewed May 2025). Nets (Nexi) processes cards (framework extended Feb 2019).
- Wallets: Google Pay (May 2019 ⚠), Apple Pay (10 Dec 2024), Vipps MobilePay (DNB's associate; international cards since autumn 2025).
- No public DNB network agreement with Mastercard or Visa ⚠. A paywalled DN article on moving 19,000 companies to Eurocard was not read ⚠.

## 3. Market
- Norges Bank (2025): BankAxept 46% of card transactions (49% in 2024), 70% at terminals; international cards 54%; mobile 27% of terminal payments; cash 3%; 210m instant payments.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Scam and fraud scoring; Agent Pay alongside DNB's agentic AI; commercial and virtual cards for LCI clients above the SEB Kort card's 250-staff limit; card-linked offers for Pluss and SAGA; open finance; debit if it is ever re-tendered ⚠.

## 5. How the page reads DNB
- Five discs: Foundation · Rails (debit, credit, business cards, wallets and Vipps, instant payments and processing, B2B checkout and FX) · Services (fraud, AI, open banking and identity, loyalty) · Business lines (everyday banking, mortgages and property, wealth and pensions, SME, large corporates and DNB Finans, DNB Carnegie) · Access (mobile bank and Spare, Sbanken, business apps, branches). Six kinds of user. Lens areas read from the inventory.
- Gaps: fraud vendors; network agreements and renewal dates; Sbanken card network; virtual-card network; market value; current Vipps MobilePay shareholding.

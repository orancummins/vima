# OCBC — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `OCBC_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and OCBC × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (1H26 results, 7 Aug 2026; FY2025 results, 25 Feb 2026; Annual Report 2025) unless marked. Card networks were read from OCBC's Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia card pages and card faces on 17 Oct 2026. The search budget ran out early; Hong Kong and China card lineups, network contracts and group customer counts are missing. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources, inference or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Oversea-Chinese Banking Corporation Ltd (SGX: O39), Singapore; formed 1932; second-largest financial group in Southeast Asia by assets (S$625bn end-2024); Aa1/AA-/AA-; close to 400 branches and offices in 19 markets; 33,323 staff (end-2025). Market cap about US$106bn ⚠.
- 1H26: record net profit S$4.19bn (+13%), 2Q26 S$2.22bn (+22%); income S$8.00bn; NIM 1.73%; loans S$364bn, deposits S$459bn; ROE 13.7%; CET1 15.7%; wealth AUM S$350bn. FY2025: net profit S$7.42bn (−2%), income S$14.6bn, credit card fees S$414m.
- Segments: Global Consumer/Private Banking, Global Wholesale Banking, Global Markets, Insurance. Entities: OCBC Malaysia/Al-Amin, OCBC Indonesia (~85%), OCBC Hong Kong, OCBC China, Bank of Singapore, Great Eastern (93.72%), Lion Global Investors; ~20% of Bank of Ningbo.
- Leadership: Andrew Lee (chair), Tan Teck Long (CEO from 1 Jan 2026), Goh Chin Yee (CFO), Sunny Quek (consumer), Regina Lim (cards), Melvyn Low (strategy and transaction banking; NETS board).
- The Next Frontier (Feb 2026): Asia, Tech (AI, Digital, Data), Net-Zero and Franchise shifts; stable to improving ROE; CIR low to mid 40s.
- Deals: PT Bank Commonwealth (2024); Great Eastern to 93.72% (2024–25); HSBC wealth and premier Indonesia (agreed May 2026, completion 2Q27, card balances +150%).
- AI and tokens: HELIOS agentic KYC and OCBC WoW avatars (Jul 2026); tokenised SGD on Swift's ledger with DBS and UOB (Sep 2026); Ant International tokenised deposits (Oct 2026); GOLDX.
- Scams: Money Lock (2023); in-app calls (2025–26); Dec 2021 phishing loss S$13.7m and MAS capital add-on of about S$330m (May 2022) ⚠ current status.

## 2. OCBC × Mastercard
- Singapore credit: Visa — 365 (flagship; 3 in 5 cardholders), VOYAGE and Premier Visa Infinite, FRANK ⚠. Mastercard — INFINITY, Rewards, NXT. Both — 90°N (Mastercard since Aug 2019). Arts, Platinum, Cashflo networks not shown ⚠.
- Singapore debit: OCBC Debit on Visa plus NETS; Premier and PPC World Elite debit on Mastercard; Business Debit Mastercard (2010) ⚠.
- Malaysia: six of seven credit cards on Mastercard; 90°N Visa. Indonesia: 1st Card with Mastercard (Feb 2026); Nyala on Visa.
- Visa Direct powers pay-to-wallet (Weixin Pay, Alipay and eight SE Asian wallets) under a strategic partnership since 2024.
- Not found: Agent Pay (Mastercard's first Singapore live transaction, Mar 2026, was with DBS and UOB), Click to Pay, Move, instalments or fraud-tool deals with OCBC.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay for OCBC WoW; passkeys and scam scoring under the Shared Responsibility Framework; Move against Visa Direct for the 50-wallet goal; the HSBC Indonesia card book; commercial and virtual cards for one in two Singapore SMEs; card-linked offers.

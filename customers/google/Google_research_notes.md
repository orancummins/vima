# Google: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Google_Platform.html`. One research thread (Google as a payments player, and Google × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Alphabet's own (Q2 2026 results, 22 Jul 2026; Q4 2025 results, 4 Feb 2026; FY2025 10-K) unless marked, with Google's blogs and developer pages, Axis Bank, NPCI data via the press, the CFPB and PayPal. Mastercard.com, Bloomberg and Finextra blocked fetching; the web-search budget was 16 calls. ⚠ marks items that rest on prior knowledge or secondary sources. Q3 2026 results are due in late October.

## 1. The company
- Alphabet Inc. (Nasdaq: GOOGL, GOOG), Mountain View. Market value ≈$4.1tn (Oct 2026); 198,933 staff (30 Jun 2026).
- FY2025: revenue $402.8bn (+15%), operating income $129.0bn; Search & other $224.5bn, YouTube ads $40.4bn, Network $29.8bn, subscriptions/platforms/devices $48.0bn, Cloud $58.7bn. US 48% of revenue.
- Q2 2026: $119.8bn (+24%); Services $94.5bn; Cloud $24.8bn (+82%), operating income $8.8bn; Gemini app 950m monthly users; H1 capex $80.6bn. Payments revenue not disclosed.
- Payments entities: Google Payment Corp. (US), Google Payment Ireland / Lithuania (EU e-money) ⚠, Google India Digital Services ⚠. CFPB supervision order (late 2024) withdrawn 7 May 2025 after the US Google Pay app closed (June 2024).
- People: Sundar Pichai (CEO); Stavan Parikh (VP/GM Payments); Vidhya Srinivasan (VP/GM Ads and Commerce); Sharath Bulusu (Google Pay, India). CFO and CBO from prior knowledge ⚠.

## 2. Products
- Google Wallet in 90+ countries: tap to pay with tokens, transit, kids' payments, passes, ID Pass (US mDLs, UK passports, Brazil, Singapore, Taiwan, India Aadhaar; EU from summer 2026 ⚠).
- Google Pay checkout and Chrome autofill; virtual cards (2022: Visa, Amex, Capital One; Mastercard promised ⚠).
- BNPL in Google Pay: Affirm, Klarna, Zip, Afterpay, all presented as **Visa** virtual cards.
- Europe (4 Jun 2026): Secure Payment Authentication with Visa, Checkout.com, Adyen; direct checkout with Airwallex, Adyen next.
- India: Google Pay 32.7% of UPI (May 2026; PhonePe 46.2%); RuPay credit on UPI; Flex by Google Pay with Axis Bank on **RuPay** (17 Dec 2025); loans ⚠.
- Agents: AP2 (16 Sep 2025; 60+ partners incl. Mastercard, Amex, JCB, UnionPay; Visa not named); x402 for stablecoins; UCP (11 Jan 2026; Mastercard, Visa, Amex support); Universal Cart (19 May 2026); AP2 into Gemini Spark.
- Google as merchant: PayPal Enterprise Payments to process cards for Cloud, Ads and Play (17 Sep 2025).

## 3. Google × Mastercard
- Tokens: Mastercard cards in Wallet (MDES ⚠ globally); MDES in Google Pay India from 22 Dec 2021 (Nikhil Sahni; Sajith Sivanandan). Visa came first in India (Sep 2020).
- AP2 launch partner (Pablo Fourez quoted); "joins Google on new protocol" UCP, 20 Jan 2026 ⚠ page blocked.
- Agent Pay: all US cardholders enabled 2025, global launch early 2026 (Mastercard 10-K); no live Gemini/Universal Cart integration public.
- History ⚠: Google Wallet Card prepaid Mastercard (2013–16); Android Pay launch network (2015); reported 2018 ad-measurement data deal.

## 4. How the page reads Google
- Five discs: Foundation (scale, leadership, licences, AI and PayPal) · Rails (Google Pay checkout, Wallet tap, tokens, BNPL, UPI, AP2, Google as merchant) · Services (authentication, identity, passes and perks, agentic commerce, data and AI) · Business lines (search and ads, Play and subscriptions, Cloud, Flex) · Access (Wallet app, India app, Chrome, Gemini and AI Mode, PSPs). Six kinds of user.
- Lens verdict: in every wallet and every protocol, but Visa gets the exclusives.
- Gaps: network split, payments revenue, Google Pay India users, Mastercard support for Chrome virtual cards, Agent Pay inside Gemini, Q3 2026 actuals.

## 1. The group
- Bank of Ireland Group plc (listed holding company since 2017); The Governor and Company of the Bank of Ireland (operating bank); Bank of Ireland (UK) plc. HQ 40 Mespil Road, Dublin ⚠. Euronext Dublin only since the LSE listing was cancelled on 29 Jun 2026. Market value ≈ €17.7bn (€18.87 × 944m shares) ⚠.
- Heritage: royal charter and opening 1783; College Green from 1808; banker to the Irish Government from 1922; 1969 merger forming the group; State recapitalisation 2009–11 (€4.7bn); State fully out 23 Sep 2022 (≈€6.7bn recovered).
- Ratings: Moody's holdco A2, bank Aa3 (deposit outlook positive, 25 Aug 2026); Fitch holdco A-/F1, bank A+; S&P BBB/positive (Jan 2025) ⚠ may have been withdrawn.
- Scale: FY2024 PBT €1.86bn (underlying €2.13bn), RoTE 16.8% adjusted; FY2025 PBT €1.39bn (underlying €1.82bn), 10.9% statutory RoTE after a UK motor provision; H1 2026 PBT €960m (+33%), RoTE 14.4% statutory, CET1 15.5% pro forma, NIM 2.68%; assets €168.6bn, deposits €108.5bn, loans €84.0bn; Wealth AUM €65.5bn; 11,030 FTE.
- Customers: 4m+ (2.2m retail and ~280k small businesses in Ireland, 650k+ wealth and insurance, 1m+ UK, ~13k corporate relationships); 81% of Irish PCA customers digitally active.
- Shares: 42% of new mortgage lending in 2025 (#1 every quarter since Q4 2022); ~33% of personal and ~45% of business current accounts; >50% of SME lending; "40% of payments in the Irish economy" (own claim).
- Segments: Retail Ireland (54% of FY25 profit), Corporate & Commercial (29%), Retail UK (11%), Wealth & Insurance (6%); Asset & Motor Finance moved to Retail Ireland from 2026.
- Leadership: CEO Myles O'Grady (Nov 2022); Chair Akshaya Bhargava (Jan 2025); CFO Mark Spain; Susan Russell (Retail Ireland), Gavin Kelly (Davy & Wealth), John Feeney (Corporate & Commercial), Gail Goldie (UK), Ciarán Coyle (Group COO: technology, digital, data, payments), Rhys Kiff (CRO), Prag Sharma (Chief AI Officer from Oct 2026, ex-Citi).
- Strategy 2028 (2 Mar 2026): statutory RoTE >16% by 2028; income >€4.75bn; costs flat ~€2.2bn with ~€250m efficiencies (AI ~20%); CET1 ~14.5%; ~€1.5bn investment; ~50% payout. Guidance raised 31 Jul 2026 (FY26 NII ~€3.5bn, RoTE >14%).
- Deals and exits: Davy (Jun 2022, ~€427m); KBC Ireland performing mortgages and deposits (3 Feb 2023, ~€7.6bn mortgages, 150k customers); UK cards to Jaja (2019); UK personal lending and AA partnership ended, Post Office savings to 2031 (Dec 2023); US leveraged acquisition finance exit (Feb 2026); UK motor finance redress provisions ~€435m.
- Technology: Temenos core ("Project Omega", 2016) ⚠ current status; nCino SME lending; enterprise AI platform, M365 Copilot (~9.5k), 500+ adaptive models; new native app from 30 Jul 2026 (~30m log-ins a month). Fines: €100.5m tracker (2022); UK CoP £3.78m (2026); Aug 2023 IT glitch.
- Market: three main banks (AIB, BOI, PTSB) after Ulster Bank and KBC left; Revolut reaches >80% of adults; bank levy ~€200m a year across the banks ⚠ Budget 2027 treatment unverified; BOI in a 37-bank euro stablecoin consortium.

## 2. Products and channels
- Debit: personal and business Visa Debit since 2010 (Laser retired); Smart Start child Visa debit (Jun 2025). UK debit Visa.
- Credit (Mastercard): Classic, Platinum Advantage, Affinity (six universities), Student ⚠, Aer (Aer Lingus Avios, Jan 2019) ⚠, Business and Gold Business ⚠ (terms imply Mastercard; one FAQ says Visa). UK credit and commercial cards still offered ⚠ network.
- Wallets: Google Pay (Aug 2020), Apple Pay (late 2020); Garmin/Fitbit not evidenced. 125.5m contactless payments in Q2 2026.
- A2A: SEPA Instant (receive Jan 2025, send Aug–Oct 2025; 11m+ in Q2 2026); Zippay (Nexi; live 10 Mar 2026 in AIB, BOI and PTSB apps; €1,000/day); Synch Payments wound up Nov 2023 without launching.
- Acquiring: BOIPA, an exclusive alliance with EVO (2014, extended 2021), now Global Payments; not a JV.
- FX: Global Markets (Dublin, London, Belfast, Stamford); First Rate Exchange Services (50/50 with the UK Post Office).
- Cash: designated entity under the Access to Cash Act (Jun 2025); new ATM fleet (€60m programme); ATM transactions −6% y/y.
- Services: ten-minute digital account opening; Coming to Ireland (Jun 2025); AI KYC (−40%); Verification of Payee (5 Oct 2025); Featurespace fraud AI (losses −25%, false positives −87%; Jun 2026); PSD2 developer portal (sandbox since Mar 2019); Stripe partnership (9 Sep 2026; Davy on Stripe since 2021); Priceless on credit; mortgage cashback; Live Life Rewards (Visa CLO, 2018) ⚠ status; EcoSaver (~94% of new mortgages).
- Business lines: PCA (€6/month), Golden Years, Graduate, Third Level, basic; BCA; MortgageSaver/SuperSaver; SIA preparations; mortgages (€6.1bn new in 2025, €37bn book, ~96% fixed); Trade Down Equity Release (2026); personal loans (€2.6bn); motor finance (HP/PCP, EV marketplace); Davy (€33bn), Davy Select, New Ireland (€27bn; ~20% share), general insurance (RSA car); SME (€7.1bn book, nCino, SBCI); Corporate (~€22bn); UK (£16.9bn loans; NI bank; Northridge; Post Office savings).
- Access: app and 365 Online; 169 branches in the Republic and 13 in NI; 900+ post offices; Business On Line; contact centre (GenAI for 99% of agents).

## 3. Bank of Ireland × Mastercard
- Credit cards: Mastercard since the September 2015 switch from Visa ("strategic partner for all credit cards"; ~40% migrated by Jun 2016; term not disclosed; no renewal reported).
- Debit: Visa (all three pillar banks' debit is Visa; Visa renewed and expanded PTSB on 10 Jun 2026).
- Visa's wider role at BOI: Featurespace fraud AI; Click to Pay "coming soon" on Visa debit; Live Life Rewards (2018).
- Mastercard touchpoints: Priceless on credit; legacy SecureCode 3DS (2017); wallet tokens on credit ⚠ inferred; Mastercard's European Tech Hub in Leopardstown (2,000+ people; second building opened 8 Oct 2026).
- No public BOI × Mastercard joint announcement found since 2015; no current Mastercard Ireland country manager confirmed ⚠.
- Where it could land: renewal of the 2015 credit agreement; debit at the next Visa renewal; Click to Pay and passkeys on credit; scam and mule intelligence on SEPA Instant (Irish APP fraud ~€75m in 2025, credit-transfer APP fraud +95%); card-linked offers in the new app; Consumer Clarity; open banking under PSD3/FIDA; Move for cross-border; carbon on card spend; positioning against wero and the digital euro.

# CRDB Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `CRDB_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and CRDB × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (1H 2026 Investors' Briefing, July 2026; 2025 Integrated Report, published May 2026) unless marked. Amounts in TZS (about TZS 2,600 = USD 1). CRDB publishes no card counts by network. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are not yet out.

## 1. The company
- CRDB Bank Plc (DSE: CRDB), Dar es Salaam; incorporated 1996, listed June 2009. About 29% of Tanzania's banking assets and deposits and 31% of loans (2025). 7.95m customers, 243 branch outlets, 736 ATMs, 38,449 agents, 4,480 staff (June 2026). Moody's B1.
- 1H 2026: PAT TZS 417bn (+20.3%), assets TZS 26.4tn (+33.9%), loans TZS 17.0tn, deposits TZS 18.5tn, ROE 28.0%, NPL 2.4%. FY2025: PAT TZS 728.6bn, ROE 29.5%, CIR 41.6%.
- Segments: Corporate Banking (2025 PBT TZS 507.4bn), Retail Banking (TZS 187.2bn), Treasury (TZS 335.9bn), plus insurance.
- Owners (June 2026): DANIDA Investment Fund 21.0%, PSSSF 13.3%, NSSF Uganda 7.9%; 101,340 shareholders; market value TZS 6,869bn.
- Group: CRDB Bank Burundi (100%, since 2012; 7.3% of group profit), CRDB Bank Congo (55%, Lubumbashi and Kinshasa; broke even Q1 2026), CRDB Insurance, CRDB Bank Foundation, Dubai DIFC representative office (opened January 2026); 20% of Cooperative Bank of Tanzania.
- Leadership: Chairperson Prof. Neema Mori; Group CEO Dr Abdulmajid Nsekela; CFO Fredrick Nshekanabo; CCO Boma Raballa; COO Bruce Mwile.
- Strategy: EVOLVE 2023–2027; 2026 capex TZS 168.2bn, 66% ICT. Temenos T24 live 2025 (from Finastra ⚠); BPC SmartVista switch June 2025; agent tokens October 2025; SimBanking 2.93m registered accounts and 99.3m transactions (2025).

## 2. CRDB × Mastercard
- Debit is Visa: TemboCard Classic to Infinite, metal, fan and Al Barakah cards; Burundi TemboCard Visa; DRC TemboCard Visa launched 10 April 2026. 8.92m registered cards (2025). Visa campaigns (2021, 2022) and a Visa activation award (2025).
- UnionPay second: Tembo UPI virtual card in SimBanking; co-brand since 2013 ⚠.
- Mastercard: World Rewards and Gold credit cards (launched February 2018 with Dr Charles Kimei; Premier bundle today); credit-card book TZS 3.1bn. New Gold and Infinite credit-card pages name no network ⚠. TemboCard MasterCard debit of 2011 ⚠ (status unknown).
- Acceptance: Lipa Hapa POS and gateway take Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay and Amex. SmartVista issues all three networks.
- Scheme fees: income TZS 13.5bn (2025; 2024: 24.3bn); paid to Visa and Mastercard TZS 15.2bn (not split).

## 3. Market
- Mobile money: 87.05m subscriptions (June 2026); M-Pesa 40.6%, Mixx by Yas 32.0%, Airtel Money 16.4% (TCRA via TanzaniaInvest).
- TIPS: about 560m transactions worth about TZS 41tn in FY2024/25 (BoT, via The Citizen); go-live date ⚠. TanQR standard effective 1 September 2022. BoT caps TIPS bank-to-wallet fees.
- NMB, the main rival, launched Mastercard SME cards (June 2025, June 2026) ⚠.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- SME and commercial cards; Mastercard Move for diaspora and Dubai–Burundi–DRC corridors; scam and fraud tools for SimBanking; a tokenised Mastercard credit card in SimBanking; merchant risk and data for 315,505 QR merchants; keeping the new credit cards on Mastercard.

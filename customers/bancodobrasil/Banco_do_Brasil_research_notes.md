## 1. The company
- Banco do Brasil S.A., Brasília (B3: BBAS3; ADR Level I). Founded 12 Oct 1808. Federal government 50.0000011%. Market value about R$114bn at end-June ⚠ (derived). Rated BB / Ba1 / BB. 83,991 staff.
- 2Q26: adjusted net income R$3.9bn, RoE 8.3%, NII R$27.5bn, cost of credit R$18.5bn, loans R$1.313tn (individuals R$357.6bn, agro R$421.6bn, companies ~R$456bn ⚠), NPL 5.61%, agro NPL ~6.27% ⚠, CET1 11.27%. FY2025: R$20.7bn, −45.4%, RoE 11.4%.
- 2026 guidance cut on 13 May to R$18–22bn; cost of credit R$65–70bn. Agro renegotiation: R$39.3bn under MP 1.314; MP 1.376 programme up to R$100bn.
- 92m customers; 35m active digital users; 94% of transactions digital. Shares: loans 16.1%, payroll 19.6%, deposits 17.3% (Mar 2026).
- Stakes: BB Seguridade 68.26%, Banco Votorantim 50%, Banco Patagonia 80.39%, UBS BB 49.99%; via BB Elo Cartões, Elopar/Livelo/Alelo 49.99%, Elo network 33.33%, Cielo 49.28% total, Cateno.
- Leadership: Tarciana Medeiros (CEO), Geovanne Tobias (CFO), Marisa Reghini (Digital & Technology), Carla Nesi (Retail); Bárbara dos Santos Lopes Freitas (Payments director, Mar 2026); Pedro Bramont (Digital Business). October 2026 elections may bring change ⚠.
- Strategy 2026–2030; BB 5.0 app (Apr 2026), 54m digital clients by 2030; R$8.2bn tech spend; AcademIA BB; Celent Model Bank 2026.

## 2. BB × Mastercard
- No network split, contract, renewal or joint initiative published or found.
- Ourocard credit: R$69.9bn book (+15.1%), 11.0m recurring users, on Visa, Elo and Mastercard. Card business result −R$649m in 2Q26.
- Elo co-owned. Visa: Altus Visa Infinite (2020), Altus Liv (4Q25), Airport Companion (2022), first agentic purchase (11 Mar 2026). GOL Smiles co-brand on Visa ⚠.
- Mastercard: Ourocard Mastercard Black, Platinum and Gold still offered; IOF cut (Mar 2024); Livelo promotion (to Jan 2026); added to Universo Ourocard (Mar 2026); Black holders at Casa BB.
- 2020 forum reports of BB halting new Mastercard credit ⚠; BB reportedly denied ending the partnership. Caixa reportedly stopped new Mastercard credit cards in 2026 ⚠ (search summary).
- Mastercard market-wide: Agent Pay in LatAm from Feb 2026 (BB not named); Brazil the #2 Click to Pay market, 40% tokenised (Feb 2025).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land

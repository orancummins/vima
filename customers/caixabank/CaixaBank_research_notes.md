# CaixaBank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `CaixaBank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and CaixaBank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (H1 2026 results and financial report, 29 Jul 2026; 2025 results, 30 Jan 2026) unless marked. ## 1. The company
- CaixaBank, S.A., registered office Valencia (since Oct 2017); listed in Madrid (IBEX 35), market value €86.2bn (30 Jun 2026). Owners: 'la Caixa' Foundation via CriteriaCaixa 31.0%, FROB via BFA 18.0%, free float 50.7%. Ratings S&P A+, Moody's A2, Fitch A– (positive), DBRS A (high).
- H1 2026: profit €3,203m (+8.5%; Q2 €1,631m), ROTE 18.0%, cost-to-income 39.6%, CET1 12.5%, NPL 1.78%, assets €693.4bn. FY2025 profit €5.89bn, ROTE 17.5%.
- 20.9m customers (Spain and Portugal), 6.4m payrolls, 12m+ digital customers, 47,580 employees, 4,543 branches, 12,233 ATMs. Spanish shares: deposits 24.7%, loans 23.3%, card turnover 31.0%, pension plans 34.4%.
- Segments: Banking and insurance; BPI (€218m H1 profit; 11.7% of Portuguese lending); Corporate centre. Brands: CaixaBank, BPI, imagin (4.2m customers), CaixaBank Payments & Consumer (34m+ cards, 16m customers, €12.9bn book, Aug 2025), Comercia Global Payments (20% stake; 805,000 POS, 30.5% share, Jun 2025), MoneyToPay (with Global Payments), VidaCaixa, MicroBank.
- Leadership: Tomás Muniesa (Chair, since Jan 2025), Gonzalo Gortázar (CEO, since 2014), Luis Javier Blas (COO), Javier Pano (CFO), Jordi Mondéjar (retail and business), Iñaki Badiola (CIB), Mariona Vicens (digital and analytics).
- Strategic Plan 2025–27: ROTE >16% by 2027, >€5bn technology spend with GenAI; 2025 targets beaten and raised.
- Heritage: 'la Caixa' ⚠ founding year unsourced; CaixaBank 2011; Bankia merger March 2021; BPI fully owned.

## 2. CaixaBank × Mastercard
- Consumer credit is Visa: MyCard, Visa Classic, Visa&Go, Vueling Visa (Sep 2021), IKEA, MediaMarkt, FNAC, Repsol; football club cards Visa ⚠; Braille card with Visa and ONCE (May 2022).
- Debit is dual-brand (Visa or Mastercard); split not public ⚠.
- Mastercard: Masterpass first in Spain (Dec 2016; Ovidio Egido); imagin travel card (Feb 2025); MoneyToTravel at the Mastercard exchange rate ⚠ (Jun 2026); Mastercard Corporate ⚠; B2B virtual payment service on Mastercard virtual card technology, >60 ERPs (1 Jun 2026; no executives named).
- Visa: first AI-agent transaction via Visa Intelligent Commerce and Agentic Ready (2 Jul 2026).
- No group-wide network contract found ⚠. BPI card networks not stated ⚠.

## 3. Competition
- Bizum: 30.6m users, 39 banks, 111,000 merchants; in shops by NFC from 18 May 2026 with CaixaBank, Sabadell and Bankinter first.
- Bizum–EuroPA–EPI (Wero) link: hub started 2 Feb 2026, 13 countries, P2P first, merchants later; cross-border merchant pilot by EPI, Bancomat and MB WAY (Apr 2026) ⚠.
- SEPA Instant with Verification of Payee; EU interchange caps (0.2%/0.3%).

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Scale the B2B virtual cards; Agent Pay beside Visa's pilot; FX and benefits on imagin; acquirer tools at Comercia; fraud and identity tools as Bizum and Wero move money account to account; card-linked offers for loyalty.

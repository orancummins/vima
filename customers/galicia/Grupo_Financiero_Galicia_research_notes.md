## 1. The company
- Grupo Financiero Galicia S.A. (BYMA/Nasdaq: GGAL), formed 1999 by the Escasany, Ayerza and Braun families; Banco Galicia dates from 1905. Market cap c.US$6.2bn (16 Oct 2026).
- Companies: Banco Galicia; Naranja X (Tarjetas Regionales); Galicia Seguros; Fondos Fima; Galicia Securities; Inviu; Nera (50/50 with Santander from Dec 2025).
- HSBC Argentina bought 6 Dec 2024 (price c.US$550m ⚠), run as Galicia Más and merged into Banco Galicia 23 Jun 2025; 1.5m+ cards migrated.
- Q2 2026: net income Ps.258bn (ROE 11.3%); H1 Ps.329bn (−25%). FY2025: Ps.196bn (−91%, after 2024's HSBC bargain-purchase gain), ROE 2.5%.
- Assets Ps.50.4tn; deposits Ps.29.0tn; capital ratio 23.7%; NPL 10.6% (Naranja X 19.7%).
- Market share 15.9% of private-sector loans, 15.6% of deposits; Galicia second by deposits after Banco Nación.
- 20.9m deposit accounts; 13.3m credit cards (15.0m at end-2025); 9,435 employees; 379 points of sale.
- Banco Galicia: 5.2m customers (4.6m individuals, 43,000 wholesale). Naranja X: 3.97m active card customers, 8.6m active account users.
- Leadership: Eduardo Escasany (Chair), Fabián Kon (CEO), Gonzalo Fernández Covaro (CFO), Diego Rivas (CEO Banco Galicia since Sep 2025), Pablo Caputto (CEO Naranja X).
- Nave merchant platform: 61,498 merchants, Ps.2.6tn of volume in 2025. MODO: Galicia owns 19.79%.

## 2. Galicia × Mastercard
- Credit cards at end-2025: Visa 5.98m (40%), Mastercard 3.58m (24%), Naranja own brand 4.80m (32%), Amex 0.63m (4%).
- Banco Galicia: Visa 3.01m, Mastercard 2.46m (up 101% in 2025; the HSBC cards appear to have moved to Mastercard ⚠), Amex 0.63m. Mastercard Black 380,427.
- Naranja X: Visa 2.97m, Mastercard 1.13m (up 253% in 2024), Amex wound down.
- Debit: Visa at Naranja X (2022); Galicia believed Visa ⚠. Naranja X certified Visa Click to Pay (2025).
- Mastercard as technology provider for Naranja X's 2022 renewal of 10m+ cards; Infoless with Mastercard (2025).
- Galicia Mastercard on Garmin watches (Feb 2020). Naranja X Mexico credit card on Mastercard (2026); banking licence pending ⚠.
- No public network agreement, renewal or named Mastercard executive found ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Converting 4.8m own-brand Naranja cards; debit; fraud and credit-risk scoring for a book with NPLs of 10–20%; Click to Pay; acquiring tools for Nave; card-linked offers for Quiero!; commercial cards (now Visa Business); Naranja X in Mexico.

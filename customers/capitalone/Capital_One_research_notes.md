# Capital One — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Capital_One_Platform.html`. Two research threads (company profile; Capital One × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Capital One's own (2Q26 results and 10-Q, July 2026; FY2025 10-K, 19 Feb 2026) unless marked. Capital One's IR site was blocked to research, and 3Q26 results were not yet out. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. Capital One — the company
- Capital One Financial Corporation, McLean, Virginia; NYSE: COF. Capital One, N.A. (Discover Bank merged in May 2025). 78,400 employees; 100m+ customers. Market value ~$124bn (16 Oct 2026). Holdco Baa1/BBB/A- (Fitch on review for downgrade since 12 May 2026).
- 2025: revenue $53.4bn, net income $2.45bn (Discover allowance). 2Q26: revenue $15.85bn, net income $3.0bn, EPS $4.73, NIM 8.01%, ROTCE 18%, CET1 13.7%, assets $673.8bn, loans $457.2bn, deposits $484.3bn.
- Segments: Credit Card (domestic card loans $259.0bn; Brex from 2Q26), Consumer Banking (360, auto $89.3bn, Global Payment Network: Discover, PULSE, Diners), Commercial Banking ($91.3bn loans).
- Deals: Discover (closed 18 May 2025; $51.8bn at close); Discover Home Loans sold (Nov 2025); Brex ($5.15bn; closed 7 Apr 2026); Hopper-built travel technology in-sourced (Apr 2026).
- Leadership: Richard Fairbank (founder, Chair, CEO), Andrew Young (CFO), Mark Mouadeb (Card), Lia Dean, Jason Hanson (Global Payment Network), Neal Blinde, Sanjiv Yajnik, Prem Natarajan (AI).
- Strategy: "a payments company"; Discover the "crown jewel"; international acceptance (Mexico, Caribbean, Canada, UK); $2.5bn synergies; all on AWS; Capital One Software (Slingshot, Databolt).

## 2. Capital One × Mastercard
- Debit: ~25m cards moved from Mastercard to Discover (from Aug 2025; "basically complete" Q1 2026). Mastercard US debit growth fell to ~1% (7–8% excluding Capital One).
- Credit: Mastercard renewed in January 2026 ("a large portion of newly acquired credit accounts", US and Canada; "several of Mastercard's services"; Miebach, 29 Jan 2026). From Feb–Mar 2026 new Savor, Quicksilver, Venture and VentureOne accounts opened on Discover; existing Quicksilver converted ⚠ by 9 Jul 2026; Savor holders lose World Elite benefits; back-book tests ongoing (21 Jul 2026).
- Staying: Spark and Venture X Business (Mastercard); co-brands (REI, BJ's, Bass Pro/Cabela's on Mastercard; T-Mobile, Kohl's on Visa); Canada and ⚠ UK. Venture X stays on Visa Infinite "for now". Walmart left in 2024.
- No joint Capital One × Mastercard product announcement (Click to Pay, tokens, Agent Pay) found for 2015–2026.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Travel and cross-border segments (Discover's acceptance gap); commercial cards and Brex; network-neutral services (fraud, identity, tokens, Agent Pay); benefits and offers to replace World Elite perks; co-brands at renewal; Canada. At risk: the back book, co-brands, Canada and UK over a ~five-year shift (analyst estimate).

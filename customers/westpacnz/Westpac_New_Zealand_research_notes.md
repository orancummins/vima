Figures are Westpac NZ's own (half-year release, 5 May 2026; FY25 via the parent's 2025 annual report) unless marked. westpac.co.nz blocked automated reading, so its releases were read through Scoop's republication; items seen only in search summaries are marked ⚠. ## 1. The company
- Westpac New Zealand Limited (WNZL), wholly owned by Westpac Banking Corporation (ASX: WBC; founded 1817). The Westpac NZ segment also covers BT Funds Management (NZ) and WBC's NZ Branch. HQ Auckland ⚠.
- HY26 (6 months to 31 Mar 2026): net profit NZ$545m (+4%; −19% on 2H25), NIM 2.29%, home lending NZ$73.3bn, business lending NZ$35.0bn, deposits NZ$83.7bn. WNZL disclosure: total assets NZ$144.1bn ⚠.
- FY25: net profit NZ$1,197m (+13%), 16% of group profit. CET1 12.5% ⚠.
- Market share (RBNZ, Sep 2025): consumer lending 18%, deposits 17%, business lending 16%. "The smallest of the four largest banks."
- Leadership: Chair Pip Greenwood; CEO Catherine McGrath (since Nov 2021); Russell Jones Chief Data, Digital and AI Officer and James Pretty CIO (Jun 2026 ⚠); Helen Ryder and Reuben Tucker run the divisions ⚠.
- Strategy: grow business lending, invest in digital, improve position and returns. Westpac One for Business; NZ$100m start-up lending; Microsoft CCaaS with AI (Apr 2026); basic bank account pilot; banking vans.
- Partners: Air New Zealand, Fidelity Life, Tower (from Jul 2026), Dosh, POLi, Akahu. Paymark stake sold (gain NZ$40m, FY19). NZ$3.64m CCCFA penalty (Nov 2025).
- Gaps: customer and staff counts, ratings, HQ confirmation.

## 2. Westpac NZ × Mastercard
- Parent went Visa-only in Australia in 2013; Westpac NZ signed a five-year Mastercard partnership in Apr 2014. No public renewal ⚠, but the credit-card terms of 31 Jul 2026 list only Mastercard: Fee Free, hotpoints ×3, Airpoints ×3, BusinessPLUS, Airpoints Business.
- Debit Mastercard with domestic EFTPOS on the same card; Airpoints Debit Mastercard and PayTag (Oct 2015).
- Apple Pay and Google Pay on Westpac Mastercards ⚠ dates; instant digital issuance (2024) and dynamic security codes (Mar 2025).
- Card controls (gambling, online, contactless) and the subscription-trap block run on card data.
- No public Click to Pay, Send/Move, scam-scoring, open-finance or commercial virtual-card work with Mastercard.

## 3. Market
- Interchange caps: NZ credit 0.3% in person from 1 Dec 2025 (from about 0.8%), online 0.7%; foreign cards capped from 1 May 2026; commercial and prepaid uncapped. Surcharge ban bill stalled (Mar 2026).
- EFTPOS (no merchant fee) switched by Worldline NZ/Paymark, which Cuscal agreed to buy (Apr 2026).
- Open banking: Customer and Product Data Act 2025; big-four designation from Dec 2025 ⚠; API Centre closed 30 Sep 2026, MBIE now runs standards; 545,681 payments in Aug 2026.

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Scam and identity tools; Click to Pay and full tokenisation; commercial virtual cards for SMEs; Mastercard Move for fee-free remittances; open-finance connectivity; offers for the renewed Air NZ partnership; merchant data for card controls.

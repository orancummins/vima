# UniCredit Bulbank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `UniCredit_Bulbank_Platform.html`. This condenses one research thread, covering the company and UniCredit Bulbank × Mastercard; the parent, UniCredit, has its own page. Figures are the bank's own (2025 Annual Report, spring 2026; 2024 Annual Report) or from the Bulgarian National Bank's bulletin for 30 June 2026. Bulbank publishes no quarterly results release. Capital.bg was blocked. Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026 (BGN 1.95583 = €1). ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or inference.

## 1. The company
- UniCredit Bulbank AD, Sofia. Founded in 1964 as the foreign-trade bank; merged with HVB Bank Biochim and Hebros Bank in 2007. UniCredit S.p.A. owns 99.45%. Fitch rates it A-/F2 Stable (upgraded in 2025). It has more than 1 million customers, 3,038 FTE, 121 branches and 161 points of presence.
- 2025 (standalone): net profit BGN 903.2m (-4.7%), assets BGN 41.0bn, loans BGN 27.8bn, deposits BGN 31.1bn, ROE 20.3%, cost/income 28.2%, CET1 20.6%. The 2025 dividend was €277m.
- H1 2026 (BNB): profit after tax €268.7m; assets €20.7bn, about 17% of the system and third behind DSK (€24.0bn) and UBB (€23.5bn).
- Market: number one in corporate loans (27.1%); 17.9% of retail loans with UCFin; about a quarter of system net profit; the lowest cost/income in the market.
- Leadership: Tzvetanka Mintcheva (CEO, also on BORICA's board), Nevena Nikše (CFO), Ivaylo Glavchovski, Borislav Bangeev, Borislav Genov; Supervisory Board chair Emilia Palibatchiyska.
- Subsidiaries: UniCredit Consumer Financing (2025 profit BGN 79m), UniCredit Leasing, Fleet Management and Insurance Broker.
- Digital: more than 800,000 Bulbank Mobile users; 1.5m transfers a month; Niki voice bot, DigiMate and an AI assistant in the app (1H26); Bulbank Online NPS +60; peer-to-peer merchant analytics from POS data.
- Deals: the ARTS Silver-2 €2.1bn risk-sharing deal with PGGM (July 2025). The euro changeover was completed with no outage.

## 2. Bulbank × Mastercard
- The May 2023 UniCredit–Mastercard group deal (13 banks, about 20m cards) covers Bulbank.
- 2024: accelerated migration of the card book to Mastercard. Mastercard paid BGN 28m of migration support (more in 2025, within BGN 10.6m of other income). The bank booked BGN 4.4m for ending Visa issuing.
- Today: debit, Gold, Kids/Teens, digital debit, Shopping Card, Gold and Platinum Smart Credit, and World Elite are all Mastercard. One Visa Gold debit is still listed ⚠ (legacy). Business-card networks are not stated ⚠.
- Touch Cards in recycled PVC with the Sustainable Card Badge; BGN 100,000 for guide dogs with Mastercard (August 2024; Vanya Manova, Mastercard regional manager ⚠ current title); Mastercard Consumer Card Award 2025; "Your Mastercard Day" cashback in 2026.
- Wallets: Apple, Google, Huawei and Garmin. Digitised-card payments rose 84% in 2024. Click to Pay was not found in Bulgaria ⚠, though it is live at UniCredit Italy and Romania.
- Not Mastercard: BORICA's Blink and SEPA Instant, the bank's own all-scheme acquiring (merchants +13.6%), fraud (€3.5m of losses prevented), AI and analytics.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Click to Pay; instalments at checkout; scam scoring for Blink and SEPA Instant; Mastercard data in the peer-to-peer analytics; gateway, risk and Tap on Phone for acquiring; commercial cards and Move for the number one corporate bank; Agent Pay; fleet cards for leasing.

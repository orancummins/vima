## 1. The company
- Booking Holdings Inc. (Nasdaq: BKNG), Norwalk, Connecticut; Booking.com run from Amsterdam ⚠. Market value ≈$126.7bn (17 Oct 2026); 25-for-1 split on 2 Apr 2026. ≈24,300 staff.
- FY2025: gross bookings $186.1bn (+12%): merchant $130.0bn (70%, from 63%), agency $56.1bn. Revenue $26.9bn: merchant $17.8bn, agency $8.0bn, advertising and other $1.2bn. 1,235m room nights; 68m flight tickets (+37%); 88m car days. Adjusted EBITDA $9.9bn (36.9%); net income $5.4bn. Merchant transaction costs +$381m.
- Q2 2026: gross bookings $51.0bn (+9%; merchant +14.5%, agency −3.3%); revenue $7.4bn; adjusted EBITDA $2.6bn. FY guide: high single digits, with the Middle East conflict weighing. Transformation savings ≈$650m by end-2027.
- Brands: Booking.com (31m+ listings), Priceline, Agoda (6m+ properties, Singapore), KAYAK (+ momondo, Cheapflights, HotelsCombined; $457m impairment in 2025), OpenTable (70,000+ restaurants), Rentalcars.com, FareHarbor, Rocketmiles; Lola and Vayo listed ⚠ roles unverified.
- Leadership: Glenn Fogel (CEO, also Booking.com), Ewout Steenbergen (CFO), Peter Millones (GC); Brigit Zimmerman (Priceline), Omri Morgenshtern (Agoda), Peer Bueller (KAYAK), Debby Soo (OpenTable); Robert Mylod (Chair). No head of payments named ⚠.
- Payments licences: Booking Holdings Financial Services, "a number of" licensed e-money institutions (entities not itemised ⚠); BHFS USA LLC money transmitter, NMLS 2436940, ≈45 jurisdictions.
- Regulation: EU DMA gatekeeper (May 2024), DSA VLOP; Spain's CNMC €413.24m fine (Jul 2024, on appeal); Amsterdam ruling in German parity case (Mar 2026). Etraveli deal blocked 2023 ⚠.
- AI: AI Trip Planner on OpenAI (Jun 2023); Booking.com app in ChatGPT (stays Oct 2025 ⚠; cars 20 May 2026) with checkout on Booking.com; AI support agents, Smart Messenger, Auto-Reply.

## 2. Booking Holdings × Mastercard
- Mastercard: Booking.com virtual cards for Pay by Booking (25 Jan 2024, Mark Barnett); VCC network named Mastercard by an integrator ⚠, issuer not public. Agoda B2B virtual cards (2023 ⚠ month). KTC–Agoda Mastercard, Thailand (30 Mar 2022). Agoda in Mastercard's Global Redemption Suite for APAC banks (20–21 Jan 2026, Matthew Driver).
- Visa: Priceline VIP Rewards Visa (Barclays; refreshed Sep 2022); Agoda interest-free card instalments (May 2022, HSBC Malaysia first); OpenTable & Visa Dining Collection (Jul 2024; renewal 6 Oct 2026 ⚠).
- Others: Affirm at Booking.com (Sep 2023) and at KAYAK, Agoda, Priceline; Stripe payouts for US/Canada homes; bank-transfer payouts; JCB × Agoda (Nov 2025). American Express owns Resy and Tock, OpenTable's rivals.
- Not found: Booking × Mastercard on Agent Pay, Click to Pay, tokens, instalments, Priceless or a Booking.com co-brand. No network switches 2015–2026.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay and agentic tokens as ChatGPT and other assistants move from redirect to checkout; Click to Pay and network tokens on $130bn of merchant bookings; Mastercard Move for cross-border property payouts; a Genius-linked co-brand (none exists); Agoda's redemption model for Booking.com and Priceline; Priceless dining against Visa at OpenTable; fraud and identity for merchant-of-record risk; commercial cards for KAYAK for Business.

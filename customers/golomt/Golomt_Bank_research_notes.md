Companion to `Golomt_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company, Mongolian payments and Golomt × Mastercard), condensed. ## 1. The company
- Golomt Bank JSC, Ulaanbaatar; founded 1995; IPO on the Mongolian Stock Exchange in 2022. Owners: Golomt Financial Group 79.77%, public 11.6%, Swiss-Mo Investment 5.21%, Bodi International 3.42%. Market value MNT 1.08tn at end-2025. Moody's B1, S&P B+ (positive), Fitch B+. About 2,800 staff.
- FY2025: assets MNT 19.2tn (22.3% of the sector, second after Khan Bank ⚠), loans MNT 8.3tn, customer funds MNT 10.8tn, net profit MNT 330.4bn, ROE 23.8%, NPL 2.5%. H1 2026: assets MNT 19.9tn, profit MNT 134.2bn, ROE 16.5%.
- 1.3m+ customers (about 36% of Mongolians); 108 branches, 403 ATMs; 1m digital users, 78% of them on SocialPay.
- Market shares: corporate loans 21.8%, mortgages 23.6%, credit-card loans 39.2%, letters of credit 60.6%, foreign payments 29% by number, gold 48%.
- Leadership: Chair Munkhtsetseg Ch.; CEO Odonbaatar A.; President Ganbold Galsan; CRO Otgon T.; CIO Sainbileg M.
- Funding: USD 300m Eurobond (2024), USD 50m Luxembourg green and social bond (Jan 2025), JPY 15bn Samurai bond (Nov 2025), IFC USD 160m blue package.
- 2026 priorities: capital markets, SMEs, VIP and wealth, asset quality, governance, green, and an "AI-enabled virtual banking model".

## 2. Golomt × Mastercard
- Mastercard issuer since 1999 (Visa 2000, UnionPay 2010, Amex agreement 2013). Debit on Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay and the ₮ Card; credit on Visa (Copper, Si, Platinum), Mastercard (Gold, World) and Amex. No split by network published ⚠.
- World Mastercard metal card (2025): credited with 17% card-sales growth and 25% more international transactions.
- Apple Pay Dec 2024 (Mastercard and ₮ cards; Visa added about Apr 2026 ⚠); Google Pay Nov 2025 (Mastercard and ₮); Samsung Pay Jul 2026 (Visa and UnionPay only).
- Bank of Mongolia chose Mastercard M/Chip as the ₮ Card's EMV standard (Nov 2019; launched Dec 2021 ⚠). ₮ Card: 2m+ cards, 45% of cards, 40% of transactions (Apr 2025).
- Mastercard Travel Rewards and Priceless on Mastercard cards; Mastercard Apple Pay campaigns Sep–Nov 2025 and Jan–Apr 2026 ⚠.
- Not found: Click to Pay, Send/Move, fraud tools, Agent Pay, commercial cards, or a named Mastercard executive.

## 3. Mongolian payments context
- Bank of Mongolia runs RTGS, a 24/7 ACH+ (up to MNT 3m) and the interbank card switch through its National Electronic Transaction Center (NETC); TDB clears for indirect participants. Law on National Payment System in force since 2018.
- QPay links all banks and about 20 wallets (SocialPay, MonPay, Pocket, Toki and others), 1% merchant fee, with WeChat Pay and GLN cross-border. Khan Bank accepts Alipay+ (2024). No national QR standard found ⚠.
- China corridor: SocialPay–WeChat QR with UnionPay (2025), CIPS RMB with ABC (Sep 2025), Bank of China MoU (Sep 2026).

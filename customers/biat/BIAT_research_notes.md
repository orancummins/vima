# BIAT (Banque Internationale Arabe de Tunisie): research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `BIAT_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BIAT × Mastercard), condensed. Most sources are French and translated here; amounts are in Tunisian dinars (TND). Figures are BIAT's own (2025 annual report, August 2026; Q1 2026 indicators, May 2026; shareholder letter 76 with H1 2026, September 2026) unless marked. Card networks come from BIAT's product pages on biat.tn. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 indicators were not yet out.

## 1. The company
- Banque Internationale Arabe de Tunisie, founded 1976 (from SMC and BBME branches), listed in Tunis since 1990; largest listed company (TND 6.9bn, 30 Jun 2026). Groupe Mabrouk about 40% since 2007. Moody's deposits Caa1, stable (Mar 2025).
- FY2025: PNB TND 1,594.8m (+7.8%), net profit TND 385.3m, ROE 16.3%, cost/income 44.6%, deposits TND 22,291m (21.4% share, first), net loans TND 13,045m (second), corporates about 62% of commitments. Group net profit TND 467.1m.
- H1 2026: PNB TND 803m (+1.9%, first), deposits TND 23,022m (+9.9%, first), net loans TND 13,209m (+1.4%, second).
- 2,897 staff, about 950,000 customers, 206 branches (70+ in the new format), Tripoli office, BIAT France (Paris). MyBIAT 415,000+ users; MyBIAT Corporate about 19,000 companies. 635,633 cards at end-2024.
- Leadership: Ismaïl Mabrouk (chairman), Elyes Jebir (chief executive since March 2024).
- Group: Assurances BIAT, Tunisie Valeurs (first broker, 29.3%), BIAT Capital Risque, BIAT Asset Management, BIAT France (IYU Europe agent since Dec 2024), BIAT Consulting, BIAT Innovation & Technology (new IT subsidiary), Fondation BIAT.
- 2025–26: Flexy split-payment card (Oct 2025), AfDB US$50m trade guarantee (Feb 2026), BIAT O'VERT green loans (Apr 2026), BIAT Privilèges with FreeOui (Aug 2026), syndications for GFR Kairouan solar, Advans and Enda.

## 2. BIAT × Mastercard
- Visa: 30+ years; six-year partnership signed Sep 2022 (fraud monitoring, analytics, products, marketing). Visa carries Infinite (first in Tunisia, Nov 2014), Premier, Express, TEMPO credit, CASH/FLY/H'DYA prepaid, and the 2024–26 campaigns (cashback, CAN 2025, "Win the world" 2026 ⚠).
- Mastercard: BIATRAVEL tourist-allowance card (international; upgraded Jan 2018 with Priceless), Business/Gold/Premium cards (Jun 2016), Platinum (Classe Platinum), the domestic Mastercard in Pack FIRST and Pack Express, and one of two choices for classic debit (split not published).
- Not stated ⚠: Flexy, CHABEB, Technologique, Tounessna/Univers prepaid. No BIAT–Mastercard agreement, tokenisation, Click to Pay or other digital programme found ⚠.
- Acceptance: TPE and BIAT E-Commerce take Visa, Mastercard and domestic cards; domestic switching through SMT ⚠.

## 3. Market and where Mastercard could land
- SMT switches domestic cards and the mobile-payment switch; CEO Bilel Darnaoui said (Dec 2025) a tokenised national scheme for cards and wallets is planned for end-2026 ⚠. BCT and SMT launched the TUNPAY label (May 2026).
- BCT, end-June 2026: 6.15m cards, 512,000 wallets, 45,600 terminals; e-payments 10.3m in H1 (+22.1%); cheques -10.7% after the 2025 reform. No instant-payment system found ⚠.
- Openings: tokens as SMT tokenises; Click to Pay for e-commerce; instalments for Flexy; Mastercard Move for BIAT France; card-linked offers for BIAT Privilèges; fraud scoring (Visa holds it); commercial virtual cards.

Figures are PNC's own unless marked: Q3 2026 results (15 Oct 2026), FY2025 results (16 Jan 2026) and the 2025 Form 10-K (20 Feb 2026). pnc.com could not be read directly by script, and the web-search budget ran out during research. ⚠ marks items that rest on one secondary source, are inferred, or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- The PNC Financial Services Group, Inc. (NYSE: PNC), Pittsburgh; bank: PNC Bank, N.A. Roots in 1845. National City (2008), RBC Bank USA (2012), BBVA USA ($11.6bn, closed June 2021), FirstBank ($4.1bn, closed 5 Jan 2026). BlackRock stake sold May 2020 (about $14.4bn ⚠ computed).
- Q3 2026: net income $2.1bn, EPS $4.97, assets $627.8bn, loans $378.8bn, deposits $466.4bn, CET1 9.8%, ROTCE 18.3%. FY2025: revenue $23.1bn, net income $7.0bn, EPS $16.59.
- Segments: Retail Banking; Corporate & Institutional Banking; Asset Management Group. Staff 55,333 (end-2025). About 2,400 branches; 300 new by 2030.
- Leadership: William S. Demchak (Chair and CEO), Mark Wiedman (President, Apr 2025), Robert Reilly (CFO), Alex Overstrom (Retail), Michael Thomas (CIB), Stephanie Novosel (AMG), Debbie Guild (Technology), Amy Wierenga (CRO).
- **FirstBank:** announced Sep 2025; Lakewood, Colorado; $26bn of assets, 95 branches; closed 5 Jan 2026; merged into PNC Bank 18 Jun 2026; 780,000 customers converted 22 Jun 2026; number one in Denver. Some customers reported access problems that week.
- No total customer count is published ⚠.

## 2. PNC × Mastercard
- Cards: consumer debit (PNC Bank Visa Debit), business debit, and consumer credit (Cash Rewards, Cash Unlimited Visa Signature, Spend Wise, Secured) are all Visa. Commercial cards appear to be Visa (Visa Commercial Pay B2B via Conferma) ⚠. Ex-FirstBank cards were reissued as PNC cards; FirstBank's own network was not found ⚠.
- History: PNC issued Mastercard credit cards through U.S. Bancorp from 2006 ⚠, converted after National City. PNC holds Visa Class B shares from Visa USA membership ($754m gain 2024; $448m gain Q2 2026).
- Visa Direct collaboration renewed (29 Oct 2025). RTP founding member; FedNow from Oct 2025; co-owner of Early Warning (Zelle, Paze).
- Mastercard: acquiring through PNC Merchant Services, Tempus, Linga and Mobile Accept (all networks); Payment on Delivery pilot on PNC's RTP access (Oct 2019; Ron Shultz and Chris Ward) ⚠ status; Mastercard judgment-sharing agreement in interchange litigation. Mastercard Open Banking through Akoya ⚠ unconfirmed.
- No PNC Mastercard card, Click to Pay, Agent Pay, Send/Move or fraud-tool announcement found from 2020 to 2026.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Open finance through Akoya; card-linked offers for TotalRewards and Purchase Payback; enrichment for the new app and the Consumer Health Check; acquirer risk for micro-merchants; Mastercard Move for payouts alongside Visa Direct; commercial and virtual cards; a second debit or credit network only if regulation requires it ⚠.

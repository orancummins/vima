# First Abu Dhabi Bank: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

These notes go with `First_Abu_Dhabi_Bank_Platform.html`. They condense one research thread, covering the company and FAB × Mastercard.

- **Figures:** the group's own unless marked: H1 2026 results (23 Jul 2026) and FY 2025 results (28 Jan 2026).
- **Mastercard sources:** mastercard.com returned 403, so its releases were read through Zawya, GCC Business News and Gulf News.
- **⚠:** marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.
- **Q3 2026 results:** not yet out.

## 1. The company
- **Identity:**
  - First Abu Dhabi Bank P.J.S.C. (ADX: FAB), Abu Dhabi.
  - Formed in 2017 from the NBAD and FGB merger.
  - Chairman: H.H. Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan. Group CEO: Hana Al Rostamani. CFO: Lars Kramer.
  - Rated Aa3/AA-/AA-. Market value about USD 56bn (Forbes ME 2026).
  - 4m+ customers, 140+ branches, 20+ markets.
- **H1 2026:**
  - Operating income AED 19.50bn (+7%), net profit AED 10.73bn, RoTE 18.5%.
  - Assets AED 1.41tn, loans AED 661bn, deposits AED 853bn, CET1 13.7%.
  - International was 22% of revenue.
- **FY 2025:** net profit AED 21.11bn (+24%), income AED 36.68bn, RoTE 19.2%, dividend AED 8.84bn.
- **Units:**
  - Personal, Business, Wealth & Privileged Client Banking (Futoon Al Mazrouei).
  - Wholesale Banking (Martin Tricaud).
  - Investment Banking & Markets (Linos Lekkas).
  - International (Omar Hafeez).
- **Entities:** FAB Islamic, Payit, Dubai First, FABMISR (Egypt), FAB in Saudi Arabia (three branches, FAB Capital), and a stake in Network International.
- **Deals:**
  - Magnati carved out (2021); 60% sold to Brookfield (2022).
  - FAB co-invested in Network International's take-private (Sep 2024).
  - Magnati merged into Network (Sep/Oct 2025). FAB's stake is undisclosed ⚠.
- **AI and digital:**
  - 30+ agentic AI use cases; Copilot for all staff.
  - API portal with 65 APIs (Oct 2026).
  - Tokenised deposits with Citi on Swift Ledger (Sep 2026); JPM Coin pilot (2024); CIPS direct participant (2025).

## 2. FAB × Mastercard
- **7 Mar 2024:** exclusive long-term global partnership across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Egypt. It "consolidate[s] our portfolios" (Futoon Al Mazrouei; Khalid Elgibali for Mastercard).
- **On Mastercard:**
  - Platinum Mastercard debit; World Business Debit (2019).
  - Own-brand credit: Z, Cashback, Indulge, Elite, Travel, Emirati Islamic. Five cards launched in six months to Aug 2025.
  - Rewards Active ring (Jan 2026); Royale World Legend Exclusive, a UAE first (Sep 2026).
  - McLaren Racing Mastercard Pass: FAB was the first Access Pass issuer (Nov 2025).
  - ADNOC Rewards World Mastercard (Dec 2025); du and GEMS co-brands.
  - Commercial cards: In Control, purchase and T&E (five-year deal, Feb 2021); SME suite (May 2026 ⚠).
  - SlicePay BNPL on Mastercard Installments (Nov 2023 ⚠ status).
  - FABMISR credit cards.
  - payit cross-border push payments (Mar 2020).
- **On Visa:**
  - Etihad Guest cards (since 2017; extended to 2027 ⚠ date).
  - SHARE (Majid Al Futtaim) and Blue FAB (Al-Futtaim) cards.
  - Letsgo Payit Visa Platinum (2023).
  - Virtual corporate cards and CTA (2020 ⚠ status).
- **Domestic:** Jaywan mono-badged debit (20 Jul 2026); Aani launch cohort (Oct 2023).

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- **Payit:** the card and Mastercard Move for Universal Account salaries and remittances.
- **Jaywan:** co-badging.
- **Agentic payments:** Agent Pay for FAB's agentic AI.
- **Fraud and scams:** scoring as Aani grows.
- **Visa co-brands:** at renewal, starting with Etihad by 2027.
- **Commercial:** virtual commercial cards through the API portal.
- **Tokenised settlement:** next to Swift Ledger.

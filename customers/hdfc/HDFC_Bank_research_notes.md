# HDFC Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `HDFC_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and HDFC Bank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own unless marked: Q2 FY27 results and presentation (17 Oct 2026), the Q4 FY26 analyst call (18 Apr 2026) and the Integrated Annual Report 2025-26. HDFC's card pages on the new `hdfc.bank.in` domain rarely name the network, so several networks are inferred. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- HDFC Bank Limited (NSE: HDFCBANK; BSE: 500180; NYSE: HDB), Mumbai. Merged with its founder HDFC Ltd on 1 Jul 2023. 103.6m customers, 210,109 staff, 9,706 branches (Sep 2026).
- Q2 FY27: PAT ₹195.1bn (+4.7%), NII ₹346.2bn, NIM 3.16%, deposits ₹33.3trn (+18.8%), gross advances ₹32.2trn (+16.3%), GNPA 1.11%, CET1 17.1%. FY26 PAT ₹746.7bn (+10.9%).
- Market shares: deposits 11.5%, advances 14.0%; acquiring about 35–36%, card issuance 21–22%, card spend 26–28% (CEO, Apr 2026). Market cap about ₹11 lakh crore at ₹721 (1 Oct 2026) ⚠ own calculation.
- Segments: Retail Banking, Wholesale Banking, Treasury, Other Banking Business. Subsidiaries: HDB Financial (74.1%, listed Jul 2025), HDFC Life (50.5%), HDFC ERGO (50.2%), HDFC AMC (52.3%), HDFC Securities (93.9%).
- Leadership: Chairman Rajiv Kumar (RBI approval 15 Jul 2026) after Atanu Chakraborty resigned on 18 Mar 2026; CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan to 26 Oct 2026, then Anup Bagchi (RBI approval 1 Oct 2026); Deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha; CFO Srinivasan Vaidyanathan. Payments head Parag Rao retired Sep 2025.
- Technology: Neev in-house AI platform (MCP, Agentic Studio, Agentic Mesh); new app and NetBanking (60m registered); 97% of transactions digital.

## 2. HDFC Bank × Mastercard
- Four networks: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Diners Club (Annual Report). No split published ⚠.
- Mastercard: Swiggy BLCK and ORNGE (9 Mar 2026; World golf benefits); Millennia launched on Mastercard (Aug 2019); Mastercard variants of proprietary and debit cards ⚠; Samsung Pay (2017) and Google Pay tokens (Dec 2021); recurring mandates (Oct 2021); US$100m MSME facility with USAID and DFC (Oct 2021) ⚠ status.
- Visa: Pixel and the FIFA World Cup 2026 Pixel Play Visa card (Apr 2026); Tata Neu, IndianOil, Pine Labs and Paytm co-brands. RuPay: UPI RuPay credit, Bharat, RuPay variants of the big co-brands. Diners: Black Metal and Marriott Bonvoy (deal since Oct 2011). PhonePe co-brand network not stated ⚠.
- Regulation: RBI curbs on HDFC new cards (Dec 2020 – Aug 2021) and on Mastercard new cardholders (Jul 2021 – Jun 2022); network choice at issue and renewal in force since 6 Sep 2024; UPI merchant fee of 0.4% above ₹2,000 from 15 Oct 2026.
- Agent Pay: Mastercard's first India payments (Feb 2026) used Axis and RBL cards, with Swiggy among merchants; HDFC not named.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Network choice on each new and renewed card; Agent Pay for HDFC's agentic build and the Swiggy card; cross-border (HDFC building its own rails and AD-1 APIs; PayZapp for NRIs); acquirer risk and merchant tools for a third of India's acquiring; commercial and virtual cards for a top-two MSME bank.

# Access Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Access_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Access Bank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Access Bank Plc's own (2025 annual report, published June 2026; unaudited Q1 2026 results, 27 Apr 2026) unless marked; Access Holdings Plc is the listed parent. Access Holdings' website sat behind a bot check and Mastercard's newsroom returned 403, so Mastercard items rest on search results and secondary coverage. H1 2026 audited results (deadline extended to 30 Sep 2026, subject to CBN approval) were not found. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Access Bank Plc (Lagos), wholly owned by Access Holdings Plc (NGX-listed since 2022; market cap about N1.46trn, Aug 2026). Nigeria's largest bank by assets since the 2019 Diamond Bank merger. Group: 60m+ customers, 24–25 countries, 28,000+ bank staff (31,000+ group).
- FY2025 (bank group): assets N51.27trn, deposits N34.56trn, gross loans N16.23trn, PBT N954.2bn, net fees N537.9bn (+41.5%); 52% of PBT from outside Nigeria. Access Holdings FY2025 PBT N1.01trn.
- Q1 2026: bank-group PBT N253.6bn, assets N53.1trn; Nigeria standalone PBT N61.2bn. Access Holdings PBT N272.2bn.
- Capital: N351bn rights issue (Dec 2024 ⚠), first to meet the CBN's N500bn international-licence minimum; Fitch 'B' Stable (May 2026); $500m Eurobond due Sep 2026 and $500m AT1 callable Oct 2026.
- Segments: Corporate & Investment, Commercial, Retail (Consumer and SME); geography Nigeria / Rest of Africa / International.
- Leadership: Access Bank chair Ifeyinwa Osime (after Paul Usoro), MD/CEO Roosevelt Ogbonna. Access Holdings chairman Aigboje Aig-Imoukhuede, Group CEO Innocent Ike (from 29 Aug 2025) after Herbert Wigwe's death (Feb 2024) and Bolaji Agbede's acting period.
- Deals: Standard Chartered Angola, Sierra Leone, Gambia, Cameroon, Tanzania consumer (2023–25); National Bank of Kenya (2024); AfrAsia Mauritius 76% (Jul 2025); Bidvest Bank lapsed (Jan 2026); Hong Kong branch and Malta bank (2024).
- Strategy "from scale to value": payments and trade leadership, 5m new retail customers in 2026, QuickBucks 3.0, Project Everest core, AccessPing (AI), Wallet-as-a-Service, stablecoin trials.
- Channels: AccessMore 8.4m users, 1.18bn transactions (N168trn) in 2025; USSD *901#; about 678 Nigerian branches ⚠; 750,000+ Closa agents; 7.5m MSME customers.

## 2. Access Bank × Mastercard
- Credit: all Visa (naira/USD, Black Card on Visa Infinite, Visa Corporate Credit Card Jan 2026). Amex Gold and Platinum since Jun 2023 (first in Nigeria).
- Debit: Visa (premium tiers, business debit since Dec 2020), Mastercard Debit and Verve. Prepaid: Visa (4 currencies), Mastercard (naira), Verve. AfriGO: first live card (Mar 2023). Virtual and co-branded cards: network not stated ⚠. No network volumes published ⚠.
- Mastercard Move powers Access Africa cross-border payments to 150+ countries (May 2024; Fable Fintech; Mark Elliott).
- AccessU age-staged Mastercard cards in Ghana (11 Jul 2025; Folasade Femi-Lawal, Pearl Nkrumah).
- "Mastercard Franchise Award – EDGE 2025" in the annual report.
- No public evidence of Mastercard tokenisation, Click to Pay, fraud tools, Agent Pay or commercial cards at Access ⚠.

## 3. Competition and market
- Visa: credit, premium debit and corporate cards. Verve: 70m cards in Nigeria (Oct 2024), 100m in Africa (Dec 2025). AfriGO: 1m+ cards, N70bn by Sep 2025. NIBSS NIP: 11.2bn transfers a year; N285trn in Q1 2025. Amex: Access's partner. Mastercard pushing low-cost debit with Zenith (Nov 2025) and BMONI (May 2026).

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- Move inside AccessMore and for diaspora and SME payouts; scam and identity scoring for transfer and USSD fraud (5,931 successful e-frauds in 2025); SME and commercial cards; tokenised virtual cards; card-linked offers for AccessRewards+; AccessU across the subsidiaries, AfrAsia and NBK.

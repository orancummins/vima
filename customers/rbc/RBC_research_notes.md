## 1. The company
- Royal Bank of Canada, founded 1864; TSX and NYSE: RY. Canada's biggest bank; market value C$406bn (31 Jul 2026). 19m+ clients in Canada, the US and 27 other countries; 105,000+ employees. Ratings Aa1/AA-/AA/AA (high), stable.
- Q3 2026: record net income C$6.0bn (+11%); ROE 17.9%; CET1 13.5%; assets C$2,498.8bn; deposits C$1,644.6bn. FY2025: net income C$20.4bn; ROE 16.3%; fiscal 2026 ROE objective raised to 17%+.
- Personal Banking – Canada: about 15m clients; #1 in all key retail products; credit card purchase volumes C$196.6bn and average balances C$25.2bn in FY2025. 1,249 branches, 4,097 ATMs (Q3 2026).
- Segments: Personal Banking (incl. Caribbean & U.S. Banking), Commercial Banking, Wealth Management (incl. City National, Brewin Dolphin), Insurance, Capital Markets.
- Leadership: Dave McKay (CEO), Katherine Gibson (CFO), Jacynthe Côté (Chair), Erica Nielsen, Sean Amato-Gauci, Neil McLaughlin, Jennifer Publicover, Derek Neldner, Bruce Ross (Group Head, AI), Naim Kazmi (T&O); Vinita Savani (Cards & Loyalty).
- Strategy: Investor Day (27 Mar 2025) set a 16%+ ROE and C$700m–1bn of AI value by 2027; unit targets secondary ⚠. AI Group (Feb 2026); #3 globally in the Evident AI Index (Oct 2026); ATOM foundation model.
- Deals: HSBC Canada closed 28 Mar 2024 (about C$13.5bn ⚠; 780,000 clients); Pinch Financial (Mar 2026); Global Transaction Banking unified (Aug 2026); Moneris (50/50 with BMO) agreed for sale to Francisco Partners for about C$2bn (10 Aug 2026), closing by end Q1 FY27, with exclusive referral arrangements.
- Digital money: one of six banks exploring a CAD tokenised deposit (22 Sep 2026).

## 2. RBC × Mastercard
- Visa: Avion Visa Infinite, Privilege and Platinum; British Airways Visa Infinite; ION and ION+ Visa (2022); moi RBC Visa and More Rewards RBC Visa (2025); Visa Platinum and Classic Low Rate; all commercial cards; Avion Visa business cards; Virtual Visa Debit; RBC Bank U.S. Visa Signature Black; City National Visa cards; Installments enabled by Visa (Nov 2022).
- Mastercard: WestJet RBC World Elite and WestJet RBC Mastercard (since about 2009 ⚠; refreshed Nov 2025); WestJet RBC World Elite Mastercard for Business (Aug 2025); RBC Cash Back and Cash Back Preferred World Elite; Business Cash Back Mastercard. About 11 Visa and 4 Mastercard personal cards on offer.
- Debit runs on Interac; no Debit Mastercard found. Caribbean debit is Visa ⚠.
- HSBC Canada's cards were Mastercard; the World Elite moved to Avion Visa Infinite, Cash Rewards to RBC Cash Back Mastercard, others probably to ION Visa ⚠.
- Contact: McKay and Michael Miebach at Mastercard's Canadian forum, 22 Apr 2026 ⚠. No network agreement or renewal date public. Not found: Click to Pay, Installments, Send/Move, Finicity, Agent Pay, Commercial Express, MDES named.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Offers and data inside Avion Rewards and the linked-card partners (Canadian Tire, Petro-Canada, Metro, Pattison); Agent Pay and Click to Pay on the WestJet and cash back cards (RBC's 2026 priority is agentic AI); Mastercard Move for International Money Transfers and newcomers; open finance as consumer-driven banking starts; Multi-Token Network for the CAD deposit token; risk tools through Moneris's new owner.
- Watch-outs: Visa holds the flagship, the commercial book and every recent launch except WestJet; Interac and the Real-Time Rail (Q4 2026) carry account payments; RBC builds its own loyalty, tokens and AI.

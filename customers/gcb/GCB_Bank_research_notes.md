# GCB Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `GCB_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and GCB × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own as reported in the Ghanaian press (H1 2026 statement, Daily Graphic, 29 Jul 2026; FY2025 results, 25 Mar 2026, via IC Securities and the Ghanaian Times) unless marked. GCB's live website is behind a bot check, so product pages were read from Internet Archive snapshots (Jan–Apr 2026) and the 2025 tariff guide. No annual report text, investor deck or agency rating was reachable. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- GCB Bank PLC (GSE: GCB), formerly Ghana Commercial Bank; founded 1953 as the Bank of the Gold Coast; listed 17 May 1996; 265m shares; government about 21% ⚠. Took on UT Bank and Capital Bank assets in 2017.
- H1 2026: PAT GH¢1.23bn (+46.4%), deposits GH¢51.5bn, net loans GH¢22.2bn, assets GH¢67.4bn, NPL 4.7%, CAR 15.9%, fees +98%. FY2025: PBT GH¢3.17bn, PAT about GH¢2.1bn, cost-to-income 47.2%.
- Largest lender (17.8% of loans) and deposit taker (12.37%) in 2025 (PwC 2026 survey). 184 branches, 340+ ATMs, 1m+ active app users. Market cap about GH¢10.9bn at Mar 2026 prices ⚠.
- Leadership: Prof. Joshua Alabi (Chair), Farihan Alhassan (MD since Jan 2025), Patrick George Quantson (CDO).
- Subsidiaries: GCB Capital; G-Money Financial Services (one of five Bank of Ghana dedicated e-money issuers).
- Strategy 2025–28: towards wholesale, commercial and transaction banking; digital; Afro-Asia desk (Ghana–China, CIPS/RMB proposed Oct 2025 ⚠); PAPSS gateway bank. FY2024 dividend blocked May 2025 over COCOBOD exposure.

## 2. GCB × Mastercard
- Oct 2008: first Mastercard principal in Ghana; Mastercard debit and Corporate Mastercard launched.
- Today: Mastercard and Visa debit tier for tier (Standard/Classic to Platinum); split not published ⚠. ReadyCash (default card) on gh-link. UnionPay since 2020.
- Visa: Signature and Infinite (Dec 2024), business debit and prepaid (about 2022 ⚠), expanded collaboration on data and marketing (Jun 2026). GhIPSS processes Visa domestic transactions (Jan 2024).
- Mar 2026: MD nominated to the Mastercard Africa Leadership Council, the first Ghanaian.
- No credit cards found ⚠. No tokenisation, Click to Pay, Move or fraud-tool deal with Mastercard found.

## 3. Market
- Mobile money GH¢4.54trn in 2025, 26.7m active users, 491k agents; GhIPSS Instant Pay GH¢712bn; gh-link switching shrinking. Mastercard opened an Accra office in Jan 2025 and has a card on every MTN MoMo wallet (2024).

## 4. Where Mastercard could land
- A World/World Elite tier against Visa Infinite; commercial and government virtual cards; a G-Money companion card; Mastercard Move for diaspora inflows; Tap on Phone for Lite Pay merchants; fraud scoring, tokens and offers for the app.

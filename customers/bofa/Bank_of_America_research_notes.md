# Bank of America — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Bank_of_America_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and BofA × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own (Q3 2026 results, 14 Oct 2026; Q4 2025 results; 2025 Form 10-K; Investor Day, 5 Nov 2025) unless marked. The Mastercard newsroom blocked automated access, so Mastercard-side confirmation of joint programmes is the main gap. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- Bank of America Corporation (NYSE: BAC), Charlotte. Nearly 70m clients; 38.9m consumer checking accounts (92% primary); about 4m small businesses; 50m active digital users. About 210,000 staff.
- Q3 2026: revenue $30.0bn, net income $6.9bn after a $1.2bn charge for OCC and FinCEN anti-money-laundering consent orders; ROTCE 12.6%; CET1 10.9%; deposits $2.039tn, loans $1.251tn. FY2025: net income $30.5bn, ROTCE 14.2%. Market cap $376.8bn (30 Sep 2026).
- Segments: Consumer Banking, Global Wealth & Investment Management, Global Banking (with Global Payments Solutions, $11bn revenue in 2024), Global Markets.
- Leadership: Brian Moynihan (chair and CEO), co-presidents Dean Athanasia and Jim DeMare, Alastair Borthwick (CFO), Holly O'Neill (consumer), Sharon Miller (business banking and merchant services), Mark Monaco (GPS).
- Investor Day targets: ROTCE 16–18%, efficiency 55–59%, card penetration of checking clients from 71% to 80%. Technology spend $14bn a year ⚠ another release says $13bn.
- Heritage: BankAmericard (1958), spun out as Visa (1976). Merger history ⚠ not re-checked. Deals: MDSec (Jul 2026), up to 49.9% of Jio Credit (Aug 2026).

## 2. BofA × Mastercard
- Visa-led consumer book: Customized Cash, Unlimited Cash, Travel Rewards, Premium Rewards and Elite (some ⚠ from card art); Atmos, Royal Caribbean, Air France KLM and Allegiant co-brands; debit ⚠ third-party sources. 2026 FIFA marketing with Visa.
- Mastercard: the Business Advantage small-business line (all three cards), the NCL World Mastercard, the BankAmericard ⚠ (card art, WalletHub). Acceptance and Maestro PIN routing at BofA Merchant Services.
- No BofA × Mastercard joint announcement since 2015 found; a 2007 pricing deal and Mastercard NFL debit are the last public ones. Commercial-card network not found.
- Bank-owned and in-house alternatives: Zelle ($556bn in 2025), Paze (all cards since Dec 2024), Custom Pay Plan (Jun 2026), BofA Rewards (17.6m enrolled), cross-border RTP (Jun 2026).
- Atmos single-issuer consolidation could pull Hawaiian's Mastercard volume to Visa ⚠ inference.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay for agentic commerce (BofA joined trust principles, 22 Sep 2026); fraud and payee-risk tools for Zelle and RTP; stablecoin rails via BVNK and the Global Money Platform; commercial virtual cards; card-linked offers for BofA Rewards and Deals; defending the small-business book.

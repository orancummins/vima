# Rabobank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Rabobank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Rabobank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (H1 2026 results, 4 Aug 2026; FY2025 results and appendix, 10 Feb 2026) unless marked. Rabobank's websites returned 403 to direct fetches and were read through a text proxy; the Mastercard and Visa newsrooms were not reached, so their side comes from Banken.nl. ⚠ marks items that are inferred, uncertain or secondary. FY2026 results due 9 Feb 2027 (Rabobank reports half-yearly).

## 1. The company
- Coöperatieve Rabobank U.A., Utrecht. Cooperative with members, no shareholders; 78 local banks in 5 regions. Roots in 1898 (Raiffeisen-Bank, Boerenleenbank), merged 1972, one legal entity from 2016. Rabobank Certificates on Euronext ⚠ (not re-checked); member count not found ⚠.
- H1 2026: net profit €2,694m (flat), impairments €562m (two South American wholesale cases), C/I 50.2%, RoE 9.5%, CET1 20.2%; DRB deposits €375.1bn, loans €293.0bn; W&R loans €142.7bn; DLL lease book €47.1bn; Private Banking AuM €83.1bn.
- FY2025: net profit €4,957m, income €16,424m, total assets €638.9bn, CET1 20.3%, mortgages 22.4% of new production (book €203.7bn).
- Segments: Domestic Retail Banking, Wholesale & Rural, Leasing (DLL), Property Development (BPD), Other. Brands: Rabobank, Obvion, Freo (+ Alfam/Defam), DLL, BPD, Rabo Investments.
- Ratings: Moody's Aa3 (21 Apr 2026), Fitch AA- (12 May 2026), S&P A+ ⚠ (2024 table).
- Leadership: Stefaan Decraene (Chair/CEO, since 2022, second term 2026), Bas Brouwers (CFO), Carlo van Kemenade (Retail NL), Suzan van Eeten (Tribe Lead Payments & Savings).
- ~48,000 staff (Aug 2026). Up to €2bn over three years for data, IT and AI; Agentic Hub (Jul 2026); new personalised app platform (Jun 2026); Rabo App #1 in NL in Sia benchmark (Oct 2026).
- Deals: Alfam from ABN AMRO to merge with Freo (Nov 2025; closing expected Q3 2026 ⚠); Belastingdienst house bank from 1 May 2026 (~275m transactions/yr); Qivalis member (May 2026); Belfius stake interest ⚠ press speculation.
- Food & agri abroad: W&R is the "global Food & Agri bank"; F&A 59% of W&R loans; rural lending AU €13.1bn, NA €9.3bn, NZ €6.9bn, Brazil €5.3bn; online savings AU/NZ €9.8bn (186,000 customers). No card issuing found abroad ⚠.

## 2. Rabobank × Mastercard
- Debit: Maestro replaced by Debit Mastercard (first online transaction 11 Feb 2022; rollout from Jun 2024) and Visa Debit (announced 31 Mar 2022) side by side; split undisclosed ⚠. Cards carry a Mastercard sustainability badge.
- Credit: GoldCard Visa by default since 2020 reissue, Mastercard on request; RaboCard holders got GoldCard benefits Feb 2026 ⚠ split undisclosed.
- Rabo BusinessCard: Visa or Mastercard; Mastercard ID Check / Visa Secure.
- 6 May 2026: first Dutch AI-agent payment on Mastercard Agent Pay, PayOS, Payment Passkeys (Van Eeten; Jan-Willem van der Schoot, Mastercard NL).
- Not Mastercard: iDEAL→Wero (EPI; ACI on SEPA Instant; iDEAL ends end-2027), INFORM RiskShield fraud, Rabo Identity Services (iDIN, itsme, eHerkenning), AEVI/dejamobile acceptance, Qivalis.
- No network contract, Click to Pay, Move, open-finance or commercial-card programme with Mastercard found ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay to production; larger share of debit reissues; fraud and identity for instant payments and Wero; commercial and virtual cards for W&R food & agri clients and DLL vendors; merchant tools for Smart Pay; spend insights and offers in the app; multi-token settlement beside Qivalis. All analysis ⚠.

## 4. How the page reads Rabobank
- Five discs: Foundation · Rails (debit, credit, business cards, iDEAL to Wero, acceptance, payments for the state, digital money) · Services (agentic payments, fraud, identity and APIs, personal banking) · Business lines (homes and households, SMEs and private banking, food & agri worldwide, DLL, property and investments) · Access (Rabo App, local banks, partners and portals). Six kinds of user, including farmers and food businesses. Lens areas read from the inventory.
- A "Group" key sits beside the four reported segments for group-wide areas.
- Gaps: Visa/Mastercard split on debit and credit; acquiring processor; business debit network; current S&P rating; member count; Alfam completion.

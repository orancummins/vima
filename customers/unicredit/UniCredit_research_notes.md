## 1. The company
- UniCredit S.p.A., Milan (Euronext Milan: UCG). €76.19 on 16 Oct 2026; market value about €115bn ⚠ (estimate from tangible book). Rated A- (S&P, positive) / A3 (Moody's, under review for upgrade) / A- (Fitch).
- 2Q26: revenue €6.52bn, net profit €2.91bn, RoTE 21.7%, CET1 14.3%, cost/income 35.2%; loans €442.6bn, deposits €503.8bn. FY25: net profit €10.6bn, RoTE 19.2%. About 17.5m clients (15m active); 67,118 FTE; 3,075 branches.
- Segments (1H26 net profit): Italy €2.32bn, Germany (HVB) €1.16bn, CEE €1.12bn, Austria €0.74bn, Russia (run-down) €0.27bn. Group Client Solutions runs the product factories, including Group Payments Solutions (1H26 revenue €1.3bn).
- 13 banks: UniCredit (Italy, CEE), HypoVereinsbank, Bank Austria, UniCredit Bulbank ⚠, UniCredit Bank Romania (with Alpha Bank Romania), buddy, onemarkets, UniCredit NV/SA (ex-Aion, with Vodeno).
- Leadership: Pietro Carlo Padoan (Chair), Andrea Orcel (CEO, Head of Italy), Richard Burton (Client Solutions), Ali Khan (Digital & Information), Marion Höllinger (Germany), Teodora Petkova (CEE). Head of Group Payments not public.
- UniCredit Unlimited (Feb 2026): RoTE >23%, cost/income ~33% in 2028, ~€30bn distributions over three years. Google Cloud for all 13 banks; 35+ AI apps; 360+ AI agents in Austria.
- Commerzbank: 0.485-share exchange offer (16 Mar 2026); 17.60% tendered plus 26.77% held; overall position 49.65%. ECB/BaFin cleared EURO Kartensysteme and Commerz Globalpay on 15–16 Oct 2026; other approvals pending.
- Other deals: Alpha Bank Romania (Nov 2024), Aion/Vodeno €376m (Mar 2025), Italian life JVs (Jun 2025), card complete stake sold (Sep 2025), Alpha Bank ~29.8% (Jan 2026), 2,500 First Data merchants in Austria. Banco BPM offer withdrawn ⚠; Generali stake ⚠.

## 2. UniCredit × Mastercard
- 26 May 2023: one multi-market agreement for 13 banks, 12 markets, ~20m cards, all card products; Priceless and ESG. Since then card transactions +20%, 2:3 card-to-cash ratio. End date not public.
- Germany: UniCredit Card (Mastercard debit) replaces HVB girocard with Maestro and HVB Visa Debit; girocard still issued; HVB Mastercard credit cards. Switch date not stated ⚠.
- Italy: MyOne debit "Mastercard or VISA" (split not disclosed); Flexia Gold, Miles & More, Ferrari Metal and Genius Pay on Mastercard; Flexia Classic, Genius Card and Card Click network not stated ⚠.
- Austria: Touch Card with Mastercard (Feb 2025); in-house credit card (Mar 2025), network not stated ⚠.
- CEE network splits not public ⚠. buddybank used Mastercard processing at launch (2018) ⚠. SIA (now Nexi) processes in Italy, Austria and Germany ⚠.
- Not found (not proof of absence ⚠): Agent Pay, Mastercard Move, open banking, commercial cards or acquiring work with Mastercard.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Visa's share of MyOne; the Commerzbank and mBank card estate and Commerz Globalpay; scam scoring beside HVB's AI fraud platform and instant payments; gateway, tokens and Click to Pay for Bank Austria's acquirer; Mastercard Move for FAST and correspondent flows; commercial cards for the Mittelstand; Agent Pay; links to Qivalis and the digital euro.

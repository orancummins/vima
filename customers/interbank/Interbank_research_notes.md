## 1. The company
- Banco Internacional del Perú S.A.A. – Interbank, Lima; founded 1897, privatised 1994. About 99% owned by Intercorp Financial Services (NYSE: IFS), the Rodríguez-Pastor family's financial holding, which also owns Interseguro, Inteligo and Izipay. 6,742 staff (end-2025). Market cap and current ratings not confirmed ⚠.
- 2Q26: net income S/402.5m, ROE 15.6%; gross loans S/52.6bn, deposits S/57.0bn; NIM 4.9%, cost of risk 2.1%, efficiency 42.5%. Shares: loans 13.7%, consumer 18.1%, mortgages 16.0%, commercial 11.3%, deposits 13.3%.
- FY2025: net income S/1,458.5m (ROE 16.2%); card fees S/464.9m; capital ratio 16.0%. IFS: about S/1.9bn, ROE 16.8%; 2026 guidance ROE above 17%.
- Leadership: Carlos Rodríguez-Pastor (Chair), Carlos Tori (CEO since May 2024, ex-EVP Payments and chair of Izipay), Luis Felipe Castellanos (IFS CEO), Michela Casassa (CFO), Alexander Woodman (Retail and Channels), Claudio Querol (Data, Analytics & AI).
- Digital: 86% of retail and 76% of commercial clients digital; AVI assistant; One App Negocios volumes +117% Jan–Jun 2026. Anthropic enterprise agreement (Aug 2026).
- Deals: Izipay 100% (Dec 2021 signed, Apr 2022 closed); Rappibank (2021–23); US$500m senior bond (Jan 2026).
- Risks: El Niño (H2 2026), new government (July 2026), pension-fund withdrawals.

## 2. Interbank × Mastercard
- Credit: Visa main range (Clásica to Infinite, Cashback, Premia); Amex range (Blue to The Platinum Card), issued with BCP ⚠ 25 years; Mastercard Clásica, Oro, Platinum ⚠ small, and one guide lists none.
- Debit: Visa Débito Benefit ⚠. Vea co-brand on Visa ⚠.
- Izipay (PMP): Mastercard's Peruvian acquirer from 1999 ⚠; Visa licence 2020 under multi-brand acquiring; accepts Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Diners and Plin QR; 45%+ share (own claim, 2024).
- Plin (with BBVA and Scotiabank, Jan 2020): launched on Visa and Mastercard rails ⚠ current; 2.8m monthly active; Plin with credit card (2026); Plin WhatsApp (2025).
- No joint Interbank–Mastercard announcements found for 2020–2026 (tokenisation, Click to Pay, Send, Agent Pay) ⚠.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Merchant data and risk at Izipay; tokenised cards inside Plin; scam and fraud scoring; card-linked offers for Millas Benefit; Agent Pay for AVI and Plin WhatsApp; business cards for a small-business book growing 31% a year.

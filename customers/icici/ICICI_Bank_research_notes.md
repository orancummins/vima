# ICICI Bank — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `ICICI_Bank_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and ICICI Bank × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the bank's own unless marked: the Q1 FY27 investor presentation and the Annual Report 2025-26 (both filed on BSE, 18 Jul 2026), the Q1 FY27 call (19 Jul 2026) and the card pages on `icici.bank.in`. Q2 FY27 is not yet reported. ICICI publishes no split by network; Mastercard's newsroom could not be read. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed.

## 1. The company
- ICICI Bank Limited (NSE, BSE; NYSE: IBN), registered in Vadodara, run from Mumbai. 124,324 staff (Mar 2026), 7,608 branches and 12,190 ATMs/recyclers (Jun 2026). Moody's Baa3, S&P BBB. About ₹9.5 lakh crore market value at ₹1,326 (1 Oct 2026) ⚠ secondary.
- Q1 FY27: PAT ₹148.05bn (+15.9%), consolidated ₹154.40bn; NII ₹243.84bn, NIM 4.36%; deposits ₹18.34trn (+14.0%), advances ₹16.31trn (+19.6%); ROA 2.49%, CET1 16.19%, net NPA 0.35%. FY2026 PAT ₹501.47bn.
- Book: retail ₹8.07trn (mortgages ₹5.13trn; credit cards ₹532bn, −1.9% y/y), business banking ₹3.50trn (+28.2%), rural ₹1.04trn (+35.4%), corporate ₹3.27trn.
- Segments: Retail Banking, Wholesale Banking, Treasury, Other Banking. Group: ICICI Life, ICICI Lombard, ICICI Pru AMC (53%, listed 19 Dec 2025), ICICI Securities (wholly owned Mar 2025), ICICI Home Finance, UK and Canada banks. Stake in ICICI Merchant Services sold to Fiserv (Apr 2025).
- Leadership: chairman Pradeep Kumar Sinha; MD & CEO Sandeep Bakhshi (since Oct 2018; reappointed 4 Oct 2026 – 3 Oct 2028, RBI approval 22 May 2026); EDs Sandeep Batra, Rakesh Jha, Ajay Kumar Gupta; CFO Anindya Banerjee.
- Technology: "Bank to BankTech"; iMobile one-app, InstaBIZ, iLens, iCRM, Merchant STACK; GenAI with human in the loop. No agentic-payments partner named.

## 2. ICICI Bank × Mastercard
- Four networks: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, RuPay; many cards issued in pairs (Mastercard & Amex, or Visa & RuPay). 16.1% of India's cards and 16.2% of spend (May 2026), third behind HDFC and SBI Card. No split by network ⚠.
- Mastercard: Coral, Rubyx, Sapphiro and Emeralde (Mastercard leg, with Amex); Emeralde Private Metal (Mastercard Moments); Diamant on Mastercard World (Nov 2012); MakeMyTrip co-brand (Mastercard + RuPay); Mastercard World Chip NRI debit; 2025 marketing-services case study on debit activation, about US$9.75m incremental spend ⚠.
- Visa: Amazon Pay ICICI (Oct 2018; 1m by Oct 2020, 2m by Jul 2021; RuPay variant not found ⚠); Times Black and 1838 Reserve (reported launched Oct 2026, Visa Infinite Privilege ⚠); Wealth Select Infinite debit; Business Advantage. Amex: gemstone pairs, Business Ascent and Essential. RuPay: credit on UPI since 1 Dec 2023, NCMC debit.
- Regulation: network choice at issue and renewal since 6 Sep 2024; RBI curbs on Mastercard new cardholders Jul 2021 – Jun 2022; RBI fine of ₹97.8 lakh (Apr 2025) partly on card conduct; UPI merchant fee above ₹2,000 from 15 Oct 2026 ⚠.
- Not live on Apple Pay (India launch 30 Sep 2026, Axis only). Not named in Mastercard's (Feb 2026) or Visa's (GFF 2026) India agentic pilots.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Network choice on each new and renewed card, above all the Visa-only Amazon Pay base; a World Legend answer to 1838 Reserve; card-book growth (−1.9% while loans grow 19.6%); tokens for an Apple Pay entry; Agent Pay; purchase and travel cards for MNCs, GCCs and a 28%-growth SME book; Move for Money2India remittances.

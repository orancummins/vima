# Crédit Agricole — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Credit_Agricole_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Crédit Agricole × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are the group's own (Q2/H1 2026 results, 31 Jul 2026; Q4/FY2025 results, 4 Feb 2026; IKB release, 12 Oct 2026) unless marked. The group press site (presse.credit-agricole.com) refused connections, so CAPS press releases were not listed. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results are due 30 October 2026.

## 1. The company
- Cooperative group: Local Banks (12.3m mutual shareholders) own the Regional Banks (about 39 ⚠), which own 63.5% of Crédit Agricole S.A. (Euronext Paris; HQ Montrouge) via SAS Rue La Boétie. 55m customers, 160,000 employees, 8,065 branches, 46 countries. Founded 1885/1894; CASA listed 2001.
- Q2 2026: Group revenues €10.9bn, net income €2.78bn, CET1 17.2%; CASA revenues €7.4bn (record), net income €2.05bn, RoTE 14.3%, CET1 11.3%. FY2025: Group net income €8.75bn; CASA €7.07bn (RoTE 13.5%).
- Segments: Retail Banking (Regional Banks; LCL; CA Italia, Poland, Egypt, Ukraine), Asset Gathering (Amundi, CA Assurances, Indosuez), SFS (CAPFM: Sofinco, Agos, CA Auto Bank, Leasys; CAL&F), Large Customers (CACIB, CACEIS), Corporate Centre.
- Leadership: Eric Vial (Chairman), Olivier Gavalda (CEO since May 2025), Jérôme Grivet (Deputy CEO), Gérald Grégoire (customers, development, innovation; payments), Clotilde L'Angevin (finance), Stéphane Priami (international; CAPFM), Philippe Marquetty (CEO, CA Payment Services).
- ACT 2028: CASA revenue >€30bn, net income >€8.5bn, cost/income <55%, RoTE >14%; 8m new French customers; 2m in Germany. AI: €500m 2026–28, Crédit Agricole Artificial Intelligence, agentic platform. Market cap about €47bn ⚠ estimate.
- Deals: Banco BPM 29.3%; Milleis (Apr 2026); BCC-Grupo Cajamar; CAWL bought out from Worldline (30 Jun 2026); CA Deutschland (9 Oct 2026); IKB signed (12 Oct 2026, closing by end Q2 2027). Euro stablecoin EURXT ⚠ details.

## 2. Crédit Agricole × Mastercard
- Regional Banks: every card co-badged CB; Mastercard-only tiers (Mastercard, EKO, Globe Trotter, biometric Gold and World Elite, Mozaïc youth); Visa alongside at Classic, Premier and Infinite; Cartwin both. Premium 21% of stock. No group split by network published ⚠.
- Business cards: Mastercard Professionnel, Business, Business World (no Visa listed).
- LCL: mixed (Visa Premier, Infinite, Cléo; Mastercard Platinum+, Inventive). Mouv network ⚠.
- CA Italia: Visa debit (Visa partnership Jul 2021) + Bancomat; Nexi credit ⚠ scheme; Amex. CA Polska: Visa and Mastercard, Priceless on Maxima. CA Egypt: Visa (renewed 2021).
- Joint work: biometric trial (Jun 2019, CAPS); France's first production agentic payment on Agent Pay with Worldline (25 Jun 2026; Marquetty, Barbara Sessa).
- No public Crédit Agricole item found on Click to Pay, Installments, Move, Open Finance or Mastercard fraud tools (no evidence, not proof of absence).
- Competition: CB routes most domestic payments (Yavin index 63.6% H2 2025); Wero (~5m monthly users at CA/LCL Sep 2025; e-commerce date at CA ⚠); Paiement Mobile on iPhone (Jun 2026, DMA); Amex acceptance (Oct 2023); Visa at LCL and Italy.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay at scale for customer-facing agents; tokens for Paiement Mobile; acquirer risk and Click to Pay at CAWL; scam scoring for Wero and instant payments; commercial cards for SMEs, mid-caps and IKB; cards for Crédit Agricole Deutschland ⚠ network not announced; Priceless and premium benefits in France.

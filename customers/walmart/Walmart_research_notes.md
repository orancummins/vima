# Walmart: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Walmart_Platform.html`. One research thread (the company, and Walmart and Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Walmart's own (Q2 FY27 results, 20 Aug 2026; FY26 10-K, 13 Mar 2026; Q2 FY27 10-Q) unless marked, with OnePay's newsroom, Synchrony's and Klarna's releases, and Payments Dive for the fee case. Walmart's fiscal year ends 31 January. walmart.com, Mastercard's newsroom, Axis Bank's card page and Walmart Canada's card site could not be read. ⚠ marks items from secondary sources or prior knowledge. Q3 FY27 results are due mid-November.

## 1. The company
- Walmart Inc. (Nasdaq: WMT since 8 Dec 2025, moved from the NYSE), Bentonville, Arkansas. Market value ≈$943bn (16 Oct 2026); ≈2.1m associates; 11,000 stores and clubs.
- FY26: revenue $713.2bn (+4.7%); Walmart U.S. $483.0bn (eCommerce ≈$99.6bn), International $130.4bn, Sam's Club U.S. $93.0bn; operating income $29.8bn; advertising ≈$6.4bn (+46%); membership fees $4.4bn.
- Q2 FY27: revenue $187.9bn (+5.9%); eCommerce +23%; ads +38%; membership fees +17%; ≈$2.9bn tariff refunds. FY27 guidance raised. Walmart+ member numbers not published ⚠.
- Leadership: John Furner (CEO from 1 Feb 2026, after Doug McMillon), John David Rainey (CFO, ex-PayPal), David Guggina (Walmart U.S.), Chris Nicholas (International), Latriece Watkins (Sam's Club), Daniel Danker (AI); Greg Penner (Chair). OnePay: Omer Ismail (CEO).
- OnePay: with Ribbit Capital (2021); banks Coastal Community and Lead; ≈6m monthly users, ≈$50bn annualised payments, >$4bn valuation ⚠ (Bloomberg, people familiar). Builder Card (Apr 2026), Upgrade loans (Jul 2026), teen banking (Sep 2026), crypto via Zerohash ⚠, Tempo validator (Apr 2026), Open USD consortium (Jun 2026, OnePay's role ⚠), OnePay for Agents (MCP, Jun 2026), ChatGPT and Dots plugin (12 Oct 2026), AML agent (May 2026).
- AI shopping: OpenAI Instant Checkout (Sep 2025) dropped for Sparky inside ChatGPT and Gemini (Mar–Apr 2026) ⚠ secondary; UCP co-developer (Jan 2026).
- Checkout: Walmart Pay (QR, 2016); NFC tap to pay from 24 Aug 2026, all US stores by end-2026, fuel by end-2027.
- International: PhonePe (618m users, 44.9m merchants; IPO cleared Jan 2026, postponed Mar 2026 ⚠); super.money (UPI, RuPay card); Cashi (IFPE; debit pilot Apr 2025, network ⚠); Walmex co-brands with Inbursa and Bradesco (networks ⚠); BMO at Walmart Canada (Dec 2025).

## 2. Walmart and Mastercard
- Cards: OnePay CashRewards Mastercard (Synchrony, announced 9 Jun 2025 with Linda Kirkpatrick; live autumn 2025; 5% at Walmart for Walmart+); Sam's Club Mastercard (Synchrony, renewed Jan 2025); Capital One Walmart Mastercard (2019–2024, ≈10m customers). Off-network: Spend Card (closed loop), Klarna instalments (exclusive, Mar 2025), MoneyCard (Green Dot; Visa and Mastercard named ⚠). Unstated: OnePay debit, Builder Card ⚠.
- Acceptance: every network; contactless and wallets from Aug 2026.
- Fee fight: Walmart is lead objector to the Nov 2025 Visa–Mastercard settlement (10 bp cut for 5 years; 1.25% standard cap for 8 years; honour-all-cards relief). Asked to leave the class (Dec 2025); 299-page objection (Sep 2026); networks call objectors "a vocal minority" (16 Oct 2026); final hearing 16 Nov 2026.
- Shared ground: UCP (Mastercard supports), Open USD (Mastercard a partner). No Walmart announcement on Agent Pay, Click to Pay, Installments, Move or fraud tools found.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Agent Pay and agentic tokens for Sparky and OnePay's AI plugins; tokens and Click to Pay across the new NFC estate and walmart.com; networks for OnePay debit and the Builder Card; Mastercard Installments on the OnePay card; fraud and identity for OnePay; Move for money transfers and Cashi remittances; Cashi and Walmex cards; a merchant deal beyond the settlement.

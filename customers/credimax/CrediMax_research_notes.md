# CrediMax: research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

These notes go with `CrediMax_Platform.html`. They condense one research thread covering the company and its relationship with Mastercard.

**Sources.** CrediMax publishes no stand-alone accounts, card counts or merchant counts. Figures come from:
- CrediMax's website and card pages
- its 2024 ESG report
- BBK's 2025 annual report and its H1 2026 results
- Bahraini and regional press
- BENEFIT

⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources, undated pages or inference.

## 1. The company
- **Legal status.** CrediMax B.S.C. (c) is 100% owned by BBK. The Central Bank of Bahrain licenses it as a financing company. It is based in Seef, Manama.
- **History.** It began in July 1991 as BBK's card unit, which issued Bahrain's first credit card. It became an independent subsidiary in April 1999.
- **Capital.** Its total capital ratio was 62.2% at end-2025.
- **Staff.** 109 at end-2024, of whom 99.9% are Bahraini and 38% are women.
- **Subsidiaries.**
  - Global Payment Services: 70%, card processing.
  - 973Labs: 60%, with Invita holding 40%.
- **Leadership.**
  - Chairman: Yaser Alsharifi, who is also BBK Group CEO.
  - CEO: Ahmed A. Seyadi (named May 2026 ⚠; he is not on the About page).
  - CFO: Yasser Shaaban.
  - Chief Technology and Transformation Officer: Nada Mohamed A.Rahman.
- **Parent (BBK).**
  - 2025 net profit: BD80m, a record.
  - H1 2026 net profit: BD42.5m.
  - Total assets: BD4.9bn.
  - Cards sit in the Retail segment, which had a segment result of BD25.1m in 2025.
- **HSBC Bahrain.** BBK bought HSBC Bahrain's retail business, about 75,000 customers. HSBC credit cards became CrediMax cards from 28 November 2025. The networks of the new cards are not stated ⚠.
- **Merger.** BBK and NBB signed a merger MoU in November 2025. NBB proposed terms on 26 April 2026. BBK said on 21 July 2026 that talks are continuing, with no completion date ⚠.
- **Pricing** (effective November 2025).
  - Interest: 1.85–2.75% a month.
  - FX fee: 2.75–3.3%.
  - Scheme charges come from Visa, Mastercard, JCB and UnionPay.

## 2. CrediMax and Mastercard
- **Credit range.** There are 14 credit cards: 7 Mastercard, 6 Visa and 1 JCB.
  - **Mastercard:** World Elite, World, World Premier, Platinum, Platinum Advance, the talabat card (May 2026) and Danat (with LuLu).
  - **Visa:** Infinite, Signature, Signature Premier, Platinum, Platinum Advance and Platinum Cashback.
- **Other cards on Mastercard:** iLead (youth), Cardy (prepaid), and the Corporate and World Business cards.
- **Other cards on Visa:**
  - Tayseer Islamic cards (Platinum, Signature, Infinite; terms revised July 2026 and they mention both schemes ⚠)
  - Student Card
  - Cardy EX (multi-currency)
  - Gift Card
  - Visa Corporate and Signature Business
  - Visa Commercial Pay virtual cards (2025 ⚠)
- **Mastercard strategic partnership.** Renewed on 13 July 2023 by Seyadi and Khalid Elgibali of Mastercard. It covered:
  - Digital First
  - contactless and QR
  - exploring cyber intelligence
- **Click to Pay.** First in Bahrain, launched via Mastercard Gateway in April–May 2024 by Seyadi with Mastercard's Maria Medvedeva. The gateway's Hosted Session and Hosted Checkout modes suggest Mastercard Gateway runs e-commerce acquiring ⚠.
- **Award.** Mastercard named CrediMax "Leader in spend on Premium Credit Cards" in May 2024.
- **Cyber workshop.** A Mastercard Cyber Crisis Simulation workshop ran in May 2025.
- **Acquiring.** POS terminals and a gateway that accepts every scheme plus BENEFIT debit and mada. Also:
  - instalments at the POS
  - BNPL through ONE App (Al Salam Bank, 11 Oct 2026)
  - acceptance at Bapco Tazweed stations (2023)
- **Domestic rails.**
  - **BENEFIT:** 22% owned by BBK. It processed 261.2m transfers worth BD19.3bn in H1 2026.
  - **BenefitPay:** 246m transfers worth BD5.4bn.
  - **Fawri+:** 248.1m transfers (+8%).
  - **Mastercard and BENEFIT:** they signed an MoU in May 2024.

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- Mastercard Installments at CrediMax terminals, set against ONE App's BNPL.
- Fraud and identity scoring for a market where account-to-account payments dominate.
- Premium tiers for the reissued HSBC book.
- Virtual commercial cards, set against Visa Commercial Pay.
- Card-linked offers for SAVUR, talabat and Batelco.
- Acquirer risk tools.
- A merged BBK–NBB card book.

# Klarna Group — research notes behind the platform (as at 17 October 2026)

Companion to `Klarna_Platform.html`. One research thread (company and Klarna × Mastercard), condensed. Figures are Klarna's own (Q2 2026 results, 18 Aug 2026; Q1 2026, 14 May 2026; FY2025, 26 Feb 2026; Klarna Holding AB annual report 2025) unless marked. Klarna's governance pages and SEC filings were not reachable. ⚠ marks items that rest on secondary sources or could not be confirmed. Q3 2026 results had not been published.

## 1. The company
- Klarna Group plc (NYSE: KLAR), Stockholm-founded (2005); Klarna Bank AB licensed by Finansinspektionen since June 2017, with EEA passport and branches in Denmark, France, Ireland, Germany and the UK. IPO at $40 on 9 Sep 2025; $13.02 and about $4.9bn market value on 16 Oct 2026. 2,795 employees (end-2025).
- Q2 2026: GMV $36.6bn (+18%, US +27%), revenue $1.042bn (+27%), TMD $446m, net income $9m; 120m active consumers, 1.2m+ merchants, 6.5m Klarna Card users in 16 countries, 2m paying members. FY2025: GMV $127.9bn, revenue $3.5bn, adjusted operating profit $65m.
- 2026 guidance cut in August: GMV $149–151bn, revenue $4.08–4.16bn; TMD raised; Germany (largest market) weaker; shares fell about 23%.
- Funding: about 90% deposits (SEK 121.8bn end-2025); forward flows with Nelnet, Elliott, a German facility; Värde SRT; Coinbase USDC.
- Leadership: Sebastian Siemiatkowski (CEO), Niclas Neglén (CFO), David Sykes (CCO), David Sandström (CMO), David Fock (CPDO), Camilla Giesecke (COO); Michael Moritz chairs the plc, Roger Ferguson Jr. the bank ⚠.
- Strategy: "the everyday finance network": payments first, then banking (card, Balance, savings, P2P, memberships, mobile). Agentic work with Google (AP2, UCP, Gemini) and OpenAI (ChatGPT app); KlarnaUSD on Stripe's Tempo ⚠ mainnet status. Klarna Bank USA applied for in July 2026.

## 2. Klarna × Mastercard
- Klarna Card on Visa Flexible Credential in every market (US via WebBank from July 2025; Europe and UK from Sep–Oct 2025; Marqeta processes). Earlier cards (US 2022 and 2024, German debit 2021) were Visa too; One-time card Visa ⚠.
- Visa invested in Klarna and planned a partnership in June 2017.
- Mastercard: no network agreement, issuing programme, agentic, fraud or open-finance deal found (2015–2026). Mastercard Gateway lists Klarna Pay Later, Pay Now and Financing ⚠ start date and volumes not public. Shoppers fund Pay Now and repayments with Mastercard debit cards ⚠ (third-party help pages).
- Klarna pitches its network as an alternative to card schemes and KlarnaUSD as a way to "challenge old networks".

## 3. Where Mastercard could land
- A second network or instalment credential for the Klarna Card, above all if Klarna Bank USA takes over US issuing from WebBank.
- Agent Pay beside AP2 and UCP; Move for cross-border P2P and payouts to non-Klarna users; scam scoring for Balance and P2P; card-linked offers for 2m members; acquirer distribution through Mastercard Gateway; stablecoin settlement.

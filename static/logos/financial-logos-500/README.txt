FINANCIAL SERVICES LOGO COLLECTION
Prepared 17 October 2026

500 distinct named institutions, providers and payment brands.
74 SVG assets plus 426 PNG website brand icons.
PNG copies of SVGs are supplied separately for PowerPoint and other slide tools.

START HERE: Open browse.html in a web browser. Use its search and category filter.
index.csv contains each name, category, domain, asset filename, format, dimensions and source URL.

SELECTION
This is a curated global reference collection, not a verified ranking of the world's top 500 firms by assets, revenue, payment volume or Mastercard revenue. It includes banks, selected investment firms and credit unions, networks, processors, acquirers, PSPs, wallets, payment infrastructure and fintech brands. Subsidiaries/product brands can appear separately from their parent. Categories are practical groupings, not regulatory classifications.

ASSET QUALITY
Website brand icons are often symbols or compact marks rather than full wordmarks. 123 supplied PNGs are below 64 pixels wide; their dimensions are listed in index.csv. They have not been upscaled. SVG-to-PNG copies are 512 pixels wide. Repository assets can include background shapes or older branding. Assets have passed file-format checks; all 500 identities and current branding have not been individually visually certified. Check important marks against the institution's current brand kit before external publication.
